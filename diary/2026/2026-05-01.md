Cleared the barn loft. Seventy years of hay, some of it older than me; a hay sledge with iron runners, which the folk museum in Utne was delighted to take; three wooden rakes; a box of letters addressed to Ragnhild's mother, which we have put aside for Ragnhild without reading; and a swallows' nest from last year in the rafters, which we have promised to leave exactly where it is. Swallows come back. The beams are good, Jonas says, all but two. The roof is not good at all.
