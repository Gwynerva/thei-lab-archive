Seven years ago today I went to the rescue centre in Edinburgh to look at a black cat called Duchess and came home with a ginger tom who had no name, a torn ear and a way of looking at me through the bars as if I were late.

## Where he has slept

- on whatever I was drawing on the table in Leith, for two years, so that half my maps of that time have a ginger hair in the ink
- on the roof, once, in October 2020, which we do not discuss
- in a crate in the hold of a plane, for nine hours, never forgiven
- on the windowsill in Sandviken, counting the rain with me
- in the barn loft, last June, for one very long night
- on the bench by the stove, now, mostly

## Now

He is about ten. Stiffer, thinner, greyer round the mouth, and more affectionate than he has ever been, which the vet says is common and which I choose to take personally. He eats a little and often. He sleeps about sixteen hours. He still bites the vet.

I recorded him this evening, on my lap by the stove, because I have recorded rain on three roofs, a harbour full of gulls and the first fire in this house, and never once him.
