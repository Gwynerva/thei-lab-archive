The autumn term began tonight, my fifth cohort. I taught from the kitchen table, oak, made by my husband (still a strange word, getting less strange by the day), with the fjord going dark in the window behind me and Biscuit asleep on the warm laptop charger. Faces in little boxes, bookshelves and cats behind them, eight Monday evenings ahead. I said the first sentence I always say: "Every map is an argument about what matters." Somebody wrote it down. That always makes me nervous.
