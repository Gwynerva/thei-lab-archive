The old kitchen's door frame has been standing against the barn wall since November, waiting for the stove. Today I looked at it and saw four sides of a loom.

Nana had one, Mum says, a little upright one in the corner of her front room at the Bay, sold in the seventies. There is one photograph of it and nothing of anything she wove. Jonas measured the frame, said "a weekend", and drew it on the back of an envelope. In boatbuilder, a weekend means a fortnight.
