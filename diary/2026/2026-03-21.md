## Five o'clock

The electrician whistled all day, as promised, and at five o'clock switched everything on. The lights came on. The fridge, which has lived in the hall since November, came in from the cold and hummed. The first thing we cooked in the new kitchen was pancakes, because that was what we had. The second thing was Sigrid's fish soup, for Sigrid and Eirik and Kari, at the new table under the west window, with the evening light that Ragnhild asked us to keep.

## Washing up

After they had gone I washed up, in a sink that drains into a pipe and not a bucket, and it took me twice as long as it needed to, because I kept stopping with my hands in the hot water to look at the fjord going dark. Jonas dried. Neither of us said anything for a long time, and then he said "Well," and I said "I know."
