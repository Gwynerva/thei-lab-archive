Out of Lerwick at eight in a flat calm, motoring, the sails up and doing nothing, past Bressay lighthouse the other way. Shetland went down behind us faster than Norway did on the way out, being so low; by noon it was only a cloud that might have been land.

The sea like grey silk all day. Jonas said Nana's line, the one about calm seas, in what he believes is a Yorkshire accent. I have told him it is not.
