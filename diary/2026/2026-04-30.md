The thirtieth map, inked and washed and lettered, finished at eleven at night, and the scans sent to Fjellstue before I could change my mind. I put the brush in the jar and sat for a while and did not feel what I had expected to feel, which was triumph. It was more like the end of a long walk: tired, quiet, a little sorry to stop.
