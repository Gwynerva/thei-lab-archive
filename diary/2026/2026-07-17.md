Last full day. Swimming in the shallows by the boathouse, Isla in armbands shrieking at the cold and asking for more. Clare in up to her waist, swearing in a nurse's vocabulary. Me in properly, to show off, at sixteen degrees, which is warm for here. Supper on the porch until ten, and Isla fell asleep on Jonas, who did not move for an hour in case he woke her.
