Fetched Biscuit from Sigrid's. He had been, she reported, "a gentleman", and had slept every night on the dog's bed while the dog slept on the floor. He came home, walked through every room as if checking the inventory, and lay down on the bench by the cold stove. We missed Sankthans; the bonfires along the shore were the best for years, Sigrid says. We were on the North Sea. It was a fair swap.
