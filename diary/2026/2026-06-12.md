## The day

The early ferry from Utne with the last of the food, the long road in over Kvamskogen, and the whole afternoon on the pontoon in Bergen, stowing and restowing and checking things we had already checked. Jonas went up the mast to look at a shackle that did not need looking at. I bought ice cream at the fish market and ate it sitting on the lifelines, looking at the boat and thinking: that, across that.

The last things aboard, in the order I ticked them:

- [x] the fruit cake from Whitby, in the dry locker, unopened
- [x] the paper charts in their plastic sleeve, the old one of the Sound on top
- [x] two packets of pills, one in each jacket
- [x] a notebook, a pencil, a spare pencil

## Going

At six in the evening we slipped the lines. Bryggen went by in the low sun, then Nordnes, then the last islands, and at about nine Jonas said "That's Norway, then," in the voice he uses for the end of a film.

## Midnight

It is midnight and I am on watch. The sea is calm and very large. The compass light is red. I can hear Jonas asleep below, and the water going past the hull, and nothing else in the world at all.
