To Bergen for a shakedown sail, the boat back on her own pontoon in the city: up the Byfjord in a fresh northerly with a reef in, out past Askøy and home again. Everything worked except the forward hatch, which leaks, and me, who was sick once over the lee rail and then fine. Jonas fixed the hatch. Nobody can fix me; I will take the pills.
