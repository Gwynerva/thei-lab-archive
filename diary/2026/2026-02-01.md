Biscuit did not come up to bed last night. I found him at six on the bench by the stove, curled round himself on the old blanket, and he opened one eye and shut it again as if to say he had moved downstairs and had not thought to mention it. He has the ramp. He does not want the ramp. He wants the stove. So the stove it is, and I have started banking it up at night for him, which Jonas says is how it begins.
