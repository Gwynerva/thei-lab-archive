The loom. Jonas cut the door frame into four pieces at the weekend, showed me how to mark a half-lap joint, and went away, which is how he teaches. My first four corners were bad. I cut them again, less bad. Tonight they are glued and pegged with oak pegs from the loft and clamped on the trestles with every clamp he owns. He keeps going out to look at them, and coming back in, and not saying anything.
