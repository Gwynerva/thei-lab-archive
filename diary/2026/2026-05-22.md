## The proofs

They came on the morning ferry and the post van brought them up the hill: the whole book, on proper paper, the maps in colour for the first time. I laid them out on the new table and walked round it for half an hour before I could pick up a pen.

::media[The map the book's overview page is built on, before I drew over it]{file="map-hardanger" layout="natural"}

## What I see

The maps are good. I can say that now, which I could not have said in the winter. The colours have printed a shade warmer than I painted them, which suits the fjord and does not suit the glacier. The lettering holds. The little drawings, the red hut on the vidda, the monks' steps, the boathouses at Utne, look as though they belong.

## Sigrid

She came up at four to see, turned every page without a word, and at the second walk stopped and tapped the page and said, "That's my farm." It is: the path goes right past her gate. She asked for a proof of that page to take home, and I gave her it, and she folded it into her apron pocket as if it were a letter.
