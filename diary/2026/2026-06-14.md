## Land

A rough night and then a morning of fog so thick we took turns at the bow, listening. When it lifted, a little before eleven, there was a low grey line on the starboard bow that I took for more cloud for half an hour, and then didn't. Shetland: no mountains, no trees, just long low hills the colour of a seal, lying on the sea. I shouted "Land!", which I have wanted to do since I read *Swallows and Amazons* at nine.

## Into Lerwick

In with the town on our left, grey stone and painted gables and boats, and tied up at half past one. Forty-three hours from Bergen.

## The first night ashore

Showers that lasted twenty minutes each. Chips. A long walk round the town, holding on to walls. Then back to the boat, which is the only place that keeps still now, and sleep from eight in the evening until eight in the morning, the longest I have slept since Christmas, the boat moving under me very gently, as if to say well done.
