Read Isla the picture book I made for her in 2024, on the porch steps in the sun. She knows it by heart. She turned the pages before I had got to the end of them and said the last word of every line, and on the page with the cat she pointed at Biscuit, who had come down as far as the doormat, and said "*Bis-kit*", very firmly, as if introducing him to himself. Clare had to go inside for a minute.
