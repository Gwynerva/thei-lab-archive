too much at once today. The proofs due on Friday, Shetland in three weeks, the wedding in three months, a client who wants his map "more fun", and Biscuit off his food. I felt the old tightness come into my chest at the desk at eleven and I stopped, the way I learned to.

Walked down to the fjord. Lay on the warm rock. Counted things: seven boats, two eagles, one cloud. Came back up and did the next small thing, which was lunch.

It passed. It passes. Writing that down for next time.
