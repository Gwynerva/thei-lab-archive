Clare and I sat up on the porch again after Isla was asleep, with a bottle of the cider, and talked the way we haven't since we were girls sharing a room above the shop. About Mum getting older. About Dad's heart, which is fine, and which we both check on anyway. About Nana. About whether I want what Clare has, which she has never asked me before and asked very gently, and which I answered honestly: I don't know yet, and I'm thirty-four, and both of those are true.

She is going to be my witness. She cried. Then I cried. Then the midges came and we went in.
