*Påske*. Half of Norway is in a cabin in the mountains with skis, a crime novel and a bar of chocolate called Kvikk Lunsj, and so are we, except that our cabin is the house, our mountain is the slope behind it, and the skis are Jonas's old ones, which I fell off twice before lunch. Sigrid's lamb for supper. The orchard is bare, but the buds are fat.
