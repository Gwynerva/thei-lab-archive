The snow came off the barn roof at last, all at once, a long slide and a thump that shook the house and sent Biscuit under the bed. Underneath, the roof sags in the middle like an old horse. Jonas went up the ladder and came down with a face.

In the afternoon Sigrid made us prune, and stood over us while we did it. "For the tree you want in ten years," she said, "not the one you have." I cut off more than I could bear and she made me cut off more.
