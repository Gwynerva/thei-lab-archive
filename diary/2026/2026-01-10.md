The orchestra's spring evening is in April, in the church in Sandviken: anyone who wants to plays something on their own. I put my name down this morning, before I could think about it, which is how I say yes to most of the things that turn out to matter. I have not played alone in front of people since I was fifteen and froze in the second bar of a Saint-Saëns at the Whitby festival.

Practice log: fifty minutes. The prelude first, slowly, then the minuets, which are kind to the fingers and less kind to the nerves.
