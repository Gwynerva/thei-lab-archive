An extra evening for the course tonight, for the four who are behind with their maps, and one of them is the farmer from across the fjord, who has drawn every ditch on her farm and now cannot stop. I told her that is the most dangerous thing about maps: once you start, there is always one more ditch.

The printed sheets come any day now, the printer says, and the roofer soon after. The house is quiet tonight: Jonas reading, the stove ticking, Biscuit on the bench, a little thinner than last month, purring.

:::private
I keep thinking of the vet's word, months. I am trying to live in the months and not in the word.
:::

Rain: day three.\
Waiting for the printer.
