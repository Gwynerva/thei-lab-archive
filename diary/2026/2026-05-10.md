The orchard is out, two days earlier than last year, and whiter, which Sigrid puts down to the pruning in March and which I put down to Sigrid. All forty at once after a warm wet week: white and pink against the dark of the fjord, Folgefonna white behind, so that the whole view is three different whites.

::media[One branch by the porch, close]{file="hardanger-blossom-2"}

Sigrid walked the rows with me again, as she did last year, and this year I knew most of the names before she said them. She noticed. She said nothing. Then, at the bottom gate, she said, "Next year, all of them."

A year ago I stood in this orchard not knowing whether we would stay. In August we are getting married in it.
