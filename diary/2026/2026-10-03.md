## Again

The second pressing. The Nesheims' press up the track on its trailer again, the mill roaring, the sheep at the fence, the grandson taller, Sigrid the same. This year I knew what to do without being told, and by the end I could hear in the sound of the juice when to stop winding and wait.

I went back to last year's list, the five things I would do differently, and read it out at breakfast like a teacher. We picked earlier and in the mornings. The crates were washed. There were more cloths, though never enough. Sigrid had a chair by the press, and sat in it for a total of four minutes.

## The count

Two hundred and fifty-seven litres, in eleven carboys. The two nameless trees went into a carboy of their own, as I promised myself last year, to see what they are when nothing else is in the way.

There was none of last year's cider to open for the helpers. The wedding drank the last of it. So we toasted with juice, straight from the press, cloudy and cold, and Eirik raised his glass to the house, and I raised mine to the trees.
