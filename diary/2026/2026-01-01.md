New Year's Day. A week since [that evening](ref:event:jonas-asks "the snowglobe night"), and I still look at my left hand about forty times an hour.

Fireworks along the fjord at midnight from every farm on both shores, small and far off and doubled in the water. We watched from the porch in our coats with a glass each of Sigrid's cider, and then came in and started a list on the back of an envelope, the first of what I suspect will be very many lists. At the top, in Jonas's writing: *the orchard*. Underneath, in mine: *August*.
