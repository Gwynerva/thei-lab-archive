The first of three Saturdays at a letterpress studio in Bergen, learning to set type from a woman with ink to the elbows who would not let me touch the press until I could mind my p's and q's, which turns out to be a printing joke as old as printing.

The words took longer than the type. We have written them six times and crossed them out five.
