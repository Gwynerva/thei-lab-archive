The charts came: the coast out of Bergen, the North Sea crossing, Bressay Sound and Lerwick. I spread them on the new kitchen floor and lay on my stomach over them for an hour like a child with a comic. I also found a scan of the old Admiralty chart of the Sound from 1897, soundings in fathoms and hills drawn as little caterpillars, and printed it to compare.
