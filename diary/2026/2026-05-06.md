Night sail on the fjord to practise the watches. The boat's owner has let us keep her at the guest pontoon in Utne for May. Out at ten, round the islands, back at four. Three hours on, three off: the one on watch at the helm with the chart and the flask, the one off watch asleep below in her oilskins because it was too much trouble to take them off. It never really got dark; at two the sky behind Folgefonna was already going green. I steered by a farm light on the far shore for an hour and felt, for the first time, like somebody who could do this.
