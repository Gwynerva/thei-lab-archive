Ponies on the hill road this morning, eleven of them, standing in the road as if somebody had posted them there. I will be smiling about them for a year.

In the evening the small boat out of Sandwick to Mousa, to see the storm petrels come home. We waited inside the old round broch in the half-dark until nearly midnight, with a dozen strangers whispering, and then they came: tiny black birds, no bigger than swallows, flitting in from the sea in their hundreds and vanishing into the cracks between the stones, purring. The whole tower purred. I have never heard anything like it and I had nothing to record it with, which may be for the best.
