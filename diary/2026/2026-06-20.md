## The night

Home. The night was long and loud and wet. What I want to keep from it is one moment, at about four, when the sky astern went from black to grey and I realised I had been steering for an hour by the feel of the waves lifting the stern, and not by the compass at all. Jonas put his head out of the hatch, looked at me, looked at the sea, and went back to sleep. I have never been paid a bigger compliment.

## The coast

At dawn there was Norway: first a line of blue teeth, then the islands, then the whole wet green coast I know, as if it had been waiting up for us. Jonas came up with two mugs of tea and we drank them standing, not talking, watching it get bigger.

Bergen by the afternoon, the fish market, the same three gulls (I swear). Close to four hundred nautical miles there and back, nine days, no damage but the leaking hatch and a bruise on my hip in the shape of a winch. We were asleep in the car on the ferry by six.

::media[A lighthouse on its skerry on the way in, the sea flat at last]{file="lighthouse-norway-1"}

Jonas tied up the boat, stood on the pontoon, and said, "Next year, the Faroes." I said we'll see. I mean yes.
