Everyone gone by the noon ferry except Mum and Dad, who leave tomorrow. The orchard looks like a battlefield after a picnic: flattened grass, a hay bale burst open, a single shoe (whose?), petals everywhere. Jonas and I raked it in silence for an hour this morning in our oldest clothes and our new rings, and it was one of the happiest hours of my life.
