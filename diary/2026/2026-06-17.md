The puffins at the south end, at last. I took a hundred and forty photographs and one of them is good, and I stood so long at the wall above the colony that my face went stiff in the wind.

Afterwards we walked up to the lighthouse and round the head, the cliffs falling away green to a grey-blue sea with a ship on the horizon, and sat in the lee of a wall eating oatcakes. My favourite day of the trip, and I have said that about every day.
