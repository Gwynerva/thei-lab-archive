To Sigrid's for cherries. Isla ate them faster than we could pick them and had a red face to the ears by lunch. Sigrid, who has firm views on children, took one look at her and gave her a basket of her own and a job, and Isla followed her round the trees all afternoon like a duckling, carrying it in both hands and asking her why, about everything, in English. Sigrid answered every one, in Norwegian.
