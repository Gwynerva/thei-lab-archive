The guest list. We sat at the new worktop with two pencils and crossed people out and wrote them back in until midnight. Jonas has cousins in numbers I did not know existed. I have a family of five and friends spread over three countries.

Things I will not say out loud: that I would give up half the list for Nana to be on it. That I am afraid Dad will make a speech and cry. That I am afraid he won't.

We got it down to forty-one. Then Sigrid rang and added two. Then Jonas took two off when she wasn't looking.
