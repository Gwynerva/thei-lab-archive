Still calm in the morning, still motoring. At ten, a minke whale: a long dark back rolling once, slowly, fifty metres off the bow, and gone. We both stood up and said nothing.

In the afternoon the swell started coming in from the north-west, long and slow, the kind that means wind somewhere you can't see. By supper there was wind. By dark there was a reef in, then two. Jonas has put the kettle on the gimbals and is singing. I am writing this wedged in the corner of the saloon, and then I am on watch.
