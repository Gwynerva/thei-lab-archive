Biscuit to the vet in Odda. He has been drinking a great deal and eating very little, and she took blood, and rang this afternoon: his kidneys are failing, slowly, the way old cats' kidneys do. Not this week. Not this month, probably. A special food, fluids under the skin, which I am to learn to give, and keeping him warm. Months, she said, perhaps longer, if he is lucky and we are careful.

I sat on the floor by the stove with him for a long time after that. He purred. He doesn't know. Or he does, and is being kind.
