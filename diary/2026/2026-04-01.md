April, and two things begin. One is [the barn](<ref:section:farmhouse/the-house#h:The barn> "the next job"), which sags and leaks and holds seventy years of other people's hay, and which Jonas has been measuring with a look in his eye since we came. The other is Shetland, which he has wanted to sail to since he was a boy and which we are going to do in June, in a borrowed boat, the two of us.

One of these is a dream and one is a job. Some days I can't tell which.
