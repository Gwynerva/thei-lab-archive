## A year

A year ago today we followed a hired van up the track, with Biscuit's carrier between my feet and the cello between my knees, and slept on a mattress on the floor with the rain coming in and both of us in hats.

Since then: a roof patched, a well declared fit to drink, fourteen windows, a leak, a stove, a kitchen. A summer of walking, a winter of drawing. A ring. One pressing, one bottling, and the trees already in bud for the next.

## What the house has taught me

That it was here first. That it will be here after. That you do not renovate an old house so much as negotiate with it, slowly, on its terms, and that the negotiation turns out to be the life.

Ragnhild said, the day she gave us the keys, "Look after the windows and they will look after you." I thought she meant the draughts. I think now she meant the view.

## Three good things, a year in

1. The well water, still tasting of stone.
2. The pencil dates on the pantry door, Ragnhild's and now mine underneath.
3. Waking at six to the sound of the ferry a full minute before I see it, and knowing exactly which one it is.
