Bottled. Nine carboys up the cellar stairs, siphon, fill, cap, wipe, box, until four. I drew the label in the evenings last week: the house, the slope of trees, the fjord, and the lettering I learned on the course in 2024. It reads:

*Øvre Lid 2025*\
*forty trees, one press, a great deal of help*

The first bottle we opened standing in the cold barn at five, out of jam jars, while the light went. Dry, pale, a little cloudy, and it tasted of the orchard in October. Sigrid held hers up to the last of the window, drank, and said two words in Norwegian, and Jonas went pink with pleasure on my behalf.
