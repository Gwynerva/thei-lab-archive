## Arrivals

They came all day off one ferry after another, like a tide coming in: Mum and Dad at eleven, Clare and Dev and Isla at two, Jonas's cousins at five in two cars and a van. Dad went straight to look at the windows. Mum went straight to the kitchen and was found an hour later making scones in it as if she had always lived here. Isla went straight to Biscuit, who has decided she is allowed.

## The jobs, given out at tea

1. Dad and Dev: chairs, from the hall in Utne, up the track in Eirik's van
2. Mum: the kitchen, which she had already taken
3. Clare: the dress, my hair, me
4. The cousins: whatever Sigrid says
5. Isla: Biscuit
6. Jonas and I: to stop walking round the orchard looking at it, Mum says

## Supper

At the long trestles in the barn with the doors open to the fjord. Sigrid made the fish soup. Dad stood up halfway through with his glass and everyone went quiet, and he said, "Not yet. Saturday," and sat down again, and that was the best speech of the week. The house is full to the eaves, and so am I.
