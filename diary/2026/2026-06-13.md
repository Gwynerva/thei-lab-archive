## A whole day of sea

No land in any direction, all day, for the first time in my life. The sea grey, then blue, then a green I do not have a word for, and the sky three times the size it is at home. Fulmars following us for hours, stiff-winged a hand's breadth above the swell, never seeming to move a feather.

Oil platforms on the horizon in the afternoon, lit up like small towns on stilts. We gave them a wide berth and a wave.

## Watches

Three on, three off. You sleep in your clothes and wake in the dark not knowing which way up you are. Jonas is a different person at sea: quieter, slower, entirely happy. I am a different person too: hungry, salty, and asleep the second I lie down.

:::private
At four this morning, at the helm, alone, I was afraid for about an hour: of nothing in particular, of the size of it, of being one small warm thing on a great deal of cold water. I didn't wake Jonas. It went with the light. I think it is the kind of fear you have to have once.
:::

We opened Mum's cake at teatime. It is the best thing I have ever eaten. The wind is getting up from the west as I write this, and Jonas has just put a reef in, whistling.
