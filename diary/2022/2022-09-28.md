Sverre on the stairs this morning, holding the barometer in both hands like a baby:

> She's going down and she's not stopping. A big low coming in from the west, and another one behind it. You'll want your good coat for a while.

Rehearsal tonight: the Holberg, the Rigaudon, fast enough to make the bows smoke. Walked home in a light rain that I now suspect was the start of something.
