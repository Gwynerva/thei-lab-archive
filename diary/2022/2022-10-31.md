Took the weather station down yesterday. The humidity sensor has been dead since July and the anemometer squeaks, and after two and a half years and two countries it has earned its rest. The last reading: 7.2 degrees, 1004 hectopascals, wind south-west, 0.4 mm of rain in the hour, which is as Bergen as a reading can be. The little computer is in a drawer with its settings, just in case. The rain counter takes its numbers from the Institute now. It doesn't need me.
