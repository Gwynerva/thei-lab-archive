Dad came home at two in a taxi, walking slowly and refusing the wheelchair at the door. Mum has made a timetable for his tablets and stuck it to the fridge with the magnet shaped like a puffin. He's to walk a little further every day. Today it was from the kitchen to the gate.

In the evening I walked the West Cliff on my own, to the whalebones and back, the abbey black against a yellow sky across the harbour, the swing bridge opening for a boat. I rang Jonas from the bench under the arch. He said the hull is waiting and so is Biscuit, "and so am I, in that order".
