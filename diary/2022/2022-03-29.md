Workshop evening: little dabs of thickened epoxy between the wire stitches, so the seams hold their shape when the wires come out. Jonas asked about Whitby, so I told him about the shop: the smell of tarred rope, Dad splicing on the counter, the brass bell that rings when the door opens. He said he'd like to see it. I said Dad would like him to see it, and then I thought about that for the rest of the evening.
