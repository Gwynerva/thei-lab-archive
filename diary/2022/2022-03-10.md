## Henningsvær

A fishing village on a string of little islands joined by bridges, the mountains of Austvågøya behind it and the sea everywhere else. We drove over yesterday; today we walked all of it, which takes about an hour if you stop for everything, and we stopped for everything.

## The pitch

At the far end, on the last flat rock before the open sea, someone has laid a football pitch. Real grass under the snow, real goals, the sea on three sides and the fishing boats on the fourth. Priya stood in one goal and I stood in the other and we shouted across it, and a gull sat on the crossbar and ignored us both. Whoever has to fetch the ball when it goes in the sea: I salute you.

## Stockfish

Everywhere, on wooden racks taller than a house, cod hanging in pairs to dry in the wind, as they've done here for a thousand years. *Tørrfisk*, stockfish. The racks are *hjeller*. The smell is — I'll say strong. By the summer they'll be as hard as wood and on a ship to Italy.
