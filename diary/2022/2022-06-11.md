First swim in Norway: the sea bath at Nordnes, off the rocks at the end of the park. Fourteen degrees. I lasted four minutes, came out bright pink, and felt better than I have in a month. There were old men there who'd been in for an hour and were reading the paper on the steps afterwards in their trunks.
