The transcribing is done: every letter in the tin typed out, Nana's to Grandad and his back to her, from 1952, when he went to sea, to 1959, when he came home for good. Forty-one of them, the spelling left as she spelled it. It has taken nearly two years of Sunday evenings.

::file[One of them, typed out, so you can hear the voice]{file="letter-1953" title="A letter of 1953"}

Now I'm going to make them into a book for Mum for Christmas: printed, folded, sewn and bound by hand. She doesn't know. Clare knows. Dad suspects.
