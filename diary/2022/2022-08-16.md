Forgot to write it down: on Saturday we did Sandviksfjellet, the sixth of seven — straight up from my own front door, through the woods behind the house, up the steps and out onto the top in an hour and a bit. I've looked at it from the kitchen window every morning for a year. From the top I could see the white house, my balcony, the tomatoes and — Jonas swears — Biscuit on the wall.
