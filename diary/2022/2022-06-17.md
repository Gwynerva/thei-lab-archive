The summer party, on a boat: an old wooden passenger boat that Anna somehow hired, out of the harbour and round the islands for five hours with the whole company aboard, forty of us. *Reker* on deck — prawns, piled up on newspaper, peeled with your fingers and eaten on white bread with mayonnaise and lemon — white wine in plastic glasses, and the sun not going down. Anna made a speech about the year and said something kind about "the new Englishwoman who checks everything twice", and the whole deck said *skål*.

::media[Back in the harbour at midnight, the fishing boats asleep]{file="vagen-1"}

Someone had an accordion. There was dancing. I'll say no more.
