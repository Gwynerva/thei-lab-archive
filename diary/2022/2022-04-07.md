Class: Easter words, because the whole country stops next week.

- *påske* — Easter
- *påskeegg* — a cardboard egg the size of a melon, full of sweets
- *påskekrim* — the crime novel everyone reads at Easter, at the cabin, by law
- *hytte* — the cabin
- *påskefjellet* — the Easter mountains, where everybody goes

Everyone in class has plans for the mountains. I said I'm going to a cabin, and the teacher said "*Med hvem?*" — with whom? — and the whole class went "Ooh", in Norwegian.
