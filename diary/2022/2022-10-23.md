Rain: day 21. Three weeks. Twenty-one days in a row of rain, every one of them wet by the rules, some of them by a lot. The wettest was the 12th, the day the bus stop at Sandviken became a pond and a man crossed it in waders. I don't know where he got waders.

The forecast says dry tomorrow. Sverre's needle, consulted at six, has gone up for the first time in a month, and he tapped it and said "*Ja*," and that was all.

## What it's been like

- The flat has smelled of wet wool for three weeks.
- Biscuit hasn't been out since the 9th and has eaten a houseplant.
- The counter, a joke I wrote on a Wednesday night, has four hundred users and its own sweepstake, and I've had emails from two schools.
- I've recorded the rain eleven times: on the roof, on the bus shelter, on the plastic sheet over the hull at the workshop.
- I've been sadder than I said, and less sad than I feared.
