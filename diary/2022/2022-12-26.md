Boxing Day: Dad and I walked out to Saltwick Bay along the cliff, the walk we did in 2016 when the dissertation began — him slower now, me pretending not to notice and stopping to "look at the view" every five minutes. Black Nab still standing in its pool at low tide. The cliff path has moved inland again since I measured it; the fence posts are new.

Dad stood at the edge and asked how much had gone since then. I said about a metre and a half, at the average. We both looked at the sea for a while, as if it might apologise.
