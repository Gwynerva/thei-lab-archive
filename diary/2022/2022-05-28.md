The concert was in an old church above the city, high and cold, the light coming in long and blue through the windows at nine in the evening. A string orchestra of about twenty. They played a slow movement of something I didn't know and then Grieg's Holberg Suite, which is Bergen's own — Grieg was born here — and which I once played the second-cello part of, badly, in a school hall in Whitby.

::media[The church, inside, before the lights went down]{file="church-interior-1"}

Halfway through the Sarabande I realised I was gripping Jonas's arm, and that my left hand was doing the fingering on his sleeve. He noticed. On the way home he said, "You should be up there." I said I haven't played with anyone since Leith. He said, "So?"

So. I'm going to find an orchestra.
