Rain: day 15. It isn't funny any more. Dark when I get up, dark by half six, the rain never stopping and the sky the colour of a wet paper bag, and something in me has gone grey to match. I cried on the bus for no reason, quietly, and a woman handed me a tissue without looking at me, which was the kindest thing anyone could have done. I miss Mum. I miss the Whitby sky, which at least changes. I'm not writing this anywhere anyone will read it, so: I'm not all right today. Tomorrow I will be. Jonas is coming round with soup.
