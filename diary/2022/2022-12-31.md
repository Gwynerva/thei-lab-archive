## The year, in a list

On New Year's Day I wrote three things for the year: B1 by the summer, a mountain I hadn't climbed, something with my hands bigger than a spoon. I got the B1 (both parts). I climbed four mountains. And the thing bigger than a spoon is five metres long, glassed, with a deck stitched on, sitting in a workshop across the water waiting for its varnish.

The rest of it, which I didn't plan:

- Covid, a week of it, and Sverre's soup.
- Lofoten with Priya, and the sky moving.
- Thirty.
- Skis, eleven falls, an orange on a snowdrift.
- Hardanger in blossom, and Sigrid, and a house with fourteen windows seen from a ferry.
- An orchestra, at thirty, the back desk, two hundred chairs.
- Dad's heart, and Dad's heart mended.
- Twenty-one days of rain and an app that counted them.
- Twelve lanterns up a hill.
- Jonas meeting Whitby, and Whitby approving.

## Tonight

Fireworks from the balcony again, the whole city crackling; Biscuit under the duvet again; Jonas beside me with a glass of Sigrid's cider. Every year of [the diary](/diary/) ends with me promising to write more next year. I won't promise. I'll just write.
