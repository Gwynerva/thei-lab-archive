A knock at seven: nobody there, but a pot of fish soup on the mat with a lid on it and a note in capitals — *GOD BEDRING. S.H.* Get well soon, from Sverre Haldorsen, retired ship's mate, downstairs. Salmon, cream, a lot of pepper. I ate the whole thing in one go and slept for four hours.
