A knock at seven: nobody there, but a bag of oranges on the mat and a note in capitals — *GOD BEDRING. S.H.* Get well soon, from Sverre Haldorsen, retired ship's mate, downstairs. I ate three in one go, with a tin of soup, and slept for four hours.
