Dad walked me to the end of the road this morning, slowly, with Mum behind us pretending not to watch. At the corner he said, "Go on. Your boat's waiting," and patted my arm twice, which for him is a speech.

Newcastle, Amsterdam, Bergen. Rain at Flesland, of course.

---

Home by nine. Sverre had left the hall light on for me and a note on the table, in English: *Cat fine. Ate fish. Your father? S.* I wrote *Better* underneath and left it on his mat. Biscuit followed me from room to room for an hour and then lay on my chest until I could barely breathe, which seemed fair.
