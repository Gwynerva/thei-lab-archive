To Robin Hood's Bay, the two of us, on the bus over the moor. Down the steep lane to the little cottage that was Nana's: white still, a different car outside and different curtains, and the rowan in the front garden cut back but strong, a few red berries left at the top for the birds. I didn't knock. We stood across the lane for a minute while I told Jonas about the jelly, the quilts, the sewing box, *make do and mend*, the sound of the sea from the back bedroom.

Then down to the beach at low tide, the rock scars running out into the sea like ribs, and back up the hill in the dark.

::media[The village from the north cliff, the beach shining at low tide]{file="robin-hoods-bay-2"}

On the bus back Jonas said, "I understand you better now." I didn't ask what he meant. I think I know.
