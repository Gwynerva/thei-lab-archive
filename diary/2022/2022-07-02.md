Biscuit has discovered the wall at the end of the garden, warm in the afternoons, and lies along the top of it like a draught excluder, watching the gulls with contempt. Sverre calls him *Kaptein*. He comes when Sverre calls him that. He has never once come when I call him Biscuit.
