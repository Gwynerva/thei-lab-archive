A plan for the shortest day: twelve paper lanterns, one for each month of the year, carried up the hill behind the house at dusk on the 21st with whoever will come. I started today with thin paper, strips of bamboo and a lot of folding. Lantern one is January: white, with a tiny kayak cut out of the side so the light comes through it.
