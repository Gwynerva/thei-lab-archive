Twenty centimetres overnight, which in Bergen is news; it usually falls as rain, or melts by lunch. The whole of Sandviken went quiet and white.

::media[The lane behind the house this morning, before anyone had been out but us]{file="bergen-snow-2" layout="natural"}

Biscuit had seen snow before — a sprinkling on the balcony in Leith, which he ignored. This was the real thing. I opened the balcony door and he walked straight out into it, sank to his chest, and stopped. Then he lifted each paw in turn and shook it, very slowly, like a man stepping out of a puddle in good shoes. Then he sat down in it. Then he looked back at me as if I had arranged this.

He stayed out for four minutes, ate some, and came in to sit on the radiator and steam.

::media[Biscuit afterwards, ruffled, deciding whether to forgive me]{file="ginger-cat-13"}

I built a very small snowman on the balcony rail. By three o'clock it had slid off.
