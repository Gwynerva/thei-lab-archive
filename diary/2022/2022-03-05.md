## Bodø

Flew up last night, Bergen to Bodø along the coast in the dark, the lights of little places strung out below like a dropped necklace. Priya came in from Oslo an hour later with a rucksack bigger than she is, and we hugged in the arrivals hall for so long that a man asked if we needed help. It's been more than two years. She looks exactly the same, and also like a doctor of marine biology, which she is.

## The ferry

This morning the car ferry to Moskenes: three and a half hours across the Vestfjord, with the hire car down in the hold and us up on deck, watching the wall of mountains come up out of the sea ahead, white and black and impossible. The swell was long and slow. Priya was fine. I was fine until the café opened and I smelled the sausages.

## Sakrisøy

Our *rorbu* — a fisherman's cabin on stilts, one room, two bunks and a window that is all mountain — is at Sakrisøy, a bridge away from Reine. The sea goes in and out under the floor. I drew the map of the week on the back of the ferry ticket: Reine, Hamnøy, the long road east, Henningsvær at the end of it.
