## Midsummer Eve

*Sankthans* — [St John's Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midsummer "the Wikipedia page, which explains it better than I can") — when every beach and point along the coast has its bonfire, a *sankthansbål*, and people sit round it until the sky has gone from blue to pale green and back to blue without ever being dark.

## The fires

We went down to the rocks below the old battery, where the neighbours had been building the fire all week, with a bag of sausages and a guitar nobody could tune. The fire was taller than me when it went up, driftwood and pallets round a Christmas tree that someone had been saving since January for exactly this, and in the end it was Sverre, not Jonas, who lit it, with one match. Across the water on Askøy we counted eleven others along the shore, little orange points under the pale sky, and the smoke from all of them lay along the fjord in one long thread.

## The witches

Somebody's grandfather told me the fires are to keep the witches off on their way to the mountain. Somebody's grandmother said it's for the sausages. Jonas said in Hardanger they used to put a straw witch on top, and that his mother still would if anyone let her.

## Feet in the sea

At midnight I sat on a rock with my feet in the sea, which was cold enough to hurt my teeth, and watched the fire and the water and Jonas throwing on driftwood, and thought: I live here. I actually live here.
