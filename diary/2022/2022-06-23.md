*Sankthans*, Midsummer Eve — [St John's Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midsummer "the Wikipedia page, which explains it better than I can") — when every beach and point along the coast has its bonfire, a *sankthansbål*, and people sit round it until the sky has gone from blue to pale green and back to blue without ever being dark.

We went to a little beach north of the city with Jonas's friends from the workshop, a car boot full of firewood and a bag of sausages. The fire was taller than me when it went up, and across the water on Askøy we counted eleven others along the shore, little orange points under the pale sky. Somebody's grandfather told me the fires are to keep the witches off on their way to the mountain. Somebody's grandmother said it's for the sausages.

I sat on a rock with my feet in the sea, which was cold enough to hurt my teeth, and watched the fire and the water and Jonas throwing on driftwood, and thought: I live here. I actually live here.
