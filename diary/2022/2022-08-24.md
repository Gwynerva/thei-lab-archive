First rehearsal with Sandviken Strykeorkester. Thirty-one players in a school hall that smells of floor polish and oranges; seven cellos; a conductor who stops every eight bars to say something kind and devastating. We read through the autumn programme: Elgar's Serenade for Strings, the Holberg, and a new piece by a Bergen composer with no tune and a great deal of counting.

All the instructions are in Norwegian, so now I have music words: *nedstrøk* and *oppstrøk* (down-bow, up-bow), *svakt* (quietly), *fra begynnelsen* (from the top, said wearily, often), and :hint[opptakt]{note="the upbeat, the breath before the first bar, which our conductor says is where the music really starts"}.

My desk partner is a retired dentist who has played in the orchestra for twenty-two years and offered me a mint at the break. I think we'll get on.
