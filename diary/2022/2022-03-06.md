It snowed all night, and in the morning the world had been redrawn: the peaks above Reine white to the waterline, the red cabins wearing thick white roofs, and everything silent except the sea under the floor.

## Reine

We walked across the bridges into Reine with the snow coming down so thick we could barely see the next cabin, and then it stopped all at once, like a curtain going up, and there were the mountains, standing straight up out of the water on every side. Priya said "Oh," and then nothing at all for a long time, which for Priya is a record.

Coffee in a bakery that sells cinnamon buns the size of a fist. Then the road to Hamnøy, where the red cabins sit on the rocks with the sea pulling in and out around them, and an old man shovelling a path down to his boat told us it was "*litt snø*" — a bit of snow — and laughed at us for photographing it.
