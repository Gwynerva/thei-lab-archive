To Whitby, the two of us. Bergen in rain at seven, Amsterdam in fog, Newcastle in wind, then the train to Middlesbrough and the little train down the Esk Valley, Jonas with his face to the window at every moor and every stone bridge.

Dad was waiting on the platform at Whitby in his good coat, taller than I remembered from September and pink in the face. He shook Jonas's hand and looked him over the way he looks over a second-hand boat, and then said, "You'll want to see the shop," and they were gone before Mum had got out of the car.

I stood on the platform with both suitcases and laughed until I had to sit down.
