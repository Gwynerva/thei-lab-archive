## English style

Christmas Day, English style, and Jonas met all of it at once: stockings at the end of the bed (Mum had done him one, with a tangerine in the toe and a pair of Whitby socks), crackers (explained on the plane, still a shock), paper hats worn all day without irony, the television at three, which Dad slept through.

## Lunch

Clare and Dev came over from Leeds. Dev and Jonas discovered that they both have a bad left knee and spent an hour on the sofa comparing exercises. Turkey, roast potatoes, sprouts (Jonas: "Why?"), bread sauce (Jonas: "Why?"), and a Christmas pudding carried in with the lights off and set alight with brandy (Jonas: "Why?" — but delighted, and he had seconds). Clare asked him what he thought of England so far and he said, "Everything is on fire or in a sauce," which Dad has been repeating since.

## The walk

After lunch we all walked along the West Cliff to the whalebones, Dad included, slowly, in a cold bright wind with the sea white below.
