Christmas Day, English style, and Jonas met all of it at once: stockings, crackers (explained on the plane, still a shock), paper hats, the television at three, which Dad slept through.

Clare and Dev came over from Leeds for lunch. Dev and Jonas discovered that they both have a bad left knee and spent an hour comparing exercises. Turkey, roast potatoes, sprouts (Jonas: "Why?"), a Christmas pudding set alight with brandy (Jonas: "Why?" — but delighted).

After lunch we all walked along the West Cliff to the whalebones, Dad included, slowly, in a cold bright wind with the sea white below.
