The fireworks went on until two. Bergen doesn't do one big display; it does ten thousand small ones, from every garden and balcony on every hill, so the whole bowl of the city crackles at once and the smoke sits on the harbour like fog. Biscuit spent it under the duvet.

Today: grey, mild, a walk along the water to Bryggen and back, and a list for the year, which I'm keeping short so that I can keep it.

- B1 by the summer.
- A mountain I haven't climbed. (There are seven. I've done three.)
- Something with my hands that's bigger than a spoon.
