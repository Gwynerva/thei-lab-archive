While I was in Lofoten, Jonas joined the plywood sheets into five-metre lengths, as "the rest of the birthday present". Last night we drew the hull panels out on them from the plans, with a long flexible batten and a lot of pencil, and cut them: eight long curved pieces, laid out on the floor like the parts of a giant fish. Tonight I drilled the holes along every edge, one every ten centimetres, nearly a thousand. Tomorrow, the wire.

::media[The copper wire, before it's cut and twisted]{file="copper-wire-reel"}

*A stitch in time is still a stitch*, Nana used to say. She'd have liked this boat.
