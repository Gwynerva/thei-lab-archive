Lanterns seven to ten: July, a tent on an island; August, a cello; September, a heart, for Dad, which came out slightly lopsided, which is right; October, rain, obviously — blue paper with a hundred pinpricks. Jonas made one of his own, unasked: a boat, a proper little boat-shaped lantern with a paper sail, for no month in particular.
