Went to see the workshop. It's a long shed by the water with a stove at one end and doors at the other wide enough to get a boat out, and it smells of exactly what I hoped: sawdust, varnish, coffee, tar and cold. Two boats on trestles — a rowing boat being re-planked and someone's half-finished kayak, all panels and clamps.

::media[Somebody else's hull on the frame, by the window where mine will be]{file="boat-workshop-2"}

Jonas showed me the bench by the window where mine would go, the plywood store, and how to sharpen a chisel properly, which I thought I knew. I didn't.

I said yes. I'm going to build a boat.

It started snowing on the bus home, properly — big wet flakes sticking to everything.
