Christmas words, for the list:

- *julebord* — the Christmas party; see Friday
- *gløgg* — mulled wine, with raisins and almonds in the bottom of the cup
- *nisse* — the little Christmas gnome who lives in the barn and must be given porridge
- *risengrynsgrøt* — the porridge: rice, with a single almond hidden in the pot
- *pepperkaker* — gingerbread, thin and everywhere
