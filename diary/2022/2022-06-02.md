The B1 exam.

The written part in the morning, in a school hall with forty other people from everywhere: reading, listening, and a piece of writing about "a place that means something to you". I wrote about the workshop, then panicked that I didn't know the word for strongback and wrote "the long table that holds the boat straight".

The oral in the afternoon: two examiners and me at a table. We talked about rain (they laughed), about my job (they nodded), and about what I miss from England. I said the sea at Whitby, which is the same sea as this one, only from the other side, and it came out in good Norwegian, and one of them smiled at me in a way I'll keep.

I walked home along the water and bought myself an ice cream, and it rained on the ice cream, and I didn't care.
