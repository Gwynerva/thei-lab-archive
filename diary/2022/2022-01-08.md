Homework, and the week's words:

- *oppholdsvær* — :hint[pause weather]{note="the forecast's word for a dry spell, however short; in Bergen it counts as good news"}, the best thing a Bergen forecast can promise
- *pålegg* — whatever goes on a slice of bread, from cheese to jam to fish paste; there are whole aisles of it
- *skodde* — fog, the thick kind that sits in the fjord all morning
- *uff* — the noise for everything mildly bad; I say it constantly now
- *takk for sist* — thanks for last time, which you say to anyone you've met before, even if last time was nothing to thank anyone for
