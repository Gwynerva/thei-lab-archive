Home. Mum cried at the door and said it was the wind. Dad shook Jonas's hand and then, after a pause, hugged him, briefly, the way you'd fend off a boat coming alongside. Newcastle, Amsterdam, Bergen, rain. Sverre had left the hall light on again. Biscuit had been sick on the scarf Jonas left here before we went, which Jonas said was fair.
