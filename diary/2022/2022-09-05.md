Mum rang at ten past seven. Dad: chest pain in the shop at half six while he was opening up; he sat down on the coil of rope by the door and couldn't get up. An ambulance. They've taken him to the heart unit at the hospital in Middlesbrough. He's awake. He's talking. He's annoyed.

Flights: Bergen, Amsterdam, Newcastle, the last seat on the last plane. Sverre has the key and the cat, and said "*Reis*" — go — before I'd finished explaining. Jonas drove me to the airport and held my hand at the barrier for longer than the man behind us liked.

Clare met me at Newcastle at midnight with her hospital face on. She says he's stable. She says "stable" the way other people say "fine".
