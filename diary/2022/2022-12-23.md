Jonas spent the morning at the shop with Dad. They re-spliced the eye in the big mooring line that hangs by the door, which Dad has been meaning to do since 2009, argued about knots in two languages, and came home for lunch smelling of tar and very pleased with each other.

In the afternoon Mum took us up the 199 steps, slowly, Dad in front this time, not stopping at the bench halfway, making a point. At the top: the abbey, the churchyard, the wind, the whole town going down the hill in the dusk with the lights coming on and the harbour mouth beyond.
