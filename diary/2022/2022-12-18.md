Recorded the rain again, at the workshop this time, on the plastic sheet over the hull: a different rain entirely, big slow drops off the gutter, each one a little drum. The recordings are adding up — rain on slate, rain on glass, rain on the bus shelter, rain on a boat that hasn't been in the water yet.
