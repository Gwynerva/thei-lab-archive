Recorded the rain at the workshop again, but under the gutter this time: big slow drops falling onto the plastic sheet over the hull, each one a little drum, a different rain entirely. The recordings are adding up — rain on slate, rain on glass, rain on the bus shelter, rain on a boat that hasn't been in the water yet.
