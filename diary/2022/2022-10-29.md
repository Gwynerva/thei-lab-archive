Rundemanen, the seventh. In sleet.

## Up

Up Fløyen in the funicular for the first part, out of shame at the weather, then on foot along the gravel road over the top, past the lakes — black water, brown heather, the birches bare — with the path getting whiter as we climbed. We met four people the whole way, all coming down, all Norwegian, all cheerful, one of them in shorts. Each of them said *god tur*, have a good trip, as if there could be no doubt about it.

## The top

At the top: the mast, a wind straight off the North Sea full of ice, and the city completely invisible somewhere beneath a lid of cloud. We stood at the cairn for exactly as long as it took Jonas to take a picture and me to say *sju* — seven — out loud, and then went down again, fast, laughing, sleet in our teeth. Hot chocolate at the café on Fløyen, steaming like horses.

## Seven

All seven, one at a time, in fourteen months: Fløyen, Ulriken by way of Vidden, Løvstakken, Damsgårdsfjellet, Lyderhorn, Sandviksfjellet, Rundemanen. I'd like to do them all in a single day one summer. Jonas says that's about thirty-five kilometres. I've written it in the margin anyway.
