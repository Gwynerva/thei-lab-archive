Rundemanen, the seventh. In sleet.

Up Fløyen in the funicular for the first part, out of shame at the weather, then on foot along the gravel road over the top, past the lakes, the path getting whiter as we climbed. At the top: the mast, a wind straight off the North Sea full of ice, and the city completely invisible somewhere beneath a lid of cloud. We stood at the cairn for exactly as long as it took Jonas to take a picture and me to say *sju* — seven — out loud, and then went down again, fast, laughing, sleet in our teeth.

::media[Rundemanen and its mast, the cloud going over at speed]{file="rundemanen-1"}

All seven, one at a time, in fourteen months. I'd like to do them all in a single day one summer. Jonas says that's about thirty-five kilometres. I've written it in the margin anyway.
