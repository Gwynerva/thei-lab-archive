He opened the logbook, turned it over, looked at the spine for a long time, ran his thumb along the stitching and said, "*Kobbertråd?*" — copper wire? — and I said no, linen thread. He said it was the best present he's ever had, and then wrote the first entry in it at the kitchen table: *4.11.22. Skrog glassert. Mira laget denne boka.* Hull glassed. Mira made this book.
