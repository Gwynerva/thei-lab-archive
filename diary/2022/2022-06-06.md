*Andre pinsedag* and flat calm, so Jonas borrowed two kayaks from the club and we paddled north along the shore for three hours. My first time on the water since January, and I didn't fall in, and I braced without stirring porridge. The water was the colour of bottle glass. A sea eagle went over, low and enormous, like a door flying.
