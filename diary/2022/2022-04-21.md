Did the sums for the boat. Plywood, epoxy, wire, glass cloth to come, varnish to come, the hire of a respirator, all the little things that add up. It's more than I told myself it would be. The flat is dear, the krone's been kind to me and then not, and I still send a bit home every month in my head even though nobody asks me to.
