The last of the glassing: three months of it, from the first dry cloth in September to tonight's last brush of resin round the cockpit. Jonas cut the deck panels this evening while I tidied, and we laid them on the hull just to look. The deck next. Then the varnish. Then the water. In May, he says. *Kanskje.*
