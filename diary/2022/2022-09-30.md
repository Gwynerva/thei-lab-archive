The last of the balcony: twenty-two green tomatoes picked and put in a drawer with a banana to ripen, the sugar snaps pulled up, the nasturtiums still going, orange in the rain. The pots are stacked by the door for the winter. Biscuit lay in the empty soil of the biggest one, looking enormously pleased.
