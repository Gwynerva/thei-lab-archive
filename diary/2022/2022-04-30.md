Damsgårdsfjellet, the fourth of Bergen's seven: the small one across the water. Up from Laksevåg through the woods, past back gardens and a kindergarten, onto a bare top with the whole city in front of it — the harbour, Bryggen, Fløyen opposite, the houses of Sandviken climbing the far side, and somewhere among them, I'm fairly sure, my balcony and my cat.

::media[Damsgårdsfjellet seen from Fløyen in December, the only picture I have where it looks like a mountain]{file="damsgardsfjellet"}

Three to go: Lyderhorn, Sandviksfjellet, Rundemanen. The one right behind my own house is still the one I haven't done.
