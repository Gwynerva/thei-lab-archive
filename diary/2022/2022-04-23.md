*Dugnad* at the white house. Once a year everybody in the building turns out on a Saturday morning to rake the garden, sweep the steps, wash the windows nobody can reach and paint whatever's peeling, and afterwards there are waffles and coffee on the steps. Sverre supervised from a kitchen chair with a rake across his knees. I painted the gate. Nobody pays anybody and everybody comes, and I think it's the most Norwegian thing I've seen yet.
