Rain: day 8. The counter says 8, in a font size I chose to be funny and now regret. In its honour, the rain words, continuing the list in the back of my notebook from where it stopped last winter:

12. *yr* — the finest drizzle, which hangs in the air rather than falling
13. *duskregn* — drizzle, a little heavier
14. *regnvær* — rainy weather, the default setting
15. *pøsregn* — pouring rain; the word sounds like what it does
16. *styrtregn* — a cloudburst, rain falling like a wall
17. *sludd* — sleet, coming in November, I'm promised

They'll go on [the page of weather words](<ref:page:norwegian-words#h:Weather> "words for weather, which is to say mostly rain") once I've used each of them in a sentence to a Norwegian without being laughed at.
