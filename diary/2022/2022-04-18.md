*Andre påskedag*. Cleaned the cabin, carried the rubbish down the track on our backs, drove home in a queue of every car in western Norway. Biscuit had knocked a seed tray off the windowsill in protest and was lying in the soil. I have a sunburnt nose, a bruise on my hip the shape of Iceland, and a new word for the brown face everybody comes home with: *påskebrun*.
