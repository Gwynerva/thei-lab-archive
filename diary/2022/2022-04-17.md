Easter Sunday, *første påskedag*. Snow all morning, so we stayed in: cards, coffee, the stove, the window. Jonas told me about Hardanger, where he grew up, and about his great-aunt Ragnhild, who still lives on her own in an old farmhouse above the fjord near Utne, at eighty-something, and splits her own wood. He said the house has "too many windows to keep warm", and I said that sounded like the best kind, and he gave me a look I've been thinking about since. In May, he says, when the blossom's out, we'll go and see his mother.
