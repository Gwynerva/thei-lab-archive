Glass on the hull, outside and in, all the fill coats done, and the workshop smells of resin from the door to the stove. The hull is a different creature now: hard, stiff, ringing like a drum when you knock on it, and glossy enough to see your face in, upside down. Next the deck, and the cockpit, and a hundred other things, but today I sat on a bucket and just looked at it.
