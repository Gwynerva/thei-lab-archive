It didn't rain yesterday. Not a drop, by the rules, at Florida. The counter went back to 0 at midnight and showed, for the first time, its little white square, and four hundred people opened it to look at a zero. Today it rained again, and the counter says 1, ~~and I've stopped checking~~ and I checked at nine, like everyone.
