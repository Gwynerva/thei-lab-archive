September, and the glassing begins — or the getting ready for it does. The hull came off the forms today and went back on the strongback upside down, and I spent the evening going over every inch of it with a sanding block and a torch held low, looking for the shadows that mean a bump. Nineteen degrees at the bench. Jonas says three weeks of preparation for one day of glass. Everything about this boat is three weeks of something for one day of something else.
