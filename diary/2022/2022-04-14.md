*Skjærtorsdag*, Maundy Thursday. Up the road out of Bergen in Jonas's van with the skis on the roof, through the long tunnel and out into winter: snow walls taller than the van, white birches, the light suddenly enormous.

The cabin is dark wood with a red door — three rooms and a stove — at the end of a track you ski the last bit of with your bag on your back. No running water: there's a stream under the snow, a pump, and an outside loo with a view. Jonas lit the stove, and it ticked and roared, and the cabin went from minus four to *kos* in an hour.

Tomorrow: skis. He says we'll start on the flat.
