Out to the islands in borrowed kayaks with a tent, a stove, too much food and Jonas. Four hours' paddling west among the skerries, then a little island with a shingle beach and a flat patch of grass above it, and nobody. In Norway you can camp anywhere for a night, out of sight of houses, as long as you leave nothing behind — *allemannsretten*, everyman's right — which still seems too good to be true.

::media[Camp on the island at ten in the evening, still light]{file="kayak-shore-2"}

We ate noodles and watched the sun go down for about three hours. An oystercatcher shouted at us until midnight. I'd like to do this every summer for the rest of my life.
