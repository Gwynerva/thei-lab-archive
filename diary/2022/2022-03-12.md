The last morning: ski tracks across the frozen lake above Svolvær, birches and white light, Priya on a borrowed pair of cross-country skis, going backwards. Then Svolvær to Bodø on a propeller plane that shook like a washing machine, Bodø to Bergen, the bus, the stairs, the door.

::media[The lake above Svolvær, frozen, the last morning]{file="lofoten-winter-3"}

Biscuit sat with his back to me for two hours, then got on my lap and stayed. Sverre reports "no problems" and "a little fish".

Nine days. I've filled a notebook. [The whole trip](ref:project:lofoten "Reine, the lights, Henningsvær") will go in properly once I've slept.
