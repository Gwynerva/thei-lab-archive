Two years since Nana.

I lit a candle in the window at six, and sat with the tin of her letters on my knee and didn't open it. Mum rang at seven. We didn't talk about it, and then we did, for an hour. She says the rowan in the garden at the Bay has been cut back by the new people — not down, just back — and that the berries were thick on it this year anyway.

A year ago tonight I finished the quilt. This year I've nearly finished the letters. Next year, I hope, something with less grief stitched into it.

Nana: I've built half a boat. I've met someone. I've been to the top of all seven mountains. I've a cat who understands Norwegian. You'd have liked him. Both of them.
