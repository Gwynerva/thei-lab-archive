## Thirty

Pi Day, as I've told everyone at work, twice. Thirty. I don't feel thirty. I feel about nineteen and also about sixty, depending on whether I've been at the workshop.

Mum and Dad rang at seven in the morning and sang, both of them, slightly apart. Clare sent a card with a picture of a cake and the words *it's all downhill from here, see you at the bottom*. Priya sent a voice message from her lab with what I think was a whole department singing in the background. Marta sent a recording of her violin playing "Happy Birthday" as a fugue.

## At the office

Anna brought a *bløtkake* — sponge, cream, strawberries, more cream — and the whole team sang *Hurra for deg som fyller ditt år*, the Norwegian birthday song, which has more verses than seems necessary and a lot of stamping.

## In the evening

Jonas came round with pizza and a parcel wrapped in brown paper and string. Inside: an old block plane, cast iron, the blade freshly ground, the knob polished. "For the joints," he said. It's the best present I've had since the cello.

I'd like the next ten years to have him in them. There. Thirty, and saying things out loud to a diary.
