The hull has stood up. All weekend we've been folding the panels up from flat and stitching them together along their edges with twists of copper wire, and this afternoon, quite suddenly, it stopped being plywood and became a boat: two long curves meeting at a keel, the bow lifting, the sheer line sweeping back. Five metres of it.

::media[The workshop on a Sunday, my hull on the strongback, everyone else's clutter around it]{file="boat-workshop-1"}

I walked round it three times. Jonas says it's the moment every builder remembers. I'll remember the smell: wood, warm copper, coffee.
