Rain: day 12. Since the streak ended the counter has had one dry day and then twelve wet ones, and the office now argues about whether the 24th of October was a dry spell or a clerical error. The banner at the top still reads *Longest streak: 21 days* — and somebody has added, in the code, without asking, *& counting (!)*. I've decided to leave it. It's true in spirit.
