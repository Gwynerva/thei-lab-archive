Last day of February at the workshop. The strongback's done, the forms are on, and the plywood is stacked by the bench: eight sheets of okoume, four millimetres thick, pinkish brown and smelling faintly of pepper. Next is joining the sheets into five-metre lengths, after Lofoten. I ran my hand down the top sheet before I left, like you would a horse.
