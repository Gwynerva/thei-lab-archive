Printed this year's Christmas cards yesterday on the kitchen floor, the way I always do: a kayak, of course, long and low on a line of waves, cut from lino over three evenings with a gouge that slipped twice and left me a nick on the thumb. Sixty cards, black ink on cream, the floor covered in them drying.

::media[Lifting the first print off the block, Jonas holding the other corner]{file="linocut-2"}

And today, at the workshop, the deck: we fitted the panels and started stitching them on with copper wire, back where we began in March. It will be a boat with a lid by the new year.
