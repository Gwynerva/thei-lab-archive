## What a strongback is

Before you build a boat you build the thing that holds it straight. A strongback is a long, dead-level beam on legs — ours is a box of two spruce planks and plywood, six metres long, standing at hip height down the middle of the workshop — and everything else is set up on it. If it's crooked, the boat will be crooked, forever, and it will paddle in circles and everyone will know it was you.

## Today

We started at nine. Jonas cut nothing; I cut everything, and he watched and said *ja* or *nei*. Two planks ripped to width, glued and screwed to plywood webs; legs; feet; a great many shims. Then the level, which in Norwegian is a *vaterpass*, and a laser line along the top, and an hour of tapping wedges in by a millimetre and checking and tapping them out again.

At four o'clock the bubble sat in the middle all along the six metres, and Jonas put a mug of coffee on one end and said it wouldn't slide off, and it didn't.
