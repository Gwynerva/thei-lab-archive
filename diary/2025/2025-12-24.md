Ragnhild by the stove all afternoon in her good cardigan. Whitby on the laptop at five, with paper hats. Pinnekjøtt. Sigrid washing up and refusing help. All of that is written down elsewhere.

This is the part that isn't.

After the dishes, after Eirik had driven Ragnhild home and Sigrid had gone, Jonas said come and look at the stars, and I said it was minus six, and he said come anyway. At the bottom of the orchard in the snow he pointed low in the south, and there it was, the star I am named after, the one Grandad showed Nana from the deck of a ship. He had looked it up. He had a ring in his pocket that he had twisted from the copper wire we stitched *Nana June* with, and he got as far as *vil du* before I said yes.

In June, at her kitchen table, Sigrid asked me when we were going to get married, and I wrote here that I was not going to be the one to ask. I wasn't. I am glad I waited.

We came in with snow on our boots and sat on the floor of the *stova* in front of the stove until two, not saying much, the ring on my finger catching the firelight, gold and then green and then gold. Biscuit slept through the whole of it on the bench, which is exactly what Biscuit would do.

We have told nobody. In the morning.
