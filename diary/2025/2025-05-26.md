Our first proper row in the house. About money, about the roof, about who was meant to ring whom — but really about being tired, and about Jonas being in Bergen two nights a week while I'm here with tarpaulins and men on the roof and a client's deadline. I said things. He said things, which for Jonas means four sentences. Then he went out to the barn and I went to the orchard, and after an hour we met at the stream and both said sorry at the same moment. Writing it down because it matters that we can.
