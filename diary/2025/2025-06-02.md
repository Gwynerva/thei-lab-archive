## Window one

The first of the fourteen came out today: the small one on the stairs, the one that sticks, the one Ragnhild laughed about. Jonas cut through the old paint round the frame, I held it, and it came out of the wall in one piece with a sigh, like a tooth that had been waiting. We carried it to the barn and laid it on trestles, and the hole in the wall looked out at the orchard with no glass in the way. It's boarded up tonight.

## The plan, for each of them

- [x] number it, and the hole it came from
- [ ] strip the old paint, with heat and patience, not chemicals
- [ ] take out the panes, every one, and keep the old glass, wavy as it is
- [ ] mend the rotten corners with new pine, scarfed in
- [ ] linseed oil, new putty, linseed paint, white
- [ ] put it back and make it open and shut like a new one

## Fourteen

Sigrid thinks we're mad not to buy new windows. Ragnhild, when I rang her tonight, said her husband's grandfather made them, and that they'll last another hundred years if somebody loves them a little. That's the plan. One a week, if the summer lets us, and each one gets a page in the notebook.
