Mum rang to say the card came: the linocut of the house with all fourteen windows lit. It is on her mantelpiece next to the abbey, she said, which is the highest honour our family has.

Jonas's present, written here, where he never reads until I tell him to:

:::spoiler
A map of every trip we have paddled together since the course in January 2022, drawn and coloured and framed in the old frame we found in the barn loft: Sandviken, Herdla, the islands, the fjord at home. Sixty-one trips, a line for each, in his favourite blue.
:::
