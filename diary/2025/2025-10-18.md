## The first fire

Half past six, dark, the first frost forecast and our breath showing in the hall. Jonas laid it the way his grandfather taught him, birch bark and small sticks, and handed me the matches. It caught at once.

::media[The fire, an hour in, the door open a crack]{file="video-fire-1-loop"}

## Who moved in first

Biscuit, of course. He came in from the hall, looked at the stove, and lay down in front of it in the exact spot where the heat is best, as if someone had told him in advance where it would be. He has not moved since. He is ten this year, about, and his hips are stiff in the mornings. This is going to be his winter.

## The house, warm

By nine the *stova* was warm enough to take our jumpers off, for the first time since we came. We ate supper on our knees in front of the stove like students. I wrote the date on the pantry door in pencil, under Ragnhild's dates for the first snow of every year since 1952.

Three good things:

1. The draw of the chimney, steady as a breath.
2. Jonas asleep in the chair by half past nine with his socks steaming on the fender.
3. Biscuit's belly, turned to the fire.
