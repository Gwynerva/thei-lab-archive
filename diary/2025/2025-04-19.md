Three weeks indoors, the rule for a cat in a new house, and today the door was opened. Biscuit sat on the step for twenty minutes considering the orchard, then walked the edge of the tun with his tail straight up, smelled every corner of the barn, ate some grass, was sick, and came back in for lunch. A good first outing.
