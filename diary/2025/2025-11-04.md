Day two of the kitchen. Under the last of the lino, the boards: wide pine, grey and dry and sound, worn into soft hollows in front of where the sink and the range have stood since 1891. You can see where people stood for a hundred and thirty years. Under one loose board, pushed in as packing, a newspaper from March 1958 with the price of herring and a photograph of the new ferry. Into the archive box it goes.
