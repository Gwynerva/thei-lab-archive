My editor from Fjellstue came out on the morning ferry to walk one of the shore walks with me and talk about the drawings. She walks faster than I do and talks faster than she walks. I came home with a list, a sunburnt neck and a blister, and a feeling, for the first time, that the book is a real thing other people are waiting for.
