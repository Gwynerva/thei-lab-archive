Written at the kitchen table at the end of a long day that started at two in the morning with a drip on the stairs.

The tarpaulin Jonas nailed up inside the roof is holding. The jam pan and the washing-up bowl are still on the stairs, in case. There is a brown stain on the ceiling over the turn in the shape of a hand, and a smell in the hall of wet plaster and old wood that I recognise from every house in Bergen in October.

Rain: day three. Fourteen windows tight as drums, and the water comes in through the roof. Nana would have had something to say. She would have said it while handing me a bucket.
