*Første juledag*. We rang Whitby at nine with the news. Mum cried before I had finished the sentence. Dad asked whether the ring fitted and then said, gruffly, that he had wondered when. Clare screamed so loudly that Isla started crying in the background, and then wanted to talk to Biscuit about it.

Then Sigrid, who said she had known since June and had only been waiting to be told.

The rest of the day: cold pinnekjøtt, a walk to the bottom gate in the snow, and the copper ring going green and gold by the light of the stove.
