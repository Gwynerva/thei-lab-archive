## What this year is

New Year's Day, slept late, fireworks still in my ears. Wrote down what 2025 holds, as far as I can see it:

- a house, and a move, and a roof
- the QGIS course again in February, and in autumn if anyone comes
- maps for anyone who'll pay for one, and a few for nobody
- swims in a fjord instead of a sea bath

That's a great deal for one year, and I know what I'm like. So the other list, shorter, is what stays the same wherever the house is: the Sunday call with Mum, the cello for twenty minutes most days, a walk before noon, Biscuit's portions, and writing this down.
