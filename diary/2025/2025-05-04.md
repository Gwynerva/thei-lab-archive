First swim in the fjord. Off the rocks below the house, nine degrees, Jonas holding the towel and Biscuit watching from the wall in total disapproval. The water is greener than the sea at Nordnes, and stiller, and tastes less of salt. *Hver dag*, the old woman said. Not every day. But the first.
