*Sankthansaften*. A bonfire on the shore below the village, the whole of Utne there with sausages on sticks, the fire reflected in the flat fjord and the mountains pink at eleven at night. Someone had a fiddle. The Nesheims' grandchildren threw pine cones in to watch them flare. I stood with my back to the heat and my face to the water and thought: this is home now. I didn't say it out loud. I don't need to.
