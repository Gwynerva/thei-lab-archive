a flat day. Not the old kind, I think, not 2023, but it had the same grey edge. Too many things open at once: the maps not started, the wall half done, the course in a week, two invoices. I sat at the desk for two hours and drew nothing.

What Tove taught me, written here so I can find it: name it, make it small, do one thing. I named it (tired, behind). I made it small (it is Saturday). I did one thing: walked down to the fjord and put my hands in it. It was very cold. It helped more than it had any right to.
