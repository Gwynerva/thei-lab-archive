## The pitch

Sent it this afternoon, after three evenings and a whole Sunday. Fjellstue is a very small :hint[forlag]{note="a publisher; a fjellstue is a mountain lodge, which suits them"}, and the man with the grey beard does everything there but the drawing. What I proposed:

- **thirty walks** around the Hardangerfjord, from an hour to a long day
- **thirty maps**, drawn by hand in ink and watercolour, one to a spread
- for each walk its distance, its climb, where to park or which ferry, and one thing to look for that isn't in the other guides
- a book that fits in a coat pocket and doesn't mind rain

With it went two small sample maps, of the walk above Utne and the old church road, and the list of all thirty, which at the moment is twenty-two walks I know and eight I've only seen on a map and want to.

## The title

I proposed *Thirty Walks Around the Fjord*. Plain, and true. He likes his titles plain. If he changes it, I'll live.

## The fear

That it's too much. That I'm doing what I've always done, taking on one project too many — a house, a roof, a course, clients, and now a book. Jonas, reading over my shoulder, said: "You'd walk them anyway." That's true. I would. The book only asks me to write down where I went.
