Practice log: forty minutes. Scales, then the prelude from the first suite, slowly, the way Mrs Ogilvie made me play it in Leith with the metronome at sixty. The cello has opinions about the damp in this house, and the A string will not stay where I put it.

The Sandviken strings start again in September. I will drive to Bergen every other Tuesday and sleep on Anna's sofa, which she says is the price of a good second desk.
