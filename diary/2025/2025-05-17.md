## Gratulerer med dagen

My fourth 17 May in Norway and the first in a village, which is a different thing entirely. In Bergen it's a city in its best clothes; in [Utne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utne "the village below the house, for anyone wondering where we are") it's a family party that everyone is invited to whether they like it or not.

## The parade

Forty children and a brass band of eleven, marching from the school to the church and back, with flags bigger than some of the children. Half the village in :hint[bunad]{note="the embroidered folk costume, different for every valley; Hardanger's has a red bodice and a beaded front"}, the other half in suits, and me in my good dress and proper shoes, as instructed. Sigrid in her mother's bunad, the silver catching the light, looking a foot taller than usual.

## Afterwards

Speeches in the school, which I mostly understood. Hot dogs, ice cream, a cake competition (Kari won, again). A boy of about seven asked if I was the English lady with the cat, and when I said yes he asked whether the cat was English too. He is. He's a Leith cat. The boy told everyone.

Walking home up the hill, Jonas took my hand and said: "Next year you'll know everyone's name." I said I already know eleven. He said that's a start.
