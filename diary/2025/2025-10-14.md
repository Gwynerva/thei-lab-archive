The old post road along the shore towards Kvanndal, two hours on a ledge of a track with the water underneath and the birches gone yellow. The last of the thirty I had not yet walked even once. I walked it alone, slowly, on purpose. My boots have a hole in the left heel which I have decided is part of the book.

The publisher has put up a page for it already, with a cover that is a placeholder and a title that isn't:

::link[Thirty Walks Around the Fjord, at Forlaget Fjellstue]{url="https://example.org/fjellstue/thirty-walks"}

Now the drawing. Thirty maps by the end of April. I have done the arithmetic and then put it away in a drawer.
