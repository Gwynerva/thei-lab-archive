## The orchard, white

It happened overnight, or it felt like it. Yesterday the trees were knuckles of pink buds; this morning the orchard was white, and it hummed with bees and smelled of honey and something green. I stood at the bedroom window in my nightie and said "oh" out loud, the small round *oh* of my students.

## Sigrid

She came up at noon, walked the rows with her hands behind her back like a general, and counted the trees in flower: forty, the old Gravenstein by the gate last of all, which she said had been "thinking about it". Then she stood under the biggest tree and said the thing she must say every year, and which I'm going to put on the kitchen wall when we have a kitchen wall:

:::quote[Sigrid, under the old tree]{align="center"}
The blossom is the promise. The apples are the work. Don't confuse the two.
:::

## The day

I didn't work. I sat in the orchard with a notebook and drew the trees, one by one, and gave them numbers. Number one is by the gate. Number forty is the one leaning over the stream. Jonas brought coffee and lay in the grass and slept. Biscuit stalked a bumblebee for an hour and lost.

Three good things, though it's one thing three times:

1. The blossom.
2. The blossom.
3. The blossom.
