## Signed

Signed the contract for [the atlas](ref:project:walk-atlas "thirty walks, thirty maps") at the kitchen table first thing, with the fountain pen Mum gave me when I graduated. Then I took the early ferry and the long road into Bergen and climbed the stairs above the bike shop to hand it over at the office of Forlaget Fjellstue — four desks, a dog, maps all over the walls. He shook my hand and the dog sneezed.

## What I've promised

- thirty walks, walked, measured and written up
- thirty maps, drawn by hand, the finished drawings by next spring
- proofs in the summer after, and the book in the autumn
- no walk I haven't done myself, in weather

The last one isn't in the contract. It's mine.

:::private
The advance is modest, which is the polite word. It will pay for six windows' worth of putty and paint and a few months of the rent to Ragnhild, roughly. I don't care. Somebody is going to print a book with my maps in it.
:::

## Nordnes

Afterwards I went to the sea bath at Nordnes in my good clothes and stood at the rail. The old woman was in the water. She shouted *hver dag?* and I shouted back *nesten*. Nearly. Then I bought a cinnamon bun at the bakery in Sandviken, out of habit, and ate it on the ferry home.
