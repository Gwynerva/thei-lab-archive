Sigrid, at her kitchen table, over coffee, with no warning at all: "So. When are you two getting married?" Jonas was out in the yard. I said we hadn't talked about it. She said "Hm," in the voice she uses for trees that are thinking about it, and passed me a biscuit.

We haven't talked about it. I find I'd like to. I find I'm not going to be the one who asks.
