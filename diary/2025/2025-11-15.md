Written by the stove at ten at night. Snow since before dawn, fifteen centimetres and still coming, the road unploughed until two, and the whole day in the house with nowhere to be. Jonas dug the car out for no reason except that he couldn't bear not to. I stood at the landing window for a long time with my tea, watching the fjord go black between two white shores, like a map with only two colours left in the box.
