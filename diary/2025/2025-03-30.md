First morning at Øvre Lid. Woke at six to a silence so complete I could hear my own ears, then a blackbird, then the ferry's engine far below. Cold in the house — the old stove isn't to be lit until someone has looked at the chimney — so we made coffee on the hotplate in our coats and drank it on the front step, watching the fjord go from grey to silver. Folgefonna white across the water. I'm going to have this every morning. I can't get used to the idea.
