Five years tomorrow since Nana. I always think I will be fine and then the day before I am not. Rang Mum; she had been to the churchyard at the Bay with rowan from the tree in the lane, which is still there though the cottage has new people and a hot tub in the garden.

I got out the tin of her letters and read the one from 1953 about Grandad's ship coming into Whitby in a gale and her standing on the pier all night. Then I did nothing useful for the rest of the day, and that was the right thing to do.
