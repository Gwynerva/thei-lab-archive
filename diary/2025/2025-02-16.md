A weekend at Øvre Lid with Sigrid and a pair of loppers, pruning the orchard in a thin cold sun. She showed me on the first tree, then stood back with her arms folded for the second, saying *nei* and *ja* and once *absolutt ikke*. You cut out what crosses, what grows inwards, what points down. You leave the tree room to breathe. By the tenth tree my arms were jelly; by the twentieth I'd stopped asking her. One shrivelled apple was still hanging on the oldest tree from last autumn. I left it there.
