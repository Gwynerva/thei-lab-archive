Taught the last evening of the spring course from the kitchen table at Øvre Lid, over the phone's hotspot, with a blanket round my shoulders and boxes behind me. Eleven maps presented. The connection dropped twice, and when it came back the second time they were all waving at the window behind me: the fjord, at dusk. "That," said the woman from Florø, "is your next map."
