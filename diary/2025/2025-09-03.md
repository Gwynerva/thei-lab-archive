Jonas has taken the panels off the inside of the north wall, so that the boards for the insulation can go in when we are home from Georgia. The house has its ribs showing. Inside the wall: air, mice, newspaper from 1911, a broken clay pipe and one very small child's shoe in old leather. Jonas wrapped the shoe in a cloth with a note of the date and put it back where it was. I did not ask whether that is a Norwegian custom or a Jonas one. I didn't want to find out by not doing it.
