Anna came to help, in old clothes, with a box of *wienerbrød* and the manner of a team lead: she made a list, gave us each a room and timed the coffee breaks. By four the kitchen was in boxes. Walking her home, she stopped at the harbour and said she'd miss me at the swims. I said she has never once come to the swims. She said she'd miss knowing I was there.
