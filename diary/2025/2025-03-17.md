Sad today, properly, in a way I didn't expect. Bergen is the city I came to alone, with a cat in a crate and a job I was afraid of. It's the city I got well in. The steps, the sea bath, the woman at the bakery, the rain I learned to count. Leaving it feels like leaving behind the person who learned all that, and I'm not sure she's coming with me. Jonas found me on the floor of the empty workroom, sat down beside me and said nothing. That was right.
