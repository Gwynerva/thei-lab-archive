## The ferry

Jonas drove to Flesland to fetch them and I waited at the quay in Utne, which was silly, because I could have gone with him, but I wanted to see their faces when the ferry came round the point.

Dad stood at the rail the whole way over from Kvanndal, reading the water the way he reads the harbour at home. Mum held on to her hat. When they came off, with two suitcases, one of which clinked, she said the fjord looked like a postcard somebody had forgotten to stop painting, and Dad said nothing for a long time and then asked what the tidal range was.

## The house

I had planned a tour. There was no tour. Mum stopped in the hall and put her hand flat on the old panelling, the way she touches a cake to see if it is done, and said, "Oh, it's *old*," as though that were the best news she had had all year. Dad went straight out again to look at the windows from the yard, counted them under his breath, and asked which ones were finished. Three, I said. He said, "Right," and I knew what his week was going to be.

## Sigrid

She came up at seven with flatbread and a bowl of new potatoes, and she and Mum looked at each other over the porch rail for a second like two cats on a wall, and then started talking about redcurrants. Light until nearly midnight. Mum kept checking her watch as if it were broken.
