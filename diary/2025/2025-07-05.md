Jonas drove to Flesland to fetch them and I waited at the quay in Utne, which was silly, because I could have gone with him, but I wanted to see their faces when the ferry came round the point.

Dad stood at the rail the whole way over from Kvanndal, reading the water the way he reads the harbour at home. Mum held on to her hat. When they came off, she said the fjord looked like a postcard somebody had forgotten to stop painting, and Dad said nothing for a long time and then asked what the tidal range was.

::media[The white hotel by the quay, where I waited for the ferry]{file="utne-2"}

Sigrid came up at seven with flatbread and a bowl of new potatoes, and she and Mum looked at each other over the porch rail for a second like two cats on a wall, and then started talking about redcurrants. Light until nearly midnight. Mum kept checking her watch as if it were broken.
