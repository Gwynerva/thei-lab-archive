Booked it. Tbilisi on the fifth of September, the night train west, Mestia, four days on foot to Ushguli, and home on the fifteenth. Jonas has wanted to see the Caucasus since he was fourteen and read about the towers in a library book. I want a holiday where nobody hands me a putty knife. Both wishes granted, if the weather holds.
