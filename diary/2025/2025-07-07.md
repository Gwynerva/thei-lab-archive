Rain all morning, so Dad and I did window six on the trestles in the barn. He dug out the old putty in a quarter of the time it takes me and then stood at my shoulder while I bedded the glass, which was exactly like being twelve in the chandlery. "Thinner," he said. "You're not icing a cake."

Jonas and Dad spent the afternoon at the other end of the barn talking about planking. Mum labelled jam. Nobody asked anybody anything, and everyone was happy.
