## Five days

The move is done, in the sense that everything is inside the house and nothing is where it will stay. Five days: the van, the ferry, the cake, the cold, the course, the boxes, and today the last of them flattened and stacked in the barn for the next people who move, whoever they are.

What's where, for now:

- the plan chest in the north room, damp and all, up on bricks
- the cello in the sitting room by Ragnhild's chair
- the bed in the room with the window over the orchard
- Biscuit everywhere, inspecting

## Things I didn't know

That you can hear the ferry's engine from the house a full minute before you can see the ferry. That the kitchen floor slopes enough to roll a pencil. That Eirik Nesheim walks past every morning at seven with his dog and lifts one hand without looking up. That the stars here are not the stars in Bergen. That I'd sleep nine hours a night and wake before the alarm, every day, without trying.
