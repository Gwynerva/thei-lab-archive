Drove to Utne with Jonas to measure. The house in winter is a different house: the fjord grey, the orchard bare, the snow on the hills right down to the road, and indoors the cold that comes up through the floor. We measured every room with frozen fingers and drank Sigrid's coffee in our coats. The stairs are narrower than I thought. Jonas said "I told you" with his eyes only.
