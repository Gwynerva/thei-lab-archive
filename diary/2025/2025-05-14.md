The results from the well came back: the water is fine. Better than fine; the woman at the lab said it was "the kind people pay for in bottles". It comes from a spring up the hill, through a pipe Ragnhild's father laid after the war, into a stone well-house behind the barn. I went and stood in the well-house, in the cool and the dark, and recorded the sound of it running in.
