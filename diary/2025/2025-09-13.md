The jeep back to Mestia in the morning, three hours of potholes, and in the afternoon one more glacier, the gentle one, through the pines to the ice.

What I keep thinking about is not the ice but the signs on the way up to it: small posts with years on them, where the glacier reached in that year. The posts get further and further apart and further and further from the ice. I photographed every one, which is a cartographer's habit, and Jonas waited and did not say anything, which is one of the reasons.
