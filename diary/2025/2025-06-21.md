He was in the barn. In the loft of the barn, which we didn't know he could reach, shut in when Jonas closed the hatch yesterday afternoon. At six this morning, looking for torch batteries, I heard a furious, offended, hoarse little voice from above my head, and there he was at the edge of the hatch, covered in hay and cobwebs, utterly outraged. He ate two breakfasts and then lay on my chest on the sofa and purred like an engine for an hour, and I let him, and cried into his fur.
