He was in the barn, we think. In the loft of the barn, which we didn't know he could reach, shut in when Jonas closed the hatch yesterday afternoon. At ten past five this morning there was a furious, offended, hoarse little voice at the door, and there he was on the step, covered in hay and cobwebs, utterly outraged. He ate two breakfasts and then lay on my chest on the sofa and purred like an engine for an hour, and I let him, and cried into his fur.
