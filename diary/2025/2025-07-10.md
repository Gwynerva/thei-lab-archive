Dad's last working day, he called it, though he is on holiday. Three windows, a record:

7. The east one in the small room behind the kitchen. Dad dug out, I bedded, Dad faced off the putty with the old knife from his shop that he brought in his luggage on purpose.
8. The small one on the stairs, the one Biscuit sits in. Two panes cracked; we had one spare from the box in the barn and one that came out of a picture frame.
9. The second kitchen window, done by Dad alone while I made the tea, as a point.

He wrote the year in pencil on the inside of each frame, *M.T. 2025*, small, under the paint, where the next person will find it.
