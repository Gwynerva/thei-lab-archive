Wrote to Nana again, the way I do every November, on the page nobody else reads. Told her about the windows, the snow, the stove, and Dad in a kayak. She would have liked Sigrid. They would have argued about jam and both been right.

> A stitch in time is still a stitch.

She said it about everything that was late. I think she meant that late is not the same as never.
