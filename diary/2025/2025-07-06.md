Mum found the redcurrant bushes behind the barn before breakfast, before I had shown her anything at all, and by lunchtime the porch smelled of jam and Sigrid's big pan was on the camping stove.

Dad has found a stick, marked it in tenths with my pencil, and taken his watch down to the bottom of the orchard to measure the tide. He is not to be disturbed. He came up for tea looking pleased and disgusted in equal parts, which is how he looks at most things he loves.
