## The year

A year ago tonight I was in the sea at Nordnes for the hundredth time, and we still lived in Sandviken, and Øvre Lid was a house I had seen once, in the rain, with Jonas's hand on the small of my back. Now we are snowed in at the top of the track, happily, with a stove, no kitchen, fourteen windows and a ring made of copper wire.

## 2025, in a list

- **March**: we moved, in a hired van, with the cello on my knees.
- **May**: the orchard in blossom, and a letter from a laboratory saying our water is fit to drink.
- **June to August**: fourteen windows.
- **September**: Georgia, the towers, the river under the glacier, a horse.
- **October**: two hundred and twelve litres of apple juice, and a fire in the stove.
- **November**: the kitchen out, the first snow, the first maps.
- **December**: the star I am named after, low in the south.

## Three good things, for the whole year

1. Dad in a kayak on the fjord, shouting Nana's line about calm seas.
2. The evening the stove first drew and the house got warm.
3. The twenty-fourth.
