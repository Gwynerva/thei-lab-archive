They left on the eight o'clock ferry. Jonas drove them to the airport; I stood on the quay and waved until the boat went round the point, which is what Mum does at Whitby station, so I suppose it runs in the family.

The house is very quiet. Mum left the last jar of redcurrant jam on the shelf with a note: "Your father says number nine wants another week before it's painted." Dad left the putty knives cleaned, oiled and laid out in order of size on the bench in the barn. Neither of them said goodbye properly, and both of them did it anyway.
