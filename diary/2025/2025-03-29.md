## Moving day

We had the van at seven, the biggest the place in Åsane would give us, and by ten the first load was in it and the flat was a box with our voices in it. Stood in the street for a minute looking up at the window. Then the long road east: Jonas driving, Biscuit in his carrier between my feet and the cello upright between my knees, the rain stopping at Norheimsund as if someone had turned it off.

On the ferry from Kvanndal Biscuit made one long low noise, the one he keeps for the vet, and a little girl came to look at him through the bars and told him, very seriously, that boats are nice. He was not persuaded.

## The house

At the gate: Eirik and Kari Nesheim from the farm below, with a cake and a crate of last year's apples; Sigrid comes tomorrow, with opinions. The van could not get round the last bend, so the first load went the last hundred metres on our backs and in Ragnhild's wheelbarrow, and the sofa did not go up the stairs, exactly as Jonas said. It's in the sitting room by Ragnhild's window, where I think it was always meant to be.

## Nana says

> Make do and mend.

The kitchen, as a kitchen, is a sink, the fridge we brought, the camping stove on the porch and a table. It'll do. We ate Kari's cake for dinner.

It's three and a half years since I came to Bergen with a cat and nothing else. Tonight there's the same cat, and Jonas, and a house. I keep thinking back to [the viewing](ref:event:house-viewing#:~:text=fjord) in November, and how far away all this seemed.
