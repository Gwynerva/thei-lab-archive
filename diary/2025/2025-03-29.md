## Moving day

The van came at seven, two men and a great deal of cheerfulness, and by ten the flat was an empty box with our voices in it. Handed back the keys. Stood in the street for a minute looking up at the window. Then the long road east: Biscuit in his crate on my knee in the front of our car, Jonas following the van, the rain stopping at Norheimsund as if someone had turned it off.

On the ferry from Kvanndal Biscuit yowled the whole crossing, and a little girl came to look at him through the bars and told him, very seriously, that boats are nice. He was not persuaded.

## The house

At the gate: Sigrid, of course, and Eirik and Kari Nesheim from the farm below, with a cake and a crate of last year's apples. The van men carried everything up the slope, and the sofa did not go up the stairs, exactly as Jonas said. It's in the sitting room by Ragnhild's window, where I think it was always meant to be.

## Nana says

> Make do and mend.

The kitchen, as a kitchen, is a sink, the fridge we brought, a two-ring hotplate and a table. It'll do. We ate Kari's cake for dinner.

It's three and a half years since I came to Bergen with a cat and nothing else. Tonight there's the same cat, and Jonas, and a house. I keep thinking back to [the viewing](ref:event:house-viewing#:~:text=fjord) in November, and how far away all this seemed.

Biscuit came out of the crate at nine, walked the whole ground floor with his belly low, ate, and went to sleep on the sofa as though he'd lived here all his life.
