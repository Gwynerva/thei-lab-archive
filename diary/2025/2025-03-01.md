## The plan

Four weeks to empty a flat we've filled for nineteen months, with two years of my old flat folded into it. Written on the inside of a cupboard door, where I'll see it every morning:

- [ ] week one: books, and the things we never use
- [ ] week two: the workroom
  - [ ] maps, flat, in the plan chest
  - [ ] paints and inks, sealed
  - [ ] the cello stays out till the last day
- [ ] week three: the kitchen, all but one pan and two of everything
- [ ] week four: clothes, Biscuit's things, the cleaning
- [x] book the van
- [x] tell the landlord, in writing

## The first box

Started with the books, because books are honest: either you'll read them again or you won't. I won't read most of them again. I packed them all anyway. Fourteen boxes, and the shelves look like a mouth with the teeth out.

## Nana

In the back of the wardrobe, wrapped in a pillowcase, her sewing box and the quilt. I sat on the floor with them for a while. She moved house once in her life, at twenty-two, and then stayed nearly seventy years. I've moved eleven times. I think she would say it's about time I stopped. The quilt is going in the car with us, not the van, on my knee if need be.
