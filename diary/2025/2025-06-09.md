Whit Monday, *andre pinsedag*, and the roads full of cars with bikes on the back. My eleventh atlas walk, along the slopes above Lofthus, through orchards that were white three weeks ago and are green now, the fruit already set, small and hard as beads. Eleven walks of thirty. More than a third of a book.
