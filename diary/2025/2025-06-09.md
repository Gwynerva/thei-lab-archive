Whit Monday, *andre pinsedag*, and the roads full of cars with bikes on the back. Atlas walk ten, along the slopes above Lofthus, through orchards that were white three weeks ago and are green now, the fruit already set, small and hard as beads. Ten walks of thirty. A third of a book.
