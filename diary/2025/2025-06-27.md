Half a year, more or less, and this is where it has got to: three months in the house; a roof that keeps the rain out; four windows of fourteen out, two of them back in and opening as if they were new; eleven walks of thirty; one cat lost and found; two flags still on the gatepost from 17 May that nobody has taken down. Jonas is home. The fjord is very still tonight, and I can hear the ferry coming a full minute before I see it.
