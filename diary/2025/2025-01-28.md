Coffee in town with the quiet man with the grey beard from the Christmas market. He runs Forlaget Fjellstue, a small publisher of walking guides and local history, three people and a dog in an office above a bike shop. He's had my seven mountains print on his wall since December and has been thinking. What he's been thinking is a book: walks around the Hardangerfjord, each with its own hand-drawn map. "You're moving there anyway," he said. I said I'd think about it, and thought about nothing else all the way home.
