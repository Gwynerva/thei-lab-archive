The rain stopped on Monday night and the roofer came over from Kinsarvik first thing, with the three ladders. New battens over the stairs, new boards where the old ones had gone soft, the slipped tiles back, the tarpaulin down. It will hold, he said, until the spring, when the whole of that side wants doing properly. The stain on the ceiling over the turn is drying in the shape of [the leak](ref:event:roof-leak#:~:text=stairs) itself, a long brown finger pointing down the stairs.
