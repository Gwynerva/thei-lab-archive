Picked all day for tomorrow with Sigrid, who arrived at eight with the back of her van full of borrowed crates and the face of a general. Gravensteins, Aroma, the last of the Summerreds, and a few from the two trees nobody can name, which are small and sour. "For the acid," Sigrid said. "Cider without acid is a sweet drink for children."

Arms like noodles. Crates in the barn, stacked to my shoulder. The whole barn smells of September.
