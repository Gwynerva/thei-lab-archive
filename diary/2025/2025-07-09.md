Drove Mum to Odda in the morning to see Ragnhild, who is ninety and was waiting in the hall of the home with her coat on, as if we might take her somewhere. We took her to the café by the water.

She told Mum about the house when she was a girl: her own mother at the kitchen window, the one I puttied in June, watching for the steamer from Bergen. Mum told her about Robin Hood's Bay and Nana's rowan tree. Two old houses, two women at windows. I mostly listened, and drew the café table in the margin of my notebook with the cups where they stood.

::media[Odda at the head of the fjord, from the road on the way home]{file="odda-1"}

We came home to find Dad on a ladder. More has been said about this, mostly by Mum.
