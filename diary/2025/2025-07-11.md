Walked them up the path behind the house to the old summer farm, one of the short walks in the atlas and the one I want people to do first. An hour up for us; two for Mum, who stopped at every flower and asked its name, and I knew about four. Dad sat on the wall of the old cowshed at the top and looked at the fjord for so long that Jonas went over to check he was all right. He was. He said it was the best view anyone had ever made him walk up to.
