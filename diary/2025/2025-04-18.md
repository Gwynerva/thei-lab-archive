Good Friday: my third atlas walk. Drove to the end of the valley road and walked on towards the glacier until the snow stopped us. From the top of the track you can see the ice spilling over the rock, grey and white and enormous. Ate oranges on a boulder, as everyone does here at Easter, and drew the walk in the notebook with cold fingers.
