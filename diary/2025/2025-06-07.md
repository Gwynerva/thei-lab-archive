Coffee at the Nesheims'. Eirik showed me the cider press in their barn, a great wooden thing with an iron screw like a ship's capstan, and said that in October we'll bring our apples down and press them together, as the two farms have done for longer than anyone can remember. Kari gave me a bottle of last year's to take home: dry, sharp, a little cloudy. Jonas drank most of it.
