Started drawing the atlas. The Vøringsfossen walk first, because Biscuit chose it. The old plotter from Leith drew the contours in faint pencil overnight, whirring away on the landing like a very slow insect, and this morning I inked the path over them, then the edge of the gorge, then the tarn. Four hours, and I have not started the colour. Twenty-nine to go after this one, by April.

::media[The paint box, open on the desk, the blues already muddy]{file="watercolour-1"}

We are cooking on the camping stove again, in the hall, like in April. It feels like going backwards. Jonas says in an old house that is the only way forwards.
