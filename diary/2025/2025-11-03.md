## Out

The kitchen is coming out. Jonas handed me the crowbar at eight and said I should have the first go, since it was my idea, and I put it behind the first cupboard and pulled, and the whole thing came off the wall with a noise like a sigh, as if it had been waiting. By tonight the cupboards were in the trailer, the cooker on its back in the yard like a dead beetle, and two of the three layers of lino up. Tomorrow the last layer, and whatever is under it.

## What I'll miss

Not much, honestly. But three things I want to remember about the old kitchen, now that it is going:

- the bent nails that held the cupboard doors shut, each one bent a slightly different way, by somebody, over fifty years
- the way the evening light came across the worktop in March, which Ragnhild asked us to keep, and which we will
- Sigrid's fish soup on the camping stove on our second night in the house, eaten standing up because there was nowhere to sit

## Monday

It was Monday, so I washed the plaster dust out of my hair and taught the course at eight from the bedroom, with a plank across two boxes for a desk and a sheet pinned up behind me to hide the wall. Evening five: labels, and why nobody should ever put a place name in capitals in a font that was designed for shop signs. Nobody in Tasmania noticed the sheet. The farmer from across the fjord asked whether I was in a tent.
