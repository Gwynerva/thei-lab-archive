Rain at last, all day, soft and steady. Rain: day one. The old Bergen habit came back without being asked.

Sverre's barometer in the hall had been saying so since Friday. I tapped it this morning and the needle dropped another notch, as if pleased with itself. I recorded the rain on the kitchen window from the table, for the collection, the first rain at Øvre Lid I have kept.
