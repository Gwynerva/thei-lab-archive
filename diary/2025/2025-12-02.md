The last evening of the autumn course was last night. Twelve maps, and I have looked at all of them twice. Three I keep going back to:

1. Tasmania's grandmother's island, every house with the name of whoever lived there in 1950, from the old woman's own memory over the phone
2. The frost pockets in the orchard across the fjord, blue in the low corner, exactly where the farmer says the blossom always goes brown
3. The ski tracks above Tromsø, coloured by how much the teacher swears on each one, with a legend to match

I am tired and proud and want to sleep for a week.
