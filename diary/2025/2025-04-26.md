## Odda

Drove up the fjord to Odda with Jonas to see Ragnhild at the care home. A bright room on the second floor with a view of the water and the mountains, a geranium, a photograph of the house on the windowsill. She is tiny and very upright, with Jonas's eyes, and she held my hand the whole time as if checking I was real.

::media[Odda at the head of the fjord, from the road in]{file="odda-1"}

## What she told me

She was born at Øvre Lid in 1934 and lived nowhere else until last spring. The house was built by a man called Lars "with his own hands and his brother's back", and her parents had it after him. There were cows in the barn then, and sheep up the hill in summer, and every apple went on the boat to Bergen. She showed me with her hands how high the snow came in the winter of 1966: up to the second window.

She asked whether the window on the stairs still sticks. It does. She laughed until she had to wipe her eyes.

## What she asked

That we keep the rowan by the gate. That we never paint the house any colour but white. That I play the cello for her one day, because Sigrid has told her I do. I said yes to all three, and meant it, and now I'm going to have to practise.
