Scaffolding up at seven: two roofers and a boy, a radio, and a great deal of swearing in a dialect I can only half follow. By evening the north side was stripped to the battens and you could see sky through the ceiling of the north room. We moved the plan chest into the sitting room just in time. Sleeping to the rustle of a tarpaulin.
