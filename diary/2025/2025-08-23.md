Up Buardalen from Odda to the snout of Buarbreen, the arm of Folgefonna that comes down nearly to the farms. The last part is ropes and wet rock and not for everybody, which I have to say in the book in a way that does not frighten everybody. The ice was grey and blue and ticking. Twenty years ago, the farmer at the bottom said, it came down to the bridge. I paced out where it comes to now and wrote it in the notebook with the date.
