The autumn cohort started on Monday. Twelve faces in twelve little windows: a planner from Stavanger, a geography teacher in Tromsø, a fruit farmer from across the fjord who wants to map the frost pockets in her orchard, a woman in Tasmania who joined at six in the morning her time with a cup of tea the size of her head. Each of them makes one map over the eight evenings, of anything at all, and I help them get there. Tasmania wants to map her grandmother's island. That one I am looking forward to.
