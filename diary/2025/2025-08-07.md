Window ten, in the big bedroom, had a pane cracked right across the corner. In a box under the little window at the back of the barn we found four old panes wrapped in a newspaper from 1962, the glass full of small bubbles and a faint wave, the kind nobody makes any more. One fitted, with a millimetre to spare. The bedroom now has a pane that bends the orchard a little when you move your head.
