Window ten, in the big bedroom, had a pane cracked right across the corner. In the box under the little window at the back of the barn, the one we raided for window eight, there were four old panes left, wrapped in a newspaper from 1962, the glass full of small bubbles and a faint wave, the kind nobody makes any more. One fitted, with a millimetre to spare. The bedroom now has a pane that bends the orchard a little when you move your head.
