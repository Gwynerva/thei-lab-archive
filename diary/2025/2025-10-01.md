First swim of the winter, off the steps of the boathouse below the house. Eleven degrees in the fjord, nine in the air, Jonas on the steps with a towel and a flask and no intention of getting in. I counted to sixty in the water, the way I did in Bergen, and the old feeling came back at about forty: everything in me goes quiet and very bright.

The hundredth swim was at Nordnes on the last day of last year. This is the first at home.
