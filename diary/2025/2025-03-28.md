The last box taped at nine tonight: the kettle, two mugs, the cat's bowl, the cello's rosin, the first-aid kit, a loaf. The flat is empty but for the mattress on the floor and us on it. You can hear the street through the bare walls, and the rain on the skylight, and the neighbour's radio. Last night in Sandviken. The van comes at seven.

:::private
I walked round every room at eleven touching the doorframes, the way I said Nana would. In the workroom I said thank you out loud. Nobody heard but Biscuit, and he doesn't tell.
:::
