A loop above Utne for the atlas, through birch and juniper, and every rowan on the way red with berries. I came home with a bag of them, the way Nana did every September, and made her jelly from the card in her handwriting that Mum gave me after the funeral.

::file[Nana's card, typed out, her spelling kept]{file="rowan-jelly" title="Rowan jelly"}

It set. It is the colour of the inside of a fire. Biscuit sniffed the jar and left, which is also what Grandad did, Mum says. Mum's tidied version is on [Nana's page](ref:page:nana-recipes "her recipes, with Mum's notes"), but I made it from the card.
