*Lille julaften*, little Christmas Eve. Jonas brought the tree in from the slope above the well, a small spruce, a little crooked, which is how we like them. Rice porridge for lunch with one almond hidden in it; Jonas found it, as he apparently has every year since he was four, which his mother finds suspicious.

The house smells of spruce and the stove and the gingerbread dough resting in the cold hall. Sigrid is steaming the pinnekjøtt at home and will bring it up tomorrow to finish on the old range in the corner, its first proper job in years.
