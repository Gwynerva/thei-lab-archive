Across the river on a horse at half past seven, the water up to its belly, my boots held up nearly to my chin. The horse did not care. The man leading it did not care. I cared a great deal and held the saddle with both hands while the river shouted. Jonas came over on the second trip looking as if he had enjoyed it.

Then the long climb to the Chkhutnieri pass, two hours of zigzags with the glacier on our left all the way, breaking off now and then in blue chunks, and the long green walk down to Iprari. My legs have stopped complaining and started simply reporting.
