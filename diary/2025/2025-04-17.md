*Skjærtorsdag*, Maundy Thursday. Dinner at Sigrid's farm, twelve of us at a table on trestles in her barn: lamb, apple cake and a great deal of opinion. Sigrid asked about the roof, listened to the whole answer, then said: "A roof is a hat. You don't buy a new head because the hat leaks." Jonas says she has a saying for everything and makes them up as she goes. I don't care. I'm writing them down.
