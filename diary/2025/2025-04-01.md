Unpacked the kitchen boxes into a kitchen that has two cupboards. Then, because my back hurt and the sun was out, walked the first atlas walk without meaning to: out of the gate, up through the orchard, along the old track to the ridge and down again by the stream. An hour and ten minutes. In the notebook:\
gate, orchard, the stile with the broken step, the ridge, the view both ways, the stream, home.\
That's a walk. That's one.
