Up to the old summer farm in the snow, the two of us, the ring under my glove. At the top the whole fjord was white and grey and still, and Folgefonna came out of the cloud for ten minutes as if it had been asked.

We talked about when. Summer. Here. In the orchard, under the trees, if the trees agree.
