## Arriving

We landed at three in the morning on Friday and slept until the heat woke us. Tbilisi from the guesthouse balcony: tin roofs, vines on everything, a church on every hill, and the river going through the middle the colour of milky tea.

::media[The old town roofs from the hill, the cathedral on the far side]{file="tbilisi-5" layout="stretch"}

## The market at the dry bridge

Before the heat, the flea market by the bridge: Soviet cameras, war medals, silver spoons, lace, records, and on a blanket at the end a whole box of old maps. I found a Soviet topographic sheet of upper Svaneti from the seventies, folded so often it has worn through at the creases, every tower drawn as a tiny black square. Jonas paid for it while I was still pretending not to want it. It is the best present anyone has ever bought me for the price of a coffee.

## The rest of it

The fortress, the baths, cheese in quantities we will not discuss, a clock tower that leans like a man at a bar. In a small church near the river a few people were singing, and we stood at the back until they stopped.

In the evening we sat on the steps above the bathhouse domes with a bottle of something amber from a man who said his uncle made it, and watched the balconies go dark one by one. I read about [Svaneti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svaneti "for the towers, mostly") in bed until Jonas turned the light out. Tomorrow night, the train.
