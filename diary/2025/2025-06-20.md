Biscuit went out after his supper and didn't come back. He always comes back. We called him round the tun and the barn and the orchard, then down the road to the Nesheims', then with torches along the stream, though it barely gets dark. Kari rang half the village. Nothing. It's half past one and I'm sitting on the front step with his bowl, the sky still light, and every rustle is a fox. Jonas is out on the hill again. I can't write any more.
