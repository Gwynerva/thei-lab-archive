The rowan trees in the park are heavy with berries, and it's [a year since the jelly](ref:event:rowan-jelly "Nana's kitchen, last September") in Nana's kitchen, the steam and the sugar and her hands showing mine how to tell when it's set. Picked a bag of them on the way home. Rang Nana to ask her for the recipe again, even though I have it written down, and she knew why, and told me anyway.
