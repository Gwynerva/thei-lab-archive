## The van

Callum left this morning in a hired van with his knives, his ice-cream machine, a bag of bread flour and two boxes of records. He made us breakfast before he went — the last fry-up, every pan in the kitchen, the whole flat smelling of bacon — and then washed every pan, which he has never done in two years. Priya and I carried things down the seventy-two steps, and at the bottom of the stair he carried us both in a hug that went on for some time. Then he got in the van, wound the window down, said "right, team" one last time, and drove off the wrong way up the street and had to come back past us, which spoiled the moment and improved it.

## The room

The room is empty now, the floor lighter where his rug was, four holes in the wall where his knife strip used to be. On Sunday it's Marta's. The kitchen is going to be very quiet on Saturday nights.

:::private
After the van turned the corner I went back up the seventy-two steps and sat on the top one and cried for a while, properly, the way I didn't at graduation. Then I got up and hoovered his room, because Nana says you clean when you're sad, and she's right, it helps, I don't know why.
:::
