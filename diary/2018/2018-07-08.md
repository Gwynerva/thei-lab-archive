Woke at five because the tent was an oven. Swam in a pool in the river below the campsite before breakfast: brown water, cold enough to stop your heart in a friendly way, a dipper on a rock watching us. Then the drive home, sunburnt in strange places, with the Buachaille in the mirror for miles.
