Dad and I drove to the Bay this morning and brought Nana to Mum and Dad's for Christmas, with her frame and her overnight bag and a tin of her own mince pies, which she'd made sitting down, with Mum doing the standing. Seven weeks after the fall she can do the stairs one at a time with a hand on the rail, and she did them, and wouldn't be helped, and got to the top and said, "There." The whole house breathed out.

She's asleep in my old room and I'm on the sofa, which is fine. Better than fine.
