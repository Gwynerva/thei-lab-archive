Ninety-two place cards. Every one done, in a shoebox, in alphabetical order, with my aunt spelled the least-upsetting way. Each one has a tiny drawing in the corner of something about the person — a bike, a cat, a stethoscope, a boat — which nobody asked for and which took longer than all the lettering put together.
