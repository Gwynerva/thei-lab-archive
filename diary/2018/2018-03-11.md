Mothering Sunday. Rang Mum, who'd had flowers from Clare and a phone call from me, and said both were lovely in exactly the same voice. Nana was there for lunch and came on the line to ask what the cello repairs had cost. I told her. She said:

> Make do and mend, and when you can't mend it, pay a man who can.

Twenty-nine minutes of bowing, fine.
