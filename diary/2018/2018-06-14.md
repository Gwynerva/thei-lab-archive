The flood model ran overnight and in the morning the water in the Fife town was flowing uphill, gathering behind the railway bridge in a lake three metres deep. It took me till lunch to see why: in the :hint[DEM]{note="digital elevation model, the ground as a grid of heights"} the bridge was solid, a dam, because lidar sees a bridge from above and has no idea there's a road going underneath. Cut a hole in it. The lake drained. Ines applauded.
