Callum came home from work at midnight, sat us both down at the kitchen table and told us: Glasgow. He's leaving at the end of August to cook in somebody else's kitchen through there while he saves up for his own place. He's been planning it since Easter. "A café," he said, "eleven chairs, bread every morning, and I choose the music." He looked so happy and so scared that we both hugged him at once and knocked a pan off the cooker.
