This year's card: the lighthouse at the end of the West Pier at Whitby, the tall stone one, with three lines of sea under it. Last year it was [a robin](ref:diary:2017-12-10 "the 2017 card, on the kitchen floor"); this year I wanted something of home that would print in one colour and still look like itself.

::media[The West Pier lighthouse, which I've drawn more times than anything except maps]{file="whitby-pier-1"}

Cut the block this afternoon, at the kitchen table with the radio on. The gouge slipped twice, once into the sea, which doesn't matter, and once into the lighthouse, which now has a slightly crooked window that I'm calling character.
