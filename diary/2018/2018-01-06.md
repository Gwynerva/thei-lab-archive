Saturday, so tideline. Got the four biggest constituents for Whitby in at last — :hint[M2]{note="the moon's main twice-a-day tide, the biggest of the lot"}, S2, N2 and K1 — and plotted a week of predicted tide against the printed tables Dad keeps by the till. The shape is right. The times are out by up to twenty minutes, which at Whitby harbour mouth is the difference between floating and not.
