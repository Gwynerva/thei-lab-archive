## Hogmanay

A new notebook, the third since October 2016, and a new year to put in it. Hogmanay was quiet: Priya, Callum, a pan of his stovies and the fireworks from the kitchen window, which is the correct distance from fireworks. At midnight we opened the window and listened to the whole city go off at once, and the ships in the docks sounding their horns, and Callum cried a bit, which he says is traditional for chefs.

## Calton Hill

Walked up Calton Hill at eleven this morning with Priya to inspect the damage. Half of Edinburgh had the same idea and none of us looked well.
