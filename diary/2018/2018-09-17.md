Ben Macdui yesterday: number twelve, 1,309 metres, the second highest in Britain, across the plateau in a fog so thick we walked on a compass bearing the whole way, Priya counting paces out loud. There's a legend of a grey giant who follows walkers up there in the mist. We heard footsteps behind us for about twenty minutes. They were a man from Dundee in very squeaky boots.
