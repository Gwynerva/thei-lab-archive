The invitations came back from the print shop: eighty-five of them on heavy cream card, my lettering on the front and the little map of York on the back. Folded them all on the living-room floor with Priya while Callum read out the guest list in silly voices. Clare cried on the phone when I sent her a photo, which she says is "just the shifts".
