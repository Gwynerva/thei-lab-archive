Rang Nana in hospital as I do every evening, and for the first time she sounded frightened. "I'm nearly ninety, pet. Bones don't knit at ninety the way they did." I said they'd knit if she did her exercises, and she said "bossy" in the old voice, which helped. Then I put the phone down and cried over the Munro map, which has nothing to do with anything, except that I was working on it when Mum rang the first time.
