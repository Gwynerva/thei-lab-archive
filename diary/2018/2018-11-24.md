*The Wee Oven* opened this morning in Glasgow and Priya, Marta and I were on the half seven train to be there by nine. A narrow café with eleven chairs, Callum's records on the shelf, a blackboard with his writing on it, and a queue down the street by ten. He'd been up since three baking. He kept coming out from behind the counter to hug people with flour on his arms, and then going back and making the best cinnamon buns any of us had ever eaten, and then coming out to hug people again.

::media[His first rolls of the first morning, eight of them in a cloth, gone by ten]{file="sourdough-3"}

I'll make his cutting board the best of the five.
