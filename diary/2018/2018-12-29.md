Back in the empty flat — Priya at her parents', Marta in Kraków until the fourth — and it's very quiet, and I've been at the Munro map all day with the radio on to have something to do with my hands. I keep thinking about the lane up to Nana's, and the step, and "next time". Whitby feels further away than it did a year ago. Or I'm further away from it. I can't tell which, and I'm not sure it's a difference.
