Took Nana back to the Bay this morning, as she insisted, slowly up the lane with her frame and Dad behind her in case. The cottage was cold and smelled of her, lavender and coal and paper, and she sat down in her own chair by the window and looked at the bare rowan and said, "That's better." Mum's staying with her till New Year.

On the way out I went down to the beach for ten minutes on my own, low tide, the scaurs bare and the sea coming in over them in long quiet lines, and recorded it to take home.
