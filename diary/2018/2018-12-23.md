Down the coast to Darlington, across to Middlesbrough, then the little train up the Esk valley, rain on the windows the whole way and the sky going dark at half three. Dad met me at the station in the shop van, which smells of rope and diesel and always will, and drove me round by the harbour without asking, because he knows. Climbed the 199 steps in the dusk before tea, as always, to say hello to the abbey.
