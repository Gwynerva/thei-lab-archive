Drove up after work, the three of us singing badly, Ines's car complaining on every hill past Crianlarich. Rannoch Moor was gold and dry and humming, and then the road dropped into Glen Coe and the mountains closed in on both sides, huge and dark and serious, the way they always do, even in a heatwave.

Pitched the tent by the river at half nine in full daylight. The midges arrived at ten, exactly as advertised. We ate pasta in our nets, lifting them for every forkful, like brides.
