Saturday: Stob Dubh, the top of Buachaille Etive Beag, number eight. The little herdsman of Etive, which isn't little, which is 958 metres of steep grass and then a ridge with the big Buachaille across the glen looking down on it like an older brother. Priya and I had lunch on the top in shirtsleeves. Two whole hours without a cloud. That never happens in Glen Coe. We were suspicious all the way down.
