## Thursday, red warning

A red warning for the whole of the central belt, which I've never seen before; the Met Office's map was the colour of a bad rash. Forthline closed. So did everything else: the buses stopped at lunchtime, the shops ran out of bread by ten, and Callum's restaurant sent him home before he'd even got his coat off, so he made soup for the whole stair in the biggest pan we own and went door to door with it.

Priya and I walked up into town in the afternoon because it seemed wrong not to. The snow was dry and squeaky and blew along the pavements in snakes. Nobody was in a hurry, because there was nowhere to get to.

## Indoors

Forty-six minutes of thumb position, "fine", with the radiator on full and a hot-water bottle on my feet. The hall echo is better in snow; everything's muffled outside, so the cello is the loudest thing in Leith.

Margins: a map of the walk with every drift marked as a contour.
