## Dettifoss

Dettifoss this morning, which is not a waterfall so much as a river falling off the edge of the world. Grey glacier water, the most powerful fall in Europe, the guidebook says, and you can feel it through your boots before you can see it. The track out to it crosses a desert of grey stones with nothing growing at all, and then the ground simply stops, and there's a canyon full of noise. I filmed it to show Dad, who will say "aye, that's a lot of water".

## Akureyri

Then Akureyri, which is a small, tidy, sensible town on a long fjord with a church at the top of a lot of steps (not 199; I lost count somewhere past ninety, which is a first). We bought groceries and felt like locals for a whole hour, and Priya posted a postcard to her lab with a puffin on it.

## Horses

On the way we stopped for a field of Icelandic horses, who came over to the fence to inspect us, found us wanting and went back to the grass. They're small and shaggy and very sure of themselves, like ponies who have read a lot.
