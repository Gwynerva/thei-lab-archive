A total eclipse of the moon tonight, the longest of the century, rising already red out of the sea. Priya and I took a flask to Portobello to watch it come up over the Forth and saw a long bank of cloud, exactly where the moon should have been, for an hour and a half. Then it was over, and the cloud cleared. That's the east coast.

Clare rang on the way home in a state about seating plans. The place cards are next: ninety-two names. She'll send the list "when it stops changing", which may be never.
