Ben Vane, number thirteen, the smallest of the Arrochar Munros and the steepest, a heap of rock and wet grass straight up from Loch Sloy. The tail end of the storm still around: rain in the morning, then a gap, then the most enormous view east across the hills to the cone of Ben Lomond, where all this started, eighteen months ago.

::media[From the ridge, east to Ben Lomond, number one, small and sharp on the far side of everything]{file="ben-lomond-2"}

Thirteen. That's the last of the year; the days are getting too short and my knees too opinionated. Thirteen is enough. Thirteen is more than I said in January.
