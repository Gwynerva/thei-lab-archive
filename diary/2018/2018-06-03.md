The joints start today. The community workshop in the old railway arch has a bench, a vice and a retired joiner who supervises everybody by sighing, and I paid my five pounds and spent the whole afternoon cutting my first dovetails in an offcut.

::media[My first practice corner, two bits of oak and some dovetails that very nearly meet]{file="dovetail-1"}

The joiner looked at it for a long time and said, "You'll get there." I'm choosing to hear that as a compliment. Eight more corners to cut for real, and each one has to be better than this.
