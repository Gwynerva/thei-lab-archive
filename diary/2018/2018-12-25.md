## Morning

Stockings, because Mum still does stockings for two daughters in their late twenties and a son-in-law, and now one of them has a stethoscope-shaped chocolate in it. Clare and Dev drove over from Leeds after her night shift and arrived at ten, Clare asleep in the passenger seat with a paper crown already on.

## The boards

Everybody opened their cutting board at once, which I hadn't planned, so there was a moment of five people holding up five chequerboards like a game show. Mum ran her hand over hers and didn't say anything for a bit. Dad found the knot in his straight away, as predicted, and spent ten minutes explaining it to Dev. Nana said hers was "a good weight", which from a seamstress is the highest praise there is.

## Dinner

Dad carved. Nana supervised the carving. Clare fell asleep in the armchair during the speech on the telly, and Dev put a cushion under her head. We played cards until midnight and Nana won everything, as always, with a face of perfect innocence.

Weather: grey, mild, a little drizzle.\
Wind: south-west, enough for Dad to go and look at the harbour twice.

Three good things:

1. Nana at the top of the table, with her frame parked behind her chair like a bicycle.
2. Mum and her board.
3. All of us. All of us at once, which happens about once a year now, and which I want to stop being surprised by.
