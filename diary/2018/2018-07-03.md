Lesson with every window in Mrs Ogilvie's flat open, and halfway through the scale of D somebody across the street joined in on a recorder, a third below, entirely in tune. Mrs Ogilvie went to the window and said, "Thank you," quite crisply, to nobody in particular, and the recorder stopped. She says it's been going on for eleven years.
