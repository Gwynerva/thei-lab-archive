## Morning

Started building the Munro map: a public list of all the hills with their heights and positions, cleaned up into a file of 282 points, and a blank map of Scotland to put them on. By lunch there were 282 grey dots on the screen, very neat, like a sky full of small grey stars. I made a coffee and felt clever.

## Afternoon

Mum rang at half three. Nana fell this morning on the step down to the garden, by the rowan, and lay on the path for nearly an hour before the neighbour heard her calling. Her hip is broken. The ambulance took her to Scarborough and they'll operate tomorrow. Mum's voice was very calm and very steady, which is how I knew how frightened she was.

Booked the first train in the morning. Then I sat in front of the laptop with the map open on it, the grey dots, and didn't touch it for the rest of the day.
