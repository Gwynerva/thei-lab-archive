## The Lost Valley

Up through the gorge into Coire Gabhail, the Lost Valley, where the MacDonalds used to hide the cattle they'd borrowed from their neighbours: a flat green floor hidden between walls of rock, which you'd never guess was there from the road. The stream that normally fills it had gone underground in the heat and we could hear it under the stones, running, without seeing it. I recorded it, crouched on a boulder with my phone in the gravel.

## Bidean nam Bian

Then up the headwall at the back of the valley, steep scree and then steeper rock, onto the ridge and along it to the top of Bidean nam Bian: 1,150 metres, the highest point in Argyll, number nine. From the top you can see — I'm told — Ben Nevis, the islands, half of Scotland. We saw all of it. There wasn't a cloud anywhere. Ines lay down on the summit stones and announced that she was never moving again and that we should bring her meals.

## Coming down

My knees on the scree: not good. Water: finished by three. We came off the hill at six and walked straight into the river in our boots. Callum had sent a text at lunchtime asking if we were dead. Replied from the river: "Nearly. Number nine."
