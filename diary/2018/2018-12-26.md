## The walk

Boxing Day walk: me, Clare, Dev and Dad along the West Cliff and down onto the sands and all the way to Sandsend at low tide, and back, with the wind pushing us home. Mum stayed with Nana and the leftovers. Dad told Dev about the erosion of every cliff between here and Saltwick, using my dissertation figures, and got them right, and I was prouder of that than of the dissertation. Dev, who is a physiotherapist and therefore professionally interested in how things wear away, asked good questions the whole way to Sandsend. Clare walked ahead with her hands in her pockets, kicking at the sand, the way she has on this beach since she was six.

## Clare

Clare and Dev went back to Leeds in the evening; she's on shift tomorrow. She hugged me at the car and said, "Your map's on our wall. It's the first thing anyone sees." I've kept that.
