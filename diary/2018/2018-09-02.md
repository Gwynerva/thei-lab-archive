Marta moved in, with a violin, a large plant called Grażyna ("she doesn't trust movers", she explained, and carried her up all four flights herself), a desk with three monitors on it, two boxes of books in Polish and English and a crate of tea, more tea than one person could drink in a year. She sat on the floor of the green room and said, "Oh, it's not a corridor at all." Priya made dinner, which was brave of her without Callum, and it was fine, and we drank to the stair.
