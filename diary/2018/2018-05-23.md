## The east fjords

Yesterday was the long drive up the east coast, the ring road going in and out of fjord after fjord, each one the same shape and a different colour. We stopped at a beach of black sand at the bottom of a scree slope, with nobody on it but us and a raven, and I filmed the waves coming in for no reason except that I wanted to keep them.

## Fog

This morning we drove over the pass to Seyðisfjörður and drove straight into a cloud. Snow at the sides of the road, walls of it taller than the Kettle, and then fog so thick that Priya opened her window and leaned out to read the road markings, and I drove at the speed of a reasonably confident walker. We came down out of it into a town at the end of a long fjord, wooden houses in every colour, a blue church at the end of a street painted in rainbow stripes, and the fog sitting on the water and on the mountains on both sides like a lid.

::media[The blue church at the end of the rainbow street, the cloud halfway down the mountain behind it]{file="seydisfjordur-2"}

It never lifted. We had soup in a café that was also a bookshop and a bicycle repair shop, and I bought a map of the east fjords I don't need, because the shop had it and I was there. The ferry to Denmark comes in here. In the fog you could hear the harbour but not see it.

Weather: fog, then fog. Fine by me.
