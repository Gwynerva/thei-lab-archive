The clocks went back yesterday and now it's dark at five, and today I came home in the dark and the flat was empty — Priya at a conference in Aberdeen, Marta at a gig — and I sat in the kitchen with my coat on for a while without turning the light on. Nothing's wrong. That's the problem with it: nothing's wrong and I'm sad anyway, the way I get every year when the dark comes, and every year I forget that I get like this, and it surprises me.

Made toast. Practised the Elgar for half an hour in the hall. Better.
