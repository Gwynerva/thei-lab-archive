The first rehearsal of the autumn, and now Marta and I walk there together, her violin and my cello, like a very small and slightly mismatched band. Agnes says the December concert will be the Elgar, a Handel overture and carols, and that the cellos will "rise to it". There's a storm coming tomorrow with a name. We walked home in the wind with the cello case like a sail.
