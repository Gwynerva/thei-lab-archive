Last day at work before Christmas. There was an envelope on everybody's desk from Dougie with a small bonus in it and a card he'd written himself in very bad handwriting. Mine said "Thanks for the year. Fife is lucky. D." I sat and looked at the money and did the sums I always do: the train to Whitby, the presents, the January rent, and for once at the end of the sums there was something left. I'm writing that down because I'll want to remember there was a winter when there was something left.
