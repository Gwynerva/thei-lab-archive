[Storm Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Ali) today, the first named storm of the season, and the tenement across the road is covered in scaffolding, which spent all afternoon making noises like a ship in a gale: groaning, clanging, a long metal howl when the gusts came round the corner. Two of the boards came down into the street at about three. The police closed the road. We all watched from the kitchen window with our tea, like it was the telly.

Recorded the wind from the back window, which sounds like the whole sky being emptied out:

::audio[Storm Ali round the back green in the afternoon, the scaffolding shrieking somewhere underneath]{file="sound-wind-1" title="Storm Ali"}

The office let us go early again. The trees in Pilrig Park have lost half their leaves in a day.
