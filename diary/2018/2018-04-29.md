Back to South Stack at seven this morning because Priya wanted the birds before the people. Puffins, three of them, on a ledge a very long way down. She counted razorbills into a notebook and I drew the lighthouse with its path winding over the top of the island like a piece of thread.

::media[South Stack from above, the path to the lighthouse like a thread across the island]{file="south-stack-2"}

A long drive home through rain. Five hours of Priya's playlist, which is mostly sea shanties.
