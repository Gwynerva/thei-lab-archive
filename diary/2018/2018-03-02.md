Day three of the snow, and now a storm from the south has come up to meet it, so the snow has given up falling and is just travelling. The drift at the back door has reached the handle. Still no buses, still no work. Fifty minutes on the orchestra part, "good" — the first time I've marked the Elgar good, and it took a blizzard.
