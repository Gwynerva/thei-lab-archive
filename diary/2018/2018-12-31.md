## The list

The last page of the third notebook, which I've timed perfectly by writing very small all through November. On the first page, a year ago, I wrote down what I wanted from 2018. Here it is again, honestly, as instructed:

1. Play the cello again, properly, with a teacher. *Yes.* Mrs Ogilvie, sixty days of a script, an orchestra, a concert in a cold church. I didn't get lost.
2. tideline 1.0 by Easter. *Yes*, on Easter Monday itself, and a talk, and a man in Nova Scotia and a class in Chile.
3. Ten Munros. *Thirteen.* Callum's sandwiches on number ten.
4. Something for Clare's wedding that isn't a notebook. *Eighty-five invitations, ninety-two place cards and a map on their wall.*
5. A thing out of wood that holds other things. *The bookshelf in the hall, and five boards.*

All five. I'm suspicious.

## What wasn't on it

Iceland, and an iceberg turning over to show its blue. A flood map for a town in Fife that a council thanked me for in four words. Callum leaving and Marta arriving and the flat not being the same and being fine. Nana on the path by the rowan for an hour, and Nana at the top of the stairs on Christmas Eve, saying "There."

It's all in [the diary](/diary/) — or it will be, once I've typed it up, which is a job for a year when I have more time than sense.
