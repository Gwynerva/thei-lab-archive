Week one of the Fife flood map. The job, as I explained it to Callum: you take a model of a town — every kerb and wall and dip in the road — and pour imaginary rain on it, a lot of it, and watch where it goes. Then you draw where it went, carefully, so that a council can decide where to put drains and whom to warn.

Callum said it sounded like his kitchen on a Saturday. He's not wrong.
