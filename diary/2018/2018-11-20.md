Nana went home today. Seventeen days. Mum drove her back to the cottage and has moved in for a fortnight, with a frame and a raised loo seat and a list of exercises stuck to the fridge. Nana rang me herself at six to announce that she was home and had already had a proper cup of tea, "in a cup", and that the rowan was looking bare.

:::private
Mum said, later, on her own, that the doctor had talked to her about "next time", and about whether Nana ought to be living by herself at the top of that lane at all. Nana is not to be told yet. Nobody knows how you'd tell her.
:::

Three good things:

1. Home.
2. A proper cup of tea, in a cup.
3. Her voice.
