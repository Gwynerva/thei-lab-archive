Thaw. Leith Walk is a river of grey slush and every gutter is talking. I'll remember [the snow days](ref:event:beast-from-the-east#:~:text=snow "three days of it") longer than most holidays: the quiet, the soup, the man on skis. Twenty-five minutes of bowing, good.
