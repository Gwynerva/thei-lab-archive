The cello is ready. I went up the close at lunch to hear it: a new bridge, a soundpost standing up, new strings, pegs that stay where they're put. It sounds — I don't want to overdo this — like itself, only awake. I collect it tomorrow, and the rental goes back, with something close to guilt.

Only seventeen minutes tonight, on the rental, the minuet, "tired"; mostly I sat and played open strings and grinned at the wall.
