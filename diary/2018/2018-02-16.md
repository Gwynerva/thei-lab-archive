The cello is home. A new bridge, a soundpost standing up, new strings, pegs that stay where they're put. It sounds — I don't want to overdo this — like itself, only awake. Took the rental back with something close to guilt.

Only seventeen minutes tonight, the minuet, "tired"; mostly I sat and played open strings and grinned at the wall.
