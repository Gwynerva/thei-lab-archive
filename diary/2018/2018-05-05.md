Measured the hall for the bookshelf, three times, with Priya holding the other end of the tape and offering opinions. Ninety-four centimetres at the narrow end, so the shelf can be twenty-eight deep and Callum can still get his bike past without taking the skin off his knuckles. Drew it in the notebook: five shelves, oak, dovetailed at the corners, because if I'm going to do it I'm going to do it the hard way.
