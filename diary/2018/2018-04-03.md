Twenty-two hours after 1.0, the first issue from a stranger: a man in Nova Scotia who wants to predict the tide at his mooring and says the times are good but the heights "run a little proud". Somebody I've never met has used a thing I made, to put a boat in the water. I had to go for a walk.

Thirty-nine minutes of bowing when I came back. Good.
