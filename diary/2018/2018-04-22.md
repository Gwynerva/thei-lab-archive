Ben More yesterday, number seven, and the first of the year. 1,174 metres straight up from the farm at the bottom, no messing about with ridges, just up. Snow in long patches near the top, hard enough to kick steps in; Priya went ahead and kicked them, and I followed in her footprints like a duckling. The cairn was in cloud and then suddenly it wasn't, and there were mountains in every direction all the way to the sea.

Today I can't go downstairs forwards.
