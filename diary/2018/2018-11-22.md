Glue-up day at the arch: maple and walnut strips, glued into long blocks, sliced across into end-grain slices, flipped and glued again into chequerboards. Borrowed six clamps from Fergus, who lent them with a look that said he's counted them. The joiner at the arch watched the whole operation and sighed approvingly, which I didn't know was possible.
