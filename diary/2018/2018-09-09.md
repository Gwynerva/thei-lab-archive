The morning after. Walked the walls with Mum and Dad, all three of us a bit fragile and very happy, Dad pointing out every place the river has flooded since 1947 as though he were personally responsible for the river. Then the train home. I found a place card in my coat pocket — my own, with a little cello drawn in the corner — and I've put it in this notebook.
