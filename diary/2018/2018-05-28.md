Home. The flat smells of oak and Callum's cooking, and the bookshelf wood is in the hall, eleven boards of it, rough and pale and smelling like a forest.

## What it cost, roughly

- Getting there
  - flights, about £180 each, booked in January at midnight
- The Kettle
  - nine days of hire, split two ways
  - petrol, which in Iceland is priced like perfume
- Sleeping
  - guesthouses, mostly shared rooms, mostly lovely
- Eating
  - petrol-station hot dogs, which are the national dish and correct
  - one very good fish soup

About nine hundred pounds each, all in. I'd spend it again tomorrow.

### What I'll remember

The blue of the iceberg that turned over. The noise behind Seljalandsfoss. The fog in Seyðisfjörður, and Priya leaning out of the window reading the road. The orange lighthouse.
