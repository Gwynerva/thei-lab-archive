Dougie went through my maps with a red pen: forty-one small things, mostly labels, and one big thing, a culvert I'd assumed was open when it's been blocked since a road was widened in 1994. He wasn't cross. He said that's what local knowledge is for and rang the council's drainage man, who knew all about it and has been waiting twenty years for somebody to ask.
