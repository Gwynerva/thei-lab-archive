Did nothing today, magnificently. Lay on the back green on a towel and read a whole novel. Callum made ice cream with the old machine he found in a charity shop, rum and raisin, and we ate it straight from the bowl, the three of us, on the roof, watching the light go purple over Fife. Dry day ten, by my count.
