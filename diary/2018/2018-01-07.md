Opened the cello case for the first time since I lugged it back on the train after Christmas. It smells of the music room at school: rosin and radiator. The scratch shaped like Norway is still there on the lower bout, the A string has gone, and the bridge leans like it's had a long night. I played an open D for about a minute and then had to sit down.
