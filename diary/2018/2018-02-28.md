Woke up to the window gone white and a drift halfway up the back door of the stair. Forthline told everyone to stay at home, which I'd already decided. Walked into town anyway because I wanted to see it: Leith Walk with no buses, a man skiing — actually skiing — down the middle of it, a woman pulling her shopping on a sledge, children everywhere and not one of them at school.

Weather: yes.

No practice. The cello stayed in its case by the radiator, like a sensible animal.
