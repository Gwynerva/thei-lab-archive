Last Spoke Room before Christmas. I took the spanner and hung it on the white outline where the ghost one is painted, and nobody noticed for an hour, and then everyone noticed at once and there was a cheer. The woman who runs it said it was the best present the place has ever had. Then she gave me a mince pie from the honesty tin and didn't make me pay.
