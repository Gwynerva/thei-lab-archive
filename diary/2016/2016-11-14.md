Tonight was [the supermoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermoon "the closest full moon since 1948"), the moon nearer to us than at any time since 1948. Edinburgh responded with cloud, obviously. I stood on the landing with the window open for forty minutes and got four minutes of it through a gap, very big and very white, before it went again.

Dad rang to say the tides at home will be enormous all week. That's the most excited I've heard him about anything since the new van.
