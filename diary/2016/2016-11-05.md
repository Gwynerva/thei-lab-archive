Bonfire Night from the roof. We are not meant to go on the roof. Callum knows the way: the hatch at the top of the stair, a ladder, and then you're out on the leads between the chimney pots with all of Edinburgh round you.

We took the duvet off my bed and a flask of hot chocolate with something in it, and sat with our backs against a chimney stack. Fireworks going off everywhere at once: Calton Hill, Leith Links, gardens we'll never see, a big golden one over Fife. Priya counted the colours. I tried to work out where each one was from the direction and the delay of the bang, which is a very nerdy way of watching fireworks but it does work.

Very cold. Didn't care. Climbing back in through the hatch in the dark, Callum stood on my hand.
