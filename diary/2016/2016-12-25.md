Christmas Day!

## The morning

Stockings, which Mum still does for all of us, even Dev, who looked quite moved. Dad went over to the Bay to fetch Nana in the van and she arrived in her good coat with a tin of shortbread and a jar of rowan jelly for everybody.

## Presents

- a head torch from Dad (practical)
- a quilted notebook cover from Nana, with my initials on it in tiny stitches
- wool socks from Mum, three pairs, "for your cliffs"
- a mug from Clare and Dev that says I LIKE BIG MAPS

## The rest

Dinner at two. Dev carved, Dad supervised him, Nana told the story of the Christmas pudding in 1962 that set the curtains on fire. A walk up to the abbey before the light went, all six of us strung out along the cliff path, Nana on Dad's arm.

::media[The abbey under a big grey sky, Christmas afternoon]{file="whitby-abbey-2"}

:::private
Clare got me alone in the kitchen with the washing up and showed me a ring on a chain under her jumper. Dev asked her last week. They're telling Mum and Dad at New Year, on purpose, so they don't "make Christmas about it". I'm not allowed to say a word. So: not a word. I'm very, very happy.
:::

Fell asleep on the sofa at nine with a paper hat on. A good day.
