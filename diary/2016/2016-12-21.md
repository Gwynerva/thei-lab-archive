The shortest day! Up Calton Hill in the dark to watch the sun come up at twenty to nine, with a flask and Callum, who was still up from his shift and called it "a late night, not an early morning". A handful of people, a dog in a coat, all of us facing the same way.

It came up over the sea, low and orange, and the light went straight along Princes Street. Just under seven hours of daylight today. From now on every day is longer.
