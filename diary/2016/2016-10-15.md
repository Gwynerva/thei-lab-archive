Glenmore! Minibus at seven from the George Square end, nineteen of us and the lecturer, who drove the A9 like it owed him money. Snow on the Cairngorm tops already. October!

## The job

We were surveying control points in Glenmore Forest and round Loch Morlich with :hint[GNSS]{note="satellite positioning: GPS and its cousins, the Russian, European and Chinese systems, all used together"} kit: one base station on a tripod in a car park, very still, and the rest of us out with rovers, which are receivers on yellow poles with a little screen. You stand on the point, keep the pole level with the bubble, and wait for it to fix. Then you wait some more. Then it hail-stones on you while you wait.

My group did eleven points. One of them was on a tree root and I had to stand on one leg for four minutes. Another one was in a bog. I now know exactly how deep that bog is.

## The evening

Hostel, bunk beds, a huge pot of chilli, everyone's socks on the radiators. Someone had a guitar. I was asleep by ten with my boots still drying on the window sill. Drew Loch Morlich in the margin with all eleven points on it, which is the most "me" thing I have ever done.
