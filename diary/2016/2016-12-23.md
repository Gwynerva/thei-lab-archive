The trains ran. Storm Barbara made the one out of Edinburgh rock like a boat past Dunbar, and at Berwick the sea was coming over the rocks in white sheets. Everybody in the carriage went quiet and watched. Changed at York, changed at Middlesbrough, and then the Esk Valley line in the dark, the wind pushing at the windows.

Up the 199 steps when I got in, because I always do on the first night home.

::media[The top of the 199 steps, the lamp on, the harbour lights below]{file="whitby-steps-1"}

Mum had the fire lit. Dad said "you made it", like I'd crossed the Atlantic.
