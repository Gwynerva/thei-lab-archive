Home! Edinburgh to York with a coffee, York to Middlesbrough with a sandwich, then the little train down the Esk Valley in the dark, stopping at every halt with one light and nobody on it. Dad was at the station with the van, smelling of rope and tar like always.

::media[The swing bridge from the station end, grey and red]{file="whitby-bridge-2"}

Mum made stew. My old room has become a room for Mum's seed trays. I'm sleeping next to forty pots of something that will be sweet peas.
