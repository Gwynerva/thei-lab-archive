[GIS Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIS_Day "the third Wednesday of November, every year") at the university! Maps up on every wall, a talk in the morning about mapping after earthquakes that made the whole room go quiet, and a cake in the shape of a globe that was mostly ocean (accurate). Some of us ran a table for a school group in the afternoon: draw a map of your way to school. One boy drew his entirely from the point of view of his dog.

The library brought out some of its old maps for the day, and I stood in front of this one for so long that a man asked me if I was all right.

::media[Edinburgh and Leith in 1935, every important building drawn standing up]{file="old-map-scotland-1" layout="natural"}

You can find our street on it. There's a dairy where the chippy is.
