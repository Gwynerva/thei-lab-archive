Mum told me something on the phone tonight that she didn't want to say at Christmas: the shop has had its worst year since Dad took it over. Fewer boats, a big chandlery opening in the marina, a roof repair. They're "absolutely fine" and "just being careful". Dad doesn't know she's told me.

I sat on the landing on the sofa for a long time after. I can't do anything about it, except not ask them for anything. So I won't.
