Up at half six to climb Arthur's Seat before the Spoke Room, because the forecast said frost and I wanted to see it. Every blade of grass on the way up was white and crunched. At the top, the sun came up out of the sea beyond the Bass Rock and the whole Forth went pink, then gold.

::media[North-east from the top: the frosty ridge, the edge of the city and the Forth]{file="arthurs-seat-2"}

Then down the hill and straight into a cold basement to fix brakes with fingers I couldn't feel. Worth it.
