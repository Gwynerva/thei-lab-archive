Day two. A free morning, so four of us went up Meall a' Bhuachaille before breakfast, and from the top the whole forest and the loch were laid out like the map we'd been making. Then back down to process the points in the hostel common room. Mine closed to three centimetres, which the lecturer said was "respectable", and I'm having that put on my gravestone.
