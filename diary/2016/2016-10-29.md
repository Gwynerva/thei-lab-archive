Halloween party at ours! Straight from the Spoke Room with oil still under my nails, then three hours of carving pumpkins and hoovering.

## The food

Callum made soup *inside* a pumpkin and served it out of the pumpkin with a ladle and a straight face. Then sausage rolls shaped like fingers, with an almond for each nail, which were horrible to look at and gone in ten minutes. Priya made a jelly with a plastic spider set in it. Nobody ate the jelly. The spider is still in the fridge, looking at us.

## The costumes

Priya came as a jellyfish: the clear umbrella, strips of ribbon and fairy lights hanging all round it, which is what happens when you share a flat with a marine biologist. People from her lab came as plankton. I didn't know you could come as plankton. Callum's "chef, but dead" was his whites, a lot of flour on his face and a meat cleaver made of cardboard. He was in character all night and kept telling people the special was off.

My costume took a lot of explaining.

:::spoiler
I went as a contour map: my brown jumper with wiggly white lines drawn all over it in chalk pen, getting closer together towards my shoulders, and a little spot height sticker on the end of my nose. Nobody got it until I explained it. After I explained it, they still didn't get it.
:::

Twenty-two people in a flat built for three. The neighbours came up to complain and stayed till two.
