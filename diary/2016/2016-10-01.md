Moved in today! Top floor, off Leith Walk, seventy-two steps up. I counted, obviously. Not quite Whitby's 199, but my legs don't know that. Priya has the big room because she found the flat, Callum has the one next to the kitchen because he's a chef and "needs to be near the hob", and I have the little one with the skylight, which is the one I wanted anyway. If you lean out of the kitchen window and squint, there is a strip of the Forth.

## Why a diary

I have started a diary about nine times. Bits of my life are in the margins of lecture notes, on the backs of envelopes, in three different apps and in a Year 10 notebook with a horse on it. So: new city, new course, new flat, and this time I'm going to do it **properly, in one place, for once**. One notebook. Every day I can manage. Nana says a stitch in time is still a stitch, and I've decided that counts here too: one line is still a line.

## The rules

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. Short is fine. One line is fine.
2. No tearing out pages, not even the embarrassing ones.
3. A little map in the margin when something happens somewhere.
4. Weather counts as news.
:::

Callum made pasta at eleven at night out of whatever was in the three bags of shopping we'd carried up. Priya and I ate it sitting on the floor, because the sofa is still in Morningside on a van that is "coming first thing tomorrow, honestly".
