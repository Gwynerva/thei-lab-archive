First Saturday at the Spoke Room!

## The place

It's a basement under a launderette, so it smells of wet concrete and fabric softener, and there's a paraffin heater in the corner that everyone stands near. Ten till four. Six workstands, a kettle that never cools down, a biscuit tin with a sign on it that says HONESTY. The woman who runs it showed me round in about ninety seconds and then handed me a child's bike with a flat tyre and said "go on then".

## The tool wall

The best thing in there is the tool wall: a big board with the outline of every tool painted on in white, so you can see at once what's missing. Someone has painted a little ghost spanner in the gap where the 15 mm used to be. Nobody knows where the real one went. It has become a legend.

## What I did

- one puncture (the child's bike, a pink one with streamers)
- one brake cable on a very old Raleigh
- watched someone take out a :hint[bottom bracket]{note="the bearing inside the frame that the pedal cranks turn on"} and understood about half of it
- drank five cups of tea

They give you a bit of money out of the jar at the end, and the tea is free, and a man with a Dutch bike shook my hand for the brakes. My hands are black, my jeans are black, I'm very happy.
