Spent the whole evening looking at old maps of the coast online, which I'm calling research. The first six-inch Ordnance Survey of the coast round Whitby was surveyed in the 1850s, and you can see every field, every footpath, every boundary stone, and where the cliff edge was. If I can line those up with now, I've got 160 years of the cliff in one picture.
