Nana's advent call. She rings on the first of December every year, to check I've opened the first window of the calendar she posts me. (I had. A robin.)

She talked for a long time about Grandad, about the winter of 1953 when his ship was late and she walked down to the harbour every day for a week. I'd never heard that before.

Then, right at the end, she said she's been "a bit wobbly on the stairs" and that she didn't want me to tell Mum, "because your mother fusses". I promised. I'm writing it here so I've told someone. I don't like it.
