Hogmanay on Princes Street!

## The street

Priya came back up this afternoon specially, and Callum finished at ten, still in his whites under his coat. We met him at the corner of the Mound with a bag of chips each. The street was full from end to end, tens of thousands of people, bands on stages, a man in a kilt and a Santa hat dancing on his own by the Scott Monument, a hen party in matching tiaras, a lot of very cold knees.

## Midnight

The countdown went along the street like a wave, a second behind the big screen. At midnight the fireworks went off from the castle, gold and white and a red that lit the whole rock from below, and the whole crowd went up on its toes. Then everyone sang Auld Lang Syne with strangers, arms crossed, holding hands, and the stranger on my right was an old man from Perth who knew every verse and made sure we all knew he knew.
