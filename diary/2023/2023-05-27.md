The sign came: a long flat parcel from Whitby, Mum's handwriting all over it, FRAGILE three times and once in Norwegian off a translation site. TESTWELL'S CHANDLERY, black on orange, half the letters gone back to grey wood. It smells of home: tar and salt and the back of Dad's shop. Taped to it, a note: *He thinks it's in Scarborough. Love, Mum.*
