Mum retired at quarter past three. The whole school in the hall, the head saying things about thirty-four years that made Mum go pink, the children singing something with actions. A rose bush, a voucher, a card the size of a door. In the car park she cried, and Dad held the umbrella over her though it wasn't raining, and I sat in the back seat very proud and very tired.
