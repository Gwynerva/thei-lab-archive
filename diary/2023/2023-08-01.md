Jonas moved in. We both did, really: into a white house with a blue door three streets up the hill, two rooms bigger than mine, a window on the fjord and a corner for a workbench. Seven boxes of his clothes and books and forty of tools. Biscuit inspected every one and slept in the last.

I keep walking into rooms and finding him there, and every time it's a small surprise, the good kind, like finding money in a coat.
