Christmas Eve. Low tide in the morning, so we walked the beach to Sandsend and back, Jonas and Dad and me, the sea pulled right out and the old groyne posts standing in the wet sand like a queue. Drew the walk in the margin of the tide table afterwards: three sets of footprints, one of them Dad's, very straight.
