The seventh coat. The last one.

The varnish, as a list, because it's that sort of day:

5. Easter Monday, in the yard — a fly
6. last Saturday — a run along the sheer line on the port side, sanded out
7. today — nothing. Not a fly, not a hair, not a run.

Jonas looked along her from the stern with one eye shut for a long time and then just nodded. I'll take a nod.
