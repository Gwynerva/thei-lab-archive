New year. Fireworks over Vågen from the steps by Sverre's door; Biscuit under the bed, as every year.\
Rain: day 1.

At noon I stood under Johanneskirken with the recorder in a freezer bag and got the bells, all twelve minutes of them. The plan for the winter: every church bell in town before the spring.
