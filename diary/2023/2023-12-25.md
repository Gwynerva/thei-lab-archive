Christmas Day, the quiet one, by agreement. Small presents: Mum knitted Jonas a hat in Whitby blue; Dad gave me the tide table for 2024, the little booklet from the harbour office, as he has every year since I was nine. Lunch at two. After it, all four of us walked to the end of the West Pier and watched the lights come on at four. Board games until Dad fell asleep in his chair with the dice in his hand.

::media[The East Pier light at four, the abbey dark on the cliff behind]{file="whitby-pier-3"}

Nothing happened. I think that was the present.
