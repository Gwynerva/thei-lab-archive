To Whitby with a 1.6-metre sign in a ski bag. The man at check-in in Bergen asked if I was going skiing in July and I said yes. Amsterdam, Newcastle, the bus, and Mum at the stop with the car, laughing before I'd got off. The sign's under my old bed. Dad thinks I'm on tomorrow's late train.
