Nordnes sea bath, two degrees in the air and six in the water. Jonas said he'd come and watch and then got in too. Down the steps, in to the shoulders, a noise I didn't know I could make, ninety seconds, out. My skin went bright red and for the first time since September I laughed out loud at nothing.

No Christmas cards this year. The lino I bought in October is still in its plastic. That's all right.
