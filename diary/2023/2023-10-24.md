First session with Tove. A small room above a shop on Strandgaten, two chairs and a box of tissues placed exactly between them. She asked what I do for fun. I said the kayak, and the cello, and pottery once, in Leith, and then realised I haven't done any of them since August. She didn't write anything down. She said, *we'll start there*.

Walked home the long way. It rained. I didn't mind.
