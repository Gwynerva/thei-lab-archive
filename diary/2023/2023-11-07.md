Back to clay. An evening class in town on Tuesdays, eight of us and a teacher with grey hands. My idea, more or less.

The wheel took me straight back to Leith and [the lopsided mug](ref:section:pottery/the-mug "Leith, 2019; it still holds my pens"). Centring is the whole thing: you can't make anything until the clay stops wobbling, and you can't make it stop by force. Made a bowl, lopsided. Kept it.
