the doctor wrote four weeks. :hint[*sykmeldt*]{note="signed off sick; I practised saying it in the bathroom mirror and still couldn't"}.

I didn't plan to go. I went in for something for the headaches, and she asked me to describe a normal day, and I started with the alarm at five and couldn't get past nine o'clock without stopping. She waited. Then she said, quite gently, *this isn't headaches*.

I've been on the sofa since one. Biscuit on my legs. Jonas home early, not saying anything, making tea that I'm letting go cold.

It isn't sadness. I keep wanting it to be sadness, because sadness has a shape. It's listlessness: a flat sea all the way out, nothing moving, the kind Nana said never to trust.

Rang Anna. She said, *Take as long as it takes. We'll manage. Don't you dare open your email.* I said all right. I deleted the app from my phone while she was still talking, so I'd have done something.

four weeks.
