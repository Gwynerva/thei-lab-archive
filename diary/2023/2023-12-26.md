Boxing Day. Told Mum and Dad properly, at the kitchen table, about the autumn: the doctor, the weeks off, the talking. Mum held my hand and didn't say anything. Dad said "Right," and went to put the kettle on, and then came back before it had boiled and put his hand on my head as if I were nine.
