Dougie has put my name down to give the talk in Amsterdam in a fortnight: twenty minutes at a conference on flood risk, about how we map surface-water flooding in small Scottish towns. He was going to give it himself, then remembered he doesn't like flying, or talking, or the Dutch, in that order. I've given exactly [one talk](ref:event:first-talk) in my life, about tides, to forty people in a room above a pub. I said yes before I'd thought about it, which is how I say yes to everything.
