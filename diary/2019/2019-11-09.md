Rang Nana for the tree, with a list of questions. She answered none of them and told me instead about the day in 1952 when Arthur came home from eight months at sea with a parrot in a cage that swore in Portuguese, and how her mother made him take it back to the ship. I wrote it all down. It's not on any census. It's going on the tree anyway.
