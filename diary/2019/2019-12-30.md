Back in Leith since Friday night. Biscuit met me at the door and then sulked under the bed for an hour, which is fair. Marta has made the flat smell of cinnamon and invited eleven people for Hogmanay. Priya's train gets in tomorrow at three. Played the prelude through, bars one to twenty-two, the whole first stretch of it, slowly, and it held together, just, like a lopsided mug.
