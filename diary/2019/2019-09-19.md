Worked out the shape of the talk on the bus, on the back of a rehearsal schedule:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. What surface-water flooding is: rain with nowhere to go, not a river coming over its banks.
2. Why small towns are the hard case: no gauges, no records, just people's photos of their streets.
3. How we model it: a terrain model, a rainfall table, the drains guessed at, a great deal of computer.
4. What goes wrong: the drains we guessed wrong, the walls we didn't know were there.
5. What a map is for: not to frighten anyone, but so somebody puts the sandbags at the right door.
:::

Twenty minutes. Fifteen slides. One joke, maybe, if it comes.
