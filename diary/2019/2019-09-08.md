A bad Sunday. Marta away for the weekend, and I didn't speak to anyone all day except the man in the shop and the cat. Didn't run, didn't practise; lay on the sofa watching rain on the skylight and felt sorry for myself in a dull, flat way, like a puddle. Rang Mum at six and pretended I'd had a lovely day. She knew. She talked about her tomatoes for twenty minutes, which helped more than anything.
