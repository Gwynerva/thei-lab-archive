Last hill of the season, the day after Amsterdam, which was a mistake I'd make again: Mount Keen, the most easterly Munro of all, with Ines, from the top of Glen Esk. A long walk in by the river, past a stone well with a crown on it, then up the old road over the hills that drovers used, to a big round dome with a pile of stones on top and the whole of the north-east spread out below, all heather and haze. The season ends with sore legs and a very long drive home.

This year's list, carried on from the margin of the last page:

21. Lochnagar, in September sun, with a ptarmigan.
22. Mount Keen, in September wind, with Ines and a great deal of heather.

Nine in a year. Not bad. Not Ben Nevis.
