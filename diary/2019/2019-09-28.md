Last hill of the season, the day after Amsterdam, which was a mistake I'd make again: Ben Challum from Glen Dochart, with Ines, in wind and wet, up a long boggy ridge with false tops that kept lying to us. The season ends with mud to the knee and one beautiful minute when the cloud lifted off Ben More across the glen.

This year's list, carried on from the margin of the last page:

21. Meall nan Tarmachan, in September sun, with a ptarmigan.
22. Ben Challum, in September rain, with Ines and a great deal of bog.

Ten in a year. Not bad. Not Ben Nevis.
