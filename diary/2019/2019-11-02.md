Started something with Mum on the phone tonight: the family tree, Nana's side and Grandad's. Mum has a shoebox of photographs with names pencilled on the back, half of them wrong, and Nana has the rest in her head, which won't always be there, though neither of us said so. I'll do the records: births, marriages, censuses, the parish books of Fylingdales. Mum will do the asking. We began with Nana's grandparents and got stuck at once on a great-great-aunt called either Hannah or Ann, depending on who's spelling.
