Ben Chonzie, alone, from Glen Turret: the one the books call the dullest Munro in Scotland. It's a long, round, heathery lump with a fence going up it. It is also, today, full of mountain hares still half in their white winter coats, and golden plovers piping from every tussock, and from the top the whole of Perthshire laid out like a quilt on a bed. Dull is a matter of who's walking. One more red dot.
