The Pentlands, alone, the whole ridge from Flotterstone over Turnhouse and Carnethy to Scald Law and back by the reservoir: not a Munro among them, which is restful. Skylarks going up off every tussock, sheep with lambs lying in the lee of the cairns, and from the top of Carnethy the whole of the Lothians laid out like a quilt on a bed, Arthur's Seat a small brown lump in it. Next Saturday, if the weather holds, something higher.
