Beinn Dorain on Saturday, the great cone you see from the train above Bridge of Orchy, which looks unclimbable from the platform and turns out to be a long slog up a corrie to a col and then south along a broad stony back. Seventeen. From the col the next hill north, Beinn an Dothaidh, sat looking at us, and Ines said "next week", and meant it.

Week seven of the training, too. Sunday's long run was fifteen kilometres and I finished it, which three months ago I'd have called impossible and now call merely unpleasant. Toenail situation: developing.
