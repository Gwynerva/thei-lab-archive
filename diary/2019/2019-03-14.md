Twenty-seven today, on Pi Day, as I tell everyone whether or not they ask. Twenty-seven is also three cubed, which I told everyone as well. Nobody asked about that either.

Marta made a pie, apple, with π cut into the lid, and carried it in with candles while Priya sang and Biscuit fled. Mum and Dad rang at breakfast, both on the phone at once, which means nobody hears anything; Dad said "twenty-seven, eh," and that was his speech. Nana's card came with a pound coin taped inside, as there has been in every card since I was five. Clare sent a book about cats, *for research*.

::media[Biscuit's present to me: not knocking anything off the shelf until ten]{file="ginger-cat-6"}

Three good things:

1. The pie, which had a soggy bottom and was perfect.
2. Nana's handwriting on the envelope, still the same.
3. Twenty-seven and a cat. That was the plan, more or less.
