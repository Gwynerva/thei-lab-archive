Went over to the Bay on my own this morning to say goodbye to Nana before the train. She was in her chair with the radio on, and she was pleased to see me, and she called me Clare twice. The second time she heard it and laughed and said, "Oh, I know who you are, pet, my tongue's older than my head." We had tea. She told me to mind the cat and mind the hills and come back at Easter. When I hugged her at the door she held on a long time, longer than usual, and then patted my back twice, the way she does: *that's enough now*.

Train at four, back to Leith. I cried most of the way to Middlesbrough and didn't know exactly why.
