She passed. *Minor corrections*, which she says is the best thing two people with clipboards can say to you. She came out of the building at a quarter past one, saw Marta and me on the steps, burst into tears, then laughed, then had to sit down on the steps with us.

Dr Priya Nair. Dr Nair. We've called her nothing else all evening. Champagne at the flat out of mugs, because we own exactly two glasses. Callum rang from Glasgow and shouted down the phone. Biscuit was given a small piece of smoked salmon, in honour of the sea.

:::private
She's applying for a postdoc in Plymouth, and she'll get it, because she gets things. And I'll be glad, I will. But tonight, while everyone was singing, I sat on the arm of the sofa and thought: everyone is going somewhere. I make maps of floods for councils and climb the same hills. That's an ugly thought to have on her day, and I'm putting it here so it doesn't go anywhere else.
:::

Three good things: Dr Nair; the face she made on the steps; the champagne, even out of mugs.
