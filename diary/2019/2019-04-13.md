Bus out of the mountains, a train along the coast, and then at Shin-Osaka the bullet train, which slid into the platform exactly when it said and left exactly when it said, to the second; I checked. Past Shizuoka the man across the aisle tapped my arm and pointed, and there was Fuji, white-topped, enormous and alone, looking exactly like the pictures of itself, which seemed almost rude.

::media[Fuji over the line, white-topped and alone]{file="shinkansen-1" layout="stretch"}

Tokyo after the Kumano Kodo is like turning the volume all the way up in one go. I found the hotel by following the blue dot on my phone, since my sense of direction, which is excellent in Edinburgh, gave up at the station. Bed by nine.
