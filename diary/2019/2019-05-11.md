First hills of the year. Ben Vorlich from Loch Earn, with Ines, who runs up things for fun and was very patient about the fact that I don't. Up the long ridge from the farm at Ardvorlich in sun, snow still lying in patches near the top like dropped washing. Then along and over to Stuc a' Chroin, which has a rocky step that looked impossible from below and turned out to be a scramble with very good handholds and only one moment when I had to have a word with myself.

::media[The top, and nothing but hills in every direction]{file="munro-cairn-2"}

Two in a day: two more red dots. On the summit Ines ate an entire bag of grapes and said, "The thing about hills is you can see where you've been," which I'm stealing for everything.

Legs: twelve out of ten.
