First hill of the year. Beinn Ghlas, from the old car park above Loch Tay, with Ines, who runs up things for fun and was very patient about the fact that I don't. Up the long shoulder in sun, snow still lying in patches near the top like dropped washing. The plan was to go on along the ridge to Ben Lawers, but on the col the wind got up so hard we were walking at an angle, like people in a cartoon, and we turned round. Ben Lawers will keep.

::media[The top of Beinn Ghlas, and nothing but hills in every direction]{file="munro-cairn-2"}

Fourteen: one more red dot, and one I'll come back for. On the summit Ines ate an entire bag of grapes and said, "The thing about hills is you can see where you've been," which I'm stealing for everything.

Legs: twelve out of ten.
