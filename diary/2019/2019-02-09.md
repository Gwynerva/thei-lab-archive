## The carrier

He came home in a cardboard carrier with air holes, on my knee in a taxi, saying one long low word all the way down Leith Walk. The driver said his own cat says the same word. It was a short ride and a very long one.

## The bed

Opened the carrier in my room, as the book says: one room first, door shut. He came out like poured syrup, low to the floor, and went straight under the bed. Stayed there two hours. I sat on the floor and read and pretended I had no interest in cats whatsoever. Twice I heard him wash.

## The map

At about eleven he came out, walked once round the room touching everything with the side of his face, climbed onto the table where I'd left the Pentlands map open, and lay down on it. Right on Allermuir. Turned round twice and went to sleep on the contours.

So that's who he is. He's ginger the way marmalade is ginger, the dark bits and the light bits, with a pale bib. He's heavy, five kilos of him. When he purrs it sounds like someone very small mowing a lawn in the next room.

Marta came in from her concert at midnight and lay on the floor, and he let her touch one ear. Priya says he looked at her like a tax inspector. Everyone in the flat is being ridiculous. I include myself.

Three good things:

1. He ate.
2. He purred.
3. He chose the map.
