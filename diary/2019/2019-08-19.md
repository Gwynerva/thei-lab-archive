Ben Challum on Saturday, from Glen Dochart, with Ines, in wind and wet, up a long boggy ridge with false tops that kept lying to us, and one beautiful minute when the cloud lifted off Ben More across the glen. Nineteen. Mud to the knee.

Booked tomorrow off for the county record office in Northallerton, which opens at nine, takes pencils only, and wants your documents ordered a week ahead, which I did. My list, in order of excitement:

1. A tithe map of the township that takes in Saltwick, 1840s.
2. An estate plan of the lands round the abbey, eighteenth century, *condition fragile*.
3. A bundle of surveyor's notebooks, *uncatalogued*, which is the most exciting word in the language.
