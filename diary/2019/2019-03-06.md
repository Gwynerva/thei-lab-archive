Booked it. Japan, two weeks in April, on my own. Kyoto for the cherry blossom, if it's on time; four days walking an old pilgrim road in the mountains; Tokyo at the end. I sat looking at the confirmation for a long time with my heart going, as if I'd done something illegal. Then I ticked the third box on the New Year list, in pencil, lightly, in case.
