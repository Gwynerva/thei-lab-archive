He forgave me by Wednesday. At six this morning he walked up the duvet, sat on my chest and pressed his forehead to my chin for a long time, purring like a building site. Jet lag is being treated with tea.

Unpacked: the crow for Nana, the chopsticks, the stamp book, a bag of green tea that makes the whole wardrobe smell of a garden. Then I got out this notebook, went back to [the list I made at New Year](ref:diary:2019-01-01), and inked over the pencil tick: went somewhere far away, on my own. Every box ticked, and the year isn't four months old. I'll have to make a new list.
