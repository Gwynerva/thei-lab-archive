Found the pilgrim road on the map tonight. The Kumano Kodo runs through the south of the Kii peninsula, from the coast at Tanabe inland over the mountains to the great shrine at Hongū: four days if you walk it as I mean to, with a bed every night in a different village and the bag sent ahead. I've booked the inns through a small office in Tanabe that writes back in perfect, kind English.

::media[Wakayama: Tanabe on the left, Shingū on the right, the walk somewhere in between]{file="map-kumano" layout="natural"}

Drew the route on the back of an envelope in the meantime: a wiggle from the sea to a dot.
