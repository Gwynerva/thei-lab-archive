Can't sleep, so: a confession for nobody. Some nights I look at job adverts abroad. The Netherlands, since Amsterdam; New Zealand, for no reason; a firm in Norway that makes sea charts of small harbours, whose advert I read twice. Not to apply. Just to see what the world wants. Then I close the laptop and Biscuit puts his chin on my hand, and I think: don't be daft, you've a cat and a cello and a flat with a skylight. Two in the morning. Going to sleep now.
