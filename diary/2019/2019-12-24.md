Christmas Eve in the shop with Dad, who opens until two because "somebody always needs a shackle on Christmas Eve". Somebody did: a man off a fishing boat who needed a shackle and, it turned out, someone to talk to, so Dad made him a tea and they talked about engines for an hour while I sold a tide clock and two lanterns. Closed at two. Fish and chips on the quay, in the cold, with the gulls. Then Clare and Dev arrived from Leeds with a car full of presents and a ham, and the house was full.
