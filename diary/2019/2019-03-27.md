Wrote out the Biscuit instructions for Marta, who'll be feeding him while I'm away:

1. Breakfast at seven, or he'll sit on your face at six.
2. The water bowl lives by the window, away from the food.
3. He's allowed on the table. He's not allowed on the cooker.
4. If he goes up on the wardrobe, he's fine. If he goes up on the skylight sill, check it's shut.
5. He likes his ears done, not his tummy. The tummy is a trap.

She read it and said it's the longest set of instructions she's ever been given for anything, including a violin. One day I'll type it up properly; [the cat-sitter's page](ref:page:cat-care "for whoever feeds him") should have all of it and more.
