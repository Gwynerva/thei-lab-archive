Pottery: trimming. You leave the pots to dry until they're :hint[leather-hard]{note="dry enough to hold their shape and be carved, still damp enough not to crack"}, then turn them upside down on the wheel and shave the bottoms with a little looped tool until a foot ring appears, like peeling an apple. The leaning mug from Sunday is leather-hard and still leaning. The teacher offered to show me how to straighten it. I said no thank you, it's meant to be like that. She gave me a look that said she's heard that before.
