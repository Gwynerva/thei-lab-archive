Priya left this morning. We carried her boxes down four flights to a hired van at eight, Marta and I and a man from the van firm who whistled the whole time. Forty boxes, most of them papers about the sea. Biscuit sat in her room and watched it empty, then lay down in the patch of sun where her bed had been.

At the van she hugged us both for a long time and said she'd be back for Hogmanay, and we said of course, and everyone's eyes did the thing. Then she drove off down Leith Walk, very carefully, indicating far too early at the lights, and was gone.

Three years of her in this flat. [The day Callum left](ref:event:callum-leaves#:~:text=Glasgow) I thought that was the end of something. This feels more like the end of something. Marta made pancakes for lunch, for no reason, and we ate them without talking, and then she said, "Plymouth's not so far," and it is, it's the other end of the island. We'll go in the spring.
