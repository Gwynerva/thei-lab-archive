Pottery, week three. I can centre now, about one time in three, and I made my first object that could be called a bowl if you were being kind: small, thick, slightly oval, like a porridge bowl for a mouse. The teacher picked it up, weighed it in her hand and said, "There's a lot of clay in this." Which is true. I will love it forever.
