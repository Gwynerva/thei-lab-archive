## Promoted, apparently

Dougie stopped me at the kettle this morning while I was waiting for it to boil and said, "By the way, you're a developer now. It's in the email. Congratulations." Then he took his tea back to his desk. I stood there with the kettle clicking off.

It was in the email. **GIS Developer**, from today. It means I'll build the tools the flood maps are made with rather than making the maps one at a time: the scripts, the model runs, the checks, the little programs that turn a rainfall table and a terrain model into a blue stain on a street. It's what I've been doing on the side for a year anyway, in the evenings, without being asked. Now I'm asked.

Ines bought a cake from the bakery, a whole one this time. Dougie came over at five and shook my hand, which he has never done, and said "Well," and went home. Dad, when I rang him, said "Good. About time," and then told me about a man who'd brought a rope into the shop so rotten it came apart in his hands. Mum cried a little, as she does.
