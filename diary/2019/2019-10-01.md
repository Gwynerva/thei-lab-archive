Started the prelude: the first suite, the one everyone knows from adverts for cars and banks, two pages of semiquavers that sound like water and are, Mrs Ogilvie says, the hardest easy thing ever written. She gave it to me at Saturday's lesson with the air of someone handing over a family heirloom and a box of matches at the same time:

> Slowly. Two bars a week. If you can't play it slowly you can't play it.

So: two bars a week. Bars one and two tonight, for forty minutes, until I could play them in my sleep, and possibly did.
