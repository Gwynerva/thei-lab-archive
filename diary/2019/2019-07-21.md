Back in Leith since Thursday, and my legs have forgotten how to do anything but walk. Long run today, twelve kilometres to Cramond and back along the shore, and it felt like running in borrowed legs. Afterwards, at the kitchen table, I copied the six margin maps onto one long strip of paper, Saltburn at one end and Filey at the other, and it's nearly a metre long. It'll be Dad's birthday present: eleven months late or a year early, depending how you look at it.
