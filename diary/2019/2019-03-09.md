Biscuit has found the skylight. Not the opening of it (it stays shut) but the sill underneath, which he reaches from the top of the wardrobe in one jump I didn't know he had in him. He sits up there watching the gulls on the roof opposite with his jaw going, making a noise like a tiny broken typewriter.
