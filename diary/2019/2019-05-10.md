reminder: Biscuit's teeth, check-up in six months
---

The vet took three of Biscuit's teeth out today: two at the back that were rotten through and one at the front that was "thinking about it". He went in at half eight in the morning, furious, and came out at four, groggy and wobbly, with the tip of his tongue showing at the side where a tooth used to be. He sat in the carrier on my knee on the bus home and didn't say a word, which is how I knew how bad he felt.

At home he walked into the side of the door, sat down and looked at me as if I'd arranged it. Soft food for a week. A bill for more than my phone. He's asleep now on the shelf above the radiator, breathing through his mouth, and the breath, I'm happy to report, is already better.
