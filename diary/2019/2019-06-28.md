For anyone coming tomorrow, because I've been asked four times: it starts at half seven in the church on the corner, the one with the green door. Sit on the left if you want to see me, behind the second desk of cellos; sit on the right if you'd rather not. Tea in the interval, and a raffle for a bottle of whisky that Agnes's brother-in-law wins every year. The pub afterwards is the one on the Shore. Bring cash for the raffle. Don't clap between movements, unless everybody else does.
