Advent, and time for the cards. This year's design came to me in about a second, because it's been lying on my maps all year: Biscuit, curled asleep on a map. I drew him from life this afternoon while he slept on the Pentlands sheet, which he did obligingly for two hours, and then the contours under him, simplified, so that he lies on a hill of lines like a cat on a fingerprint. Two colours: the map in blue-grey, the cat in orange. It'll be the hardest block I've cut.
