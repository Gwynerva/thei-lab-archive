A Christmas card from Nana today, the first to arrive, as it always is. The pound coin taped inside, as always. But the handwriting has gone small and shaky, the lines running downhill across the card, and she's written *To my dear Mira* and then, under it, *To Mira* again, as if she'd forgotten the first. I sat on the stairs with it for a while. Then I put it on the mantelpiece, in the middle, where everyone can see it.
