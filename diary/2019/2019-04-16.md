Home day. Tokyo to Helsinki, eleven hours of sleeping badly over Siberia, which was white and empty all afternoon with the rivers frozen into silver threads; then Helsinki to Edinburgh, and the tram into town in the evening with everyone speaking an accent I understood. Leith Walk was wet. The flat smelled of Marta's cooking and cat.

Biscuit looked at me from the top of the wardrobe and turned his back. Marta says he's slept on my pillow every night. He'll forgive me by Thursday, she says.
