New Year's Day, and the Forth the colour of an old pewter spoon. Marta and I took the bus out to South Queensferry for the [Loony Dook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loony_Dook "people who walk into the Forth on purpose"): a few hundred people in fancy dress wading into the sea under the bridge while the rest of us held their towels and felt superior. A man dressed as a lobster went in twice. Marta says next year she's going in. I said I'll hold the towel.

::media[Hawes Pier at low tide, before the lobster went in]{file="forth-bridge-1"}

In the evening, the Munro map. It draws now: all 282 as small grey dots, the ones I've climbed in red. What it doesn't do is anything else. Three more evenings, I told Marta, and she laughed in a way I didn't care for.
