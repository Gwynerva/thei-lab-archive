Found what I was looking for, or one of the things: an engraved chart of this coast from the eighteenth century, a long thin strip of shore with the soundings marked in the sea and little drawings of the cliffs in profile along the top, as a sailor would see them coming in. Every headland we walked last week is on it, drawn by someone standing on a deck. Ordered a proper scan. It'll take three weeks, which is about the speed of the eighteenth century.
