Down to Whitby on the train: Edinburgh to York, York to Middlesbrough, then the little train down the Esk valley in the dark, the carriage half empty, the windows black with my own face in them and every so often a farmhouse lit up for Christmas going by like a ship. Dad on the platform in his good coat. Mum's kitchen, the oven going, the smell of the cake.

After tea I walked up the 199 steps in the dark to the church at the top, as I've done every Christmas since I was twelve, to look at the harbour lights from up there. They're the same lights. That's the point of them.
