Beinn an Dothaidh, as promised: the same train, the same platform at Bridge of Orchy, the same path up to the same col, and then north instead of south, in cloud so thick we found the summit cairn by walking into it. The compass and I had a long, serious conversation on the way down and agreed on everything. Eighteen. Back on the evening train with wet socks, in time for the late show, which I nearly slept through and didn't.
