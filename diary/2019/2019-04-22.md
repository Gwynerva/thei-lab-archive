Easter Monday, and the rest of Britain off work except Scotland, which isn't, so I went in. In the evening I painted the Kumano Kodo map properly, from the sketches in the margins: the forest in four greens, the river blue, the shrines as red dots, the inns as little roofs. It took three hours, and Biscuit walked through the wet sea once.
