The scan of the old chart came at last, and tonight I laid it over today's coastline on the computer, stretching and pinning it at the churches and the harbour piers, the only things that haven't moved. Then the 1853 survey over that, and the tithe map's cliff edge in red over that. Three hundred years of the same coast in layers, like the rings of a tree. At Saltwick the lines step back from the sea one after another: the chart, the tithe map, 1853, the dissertation, today. At the harbour, where there's stone, they all sit on top of each other and don't move at all. It's the most beautiful thing I've made on a computer.
