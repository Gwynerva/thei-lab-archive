Mum found Arthur's discharge book in the sideboard at Nana's: the little book every merchant seaman carried, with every ship he sailed in stamped and signed, 1944 to 1981, and his height and the colour of his eyes on the first page. *Eyes: grey. Hair: brown. Tattoos: none.* She read me the ships over the phone, one by one, and I wrote them down and looked them up: tankers, cargo ships, a banana boat out of Jamaica. Nana, in the background, said, "Not the banana boat, he hated the banana boat." The tree has its first page with a picture on it.
