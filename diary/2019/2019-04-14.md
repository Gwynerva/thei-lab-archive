Tokyo, the first full day, and eighteen kilometres without meaning to. Yanaka in the morning, the old quarter that the earthquake and the war mostly missed: wooden houses, a cat on every other step (I counted eleven), a cemetery with the last of the cherry blossom coming down on the graves. Then the great crossing at Shibuya, which I crossed six times for no reason except that everybody else was.

At dusk I went up a tower in Ebisu that has a free floor at the top, and the city went on in every direction to the edge of the world, office lights coming on in the towers one by one, a whole country's worth of windows. I stood at the glass until it was dark. A map of this would need a very fine pen.
