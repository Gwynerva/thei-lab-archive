Something Dad said on the walk that I didn't write down at the time, because I wanted to think about it first. On the last evening in Scarborough he said the shop had its worst winter for twenty years, that he'd had to let the Saturday lad go, and that Mum doesn't know quite how bad it was. Then he said, "It's fine now, the summer's good," and changed the subject to cod. I don't know what I'm supposed to do with this. He didn't ask me to do anything. I think he just wanted someone to know.
