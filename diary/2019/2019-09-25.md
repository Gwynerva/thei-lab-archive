Amsterdam. The plane came down over a country as flat as a table and as wet as a sponge, fields drawn in ruled lines with water between them, and I pressed my face to the window and thought: this is the most mapped place I've ever seen from the air. Train into Centraal, then a walk to the hotel along the canals, dodging bikes, which come at you silently from every direction at once like a well-organised flock.

::media[Trees along the Singel this afternoon, boats tied up below]{file="amsterdam-canal-2"}

Registration in the evening in a converted warehouse: badges, drinks by the canal, and a great many engineers in good shoes talking about storm surges as if they were old friends. The Dutch talk about water the way Dad does: as something you deal with, not something you fear. Ran the talk once more in the hotel room, to the kettle. Twenty minutes exactly.
