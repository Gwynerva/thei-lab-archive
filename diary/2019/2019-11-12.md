Pottery: glazing night. There's a whole wall of test tiles in the studio, each a little tile of colour with a number on it, and you choose by number, because the glaze in the bucket looks nothing like the tile: it's grey and chalky until the kiln does its trick. I chose 14, a dark blue-green like the sea off Saltwick on a dull day, for the leaning mug. Dipped it, counted to three, pulled it out. Now we wait a week.
