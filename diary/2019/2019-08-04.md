Glen Clova, with Ines, for Driesh and Mayar. Driesh we got, in cloud so thick we found the summit cairn by walking into it. Mayar we didn't: the fog came down properly on the plateau, and the compass and I had a long, serious conversation and decided we'd rather be alive. Back down the Kilbo path in rain. Eighteen. Ines says Mayar will still be there next year. That's the thing about hills.
