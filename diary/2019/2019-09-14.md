Meall nan Tarmachan, from the same car park as Ben Lawers a fortnight ago but going west instead of east: a short sharp hill with a long knobbly ridge beyond it, which I walked the whole length of just for the fun of the knobs. Twenty-one. A ptarmigan on the summit, already going white at the edges for the winter, which is the bird the hill is named after, more or less. It let me get within three metres, then walked off, unhurried, like a landlady.
