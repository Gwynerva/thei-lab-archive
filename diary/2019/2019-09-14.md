Lochnagar, with Ines, from the head of Glen Muick: a long walk in under birches, then up past a little pointed top to the rim of the corrie, and there below us the lochan the hill is named for, black and still at the foot of enormous dark cliffs, with snow already lying in one gully. Round the rim to the top, out of the wind behind a boulder. Twenty-one. A ptarmigan on the summit plateau, going white at the edges for the winter. It let me get within three metres, then walked off, unhurried, like a landlady.
