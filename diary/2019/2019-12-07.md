## A cat on a map, in lino

Printing day. The kitchen floor covered in newspaper, the inks rolled out on the old glass from a picture frame, the blocks inked one colour at a time: first the map in blue-grey, forty cards, hung to dry on string across the living room like washing; then, once they'd dried enough, the cat in orange on top, lined up by eye and by the corner marks, which is the part that goes wrong.

It went wrong on six. On six cards Biscuit lies half off the map with his tail out in the white, like a cat falling off a bed. I kept those for myself. Of the other thirty-four, about thirty are good, two are so good I want to frame them, and the rest go to people who love me and won't look closely.

::media[Lifting the first print off the block: the cat, at last, on his map]{file="linocut-2"}

Biscuit took no interest in the whole business until the orange came out, when he sat beside the glass and watched the roller go back and forth with great seriousness, as if checking the colour match. Marta says he approves. I say he just likes orange. Same thing, probably.
