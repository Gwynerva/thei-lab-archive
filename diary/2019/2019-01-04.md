Munro map, evening four. The hill positions come from an open list kept by people who climb hills for fun and count them for even more fun, and it's a better dataset than half the ones I'm paid to use. The trouble was the names: every Sgùrr came out as *Sg?rr*, because I'd read the file in the wrong encoding. Two hours to find, one line to fix. Celebrated with toast.
