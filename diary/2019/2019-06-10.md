Stuc a' Chroin on Saturday, with Ines, up the Ben Vorlich path from Loch Earn again, two years on, and over the bealach. It has a rocky step that looked impossible from below and turned out to be a scramble with very good handholds and only one moment when I had to have a word with myself. Sixteen.

Since then, evenings drawing Nana's present for Saturday: a map of her ninety years. Robin Hood's Bay in the middle, of course, with the lane up to the cottage and the rowan tree drawn in; then lines going out across the sea to the ports Grandad Arthur sailed to, from what she's told me over the years: Bombay, Valparaíso, Halifax, Fremantle, Rotterdam. Each line ends in a tiny ship. Evening three of it, at the kitchen table, with the good pens.
