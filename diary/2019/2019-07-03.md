Started something new, or something old: I want to find every early map of the Yorkshire coast between Saltburn and Filey, before the walk with Dad. For the dissertation I only needed the [Ordnance Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordnance_Survey "the national mapping agency, since 1791") sheets of Saltwick, from 1853 on. But there are older ones: estate plans, sea charts from when the harbour was full of colliers, the coast drawn by people standing in boats and squinting. Spent the evening listing where they might be: the national library, the county record office, a university or two, the back room of Dad's shop.
