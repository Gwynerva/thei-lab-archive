Sunday long run: eleven kilometres out along the prom to Portobello and back, into a headwind one way and a tailwind the other, which never feels like it evens out. Stopped at the far end and stood in the sea up to my knees for a minute, in my running shoes, for no reason I can defend. Squelched all the way home. Ines, told this on the phone, said, "See? Worth it," and I wanted to push her off something.
