Ben More and Stob Binnein from Benmore Farm, with Ines again. Ben More goes straight up from the road for a thousand metres with no nonsense whatsoever, and I hated every one of them, and at the top the cloud came down so I saw nothing at all. Then down to the col and up Stob Binnein, and the cloud tore open, and there was the whole of the southern Highlands in sun: Loch Lomond, the Arrochar hills, a hundred tops I can't name yet. Ines said, "See? Worth it," and I wanted to push her off. Two more red dots. That's sixteen and seventeen, if I've counted right.
