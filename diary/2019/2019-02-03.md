Went to the rescue centre yesterday "just to look". There's a ginger tom, about three, handed in when his old person went into a home. He sat at the back of his pen and looked at me as if I owed him money. Then he came to the front and pushed his head against the wire. That was that. They do a home check tomorrow and, if we pass, he comes on Saturday. He's called Biscuit. I didn't name him, but I wouldn't dare change it.
