Mum on the phone tonight, the Sunday call a day late. Nana's using the stick indoors now, not only out, and she's stopped going down the lane to the shop because of the hill back up. Mum said it in her parents' evening voice, the calm one she uses to tell you your child is struggling with long division. Nana will be ninety in June. I keep doing the sum and not liking the answer.
