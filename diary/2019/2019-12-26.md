Boxing Day at the Bay. Took Nana home in the morning and stayed for lunch in her kitchen; then Clare and I went down the lane to the beach at low tide, as we have every Boxing Day of our lives, and walked out over the scars, the long flat ledges of rock, as far as the sea would let us. Cold, clear, the light low and yellow, people's dogs going mad in the pools. Clare found an ammonite the size of a coin. She always finds one. I never do.

::media[From the slipway, the tide going out over the rocks]{file="robin-hoods-bay-5"}

Back up the lane to Nana's for tea. The rowan tree is bare, of course, and a blackbird was sitting in it. Nana says it's the same blackbird every year. It can't be. I didn't say so.
