The longest day, and I spent it being stupid on purpose. Marta bet me on Tuesday, over the washing-up, that I couldn't walk from the flat to the top of Arthur's Seat and back three times in one day. The stake: the loser cleans the bathroom until September. I took the day off.

The first time at seven, in cool sun, the city just waking, rabbits on the slopes below the Crags.\
The second at noon, in a crowd of tourists in sandals and a school party eating crisps on the summit.\
The third at five, legs like wet rope, the top almost empty, the Forth gone silver and Fife floating on it.

Thirty kilometres, more or less, and two hundred and thirty metres of up, three times over. Marta met me at the bottom of Leith Walk the third time with a can of something cold and the face of a woman who will be cleaning a bathroom.

::media[Arthur's Seat in the evening, the third time, gold all over]{file="arthurs-seat-1"}

Then we climbed out onto the flat bit of roof by the skylight, which we're not supposed to do, with blankets and the rest of the cans, and sat there while the sky went from blue to a deeper blue to a sort of green in the north. It got dark at nearly eleven, and even then not properly: a pale band stayed over Fife all night. Biscuit watched from the skylight, appalled, as if we'd finally gone too far. It counts as this week's long run. I've decided.
