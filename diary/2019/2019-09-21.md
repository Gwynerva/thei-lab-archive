The long run: twenty kilometres, the whole Water of Leith path from Balerno to the Shore, having taken the bus out first, which Ines says is cheating and I say is logistics. The river was low and brown and full of leaves already, and at one point a kingfisher went past me low over the water like a thrown blue stone. Two weeks to the race.
