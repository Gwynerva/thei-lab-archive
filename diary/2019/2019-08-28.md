The landlord's letter: the rent goes up by sixty pounds a month from October. With Priya gone it splits two ways instead of three, unless we find someone for her room. Marta and I sat at the kitchen table with the letter between us and did the sums twice. We can manage, just, if I keep to the coffee rule and she stops buying strings she doesn't need. Neither of us wants a stranger in Priya's room. So we'll pay, and keep the room for guests, and for Biscuit, who has already moved in.
