## The second day

Sleet, which the instructor said was perfect. We spent the morning on hypothermia and the afternoon on everything else at once.

The scenario that stays with me: a walker found at the bottom of a slope, cold and confused, who wouldn't tell us his name. Four of us. We got him off the ground onto every mat we had, into a bivvy bag, hat on, a sugary drink, and then did nothing clever at all for twenty minutes but keep him warm and talk to him and write things down every five minutes. That's most of it, it turns out: keep them warm, keep them talking, write it down, and send someone for help with a written note, not a story.

## What I'm keeping

1. Look after yourself first. A second casualty helps no one.
2. Warm is a treatment, not a comfort.
3. Write down the time. Then write it down again.
4. A phone in a dead zone is a torch.
5. Nobody needs to be a hero; everybody needs a plan.
