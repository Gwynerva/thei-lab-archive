The first cold swim of the autumn: fourteen degrees at Nordnes, so it counts. Sixty-three.

Three good things:

1. The shock of it, and then the calm after.
2. A message from the café: another print sold off their wall.
3. Jonas's fish soup, which he makes as if he were cross with it, and which is perfect.
