Rain: day 11. A proper Bergen Sunday, rain sideways against the window from breakfast to dark. I got the recorder out for the first time since the birds in spring and stood it on the sill for twenty minutes. Jonas asked why we need a recording of rain when we have the rain. Fair. But in July we won't.
