## Pencil, ink, colour

A whole day on how the Sandviken map should look, before drawing a single real line of it. Notes for myself, because I'll forget:

- **Pencil** first, HB, light, the whole sheet at once so nothing comes out too big
- **Ink** in two weights
  - 0.3 for streets and the coast
  - 0.1 for steps, fences, the edges of gardens
- **Colour** last, watercolour, five pans only
  - a grey-blue for the water (Payne's grey and a lot of water)
  - a warm white, left as paper, for the houses
  - red for roofs, but only some roofs
  - two greens for the hill
  - yellow ochre for paths

## The rule

Houses are drawn as houses, from a little above, not as squares. Streets keep their names in my own lettering, which the course in spring finally made legible. The north arrow is a cat. I've decided. If anyone objects, they can draw their own Sandviken.

## Why by hand

Because I spent three years making charts so exact that nobody could tell I'd made them. This one I want people to be able to tell. Sigrid said something in May, about her trees, that I keep turning over: a machine-picked apple and a hand-picked apple taste the same, and people still pay for the hand. I'm counting on it.
