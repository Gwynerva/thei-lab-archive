Christmas Day, the English one: the pudding, flaming, with Jonas very alarmed, and crackers we'd smuggled from Whitby. In the morning I took Dad up Fløyen on the funicular, and at the top he stood at the rail a long time, looking west.\
"So that's the North Sea," he said, "from the wrong side."
