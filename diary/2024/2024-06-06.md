## The pack

Packed for the Camino on the bedroom floor, everything laid out first so I could take half of it away again. The second half was harder than the first: the book I wasn't going to read, the spare fleece, the little sketchbook *and* the big one. Jonas stood in the doorway saying *do you need that?* about every single thing, in the voice of a man who has carried kayaks over portages. Seven kilos without water. What made it:

- feet
  - two pairs of wool socks, and one of liners
  - the trail shoes, broken in on seven mountains
  - plasters, the good kind, and a needle
- everything else
  - two shirts, a fleece, and a rain jacket tested in Bergen
  - a notebook and three pens, one of them for maps
  - the scallop shell Ines posted me, tied to the strap

## The route

I'll write the route into [the stages](<ref:project:camino#h:Stages> "filled in as I go, I hope") as I walk, a stage a day. I've drawn the whole coast on one long strip of paper, Porto at the bottom and Santiago at the top, folded like a fan to fit the top pocket. No contours. The sea on the left, and that's all the orientation I'll need for a week.

Biscuit lay in the empty pack while I was packing it. He knows.
