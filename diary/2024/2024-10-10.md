Northern lights over Bergen. Jonas came in from the balcony at eleven saying "come, come, now", and the sky over Askøy was green, then red at the top, moving like curtains with someone walking behind them. The forecast on [met.no](https://www.met.no "where I'd looked at four o'clock and seen clear skies and thought nothing of it") had promised clear; it hadn't promised this. Half of Sandviken was out on the steps in dressing gowns.

The last time I saw them properly I was in Lofoten with Priya. I sent her a photo at midnight and she sent back a row of screaming faces.
