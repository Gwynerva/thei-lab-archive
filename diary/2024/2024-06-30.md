## Half a year

The last day of June, and the last day of being nothing in particular. From tomorrow I'm a business, with a number. I sat on the step this evening with a coffee, the barometer behind me reading *fair*, and looked back over the six months.

January: half time, and the long walk home. February: letters and clay. March: Isla. April: the letter, four lines. May: the last chart. June: seven mountains, two hundred and eighty kilometres, and a stamp with the date in dots.

## What helps

I've been keeping a list since the autumn, the way I was told to:

- walking, anywhere, a long way
- cold water, briefly
- making something with my hands that has no deadline
- Jonas, who never once said *come on*
- the barometer, which only ever says what is, never what should be
- writing it down here, even the empty months

## Nana

Nana would have said what she always said when something broke, a seam or a cup or a person:

> Make do and mend.

I used to think it meant *put up with it*. I think now it means mend it, and then wear it mended, and don't hide the stitches.

Three good things, today:

1. the swim at Nordnes this morning, eleven degrees, the water green and clear to the bottom
2. a message from Anna: *invoice me first, I meant it*
3. tomorrow
