Sigrid rang Jonas this evening. Ragnhild has moved into the care home in Odda for good — she is ninety, and last winter at Øvre Lid was one too many — and the house above Utne is standing empty. She doesn't want it sold to strangers. She wants, Sigrid said, "somebody who'll keep it". She asked whether that might be us.

Jonas put the phone down and looked at me, and I looked at him, and for a long while neither of us said anything. Then he said: "Fourteen windows." He has known the house since he was a boy. I've seen it once, from the road, in blossom.
