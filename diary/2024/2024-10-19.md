Swim seventy-four: eleven degrees, a north wind turning the bay into small grey teeth. Three of the Saturday women were there, and one of them, who must be eighty, said *ja, nå er det deilig* in a voice like a door. In and out in four minutes. Then coffee from the flask with both hands round the cup, shaking and grinning.
