A long call with Ines in Porto. Her blisters have gone; mine have turned into hard little pads I'm oddly proud of. We agreed that the hardest part of the Camino was the Monday after it, when nobody tells you where the next arrow is. She's going back to walk the inland way next spring and asked if I'd come. I said *maybe*, which we both know means no, and that's fine.
