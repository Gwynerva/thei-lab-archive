## The first stamp

At the cathedral at ten, up the steps with the whole city falling away below, I had my :hint[*credencial*]{note="the pilgrim's passport; you collect a stamp at least once a day, and twice a day in the last hundred kilometres"} stamped for the first time: blue ink, the cathedral's towers, slightly smudged. I looked at it for a long time, the way I looked at the sign in Whitby. One square filled. A great many squares to go.

## Porto on foot

Then Ines walked me all round her city, uphill, as if to break me in: the tiles at São Bento station, the old trams, the top deck of the bridge with the metro rattling past. Lunch at her mother's, who fed me three times and said I was too thin for a pilgrim.

## Before bed

Feet: fine. Pack: still seven kilos, though Ines's mother tried to add a cake. Tomorrow we walk out to the sea and turn right.
