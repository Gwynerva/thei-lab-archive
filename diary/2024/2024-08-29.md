The vet put a fine needle into the lump, looked at what came out under her microscope while we waited, and said: fat. A lipoma, harmless, common in middle-aged cats who enjoy their dinners. She looked at Biscuit, and at his weight on the scale, and then at me, and I felt judged on his behalf.

We came home with a shaved square on his side and instructions about portions. I have read [the list of things he eats](<ref:project:biscuit#h:Things he eats> "a long list, and longer than it should be") and crossed off the prawns, with regret on both sides.
