The vet rang this afternoon with the result: fat. A lipoma, harmless. "Old cats get them," she said, and when I told her what he eats she was quiet for a moment in a way I felt judged by, on his behalf.

He still has a shaved square on his side from the needle, and now I have instructions about portions. I have read [the list of things he eats](<ref:project:biscuit#h:Things he eats> "a long list, and longer than it should be") and crossed off the prawns, with regret on both sides.
