Swim ninety-nine, alone, sleet, the best one of the year. In the water I let myself think what I didn't say all week: Dad is older. He stopped twice on the steps up from the funicular and pretended he was looking at the view. His colour is fine, Mum says, and the cardiologist is pleased. But he stopped twice. I'm going to be a long way away, and then further.
