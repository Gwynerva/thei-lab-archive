Four years today. I wrote to her, as I do on this day, in the notebook I keep for [letters to Nana](ref:page:letters-to-nana). Told her about the house — the windows, the orchard, the heart in the shutter — and that I think she'd approve of the damp being in only one room. Told her Isla has her chin. Cried a bit over the kettle, which she would have called a waste of good water.
