## Somebody bought one

The proofs were right on Wednesday — the water grey-blue at last — and I put the Sandviken map in the shop on Thursday night with a photo taken on the kitchen table. This morning, at half seven, an order. One print, A3, to Trondheim.

The buyer wrote in the order box: *my grandmother's house is on it, the yellow one by the steps.* I looked. There is a yellow house by the steps. I painted it yellow because on the morning I drew it the sun was on it.

::media[The lane by the steps, and the house I painted yellow]{file="sandviken-1"}

## Wrapping it

I rolled it in tissue, then brown paper, then a tube, and wrote the address in my best lettering, which was a mistake because the tube rolled and smudged it. Wrote it again. Jonas took a photo of me at the post office counter, standing like someone launching a ship.

::link[The shop, such as it is]{url="https://example.org/mira-maps/shop"}

## What it means

It means a stranger saw something I made by hand and wanted it on their wall. It means 450 kroner, minus the print, the tube and the postage, which leaves enough for a coffee and a cinnamon bun. I bought both, and ate the bun on the steps by the yellow house.
