## The seven

All seven of Bergen's mountains in one day, with Jonas and a few thousand other people and a stamp card. We'd done them one at a time since my first August here — [Fløyen first](ref:event:floyen "August 2021, in the wrong shoes") — and today all of them, one after another:

1. Lyderhorn, at seven, the start, mist on the top
2. Damsgårdsfjellet, in sun
3. Løvstakken, the steep one, legs already complaining
4. Ulriken, and a cheese sandwich on the summit
5. Fløyen, crowds and waffles
6. Rundemanen, in a cold wind
7. Sandviksfjellet, last, and down the zigzags to our own front door at nine

::media[From the top: the whole city spread out, and all seven of them in view]{file="floyen-1" layout="stretch"}

## How it felt

Thirty-five kilometres and something like two thousand metres of up. My knees have opinions. My feet have a manifesto.

But what I keep thinking about is Løvstakken at about one o'clock, when I sat down on a rock and for a minute couldn't get up, and Jonas sat down next to me and didn't say *come on*. He passed me a square of chocolate and we looked at the city. Then I got up.

A year ago I couldn't open a laptop. Today I walked over seven mountains. I'm not going to make that mean more than it does. But I'm writing it down.

## For the Camino

A test, Jonas says: if I can do seven mountains in a day, I can do twenty kilometres a day for a fortnight. My feet say we'll see.
