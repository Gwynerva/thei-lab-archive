Viana to Caminha, the last day with Ines: long and flat by the sea, rocks and dunes and men fishing off them. At Caminha she saw me into the albergue and took the train back to Porto, and on the platform she said *bom caminho* and turned round fast so I wouldn't see her face.

::media[Caminha church at dusk, after she'd gone]{file="caminha"}

Alone for the first time in five days. Ate soup in the square and wrote a postcard to Biscuit.
