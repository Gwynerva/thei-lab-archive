## A hundred

The hundredth cold swim, at Nordnes, at nine in the morning, six degrees, a wind off the fjord with snow in it. Six of the regulars came because they'd heard, and somebody had brought a flag on a stick, and the old woman who says *ja, nå er det deilig* said it again, louder. I went in with Jonas. He stayed in longer than me, which I'll never forgive.

The last three, for the record:

98. Boxing Day, Dad on the steps in his good coat, saying I was mad
99. Sunday, alone, in sleet, thinking about him
100. Today, with everyone

::media[The sea bath at nine in the morning, the regulars already in]{file="sea-bath-2"}

The first one was a year and four weeks ago. I could barely get out of bed that winter. I went in for ninety seconds and came out able to feel my own skin, and kept going back for that.

## The year

The year I stopped. The year I started again, at half time, then not at all, then on my own. Isla. The notice. Santiago, and the blisters. The certificate on a bollard with an ice cream. Forty-one prints. Twelve maps on a screen. A house above a fjord with fourteen windows, and Ragnhild's key ring with a copper kayak on it.

Thankful, and I'll say it plainly, without the sugar: I'm well. Not perfectly, not always. Well.

I read back through [the diary](/diary/) tonight, the whole of 2024, and the entries got longer as the year went on. That's the best sign I know.
