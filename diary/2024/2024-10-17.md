Couldn't sleep. Everything I'm afraid of, in the order it arrived at three in the morning:

- money, and a house that old, and a roof nobody has looked at in twenty years
- leaving Bergen, which took me two years to love
- the orchestra, the swims, Anna, the steps
- being far from everyone, and a long way from an airport if Dad's heart goes again
- becoming Jonas's family's project instead of my own person

Tove used to ask me what I wanted, not what I was afraid of. What I want is a kitchen with a window that looks at water, and a table big enough to draw maps on. Which isn't an argument, I know. But it isn't nothing either.
