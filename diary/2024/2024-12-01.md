The orchestra's Advent concert in Sandviken church, the first Sunday of Advent, candles in every window and the radiators losing to the cold. We played the Elgar. I got through the second page, mostly, and in the slow movement I looked up once and saw Jonas in the back pew with his eyes shut. He recorded the end of it on his phone; you can hear someone's coat rustle and a child ask if it's finished yet.
