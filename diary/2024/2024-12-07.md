Market, day one. Eight hours at a stall between a man selling wool socks and a woman selling reindeer sausage, both of them old hands who took one look at me and lent me a heater. Sold fifteen prints and a pile of cards. A child asked why the north arrow is a cat. I said because cats always know where they're going. He accepted this completely. Drank a great deal of :hint[gløgg]{note="hot spiced wine, or the alcohol-free kind for children and stall-holders"}, the stall-holders' kind.
