17 May, my third in Bergen. I wore Sverre's flag pin, which came in the post from Voss last week with a note saying *for the 17th*, and we watched the parade from Torgallmenningen with an ice cream each at nine in the morning, which today is allowed. The :hint[*russ*]{note="the school leavers in red overalls, who've been celebrating since April and look it"} were asleep on the benches by noon.
