Started binding Isla's book. Printed the twelve pictures at home on thick paper, folded them into sections, and punched the sewing holes with the awl Fergus gave me in 2017, which still has his initials scratched into the handle. The kitchen table, thread and beeswax, the radio on, rain on the window. I'd forgotten how quiet this work is.
