Evening three: old maps. Everyone brought a scan of an old map of somewhere they love, and we :hint[georeferenced]{note="pinned an old map to real coordinates, point by point, so that it lies over a modern one"} them. A man in Voss laid his grandfather's farm map of 1902 over the modern one and found the barn had moved twelve metres. Nobody in his family had known. He went very quiet, then laughed.
