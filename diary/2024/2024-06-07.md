## Getting there

Bergen, Amsterdam, Porto: three hours in the air and twelve degrees warmer. Ines was at the arrivals gate with a sign that said PEREGRINA in her best capitals and a bag of cherries, and she hugged me so hard my shoulder bag went flat. Four years since we last stood in the same room. She hasn't changed except for one grey streak, of which she is very proud.

## The plan

[The Portuguese Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Way "the coastal variant, mostly, up the sea") runs from Porto to Santiago, about 280 kilometres the way we're going. We're taking the coast: boardwalks and beaches up to the Minho, then over into Galicia, and inland at Redondela to join the central route for the last days. Ines walks with me as far as Caminha. After that it's me and my feet.

## Porto in the evening

We ate on the Ribeira with our feet up on our bags, grilled sardines and green wine, and watched the light go off the Dom Luís bridge. Ines bought two pastéis de nata from a hatch in a wall and we ate them walking, which she says is the correct way; standing still is for tourists.

Tomorrow the cathedral and the first stamp. A day of rest, she says, that won't be.
