Went to see the gingerbread town with Jonas, the way you're supposed to once a year: hundreds of little houses made by schoolchildren and companies and grandmothers, a train, a harbour, a whole Bergen in biscuit. Somebody had made our street. Somebody had not made our house, which is fine, because we're leaving it.
