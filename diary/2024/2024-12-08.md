## Day two

Colder, busier, and I was better at it. By two o'clock I'd stopped apologising for the prices (rule iii, broken eleven times yesterday). By four the folder of Nordnes prints was empty and I was taking names for after Christmas on the back of a card.

Forty-one prints in two days. Forty-one walls in Bergen and beyond that will have a little hand-drawn piece of this city on them. I keep saying the number to myself.

## People

- a man who'd lived in Skuteviken for seventy years and corrected the position of a bench (he was right)
- two women from Stavanger who bought one each "so we don't fight over it"
- the woman from the bakery, who bought the Sandviken map with her own bakery on it and then gave me a bun
- someone quiet with a grey beard who bought the seven mountains map and asked for my card, twice

## After

At five Jonas fetched the car and carried the boxes, which were lighter than this morning in the most satisfying way. At home I counted the cash on the kitchen table twice, the way Dad does at the shop, and Biscuit lay on the notes.
