## The last morning

Half time to the very end. In at eight, out at half twelve, except that today half twelve became four. Cleared my desk into one cardboard box: a mug, two pens, the tulip pot from January, a stone from Herdla I'd forgotten I kept, and a print of the first harbour I ever charted for them, Bulandet, with my mistakes circled in red by somebody kind.

## Cake, speeches

Cake at two in the big room. Anna made a speech that was short and funny and then not funny for one sentence, and I'm keeping that sentence to myself. The team gave me a framed print of the release series, all forty harbours in a grid, a voucher for a paddle shop, and a card the new hire had clearly organised, because everybody had written something and it was in alphabetical order.

I made a speech too. Three lines. I've got good at short.

## Walking out

I walked out of the front door at four with my box, down to the harbour, and sat on a bollard on the quay with the box on my knees like a cat. Nearly three years. Forty harbours and a hundred others. One autumn I'm not going to pretend didn't happen.

::media[Vågen at four, the boats, the box on my knees just out of the picture]{file="vagen-1"}

Jonas met me there with two ice creams. Tomorrow I don't have a job. Tomorrow I'm going to sit in the sun and fill in forms.
