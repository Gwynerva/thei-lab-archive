## Over the mountain

Sigrid's blossom, as promised in February. We drove out of Bergen at seven, over the mountain and down to the water, and across on the little ferry with a coffee in a paper cup, and the whole slope above Utne was white and pink down to the shore, and the fjord bright blue under the snow on Folgefonna across the way. I've seen it before, two years ago, the first time I met her. It's different when you've been away from yourself for a while. Everything looks as if it's been washed.

## The orchard

Sigrid walked me round the orchard tree by tree, in her boots and a cardigan with holes at both elbows, telling me their names (the trees have names) and how each one came through the hard winter. Some lost branches to the snow. Two she's taking out in the autumn. One old tree, half its branches dead and grey, was flowering on the other half as if it were showing off. She put her hand flat on its trunk and said:

> A tree doesn't apologise for a bad year. It just flowers on whatever's left.

I don't think she meant me. I think she did.

## Coffee

Afterwards, coffee and waffles on the step with the bees going in the blossom like a motor left running. Jonas fell asleep in the grass. Sigrid asked what I'll do now, and I told her about maps for people, and she said, "Good. People need maps," as though that settled it. Perhaps it does.
