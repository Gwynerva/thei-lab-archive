Walked out to Nordnes in thin sunshine and sat on the bench at the point for an hour, watching the coastal steamer go out and the little ferry cross to Askøy. I've started counting what I'll miss, which is a bad habit when the leaving is still four months off. The list so far: this bench, the swims, the steps, the fish market at six in the morning, the sound of the city at night, like the sea.
