Swim ninety-eight, with Dad on the steps of the sea bath in his good coat and Mum holding the towel. Seven degrees. When I came out blue and laughing, Dad said I was mad, in exactly the voice he used when I passed my driving test. Then the four of us had waffles in the café and he asked the woman at the counter what the water temperature was, as if to check.
