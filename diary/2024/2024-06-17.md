## Redondela to Pontevedra

Twenty kilometres through eucalyptus woods and over old stone bridges, into Pontevedra in the afternoon heat: a small grey granite town with no cars in the middle, which I approve of, and a round church shaped like a scallop shell, which I approve of more.

## Feet

An inventory, because the [feet](ref:section:camino/feet) deserve one:

- the left heel: blister, lanced, healing, under a plaster since Viana
- the right little toe: a new one today, the size of a lentil
- both soles: the burn of eight days of granite and tarmac, gone by morning each time
- the knee, the old one from Leith: nothing. Not a twinge. I keep waiting.

I sat in the square with my shoes off and my feet on my pack and a lemonade, next to a Dutchman doing exactly the same, and we compared blisters like children comparing conkers.

## Halfway, more or less

Somewhere today I stopped thinking about where I was going and just walked. I noticed it happen, the way you notice you've stopped hearing the rain. No harbours in my head, no datums, no list of what I should be doing instead. Only the next yellow arrow, and the one after that, and a lemonade at the end.
