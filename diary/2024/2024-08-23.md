A bad day. Nothing happened. The old heaviness came in the morning and sat on my chest, and every email looked like a cliff. I know its shape now. I did what Tove taught me: one thing at a time, outside before noon, water, no decisions. Walked to the battery and back. By evening it had lifted to a dull grey. Writing it down so I remember that it lifts.
