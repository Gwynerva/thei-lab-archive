## A book for Isla

Easter Monday, everyone asleep after lunch, and I sat at Clare's kitchen table with the back of an envelope and drew Isla a map of Bergen: the house, the fjord, the sea bath, a little ginger cat on the roof, the rain coming in from the left in neat diagonal lines.

Clare looked over my shoulder and said, "That's a book." So it's going to be a book. A picture book for Isla, about where her auntie lives: the rain, the boats, Biscuit, the mountains you can see from the bus. Drawn and painted, bound by hand, ready for when she can hold it.

What it needs, written on the same envelope:

- a story, but not much of one: a walk, a day, the rain
- twelve pictures, give or take
- words in English, and one Norwegian word on every page
- a cloth binding she can chew

## Noticing

It's the first thing I've wanted to make since the summer. Not the first thing I've made — there were the bowls, and the letters — but the first thing I've *wanted*, the old way, with the itch in the fingers and a list forming before I've decided anything. I noticed that, and I'm writing it down here so I remember I noticed.

Isla slept through the whole planning meeting, on Dev, on the sofa, with her mouth open.
