## Out to the sea

First walking day. We took the tram out to the river mouth at seven, past the lighthouse at Foz with the fishermen already on the wall, and at the sea we turned right, and that was the Camino: Porto to Vila do Conde, about twenty-five kilometres.

Nearly all of it on wooden boardwalks laid over the dunes, mile after mile of planks, the Atlantic on our left the whole way and its spray drifting over us. Beach cafés with plastic chairs. A man walking a goat. Little chapels in the sand. The first yellow arrow I saw was painted on a lamp post in Matosinhos and I took a photo of it as if it were a monument.

## Ines

Ines talked for twenty kilometres: about Porto, about her mother, about the Forthline years and the people we both remember and the coffee machine we both hated. I'd forgotten how much she laughs. For the last five kilometres neither of us said anything at all, which was just as good.

## Evening

Second stamp, at the albergue, from a volunteer who asked where I'd walked from and wrote *Bergen* in the book when I told her. Dinner of soup and bread and more soup. My left heel is warm in a way I don't like.
