The Sandviken map is finished. Scanned it at the print shop in town in four pieces and stitched them at home; you can't see the seams unless you know where Skuteviken is. Sent the file off for a proof.

Three good things:

1. The map, finished.
2. The man at the print shop said "*Det er jo Sandviken!*" before I'd told him what it was.
3. A dry evening, the first in a week, on the steps with Jonas.
