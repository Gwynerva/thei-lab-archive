Pontevedra to Caldas de Reis. Hórreos in every garden now, the stone granaries up on their legs with little crosses on top; I've started counting them the way I used to count rain. Forty-one today. At Caldas there's a hot spring in the square and everybody sits on its edge with their feet in, pilgrims in a row, groaning.
