## They came

Mum and Dad came through arrivals at Flesland at ten past twelve, Dad pulling the case and Mum carrying the pudding in a tin in both arms, like a baby. Dad looked out of the window of the airport bus the whole way in and said the fjords were "smaller than I'd been told and better than I'd been told". Mum held my hand on the bus. She hasn't done that since I was small.

## Christmas Eve, the Norwegian way

At five the church bells rang Christmas in across the city, and we stood at the open window with the cold coming in to listen. Then the *pinnekjøtt*: the lamb ribs steamed over birch sticks for three hours, mashed swede, potatoes, *akevitt* for Jonas and Dad, who looked at each other over the little glasses like two men who have found a language.

::media[The plate, the beer, the red cloth]{file="pinnekjott-2"}

Presents after dinner, as they do here. Dad gave Jonas a tide table for Whitby, 2025, and Jonas understood exactly what it meant. Mum gave me a jar of her rowan jelly made from Nana's recipe, with Nana's handwriting copied onto the label. I had to go and stand in the kitchen for a minute.

And Jonas gave me a small parcel, very heavy for its size.

:::spoiler
A brass key on a ring with a tiny copper kayak. The key to Øvre Lid. Ragnhild sent it with Sigrid, with a note in pencil: *Til dere to. Hold huset varmt.* To the two of you. Keep the house warm.
:::

## Late

Dad fell asleep in the chair at half ten with Biscuit on his knee, both of them snoring. Mum and I did the washing-up and didn't talk much, and didn't need to.
