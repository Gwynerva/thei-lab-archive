## Registered

The letter came at quarter past nine: the register in Brønnøysund has written me down. Not me, exactly, but a business of one, an :hint[enkeltpersonforetak]{note="a sole proprietorship: one person, one organisation number, and nobody else to blame"} with my name on it and a nine-digit number I have already written on the back of my hand, the way I used to write tide times there as a girl.

I sat with the letter for a long time. Last winter I couldn't answer an email without my heart going. Today I have a company. Small, but mine.

## What isn't

I have no clients, a list of forty people who might one day want a map, and enough savings to be properly frightened in about a year. Jonas says that is how everyone starts. Jonas built a kayak with no idea where it would be kept, so I take his advice with salt.

But the Camino taught me one useful thing: you don't walk 280 kilometres, you walk to the next yellow arrow. The next arrow is the forty emails.
