## The email

At breakfast: the harbour association on the island said yes. They want a map for visiting boats — where the guest pontoon is, how deep it is at the end, where the shop is and when it shuts, where you can walk to see the sunset and where you mustn't anchor because of the cable. One sheet, folded, to hand out with the mooring fee, and the same thing as a picture for their website.

I read it twice and then read it aloud to Biscuit, who left the room.

## The job

It is, I realised in the shower, almost exactly what I did at Nordvåg for three years — a chart of a small harbour — except that this one may have a drawing of the shop's cinnamon buns on it. Nobody at Nordvåg ever let me draw a bun.

What they've asked for:

- the harbour at a scale you can read in a cockpit with wet hands
- depths in metres, rounded down, "because people are stupid" (their words)
- the walk to the old lighthouse, with the stile marked
- the shop, the toilets, the fuel berth, the bins

## What I said

I said yes, and a price, and a date: the first draft by the end of the month. Then I looked at [the freelance pages](ref:project:freelance "how it started") I wrote in June, full of brave sentences, and felt for the first time that they weren't lying.
