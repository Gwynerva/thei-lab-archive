New Year's Day swim at Nordnes with what looked like half of Bergen in woolly hats. Four degrees. In, one long breath, out, and coffee from a flask on the steps with Jonas, our feet purple.

::media[The bench by the water afterwards, frost on the boards, the sun just up]{file="sunrise-winter-1"}

Three good things, again, after a long gap:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. the water, which doesn't care how you are
2. coffee from the flask, too hot, burning my tongue
3. Biscuit on my feet all afternoon, a hot water bottle with opinions
:::

I used to end every day like this. I'm going to try again.
