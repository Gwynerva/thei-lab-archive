The last evening. Twelve maps, twelve people, ten minutes each. The sheep, all forty-three of them, with their summer grazing. The barn that moved twelve metres. A map of every bakery in Tromsø, rated. A map of the Inverness student's walk to school, with the dog that barks. I clapped at every one, alone at my desk in the dark, like an idiot, and then they all switched their microphones on and clapped for me. Eight evenings. I want to do it again.
