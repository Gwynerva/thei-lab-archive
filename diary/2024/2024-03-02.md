## The message

Dev's message came at twenty to seven, Bergen time: a photo, and four words, *She's here. Both fine.* I was awake anyway — I'm usually awake by six — and I read it on the bathroom floor with the light off so as not to wake Jonas, and then woke him anyway, because how could I not.

## Isla

Isla Mistry. Born at 4.12 in the morning in the hospital in Leeds where Clare has worked for ten years, so half the people in the room knew her mother, which Clare says was either a comfort or a nightmare, she hasn't decided which. Three point four kilos. A lot of dark hair. Furious about the whole business in the photo, both fists up like a boxer.

Mum rang at seven crying so hard she couldn't speak, so Dad took the phone and said, "Seven pounds eight. Good weight," as if she were a fish he'd landed. They're driving down from Whitby this afternoon.

## From here

I'm an aunt. Auntie Mira. I said it to Biscuit to hear how it sounded. He didn't react.

Booked flights before breakfast: Leeds, the 28th, over Easter, six days. Then I sat at the kitchen table and drew a map of the way from Clare's flat to the park, from memory, with a little pram on the path, and stuck it on the fridge.
