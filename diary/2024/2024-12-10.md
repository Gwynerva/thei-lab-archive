Posted Isla's book. Wrapped it in tissue, then in a tea towel with a map of Whitby on it that Mum sent me years ago, then in brown paper and string. The woman at the counter weighed it and asked, very kindly, whether it was something precious. I said it was a book for my niece. She stuck on a FRAGILE sticker without charging me for it.
