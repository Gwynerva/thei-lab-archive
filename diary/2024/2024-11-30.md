## The apple

Printed the cards on the kitchen floor, the way I did in Leith in 2017, with newspaper everywhere and the roller singing on the glass. Red ink for the apple, green for the leaf, two passes, sixty cards. Biscuit walked across three of them. Those are going to Clare.

::media[Lifting the first print off the block]{file="linocut-2"}

After [last year's](ref:section:linocut-cards/none "the year I never cut the block"), there's something steadying about the smell of ink again. Not a triumph. Just a thing I used to do, done again.

## The box in the cupboard

Then I switched off the home server. It has hummed in a cupboard since Leith, 2019: the photos, the backups, the weather station's old readings, a dozen things I set up on Sunday afternoons and never looked at again. We can't take a humming box to a house where the internet arrives by mobile mast and good intentions.

What I did, in order:

1. Copied the photos to two drives, one for the drawer and one for Mum's house
2. Exported the weather readings, all of them, to plain files
3. Shut it down properly, the way you're meant to
4. Sat in the sudden quiet of the cupboard

::media[The shelf where it lived]{file="server-rack"}

It's absurd to feel sad about a computer. I felt a bit sad about a computer.
