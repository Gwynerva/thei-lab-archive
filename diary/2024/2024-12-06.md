Everything for the market is in four boxes by the door: prints in tubes, prints flat in a folder, cards in bundles of five, a float of coins, a tablecloth, string, clothes pegs, a flask, and the fingerless gloves Mum knitted in 2020. Rules for the stall, written on a card for myself:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. Stand up. Sitting down makes people feel they're interrupting.
2. Say *hei* to everyone, and nothing else unless they stop.
3. Never apologise for a price.
4. Eat something at noon, even if there's a queue.
5. Biscuit stays at home, whatever Jonas says about "a draw".
:::
