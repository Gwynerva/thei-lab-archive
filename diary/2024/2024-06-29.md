The recordings, all of them, on one map at last: two winters of rain, the bells from New Year to Easter, the gulls and horns of the harbour, and this spring's birds. Sixty-three dots on Bergen. I clicked through them one after another with my eyes shut, and it was like walking the city in the dark.

::file[Every recording, and where I stood to make it]{file="sound-map" title="Map of the recordings"}

That's it for the sounds of Bergen. I'm keeping the recorder in my bag anyway.
