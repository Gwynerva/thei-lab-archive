Day 46.

Geology, unit five: fossils, and Whitby jet among them, which is fossil wood, trees like monkey puzzles squashed for a hundred and eighty million years until they turned black and could be polished. Nana has a jet brooch that was her mother's. I rang to tell her what it was made of and she said, "I know, pet, it's jet," and I said, "No, Nana, it's a tree," and she was quiet a moment and then said, "Well I never."

Clapped at eight.
