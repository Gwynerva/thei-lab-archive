Day 27.

## Eight rolls

Up at six to shape them. Eight rolls, eight flats, four floors. They came out of the oven at nine looking, honestly, like rolls. Not like the ones on the internet. Like rolls a person made in a kitchen.

::media[The eight, in Marta's striped tea towel, before the stairs]{file="sourdough-3"}

## The stair

I went down with a basket and a note tucked under each one, *from the top floor, made with Hamish, no need to give back the paper*. Knocked, stepped back two metres, waited.

- First floor: the couple with the baby, who said "Oh!", and then the baby said it too.
- Second floor: the old man who used to be a joiner, who held his roll up to the light like a jeweller with a stone.
- Ground floor: the twins, who ate theirs in the doorway before their mum could find a plate.
- Everyone else: on the mat, like milk.

## After

By lunchtime there were things on our mat: a jar of marmalade, a drawing of a loaf with a face, and a bag of plain flour, real flour, from the joiner, with *For Hamish* on it in pencil. I sat on the top step and cried a bit, which I seem to be doing a lot of, though this was the good kind.

Walk 26: just round the block, with flour on my jeans.
