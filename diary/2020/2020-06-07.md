Mrs Ogilvie says I'm ready to record it, which I'm not. Spent the afternoon on the last six bars, one line each in the margin of the score, so that I know them as places and not just notes:

37. the climb starts, all four strings
38. still climbing, don't rush it
39. the top: breathe
40. down the other side
41. the last run, like water off a roof
42. the chord. Let it ring. Don't apologise.

Practice: 90 min.
