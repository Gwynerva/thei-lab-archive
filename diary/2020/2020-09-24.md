## Morag

The physio is in a basement room off Constitution Street with a skeleton called Gerald in the corner. Morag Fleming: short, brisk, cold hands, laughs at her own jokes, which I respect. She bent the knee, straightened it, pushed it sideways, asked me to stand on that leg alone (no), and then sat back and said: "Your meniscus is torn, I'd bet my lunch on it, and your quads have gone on holiday."

**No surgery for now.** Twelve weeks of work, she said, and I'll get out of it what I put in.

## The list

On the fridge now, twice a day:

- [x] Quad sets: tighten, hold for ten, ten times
- [x] Straight leg raises, three sets of ten
- [x] Heel slides on a tea towel
- [ ] Mini squats against the wall (from next week)
- [ ] Step-ups on the bottom stair (when she says so, not before)
- [ ] Running (she laughed)

## After

Walk 185 was home from Constitution Street, slowly, without the crutch, because Morag looked at it and said "you don't need that, you need confidence", and I'd have walked home on my hands for her after that.
