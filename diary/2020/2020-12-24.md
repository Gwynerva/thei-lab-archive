Christmas Eve, Marta's way: Wigilia, twelve dishes (we managed seven), and no eating until the first star was out, which tonight was Jupiter, so it counted twice. A tin of shortbread came from Callum's café with a card that said "eat it all at once". She laid an extra place at the table, as they do at home, for the traveller who might come. I didn't say who I was thinking of. She didn't ask.
