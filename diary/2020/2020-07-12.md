Walk 111 started at ten to three in the morning, on yesterday's legs. Up Calton Hill in the dark with a flask, past two other people with binoculars who nodded like we were all in on something, and there it was, low over Fife in the north: :hint[NEOWISE]{note="Comet C/2020 F3, bright enough that July to see without binoculars"}, a smudge with a tail, as if someone had dragged a thumb across the sky.

I watched it until the sky went pale behind it and it just faded into morning.

::link[What I was looking at, so I don't forget]{url="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comet_NEOWISE"}

Back in bed by half four. Biscuit didn't move.
