Walk 146 along the shore of Loch na Keal before breakfast, mist sitting on Ben More. Then the long way home: ferry, Oban, the road. Sunday call from a lay-by above Loch Awe, on one bar of signal. Nana was asleep in her chair, so it was just Mum and Dad. Mum says she's sleeping more in the afternoons; Dad says so would he at ninety-one. Leith by nine, Biscuit at the door, demanding an account.
