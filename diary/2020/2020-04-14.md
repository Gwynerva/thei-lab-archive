Day 23.

A cello lesson by video, which I didn't think would work and which very nearly didn't. Mrs Ogilvie had her laptop on a pile of scores, and I could see mostly her ceiling rose and the top of her head. She can't hear tone through a laptop, she said, but she can hear fear. Then:

> Play it as if the person downstairs is the only person you will ever play it for.

So I did. They knocked on the ceiling at the end, twice, which we've agreed means "more".
