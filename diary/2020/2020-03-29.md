Day 7.

## Hamish

The clocks went forward in the night, which made no difference to anything. In the afternoon, with the last of a bag of rye from the health-food shop on Leith Walk (the only flour left in Leith, apparently, and only because nobody knows what it's for), I made a starter. Fifty grams of rye, fifty of water, a jar, a tea towel. He is called :hint[Hamish]{note="the sourdough starter; the name arrived with the first bubble and stuck"}. He lives on top of the fridge, where it's warm.

::media[Hamish's home, an old stoneware jar from the back of the cupboard]{file="jam-jars-2"}

Everything I know about [sourdough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sourdough "the page I read three times") came from reading the same article three times and a video call with Callum, whose starter at the café is older than his car. Callum's advice: "Feed him, ignore him, and whatever you do, don't name him." Too late.

The label on the jar, in Marta's writing:

Feed at nine.\
Never in the fridge.\
Not for Biscuit.

## The walk

Walk 6: up Easter Road and down Leith Walk, a loop of empty pavements. Somebody was playing the trumpet out of an open window on Iona Street: scales, then a hymn, then scales again.

Drew the walk in the notebook with a little jar in the corner of the page, for Hamish. Marta says a person who draws her sourdough on a map has been indoors too long. It has been a week.
