## Done

Grafted the underarms at lunchtime and sewed in the ends all afternoon (there were thirty-one ends). Soaked it in the sink and laid it flat on a towel on the floor to dry, blocked properly, not with a hairdryer, Nana.

At nine tonight it was dry enough, and I put it on.

::media[The red sweater, cables down the front, the way I see it when I look down]{file="sweater-2" layout="natural"}

## What it is

- Red, the colour of rowan berries, which I chose in April without thinking about it and think about all the time now
- Eight months, from April to today, most of it on video calls and the sofa
- One body and three sleeves, one of them frogged and one of them knitted through the worst week of the year
- Eleven balls of wool, two left over, which Biscuit already has his eye on
- **It fits.** The sleeves are the right length. It's warm.

## And

The news at four: no Christmas bubbles after all, just the day itself, and no travelling to England. So not Whitby. I'd half decided not to go anyway; now it's been decided for me, which is easier and worse.

Sat on the sofa in the sweater with Biscuit on my knee and cried a bit into the wool, which is what wool is for.
