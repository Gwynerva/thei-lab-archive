Day 4.

At eight o'clock the whole street came out and clapped. Windows, balconies, the family across the road banging a pan with a wooden spoon, a man in the flat opposite whistling through his fingers. I clapped for Clare, who was on a shift and couldn't hear it, and for Dev, and for all the nurses I don't know, until my hands stung. Then everyone went back inside, a little embarrassed, like after singing in church.

:::private
Clare texted at midnight: "Was it loud?" I said yes. She said, "Good. Tonight was bad." That's all she said.
:::
