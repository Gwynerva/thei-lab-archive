Day 21.

Easter Sunday. Three weeks.

I've knitted one scarf in my life, at school, and nothing since. Last night: a swatch, which is a small square you knit to find out how wrong you are. I was quite wrong; I went down a needle size. Today: cast on for a sweater, my first, in a wool I chose from a photograph on a screen and am learning the real colour of in daylight. It took an hour and two cups of tea to get all the stitches onto the needle and the right way round.

::media[Needles, two balls of wool and the swatch that lied]{file="knitting-3"}

Nana, on the phone, when I told her: "If it's wrong, rip it out and start again, pet. Wool doesn't mind."
