Day 12.

Dougie on the Friday call: nobody is being furloughed, the councils are still paying, and the flood maps still matter because it will still rain. Everybody unmuted at once to say thank you, and it sounded like a flock of starlings.

Three good things:

1. No furlough.
2. Hamish has risen by a third.
3. The light at seven o'clock, gold all down the street.
