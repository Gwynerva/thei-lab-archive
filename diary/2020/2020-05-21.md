Day 60.

The route map out of lockdown, published this afternoon: from next Thursday, probably, we can meet one other household outdoors, and travel a short way for exercise, "around five miles" was the phrase. I got the map out and drew a circle five miles round the flat with a compass and a pencil. It takes in Portobello, the Botanics, Arthur's Seat, and the very bottom of the Pentlands, if you squint and lean.
