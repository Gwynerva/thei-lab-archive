Day 2.

## Walk 1

The first walk. Out at five, along the Shore, up the Water of Leith path as far as Bonnington and back across the Links: six kilometres, and I passed the same man with the same dog three times from three different directions, as if Leith had been folded. The light was long and yellow, and the only noise on the Shore was a gull having an argument with a bin.

## The rules

I've decided: a walk every day, for as long as this lasts, and probably longer. The rules, written on the back of an envelope and stuck to the fridge:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. Every day, rain or shine, even if it's ten minutes round the block.
2. A different street each time, where I can.
3. No headphones. Listen to the place.
4. Draw it afterwards: one small map in the walk notebook, with the date.
:::

::media[Leith and the city, the map I'll fill in a line at a time]{file="map-scotland-2" layout="natural"}

The first map in the walk notebook: a lopsided loop in pencil, the Shore at the top, the Links like a green lung at the bottom, and the man with his dog drawn three times. I'll get better at it. I have, it seems, all the time in the world.
