Nana rang at nine, as she does on Saturdays, and I asked her about Arthur's first ship, for the family tree. She remembers the colour of the funnel and not the year, the smell of the cabin and not the port. "It was a long time ago, pet," she said, and then told me exactly what she'd worn on the quay to see it off.
