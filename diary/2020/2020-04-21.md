Day 30.

## Forty of us

The orchestra on a video call, Agnes's idea: a rehearsal, or something like one. Forty-odd faces in little boxes, most of them filmed from below the chin. Agnes in her front room with a baton and a cat. She asked us all, violins and violas too, to play the cellos' tune from the start of the Dvořák at the same time, "so we can hear ourselves again".

We couldn't. The delay on a call is half a second, different for everyone, so it came out as forty cellos out of sync: a smear of G minor, like a choir in a cathedral where everybody started a beat apart. It was terrible. Then, after a minute, it was quite beautiful, the way a lot of people trying is beautiful.

::media[My strings, caught moving by the phone camera, as wobbly as the call]{file="cello-5" layout="natural"}

## Muted

After that Agnes had us all mute, and she played the piano part with her own microphone on, and we played along at home, each of us alone with her, hearing nobody else. That was better. That was lonelier.

Marta did it from the kitchen and I did it from the box room: ten metres apart, half a second apart.
