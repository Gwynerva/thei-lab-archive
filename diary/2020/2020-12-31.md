Walk 283. The last one of the year, and of the rule.

## The walks

I said in March I'd walk every day until it was over. It isn't over, but the year is, and that will do.

- Walks: 283, none missed
  - the longest: through the Lairig Gartain and back to the tent, 15 km of bog and river
  - the highest: Ben Oss, 1,029 metres
  - the shortest: two hundred metres to the corner shop on Marta's arm, the day after the knee
  - the ones I don't remember: five, in November, in Whitby. Dad says we went to the end of the pier and back every morning. I believe him.
- Hills: some Munros (I've lost count, on purpose), and one small Pentland in the snow
- Swims: one, in August, which I keep going back to in my head

## Midnight

No street party, no fireworks over the castle, just Marta and me on the balcony at midnight in our coats, me in the sweater, a glass of something fizzy each and Biscuit's face at the window. The ships in the docks sounded their horns at twelve, all at once. Everything else is in [the diary](/diary/), where it belongs.

Three good things:

1. Marta.
2. Biscuit.
3. That it comes back. It always comes back.
