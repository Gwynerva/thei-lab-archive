Walk 104, then the Sunday call. Mum's in Nana's bubble now, so Nana was on it too, from Mum's kitchen, ninety-one and indignant about masks. I told her I'd had to undo a whole evening's knitting on the sweater, and she said:

> A stitch in time is still a stitch.

I've no idea what she meant by it and she was delighted with herself.
