Twenty people in a cold chapel, in masks, sitting apart. Singing wasn't allowed, so we hummed. I read my four lines. The last one was hers really, and Grandad's before her:

:::quote[Grandad Arthur, as Nana always told it]{align="center"}
It comes and goes, but it always comes back.
:::

Rowan berries on the coffin, from her tree.
