Day 32.

Without the traffic, the birds have got louder, or I've got quieter. A blackbird sings on the chimney pot across the street every evening at nine as if it's being paid. Recorded it tonight from the balcony, the phone held out over the railing; the bicycle you can hear is a delivery rider.

::audio[The blackbird on the chimney pot across the street, nine at night]{file="sound-blackbird" title="Blackbird, Day 32"}

The family tree is nearly done. The last name went in this afternoon: a great-great-grandmother I found on a census page, her surname spelt three different ways in three different years.
