Walk 119, slow. My legs remember Saturday. Cello after work: scales in three octaves, then the prelude once for old times' sake. Forty minutes. Mrs Ogilvie, on the laptop, says it's time for the allemande. Biscuit has claimed the Raleigh's saddlebag as a bed, which is fair: it was his cardboard box first.
