Day 45.

Biscuit has discovered the balcony now that the door is open all day, and sits among the tomato seedlings like a ginger garden gnome. He has already eaten one basil seedling, which goes on [the list](<ref:project:biscuit#h:Things he eats> "basil, apparently, now") under elastic bands and above wool. I moved the basil up onto the shelf. He watched me do it with an expression of deep betrayal.
