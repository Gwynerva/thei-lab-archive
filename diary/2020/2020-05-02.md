Day 41.

## The skip

Walk 40, and I came home with a bicycle.

It was in a skip on a street off Easter Road, under a mattress and half a bookcase: a road bike, the frame more rust than paint, both tyres flat as pancakes, the saddle split like a bread roll. But the wheels turned, more or less, and the badge on the head tube said Raleigh, and when I lifted it out it was lighter than it looked. I thought of [the Spoke Room](ref:project:spoke-room) and all those Saturdays in the basement, and I asked the man filling the skip whether I could have it. He said, "Please."

::media[The frame as it came out of the skip, before anything]{file="vintage-bicycle-2"}

## Home

Walked it home, which took an hour, people stepping into the road to give me two metres and also to stare. Marta opened the door, looked at it, looked at me, and said, "Where exactly is that going to live?"

The hall. It's going to live in the hall.

In the evening I turned it upside down on newspaper and spun the back wheel and listened. It ticked, slowly, like an old clock that has been asked to start again and isn't sure it wants to. Biscuit walked round and round it with his tail up, deciding.
