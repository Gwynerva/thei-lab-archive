Walk 110 was a Munro again! The second in a week. Ben Vorlich, the Loch Lomond one: up from Inveruglas in the car-club car at six, past the power station and its great pipes, along under the dam, and then straight up a long grassy shoulder that never once pretended to be easy. Three and a half hours up, cloud on the top, a raven sitting on the trig point as if it had been waiting for me, sandwiches in the wind.

::media[The loch from the way down, hazy, the hills folding into each other]{file="loch-lomond-2"}

Legs like string by the car. Worth every step. I'd forgotten, until last week, how much of me lives up there.
