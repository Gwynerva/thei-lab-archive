Day 19.

Good Friday, which in lockdown is a Friday. Unit one's quiz: nine out of ten, the one I got wrong being about shale, which is embarrassing for someone who's sat on a lot of it. Spent the evening reading about the Whitby ammonites, the snakestones: the old story that St Hilda turned the snakes to stone and threw them off the cliff, and the carvers who later gave them heads to sell. Every child in Whitby knows the story. I didn't know the rocks were a hundred and eighty million years old.
