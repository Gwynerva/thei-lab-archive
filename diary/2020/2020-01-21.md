Four pounds in a charity shop on Leith Walk: a print of a 1935 pictorial map of Edinburgh and Leith, every church drawn standing up, the trams like little caterpillars, the docks full of ships. Our street is on it, and our stair, more or less. It's going on the wall beside the desk, where I'll see it every time I should be working.
