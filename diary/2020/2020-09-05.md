Walk 166: Carn Liath, the first of the three tops of Beinn a' Ghlo, up from the car park above Blair Atholl. One great raw scar of a path straight up the heather, steep enough that I stopped to "admire the view" every hundred steps. From the top, the other two tops went on away to the north-east, folded and empty. Saved for another year.

::media[Heather on the way up, gone over now, the last of the purple]{file="moors-heather-1"}

The last one this year, probably. The evenings are drawing in.
