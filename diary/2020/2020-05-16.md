Day 55.

## Another project, help

The parts came this week in four parcels, which Biscuit opened for me: a little sensor board, a rain gauge with a tipping bucket, an anemometer that looks like three egg cups on a stick. Plus the Raspberry Pi that's been in a drawer since I bought it for something I've forgotten. The plan: a weather station on the balcony, taking readings every ten minutes, for ever, or until the balcony falls off.

If you're counting, and Marta is, that's the sixth thing this lockdown, after the walk, Hamish, the balcony, the rocks and the sweater. The Raleigh doesn't count, because it found me. [All projects](/projects/) is getting long.

::media[The Pi on the desk, wired to the old monitor for its first boot]{file="raspberry-pi-3"}

First reading, indoors, on the desk: 21.4 °C, 46 per cent humidity, 1018 hectopascals. It isn't weather yet. It's the box room.

Then Biscuit sat on the keyboard to see what I was looking at, and the next reading was 22.9. He is, it turns out, a heat source with opinions. The station will have to live well out of his reach, which on our balcony means the rail, which means drilling, which means Marta.
