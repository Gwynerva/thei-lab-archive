## The quay

The morning after: calm, bright, weed all over the quay and a lobster pot on the swing bridge, of all places. The shop stayed dry; [yesterday's sandbags](ref:diary:2020-02-15#:~:text=sandbags) did their job. Dad took the boards down without a word and then, at the door, said "Good," which from him is a speech.

## The Bay

Mum drove us to the Bay to see Nana, the long way round over the moor road, so I could see the becks running white down every gully and the heather black with wet. She was at the window with her coat already on, as if she'd been waiting since Friday. The beach below the slipway was all churned sand and timber and one person far out on the rocks looking for something. Nana looked at the flat sea for a long time and then said what she always says:

> Never trust a calm sea.

:::private
In the kitchen, with the kettle on, Mum told me Nana left the gas on twice in January. She's fine, Mum said. She's ninety, Mum said. Both are true.

## Home

Trains up the coast late everywhere; home to Leith at nearly midnight. Biscuit pretended not to have noticed I'd gone, then followed me from room to room until I went to bed.
