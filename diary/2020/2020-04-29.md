Day 38.

Nana rang twice tonight, twenty minutes apart, to tell me the same thing about the family tree, and the second time she didn't know she'd rung the first. I said, "You said, Nana," and wished I hadn't. Rang Mum after. Mum knows. Mum says it's the lockdown: nobody to talk to, every day the same. I want that to be true.
