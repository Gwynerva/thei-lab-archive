Walk 124 went between two mountains instead of over them.

## The Lairig Gartain

Up at five in a damp tent by the River Etive, the midges already at work. Walked back up the road to Altnafeadh and took the old path through the Lairig Gartain, the pass between :hint[the Buachaille]{note="Buachaille Etive Mòr, the great herdsman of Etive, standing at the head of Glen Coe"} and its little brother, Etive Beag. Four hours of bog and river and stepping stones, the two of them leaning over me on either side like the walls of a room, and the whole of Rannoch Moor shining behind whenever I turned round. Down the far side into Glen Etive and back along the road to the tent, past the lochan where everybody stops to take the same photograph, so I did.

## Numbers

- Distance: 15 km
- Up: about 400 m, and down the same
- Munros: none, on purpose
- People met in the pass: four, all at two metres, all cheerful
- Midge bites: I stopped counting, which is also where I've got to with the Munros

Asleep in the tent by half nine with the door open and the river going past.
