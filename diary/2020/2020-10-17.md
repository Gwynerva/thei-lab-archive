## Gone

At about eleven I came back from the shop and the skylight was open and Biscuit wasn't anywhere. Not under the bed, not in the airing cupboard, not in the bag of carrier bags. Then Marta, from the kitchen, in a voice I'd never heard her use: "Mira. He's on the roof."

He was. Out of the skylight, across the slates, sitting by the chimney stack four floors and a roof above the street, washing a paw as if he'd lived up there all his life.

## The hour

We tried everything.

- Calling him (ignored)
- Rattling the treat bag (one ear moved)
- Marta playing the violin out of the window, because she'd read that cats like high notes (they don't; he turned his back)
- Me halfway out of the skylight with a towel, the knee shouting, Marta holding my belt (abandoned, by order of Marta)
- Phoning the fire brigade, who said kindly that they've never yet found a cat skeleton on a roof, and to leave the window open

So we left it open, and sat under it, and waited.

## Back

At ten past twelve it started to rain, and there was a scrabble on the slates and a thump, and he dropped down onto my desk, walked across the keyboard and went straight to his bowl as if he'd been at work all day. I sat on the floor and cried a bit, and he came over and purred at me, loudly, like a man back from the pub who knows he's in trouble.
