First lesson in the same place as Mrs Ogilvie since March, except the place was the back green behind her tenement in Marchmont, shared with the whole stair: me on a kitchen chair on the grass, her on the bench two metres off with a mug of tea. The allemande sounded thin in the open air and she said that was the air's fault, not mine, which was kind and probably untrue. A blackbird joined in for bars nine to twelve.
