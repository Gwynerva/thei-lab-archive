Father's Day, a day after the longest. Rang Dad, who opened the chandlery again on Monday for the first time since March, with a bottle of hand gel on the counter and a sign on the door in his own careful lettering: TWO AT A TIME, MIND THE ROPES. He sounded ten years younger and told me about every customer.

Walk 90: up Calton Hill at ten at night, and the sky in the north still pale green over Fife, the way it only is up here in June.
