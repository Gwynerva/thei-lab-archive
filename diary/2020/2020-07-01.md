Walk 100.

I set out in March to walk every day of lockdown, and somewhere around walk forty it stopped being about lockdown. Today was the hundredth, and I did the route I'd been saving for it: down to the Shore, up the Water of Leith to Canonmills, and home the long way round Calton Hill. Two hours and ten minutes, 11.4 km, one heron, no rain.

## The last three

98. Monday: the Links and back in a soft grey drizzle, and a fox that stopped to look at me.
99. Yesterday: round the Links again, counting down.
100. Today: the saved route, all of it.

I've drawn every one in the walk notebook, **tiny**, a line and a dot for home. At the end of April I traced the first month onto baking paper and got a tangle. Tonight I traced all hundred onto one sheet over the city map, and the tangle has turned into a sort of flower with Leith in the middle. It'll be [the map of all of them](ref:section:daily-walk/the-map "when it's finished, which it isn't") one day.

Three good things:

1. The heron at the weir, not bothered by me at all.
2. Marta had the kettle on when I got in.
3. A hundred.
