Walk 159: Ben Narnain, the Cobbler's big rough neighbour, three weeks after Beinn Ime and from the same car park.

## Up

The direct way, which is the steep way: straight up from the head of Loch Long on the line of an old hill railway, where the sleepers have gone but the slope hasn't, then onto open hill with the loch shrinking behind me. Nobody in sight. The Cobbler busy as a high street across the glen, little bright jackets going up and down it all morning.

## The top

Near the top there's a great prow of rock they call the Spearhead. You can scramble up it, if you're braver than I am, or walk round the side, which I did, saying sorry to it. Then a plateau of boulders, a trig point, and a wind that came straight off the Atlantic with nothing in its way.

## Home

Back at the car at three, my knees grumbling again on the way down (noted again, ignored again), and home by six with the low sun in my eyes all the way along the loch. One more. I've lost count properly now, and I like it.
