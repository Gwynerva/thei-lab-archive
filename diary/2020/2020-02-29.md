A leap day, and Marta's recital, and Storm Jorge throwing rain at the church windows like gravel.

## Before

She didn't eat. I made her eat. She ate half a banana and handed me the other half like it was a relic. We walked down under one umbrella, the violin case inside her coat, the wind trying to take both of us into the Water of Leith.

## During

About sixty people, which in a church that size feels like a crowd. The Bach first, on her own, from memory. In the chaconne there's a place where it turns from minor to major and the whole thing opens like a door, and I watched a man in the front row put his programme down on his knee and just sit.

Then the Brahms, with me on the stool beside the pianist, turning pages. I turned every one of them at the right moment. I don't remember a single note.

## After

Wine in paper cups in the vestry, everyone damp, Marta pink and talking too fast. The pianist found me by the coats.

> You turned them like a professional. Which is to say, nobody noticed you were there.

The best review I've ever had.

Three good things:

1. The door in the chaconne.
2. Marta's face at the end, like someone stepping off a boat.
3. Chips on the way home in the wind, with an extra day of February to eat them in.
