Day 59.

Built the radiation shield out of eight white plant-pot saucers stacked on a threaded rod, a trick off a forum, so the sun can't cook the sensor. It looks like a tower of dinner plates. Then I compared it with a photo of a proper station and felt like a child who has drawn a horse.
