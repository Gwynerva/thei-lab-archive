Day 67.

The last day. Tomorrow the circle gets bigger.

## What sixty-seven days were

Sixty-six walks. A dozen loaves, one truly good. One starter, one sweater body half done, one balcony gone green, one bicycle in freezer bags in the hall, and a weather station on the desk that still thinks the box room is the world. One family tree. One prelude, very nearly. Forty cellos on a call. Eight rolls down a stair.

None of it was the plan. The plan was a concert, and a Munro or two, and maybe Copenhagen again in the summer.

---

## What I'll keep

The walk, every day, for the rest of the year at least. Looking at the same street long enough to see it change. The stair: we know everyone's names now, all eight flats, and the twins wave at the window when I go out.

What I won't write anywhere else: I've liked some of it. The quiet. Not having to be anywhere. That feels like a terrible thing to say while Clare is doing twelve-hour shifts in a mask, so I'm only saying it here, where it's just me.

The last clap tonight; they say it's the last. The whole street came out, and somebody's speaker played something with bagpipes, and nobody wanted to go back in.
