## At the gate

Nana is ninety-one today, and we couldn't go. Mum stood at her front gate with a cake in a tin, and Clare and I sang down the phone, which Mum held up to the window. Nana sat inside with the card I'd made (a map of the Bay, her lane marked with a heart, the rowan tree drawn in with every berry) and laughed in all the wrong places, which is what she does when she's moved and doesn't want anyone to notice.

Mum said afterwards that Nana had put on her good blouse, the one she wears with the jet brooch, the tree. She'd known it was her birthday all day, Mum said, which I hadn't dared to ask.

## On the phone

Later, just the two of us, she said:

:::quote[Nana June, ninety-one today]{align="center"}
A stitch in time is still a stitch, pet. You're the one doing the stitching now. Keep at it.
:::

I don't know exactly what she meant. I'm going to keep at it anyway. Knitted ten rows of the sweater tonight, for her, very evenly, with the radio off.
