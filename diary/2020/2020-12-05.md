I nearly didn't do the cards this year. Then I thought about make do and mend, and about whose fridge my cards always ended up on first, and got the lino out.

This year it's a loaf. Hamish's loaf, the sun one, round with the slashes across the top, because that's what this year was here: bread and the stair and the walk.

## The block

Cut it in one evening at the kitchen table with the radio on low. The slashes were easy. The crumb, all the little holes, took the smallest gouge and three hours.

::media[Cutting the block on the kitchen table, the pencil lines still showing]{file="linocut-1"}

## The printing

Twenty-four prints in brown ink on cream card, each one pressed down by hand with the back of a wooden spoon, which is Nana's trick from the potato prints she did with us when we were small. Laid out to dry on every flat surface in the flat. Biscuit walked across three of them; those are for people who'll laugh.

Who gets one:

1. Mum and Dad
2. Clare and Dev
3. Priya
4. Callum
5. Mrs Ogilvie
6. Morag and Gerald

and eighteen more, the stair included. One goes on our own fridge, crooked, the way Nana always put them up.
