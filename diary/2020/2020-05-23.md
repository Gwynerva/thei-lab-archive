Day 62.

## It worked

Loaf twelve. I did everything I'd done for the other eleven, except that I built the :hint[levain]{note="a small batch of starter fed the night before, so that it peaks just when the dough needs it"} at ten last night instead of in the morning, used less water, and stopped poking it. It rose in the oven like a held breath let out. It split along the score exactly where I'd cut it. It sounded hollow when I knocked on the bottom, which I had always assumed was a thing people said, not a thing that happened.

::media[Loaf twelve, with a sun cut into the top because I was feeling hopeful]{file="sourdough-4"}

## What changed

1. The levain, overnight.
2. Seventy per cent water, not eighty. Hamish is strong; I was drowning him.
3. Hands off. The dough knows.

## Eating it

Marta and I ate half of it standing up in the kitchen with butter and salt before it had cooled, which every bread book says not to do. The other half went down to the joiner, in return for the flour. He sent back a note on the back of an envelope: *Better than the rolls.* High praise, from a man who measures twice.

Rang Callum to tell him. He made me hold the loaf up to the camera and turn it round slowly, then cut it and show him the inside, and then he said, "Aye. That's bread," which is the most Callum sentence there has ever been.
