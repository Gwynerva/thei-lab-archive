Walk 125, at the end of the road.

## Down the glen

Packed up the tent and took the car down Glen Etive at the speed of a bicycle, stopping whenever something needed walking to: a pool so green it looked dyed (paddled, didn't swim: I'm saving that for the sea), a waterfall, a whole herd of red deer on the slope above Dalness, watching me the way the Buachaille does.

## The loch

At the end the road just stops, at an old pier where boats used to come up from Oban, and Loch Etive carries on without it between mountains that drop straight into the water. Walk 125 was along the shore from there, as far as the path went and a bit further, three hours there and back with nobody else on it. I sat on a rock and ate everything I had left and drew the glen in the back of the map: the river as a blue thread, the tent as a triangle, the deer as a row of dots.

## Home

Drove home through Glen Coe with the windows down, eating crisps for dinner, and a rainbow stood across the glen the whole way through it.
