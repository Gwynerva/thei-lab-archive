Day 39.

The end of April, and thirty-eight walks. I've been drawing each one in the walk notebook, a little map a day, and tonight I traced all of them onto one sheet of baking paper laid over the city map. A tangle of lines round Leith like a ball of wool the cat's had, thicker where I keep going back (the Shore, always the Shore), with three long arms reaching out to the Meadows, Arthur's Seat and Granton. A map of a month in which I went nowhere.

Clapped at eight. The blackbird joined in.
