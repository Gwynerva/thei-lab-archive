Storm Jorge tomorrow, because of course. Tonight I learned to turn pages for the pianist, which is harder than it sounds: you stand when she nods, not when you think she'll nod, and you never, ever look at the audience. We practised at the kitchen table, Marta humming the piano part and nodding where the pianist would nod, Biscuit batting the corner of every page I turned.
