Day 16.

The biggest full moon of the year came up orange tonight, and the whole street, which has nothing else to do, was out on balconies photographing it with phones that can't photograph the moon. I ran tideline for Leith out of habit: a big spring tide tomorrow morning, the highest of the season. Everything I'd say about why is on [the page about the moon](<ref:page:how-tides-work#h:The moon> "the short version"), and nobody on the balconies asked.
