## Take eleven

The longest day of the year, and I spent it in the kitchen. I recorded the prelude today. Fridge off, window shut, Biscuit shut in the bedroom (protesting), Marta out on a long walk so that I couldn't hear her not listening. The phone on a pile of books on the kitchen table, the cello facing the tiles.

Take one: stopped at bar 9, because I sneezed. Take four: a bus. Take seven: lovely until bar 38, where my bow arm remembered it was being recorded. Take eleven: not perfect. All the way through, every note there, the pedal point ringing at the end the way it's meant to, and the last chord left to fade into the kitchen until the phone could only hear the fridge not humming.

## Eight months

I started it in October. Twenty-two bars by Christmas, and the rest through the winter and the whole of the lockdown: the box room, the mute, the knocking on the ceiling. It's two and a half minutes of music. It's eight months of evenings.

::media[The bridge and the strings, still warm]{file="cello-3"}

Sent it to Mrs Ogilvie; to Mum, to play down the phone to Nana; and to the couple downstairs, who have listened to it get better through the floor since March and deserve to hear how it ends.
