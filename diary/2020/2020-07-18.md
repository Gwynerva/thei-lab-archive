## Out

At ten this morning a 1984 Raleigh that somebody threw in a skip in May went down the stair on my shoulder, four flights, and out into Leith.

I rode it along the Water of Leith path to Warriston, out along the old railway line to Cramond, back along the prom at Silverknowes with the wind behind me, and home through Granton. Twenty-seven kilometres. **Nothing fell off.**

At Cramond I bought a cone from the kiosk and sat on the sea wall watching the tide come in over the causeway to the island, the bike leaning on the railing beside me. Two different people asked about it while I ate.

## What it's like

- The three-speed :hint[Sturmey-Archer]{note="the hub gear inside the back wheel, older than me"} hub ticks like a clock when you freewheel.
- The steel frame takes the bumps for you. My old aluminium bike handed every one of them straight up to me.
- People smile at it. A man at Cramond said his dad had the same one in green.
- The saddle needs another centimetre.

## What it cost

Free bike, then: tyres, tubes, cables, brake blocks, a chain, bar tape, the spokes and the bell. About ninety pounds, and maybe sixty hours since May, most of them with an old toothbrush and a tin of degreaser.

Three good things:

1. Nothing fell off.
2. The tailwind on the prom.
3. The man at Cramond and his dad's green one.
