## Up

Walk 142 started at half ten at night at Flotterstone, with Marta (the first time I've got her up a hill in a year) and a flask. We went up the path towards Turnhouse Hill just far enough to put the shoulder of the hill between us and the city's orange glow, and lay down on two bin bags in the heather.

## What we saw

Thirty-one meteors in an hour and a half. I counted out loud and Marta counted in Polish, and our numbers didn't agree, because she counted one that was a plane.

- The best one went right across from Cassiopeia towards the south and left a green trail that hung there for a whole second.
- Two came at once, side by side, like a pair of swifts.
- At about midnight a satellite went over, slowly, the opposite of a meteor, and we both watched it all the way across.

## Down

Walked down by torchlight, red lights only. A sheep coughed in the dark like an old man and Marta screamed. Home by two. The station says it was 11.4 degrees on the balcony; on the hill, lying still, it felt like nothing at all.
