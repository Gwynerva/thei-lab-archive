Burns Night. With Callum in Glasgow, [Callum's haggis](ref:event:burns-night-2017 "three Januarys ago, the first of them") is now a memory, and the job fell to me. Marta had never had haggis. I did it in foil in the big pan the way he showed us, with neeps and tatties and a dram I don't like but drink once a year, out of respect.

Marta's verdict: "like a sausage that went to university".
