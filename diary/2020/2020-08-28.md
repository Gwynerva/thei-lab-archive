Ines's last day at Forthline. The leaving do was a video call: twelve people in twelve kitchens raising twelve different drinks. Dougie made a speech and his camera froze halfway through with his mouth open, which was the best bit. She flies on Sunday.

:::private
I don't know why it's knocked me so hard. I think it's that she's going home and I can't think where mine is. Whitby is Mum and Dad's. Leith is a flat I rent with a cat in it. I've been here four years and it's lovely and it isn't the thing. I would like to want somewhere the way she wants Porto.
:::
