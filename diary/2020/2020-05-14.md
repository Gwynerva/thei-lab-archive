Day 53.

The bottom bracket came out. Five days of ~~WD-40~~ proper penetrating oil, a long spanner borrowed from the joiner, and Marta standing on the frame while I leaned on the spanner with my whole weight, and then a crack like a branch breaking and we both fell over. The bearings inside were brown sludge. I've never been so pleased to see sludge.

Clapped at eight with oily hands.
