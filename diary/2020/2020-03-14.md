## Twenty-eight

Twenty-eight, on Pi Day, which I mentioned to everyone, as I do. [Last year](ref:event:birthday-27 "twenty-seven, with a cat on my lap") it was a cat. This year it was a cake shaped roughly like π, made by Marta, leaning to the left. Ines left a cupcake on the doorstep with two candles in it, a 2 and an 8, and a note: *not π, sorry*.

Priya rang from Plymouth on a video call and sang, badly, with her flatmate doing harmonies. Mum and Dad rang separately because Dad won't do the speakerphone. Nana's card came with a ten-pound note folded inside it, as it has every year since I was born, "for something silly".

## Dinner

In the evening Marta and I went out for dinner in the Old Town, the little place down the close we always mean to go to. It was half empty, and the waiter was very kind and very nervous, and we had the good wine. Walking home past the lamps, I had the feeling you get on the last night of a holiday, though it wasn't the end of anything. Yet.
