Day 13.

## Sowing

The seeds came in the post on Thursday in a padded envelope that Biscuit tried to kill. Today I sowed the balcony: a bag of compost carried up four flights, the long planter that came with the flat, a stack of tins out of the recycling with holes punched in the bottom with a nail and a hammer.

What went in:

- tomatoes
  - Tigerella, because of the stripes
  - Gardener's Delight, because everyone says
- herbs
  - basil, twice, because basil always dies
  - parsley, the flat kind
- for the bees
  - nasturtiums
  - sweet peas up the railing, if they make it

::media[The tins in a row on the balcony floor, labelled in pencil]{file="seedlings-1"}

## Why

I've never grown anything on purpose. Nana grew everything: rowan, beans up the back fence, a rhubarb the size of an armchair. I think she'd say it was about time. It's also something to look at every morning that is changing for good reasons.

Mum held my photo of the tins up to Nana's window this afternoon, and Nana sent back, by way of Mum, a list of everything I'd done wrong: too deep, too wet, too early, wrong tins. Then, at the end of the list: *tell her well done*.

Walk 12: down to the Shore and back. Two swans on the water by the old warehouses, ignoring the whole thing.
