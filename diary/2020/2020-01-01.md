## Typed, not written

The first entry of the year, and the first one typed. The notebooks are on the shelf above the desk, spines gone soft, coffee rings on most of them. From today the diary is a plain text file on the laptop: the date, and then whatever happened. I'll miss the margins. I will probably go on drawing little maps of the day anyway, on a separate bit of paper, like someone who has lost the point and kept the habit.

## Last night

[Last night](ref:diary:2019-12-31) is in the last notebook, and can stay there. All I'll add is that I woke at eleven with glitter in one ear and no memory of how it got there, and that Biscuit had slept on my coat.

## Not this term

The pottery studio sent round the spring timetable and I didn't sign up. The lopsided mug is on the desk holding pens, which is what it was always for. I'll go back to clay when I have an evening that isn't already promised to something else. So: not soon.

## For 2020

- a Munro or six

Walked to Portobello and back in the afternoon to clear my head. The sea was grey and flat and full of people in fancy dress running into it and straight back out, screaming. A good start to a year, all things considered.
