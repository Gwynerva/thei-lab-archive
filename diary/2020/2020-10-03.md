Rain from before dawn till after dark: the station says 38 mm, the most in one day since it went online in June. The tail end of [Storm Alex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Alex "named in France, where it did its worst") sat over us all day, and the radio says it's the wettest day ever recorded across the whole country. Walk 194: twenty minutes, soaked to the knees, both of them.
