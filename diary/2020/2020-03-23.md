Day 1.

At half past eight tonight they told us: stay at home. One walk a day, food, medicine, work only if it can't be done from home. Nothing else. Marta and I watched it in the kitchen standing up, the way you watch the forecast before a long drive. Then we both texted everyone we know, and everyone we know texted back, and for an hour the phones didn't stop.

I don't know what to write. I'm going to write something every day anyway. Today: it was sunny. The tree in the back green came into blossom. Biscuit sat on the sill and watched a pigeon for two hours and caught nothing, which was the most normal thing that happened.
