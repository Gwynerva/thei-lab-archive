Day 37.

Walk 36: out after work to Holyrood Park and the long way round under the Crags. Arthur's Seat went gold in the last half-hour, the whole hill lit like the inside of a lamp, and a few hundred people stood around below it two metres apart, staring up in silence, like a congregation.
