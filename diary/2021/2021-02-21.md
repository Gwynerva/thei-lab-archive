Typed up letter seven tonight and had to stop halfway through and walk round the kitchen.

It's dated the fourteenth of March 1953, two days out of Bergen, and in among the cook's pudding and the mate and the mangle, Arthur writes about a star he'd once shown her from the deck, low in the Whale, faint that month, and how it always comes back bright. *When we have a girl we could do worse than call her after it.*

They had a girl ten years later and called her Helen. Then Helen had a second girl and called her Mira, and I always knew it was a star but I thought the star was Mum's idea. It was his. Nana carried it from 1953 to 1992 and handed it on like a parcel, and never once told me where it came from.

The fourteenth of March. My birthday. Thirty-nine years before I was born, to the day.
