## Approved

Ten past eleven, in the middle of a meeting: an email from the immigration people with a long reference number and one word in the subject line that I had to look up even though I knew it. **Innvilget.** Granted.

I said "excuse me" to the drainage engineers of a small council, muted myself, and walked round the kitchen table four times.

## What it means

It means the job is real, and [the move](ref:project:moving-to-bergen#:~:text=Bergen "the whole long business, from February on") is real. It means the boxes in the hall are going somewhere and not just sitting there being optimistic. It means I can stop being a person who is waiting and start being a person who is packing, which is worse for the back and better for everything else.
