The first proper walk into town: down the road by the water to [Bryggen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryggen "the old Hanseatic wharf, on a UNESCO list and on every postcard"), round Vågen to the fish market, and back up the hill. Twenty-five minutes each way, forty if you stop at every wooden gable, which I did. The houses at Bryggen lean on each other like old men at a bar.

::media[The red gables of Bryggen from the square, a small dog included at no extra charge]{file="bryggen-4"}

Mum rang. I told her about the gables, and she said *like Whitby*, and actually, yes: red roofs down to the water, gulls, a hill behind. Whitby, if it had been rained on for a thousand years.
