## What was inside

Nana's sewing box is wooden, varnished dark, with a lid that lifts, a tray that lifts out, and a little drawer underneath that sticks. I emptied it onto a tea towel this morning and made a list, which is what I do instead of crying:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. eleven wooden cotton reels, three of them older than Mum
2. a tin of buttons, sorted by nobody, ever
3. a tape measure in inches only, soft as a ribbon
4. three ring thimbles, one bent into the shape of her finger
5. a darning mushroom
6. a paper of needles from a shop in Scarborough that closed in 1979
7. a pincushion shaped like a strawberry, with eleven pins and a bus ticket in it
:::

::media[The ring thimbles, the bent one on the left]{file="thimble"}

## What's wrong with it

- the left hinge has torn out of the lid
- a split down one side, where the wood has dried out
- the drawer sticks, because its runner has swollen

## Today

Took the hinges off, which needed the smallest screwdriver I own and some language. The screws are steel in brass hinges and sixty years of damp have welded them in. One snapped. I'll have to drill it out, which feels like surgery on someone I love. Biscuit inspected the empty box and then slept in the tray all afternoon, which is a review of sorts.
