## Twenty-nine

Pi Day, and Mothering Sunday as well this year, which the calendar did on purpose. Marta woke me with coffee and a candle stuck in a sourdough loaf, because the bakery's shut and she says a cake is only a loaf that's been to finishing school. *Gratulerer med dagen*, she said, which she'd learned off the internet for me, and she put the stress in the wrong place in the most beautiful way.

::media[Marta's birthday loaf, a sun scored into the top]{file="sourdough-4"}

## Cards

Mum and Dad's card: a lighthouse, with "love from Mum and" in Mum's writing and "Dad" in Dad's. Clare's: a cat in a party hat, captioned in biro BISCUIT, 29. Priya sent a bottle of gin from a distillery near her lab and a note that says *open after the email*.

There isn't a card from Nana. Every year since I was born there's been a card from Nana, with a postal order inside and one line in pencil on the back of the envelope: *wrap up*. I knew there wouldn't be one. I checked the mat three times anyway.

:::private
Rang Mum for Mothering Sunday and we were both fine for about four minutes and then neither of us was. She'd bought a card for Nana in the supermarket on Thursday before she remembered. It's still in her handbag. I didn't tell her I'd checked the mat.
:::

## Letter seven

In the evening I read letter seven again, the star letter, because it's dated today. Then I went out onto the balcony and looked for the Whale, which isn't up in the evenings at this time of year. I knew that. I looked anyway.

Three good things:

1. Marta's sun loaf.
2. BISCUIT, 29.
3. The click of the sewing box lid.
