## Twelve minutes past three

It came at twelve minutes past three, in the middle of a call about drainage, so I read it with my camera on and had to arrange my face.

> We are happy to offer you the position of GIS developer in our charting team, starting in August, subject to your residence permit.

Then a page about salary and pension and holidays that I read four times and understood twice. Then *Velkommen*, from Anna, on a line on its own.

## Telling people

Marta screamed, then cried, then asked who'd get my room, in that order. Priya sent a row of whales. Clare rang from the hospital car park at the end of a shift and said "you daft thing, well done", and then had to go because someone was waving at her.

Then I rang Whitby. Mum was quiet for a second and then said all the right things, warmly and at length. Dad took the phone off her and said, "Bergen. Good harbour," and handed it back.
