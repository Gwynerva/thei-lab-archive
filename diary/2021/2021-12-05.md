Addressed forty envelopes at the kitchen table: Whitby, Leeds, Plymouth, Glasgow, Leith, Porto, Mrs Ogilvie in Morningside. The postage to Britain costs more than the card. Mum on the phone was all plans: the turkey's ordered, Clare's on nights on Boxing Day, Dad has put my old bed up in the back room. I said *lovely* five times and didn't say the other thing.
