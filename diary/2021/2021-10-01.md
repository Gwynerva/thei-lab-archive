The station's first Bergen month: 238 millimetres of rain in September, against 41 for last September on the Leith balcony. Twenty-two days with something in the gauge. Humidity never once below seventy. I made a graph and took it down to Sverre, who looked at it for a long time and then said *ja, det stemmer*: yes, that's about right.
