## Priya

Priya came up from Plymouth for the weekend, the first time I've seen her in the flesh since the Christmas before last. Nine hours on trains, two changes, and she got off at Waverley in shorts and a fleece, as if she'd dressed for both ends of the country at once. We hugged on the platform for long enough that a man with a suitcase had to go round us.

She slept in my old room, the little one with the skylight, which since she left has been my study and, lately, the plotter's workshop. She looked at the plotter for a long time and said, "You've built yourself a friend," which is rude and accurate.

## The Shore

We walked down to the Shore in the evening and sat outside with chips, coats on, because it's May in Leith. She's counting seabirds off Devon from a boat that smells of diesel, and loves it, and talks about kittiwakes the way other people talk about their children.

### Lofoten

When the chips were gone she said, "When you're in Norway I'm coming, and we're going north in winter to see the lights." I said Lofoten is further from Bergen than Bergen is from here. She said, "Good. Then it's a proper trip." We shook on it, with vinegary hands.

## Late

Up till two on the balcony in blankets, talking about everything: her lab, my permit, Nana, Callum's new oven, whether either of us will ever own a house. Biscuit slept on her feet all night, which he has never once done for me.
