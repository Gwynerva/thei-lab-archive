Six of us from the orchestra played in Pilrig Park this afternoon, spaced out in a wide ring on the grass, Agnes conducting from the middle with a wooden spoon because she'd left her baton at home. The Elgar Serenade, the first movement, which we haven't played together since February last year. We were terrible. Dog walkers stopped anyway. Practice log: 50 minutes, including the Elgar, including the bit where I came in a bar early in front of a spaniel.
