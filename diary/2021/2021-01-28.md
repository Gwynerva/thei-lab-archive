Weather words this week, which I learned faster than anything else so far:

- *regn*, rain
- *yr*, drizzle, and also the name of the national forecast, which tells you a lot
- *sludd*, sleet; it sounds like what it is
- *pøsregn*, rain that comes down in bucketfuls
- *oppholdsvær*, a dry spell, literally "pause weather", as if rain were the normal state and dry a break from it

I've started reading [the Norwegian forecast](https://www.met.no "for Bergen, for no reason at all, every morning") with my coffee. The teacher says there are more words for rain in the west than she can teach us in one term, so I'm collecting them [in one place](<ref:page:norwegian-words#h:Weather> "where they all ended up").
