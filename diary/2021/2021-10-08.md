Went to Grieghallen on my own to hear the city's orchestra: Elgar, and something Norwegian I'd never heard before and now can't stop humming. Sat high up at the back, where you can see all the bows move together like grass in wind. Came home and practised for an hour, which I haven't done since Leith. I want to be in one again.
