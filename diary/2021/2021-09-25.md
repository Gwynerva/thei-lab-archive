## The room

Eight months of Norwegian on a laptop, and today, a room: a classroom above a shop in the centre, radiators that clank, a whiteboard, a window onto the rain. Fourteen of us at tables pushed together: an electrician from Gdańsk, two sisters from Aleppo, a nurse from Manila, an American who came for love, an engineer from Bilbao, a man from Asmara who already speaks five languages and is cheerful about the sixth, and me. Our teacher writes faster than she talks and draws little pictures for the words we don't know yet.

## A2

Last term, online, was A1: hello, my name is, I live in, the cat is ginger. A2 is the past tense, and getting things wrong in front of real people, who laugh, and then get the next one wrong themselves. The best moment: the man from Asmara and I both said *jeg har bodd her i to måneder*, I have lived here for two months, at the same time, and the teacher said *så dere er tvillinger*, so you're twins.

## Four o'clock

At four the country opened. In the centre people stood in the street with glasses in their hands as if they'd forgotten how bars work. Two from the class and I had my first *utepils*, the outdoor beer, at a table outside on Bryggen, at eleven degrees, in our coats, and nobody thought that was odd at all.
