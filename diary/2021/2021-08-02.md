Anna, my team lead, whom I have only ever seen from the shoulders up, left a bag on the step this morning and texted from the pavement so I could wave at her through the glass. In it: bread, *knekkebrød*, butter, coffee, apples, a cinnamon bun the size of my face, a block of brown cheese, and a card that said *Velkommen til Bergen!* with a drawing of an umbrella.

::media[Brunost on the board with the knife Anna lent me; I'm told this is breakfast]{file="brunost-2"}

New words, day two:

- Food
  - *brunost*: brown cheese, sweet, like fudge that's been told off
  - *pålegg*: anything you put on a slice of bread, a category Norwegians take seriously
  - *knekkebrød*: crispbread, which is mostly seeds
- Being polite
  - *tusen takk*: a thousand thanks
  - *takk for maten*: thanks for the food, said to whoever cooked, even if it was you
