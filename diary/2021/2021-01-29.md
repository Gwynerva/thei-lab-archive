Friday. Ten thousand steps without going further than the far side of Pilrig Park. Three good things:

1. A blue sky at four o'clock, the first in a week.
2. Marta practising the slow movement of something Czech through the wall, beautifully.
3. Biscuit asleep on the radiator cover like a loaf left to prove.
