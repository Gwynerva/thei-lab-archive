Mistakes I'm fond of, week one of A2:

- I asked at the bakery for a *kanelbolle* with *kjærlighet*, with love, when I meant *kardemomme*. She gave me one anyway, and a smile.
- I told Anna I was *glad i* the new chart, which is what you say about people you love.
- I asked Sverre if he was *sulten*, hungry, when I meant *sliten*, tired. He said he was both, and thank you for noticing.
