Biscuit has found the kitchen window, the one with the slot of fjord, and has decided it's a television showing gulls. He sat there from six this morning chattering at them through the glass. The gulls in Bergen are bigger than the gulls in Leith, and they know it.

::media[Biscuit on the kitchen floor in Sandviken, looking up as if to say: and the gulls?]{file="ginger-cat-1"}

The bed came at four in a van from the big shop out at Åsane, and I built it with an Allen key and Sverre's opinions.
