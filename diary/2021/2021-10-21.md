Quilting after dinner. I've reached the place on the border where Nana's own stitches stop, in the middle of a row: a needle's length of her thread, then mine. I measured her stitches with a ruler and tried to match them and still can't. She'd have laughed at the ruler.

> A stitch in time is still a stitch.

She said that whenever I fussed about the size of mine. Four weeks to go.
