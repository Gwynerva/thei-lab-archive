Longest day. Walked up Calton Hill at half nine with Marta and a flask, and the sun didn't so much set as slide sideways along the top of the Pentlands for an hour. Still light enough to read at eleven. In Bergen tonight it hardly gets dark at all, I'm told: something to look forward to, and something to buy curtains for.
