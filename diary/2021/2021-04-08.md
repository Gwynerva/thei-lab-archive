Walked to Portobello now that we're allowed to go further than the end of the street: along the prom and back by the Links, nine miles, cold sun, a north-easterly that went straight through my jumper. The sea was the colour of a slate roof and three people were swimming in it, shrieking. I swam there in August and felt brave. In April I watched and felt sensible.
