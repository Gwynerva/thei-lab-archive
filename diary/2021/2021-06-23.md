Couldn't sleep. Lay awake making a list of the people I'm leaving and then a list of the reasons, and the second list kept getting shorter at three in the morning and longer again by five, which I've learned is just what three in the morning does.

Marta heard me moving about and came and sat on the end of the bed with Biscuit and said, "You know you can come back. It isn't a prison. It's a ferry." Then she went back to bed. I slept until nine.
