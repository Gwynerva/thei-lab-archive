The first Sunday call from Norway. Dad's first question was about the tides. I had to tell him Bergen gets about a metre on an ordinary day, a bit more on a spring tide, and he went quiet the way he does when someone brings a rope into the shop that's been cut instead of spliced. *Whitby gets nearly five*, he said. I know, Dad. Mum wanted to know if I'd packed enough jumpers.
