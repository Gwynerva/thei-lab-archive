Printing day on the kitchen floor: newspaper everywhere, the ink rolled out on the glass from the old picture frame, forty cards drying on the bed, the floor, the chairs and the top of the fridge, and a smear of red on Biscuit's left ear that he will not let me near.

If you're getting a card, look away now.
