Sunday. Quilted for two hours in the evening with the hoop on my knee and the radio on in a language I mostly don't understand, which turns out to be restful. Nana's quilt is three-quarters done: the border is the last of it. The stitches she left in the corner still have her knots at the ends, small and hard, like seeds. I'm aiming for November. Mum asked how it's coming on and didn't ask anything else, which is how we talk about Nana.
