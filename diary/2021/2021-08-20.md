The van from Leith came up the lane at two, backwards, with two drivers shouting directions at each other in English, their only shared language. Forty-three boxes up three flights of stairs, the Raleigh, and the cello last, in its case, in its two duvets.

I opened the case on the kitchen floor before I'd even signed for it. The strings were in tune, nearly, the bridge was still standing, and she smelled of the Leith flat. I played an open C and Biscuit left the room.

::media[The cello out of its duvets on the kitchen floor, the bridge still up]{file="cello-1"}

Home is the cello and the cat and the quilt in the same place, it turns out. Now they are.
