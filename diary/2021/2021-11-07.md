Booked the flights for Christmas: Bergen, Amsterdam, Manchester, and Dad in the car at the other end. Mum is already counting the days.

In the evening I typed out another letter from Nana's tin, one from Grandad Arthur in 1953, written on a ship somewhere hot. He tells her the stars are upside down where he is, and that he's found one in the Whale that comes and goes, and that he's saving it for her.
