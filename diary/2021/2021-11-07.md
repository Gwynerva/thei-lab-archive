Booked the flights for Christmas: Bergen, Amsterdam, Manchester, and Dad in the car at the other end. Mum is already counting the days.

In the evening I read the star letter from Nana's tin again, the one from Grandad Arthur in 1953, two days out of Bergen. He tells her he's found the star in the Whale that comes and goes, faint that month, and that it always comes back bright.
