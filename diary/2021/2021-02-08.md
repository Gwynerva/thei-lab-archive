## Decided

**I'm going to apply.** Wrote it on a sticky note and stuck it on the laptop lid, so that I can't open the laptop without agreeing with myself.

## For

1. The job is charts. Real ones, of small harbours, the kind of thing Dad pins on the shop wall.
2. I've been in Leith four and a half years, and lately I walk the same seven streets and know every crack in them.
3. Arthur's sixth letter. I know it isn't a reason. It isn't *not* a reason.

## Against

- Marta, and the flat, and Biscuit's windowsill.
- Mum, who has just lost her mother, and whose daughter would then be on the other side of the sea.
- Mrs O, the orchestra, Portobello, the Shore at half past four on a winter afternoon.
- I don't speak Norwegian. I can say that I'm called Mira and that the cat is on the fridge.

:::private
I haven't told Mum. Not because she'd say no. She'd say yes too fast, and mean it, and then go and stand in the garden for a long time where nobody can hear her. I'll tell her when there's something to tell.
:::

Marta says I made up my mind in December and have been waiting to be told. She's usually right, which is an annoying quality in a flatmate. Class tonight was the days of the week; I got through all of them without once thinking about Bergen, apart from on *torsdag*.
