Sunday call. Told Mum the quilt came safe and that Dad's note made me laugh out loud in the hall. She asked if I knew how to do the stitches. I said I'd learn, and she said "that's what she'd have said", and then talked about the weather in Whitby for twenty minutes, which is how this family says the other thing.
