Friday beer with the department in a place on Bryggen with low ceilings and lower beams. I hit my head twice and was told that makes me a Bergenser. Walked home along the harbour after dark just as the coastal ship pulled out for the north, and it gave three long blasts that went round the mountains and came back. I caught the end of it.
