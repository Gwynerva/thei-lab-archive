Rain: day 12. Unpacked eleven boxes and found the cello a corner, by the kitchen window, where the light comes in sideways off the fjord. Practice log: twenty-five minutes, scales and the first page of the prelude, out of tune with myself. Halfway through, a knock: Sverre, from two floors down, who'd come up to say he could hear it in his kitchen and would I please play more. *Spill mer.*
