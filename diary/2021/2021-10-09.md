Løvstakken, alone, on a hazy Saturday: up from the south side through the trees, an hour and a bit, to a cairn with a weather vane on top that turned in no wind at all. The city looks different from this side, Fløyen and Ulriken over there where they belong, and the whole of Bergen in between like a model of itself.

::media[The cairn and its weather vane on Løvstakken, the sun right behind it in the haze]{file="lovstakken-2" layout="natural"}

The count, then:

3. Løvstakken, 9 October, in haze.
4. Damsgårdsfjellet, next door; soon, maybe.
5. Lyderhorn, the one across the water; in spring.

Four to go, counting Fløyen and Ulriken as done, which I do.
