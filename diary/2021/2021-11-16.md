Rain: day 71. The electricity bill came, nearly three times what the landlady told me to expect, because the price in the south of the country has gone mad this autumn and nobody can tell me why except *the reservoirs* and *Europe*. I've turned the heating down to sixteen and wear the sea-green coat indoors. Biscuit sleeps on the router, which is warm. I did the numbers twice. The Christmas flights are already paid for; that's the main thing. Don't tell Mum.
