Opened Forthline's present with a clear head: a 1935 pictorial map of Edinburgh and Leith, framed, every church drawn standing up and the Firth across the top like a sky. Our street is on it, and the Shore, and the docks where the office is now. It's going to Bergen in my hand luggage, not in a box.
