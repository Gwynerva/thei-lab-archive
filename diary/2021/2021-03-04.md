## Before

Up at six. Ironed the shirt, top half only, as promised. Shut Biscuit in the bedroom with a bribe. Wrote four things to say if my mind went blank on a card and propped it against the kettle, out of shot. Then *unnskyld, norsken min er veldig dårlig* forty times to the bathroom mirror.

## The hour

Two of them on the call. Anna Solheim, who leads the team, in a room with a window full of grey water and a crane, and an older man with a beard who does the depths and said almost nothing until he asked how I'd know that a sounding was wrong. I said: look at its neighbours, then look at the tide, then look at who measured it. He nodded once, slowly, like a gate opening.

Then Anna said she'd been reading tideline. She'd found it last year looking for something to check their tidal reductions against, and she had questions about how I'd picked the constituents, and for twenty minutes I forgot it was an interview and talked about the moon.

At the end I said my sentence. She laughed and said, in English, "It's better than my Scottish."

## After

Lay on the living-room floor for a while. Marta brought me a cup of tea and put it down on the carpet next to my head without a word, which is love. They'll write within two weeks.
