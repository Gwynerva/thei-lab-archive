The tax office at nine for the ID check: a queue of people from twenty countries holding the same folder of papers, and a woman behind glass who looked at my passport, looked at me, and said *velkommen* as if she meant it. The number comes in the post. Then the bank, which needs the number. Then BankID, which needs the bank. Norway is a beautiful circle.
