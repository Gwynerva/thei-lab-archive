New word: *dugnad*. Everyone in a building or a street turning out on a Saturday to do a job together for nothing, clear the gutters, paint the fence, sweep the stair, and then coffee and waffles after. The teacher said the whole country called the first lockdown a *dugnad*. Our stair would never. Our stair can't agree whose turn it is with the bins.
