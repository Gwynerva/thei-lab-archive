Coffee at Sverre's. In his hall hangs a round brass barometer from a Swedish ship he once served on, and he taps it every morning on his way out to the shop, twice, like knocking on a door. Over the coffee he gave me Bergen weather in three rules:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. If you can see Askøy, it's going to rain.
2. If you can't see Askøy, it's raining.
3. Wind from the south-west is rain, from the north it's cold rain, and from the east you take a picture, because it won't last.
:::
