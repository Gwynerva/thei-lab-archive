Snow! Proper snow, the squeaky kind, a hand deep on the balcony rail by breakfast. Walked up Calton Hill in it, which counts as local if you squint. Edinburgh looked as if someone had dusted it for fingerprints.

::media[Roofs and trees gone white across the south side, a spire sticking up out of it all]{file="edinburgh-snow-2" layout="stretch"}

Rang Mum after. It was raining in Whitby. She was delighted for me in the tone of someone who would rather have rain.
