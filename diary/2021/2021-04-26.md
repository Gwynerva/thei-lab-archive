Cried in the shower this morning about leaving Leith, which hasn't even happened yet. Pre-crying. Getting ahead of things. Places opened again today, and I walked past the one on the corner of the Shore with the green chairs, where Priya and I used to sit on Sundays, and there were people at the tables in their coats, and it was so ordinary that I had to turn round and walk the other way.

Everyone keeps saying how brave. It doesn't feel brave. It feels like jumping off the harbour wall at Whitby when I was eleven: you don't decide, you just find you've already gone.
