First stitches in the quilt. I took Nana's needle out of block 18 at last, because it was that or never start, and it lives in a matchbox now with NANA'S written on the lid. Rocking stitch, learned from a video: the needle goes in upright and you rock it, three or four little bites at a time, a thimble pushing from underneath. Nana's run nine stitches to the inch. Mine run five, and they wander.

> A stitch in time is still a stitch.

She said it about everything from hems to marriages. I'm choosing to read it as permission.
