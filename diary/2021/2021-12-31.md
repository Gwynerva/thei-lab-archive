## The hill

Half of Sandviken goes up to Fjellveien on New Year's Eve, it turns out, because from there you can see the whole city. Sverre, me, a thermos of *gløgg* made to a recipe he won't tell me, and two folding chairs from his cellar, carried up the steps at half past eleven. A little snow had come in the evening, the first that stayed, and the roofs below us were white.

## Midnight

At midnight the whole bowl of the city went up at once: rockets from every garden and balcony and street, the boats in Vågen sounding their horns, smoke drifting over the harbour, coloured from underneath. It went on for half an hour. Sverre said *godt nytt år* and shook my hand, formally, like a captain to his mate.

## The year

I began it in a lockdown in Leith with a quilt on my knee and a Norwegian lesson on a laptop, and I'm ending it on a mountain in Norway with a retired ship's mate and a thermos. Three good things, one last time this year:

1. Thirty-four, in fog.
2. Biscuit's face through the grille at the cargo hall.
3. The last stitch.

*Godt nytt år.*
