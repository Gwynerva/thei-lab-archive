Mum rang at lunch from Nana's cottage. Clare says she isn't meant to be there on her own, not with everything shut again, but Mum says it's her mother's house and the virus can take it up with her. She's been doing the wardrobe. She found the quilt in the back bedroom, still on its frame, the needle parked in the last block where Nana left it in November.

She asked if I wanted it. I said yes before she'd finished the sentence. Then neither of us said anything for a bit, and that was all right.
