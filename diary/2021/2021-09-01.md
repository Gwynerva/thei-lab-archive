The weather station is up again, on a bracket outside the kitchen window that Sverre helped me fix to the wall with a drill older than I am. Readings every ten minutes, as before. The first Bergen number, at ten past seven this evening: 12.8 °C, 1004 hPa, humidity 94%. Ninety-four. Sverre went downstairs, tapped his barometer, and came back up to say it agreed.
