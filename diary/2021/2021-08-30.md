Rain: day 17. A week ago I was standing on [Fløyen](ref:event:floyen) in sunshine looking at forty islands. Today the top of it has gone, cut off flat by a lid of cloud at about two hundred metres, and from the office window I watched the funicular go up into it and not come out. Anna says this is what it looks like most of the year, and I'd better enjoy the mountain when it's there.
