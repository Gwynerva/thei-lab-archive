The West Highland Line to Fort William with Marta, her violin left at home for once and a paper bag of tablet in its place. Rannoch Moor out of the window for an hour: brown, flat, shining with water, and not one house. We're in a hostel in Glen Nevis with eleven Germans and a forecast that says *cloud on the tops*. Tomorrow is the last one, the one I saved.
