Callum came through from Glasgow on the train with a cake box from *The Wee Oven* on his knees the whole way: cardamom buns, a lemon tart, and a haggis bridie for old times' sake. The three of us ate it on the floor among the boxes, Priya on the laptop propped against the one marked BOOKS (HEAVY), and for an hour it was 2017 again. Callum wants a postcard from every mountain in Bergen. I said there are seven. He said *good, that's seven postcards*.
