Saturday: the fish market, early. Bought nothing raw, because I don't trust myself with a whole fish yet, and had fish soup at the counter instead, creamy, with salmon and dill, the window steamed up so the harbour outside was only colours. The man at the stall heard my accent and switched to English, and when I said *nei, norsk, takk* he switched back and talked to me very slowly about cod for ten minutes.
