*Romjul*: the days between Christmas and New Year, when nobody works and nobody knows what day it is. Too much time to think. I've been asking myself whether I did the right thing: the move, the job, the sea between me and all of them. The answer today, at the kitchen window, with the rain going sideways and Biscuit on the sill: yes. Mostly yes. Ask me again in the spring.
