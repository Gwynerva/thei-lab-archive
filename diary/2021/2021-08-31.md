End of August, and my first pay in kroner landed in a bank account that exists now, with a BankID that exists now. I bought a raincoat, a proper one, with a peak to the hood, in a colour the shop called *sea green* and Biscuit calls an outrage.

:::private
The numbers, honestly. The salary looks enormous until the tax takes its bite and the rent takes another, and a pint here costs what dinner cost in Leith. The deposit on the flat was three months' rent and sits in an account I can't touch until I leave. The relocation money comes in September. I made a spreadsheet, and it says I'll be all right by Christmas if I don't buy anything I can't eat.
:::
