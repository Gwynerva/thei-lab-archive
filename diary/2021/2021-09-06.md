Anna showed me the chart drawer: the paper charts the company made before everything was a database, harbours drawn by hand in ink in the seventies, with soundings in *favner*, fathoms, on the oldest ones. Someone drew every rock with a tiny cross and every cross is exactly the same size. I want to frame one. Anna says everyone who sees the drawer says that.
