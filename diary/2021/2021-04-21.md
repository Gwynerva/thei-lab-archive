The question of Hamish is settled. People do take starters abroad, dried, in envelopes. But Hamish has lived on the same shelf above the same radiator for a year and three weeks, he has opinions about flour, and Marta has asked for him. So Hamish stays in Leith. Not even a spoonful comes with me. A clean break; he'd hate Norwegian flour, and he'd say so.
