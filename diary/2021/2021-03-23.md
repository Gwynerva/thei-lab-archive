Told Dougie on the morning call, before the others joined. He was quiet for long enough that I thought the call had frozen, and then he said, "Norway's lucky," and asked if I'd stay till the middle of July to see the council job out. Yes. Of course yes. He started the company in a spare room with one plotter and a kettle; I've been there three and a half years; I owe him a finished job.
