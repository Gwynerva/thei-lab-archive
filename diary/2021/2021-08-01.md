Edinburgh, 06.10.\
Amsterdam, 10.35.\
Bergen, 12.15.

## Coming in

The plane came down over islands, hundreds of them, bare rock and heather and a white house on every third one, and then the land folded itself up into mountains with the sea running in between, and there was the city in the bottom of a green bowl. The man next to me, Norwegian and going home, said *you're lucky, it's not raining*, in the voice of someone saying *you're lucky, the bear's asleep*.

## The room

Anna found me the quarantine place: a studio on Nordnes, one room at the top of a painted wooden staircase, a bed, a table, two rings to cook on, and a window. The taxi driver carried my suitcase up without being asked. The quilt was on the bed within five minutes; I couldn't not.

## Ten days

Ten days in here before I'm allowed out. Food comes from a shop that delivers, and from Anna, who has promised *a bag*. I've brought: the quilt and its hoop, two novels, the Norwegian textbook, the laptop, the 1935 map of Leith, and a great deal of time.

::media[Where I am now, for Mum: Bergen is the dot on the left-hand edge, nearly in the sea]{file="map-norway-1"}

Rain: day 1, from about six, as if it had been waiting for me to land. Everybody says this is normal. At half past ten at night it is still light enough to read by the window.
