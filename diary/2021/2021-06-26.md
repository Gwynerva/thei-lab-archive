Last rehearsal with the Leith Scratch Orchestra, outdoors in the garden behind the church hall, twenty of us spread over the grass like a seating plan for a wedding. We played the Elgar from start to finish without stopping for the first time since February last year. Agnes made a speech that was mostly about my rhythm in 2018 and ended by saying the orchestra would keep my chair for me, which is a lie, and a kind one.

They gave me a card signed by everyone and a cake of rosin in a tin with a map of Norway drawn on the lid in biro, Bergen marked with a cello.
