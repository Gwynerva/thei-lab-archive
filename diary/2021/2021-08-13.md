Biscuit landed at Flesland at twenty past two, in a crate, in the cargo hall, after a morning in Amsterdam doing who knows what. The man from the pet transport company wheeled him out on a trolley; a vet read his chip, looked at his papers, stamped something; and then I was allowed to put my fingers through the grille, and he bit them, gently, which is how he says hello.

::media[The travel crate on the counter at the cargo hall, stickers all over it: live animal, this way up]{file="cat-carrier"}

He didn't say a word in the taxi the whole way along the motorway, which was worse than yowling. At home he came out of the crate slowly, walked round every wall of both rooms, sniffed Sverre's chairs, and fell asleep on my lap on the air mattress.
