Day two. I learned where the coffee is, the code for the printer, and that the man who sits opposite me has sailed to Shetland four times and thinks Lerwick has the best fish and chips in the North Sea. Spent the afternoon reading soundings round Fedje, thousands of little depths in metres and decimetres, and it felt like being handed someone's handwriting.
