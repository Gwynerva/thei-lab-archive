Sunday call. Dad was watching the football in the front room and shouting through the wall, so Mum gave up and put me on speaker in the kitchen next to the kettle. She asked how far Bergen is from Whitby *as the crow flies*. I looked it up: about 750 kilometres, straight across the North Sea. Further than Cornwall, but not by as much as you'd think. She didn't believe me, so I'm going to draw it for her.
