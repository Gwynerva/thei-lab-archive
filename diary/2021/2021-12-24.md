## Five o'clock

Christmas here is the twenty-fourth, and it begins at five, when every church in the country rings Christmas in at once. Anna's husband fetched me at four in a car that smelled of oranges, and we drove out past the airport road and arrived just as the bells started, coming over the dark fields from two directions.

## The table

Anna's mother made :hint[pinnekjøtt]{note="salted, dried lamb ribs, steamed for hours over birch sticks; the name means stick meat"}, which she has made every Christmas since 1974 and will not discuss. Ribs on a bed of birch sticks in a big pot, steaming since the morning, with *kålrabistappe*, mashed swede, and potatoes and sausages and the fat from the pot poured over everything, and Christmas beer, and a small glass of aquavit that I was told to drink *slowly* and didn't.

::media[Pinnekjøtt on the red tablecloth, with swede and a potato and a glass of Christmas beer behind]{file="pinnekjott-2"}

Then *riskrem*, cold rice pudding with cream and a red berry sauce, with one almond hidden somewhere in the bowl. Whoever finds it gets a marzipan pig. There was a great deal of very slow chewing.

## The tree

After dinner everyone held hands and walked round the Christmas tree in a ring, singing, the boys dragging us faster with every verse. I didn't know the words and sang anyway, and Anna's mother squeezed my hand at the end of each song as if to say *close enough*.

## Presents

The boys loved their maps; the elder has already found a mistake in his (the lamp post is on the wrong side of the road; he's right). Anna held her spoon up to the light for a long time without saying anything. Her mother put the rowan jelly straight on the table, next to the lingonberries, as if it had always been there. I was given a pair of red mittens with a pattern of stars, knitted by Anna's mother, who says they are for *Bergen hands*.

## Home

A taxi home at one, the city completely still, wet and lit. I'd thought this would be the worst Christmas of my life. It was a good one, just a different one. Biscuit was asleep on the quilt.
