Saturday. Marta and I sat on the balcony among the tomatoes with our feet up on the rail and made a list of everything we have to do together before the end of July: Portobello at dawn, a last fish supper from the place on the Shore, the Botanics, one Bach each, to each other, badly. We did that one tonight. She played a partita, I played the first suite, and Biscuit left in protest halfway through both.
