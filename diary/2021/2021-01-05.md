Lockdown again, from today. It's less of a shock this time, more like putting on a coat that's still wet from yesterday. Marta has moved her music stand back into the hall, where it lived most of last spring, and I've reclaimed the end of the kitchen table that faces the window.

Walked up the Water of Leith as far as Canonmills and a bit beyond. The river was brown and high and in a tremendous hurry, as if it had somewhere to be.
