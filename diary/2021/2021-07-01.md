Took the weather station off the balcony rail this evening. The last Leith reading, at 21.40: 14.2 °C, 1012 hPa, humidity 81%, a breath of wind from the west. A year and two weeks of numbers every ten minutes, and now it's a Raspberry Pi in a shoebox with the sensors wrapped in a tea towel.

::media[The Pi on the kitchen table, unplugged from the balcony and wired to the old monitor for one last look at its logs]{file="raspberry-pi-3"}

The balcony looks bald without it. Biscuit sat in the gap where the mast was and looked at me as if I'd switched off his television.
