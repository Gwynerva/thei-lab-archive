Mrs Ogilvie came up all four flights for tea this afternoon, which at her age is a statement. She brought a new cake of rosin in a tin and a card in her fierce slanting hand:

> Don't you dare stop. Find an orchestra by Christmas.

Three and a half years of Tuesdays. I played her the first bars of the prelude and she corrected my bow hold in the middle of it without a word, two fingers on my wrist. I'll miss that more than I can say.
