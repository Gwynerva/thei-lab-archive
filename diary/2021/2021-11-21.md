A year ago today Nana died. Today I finished [the quilt](ref:project:nana-quilt "a year of evenings") she left on her frame.

## A year of evenings

She'd pieced it all, stars on cream, out of Grandad's shirts and Mum's old dresses and a curtain I remember from the Bay, and quilted a third of it before her hands stopped letting her. I started on the ninth of January, in the second lockdown, with her hoop and her thimble and her tin of needles. It came with me in my hand luggage to the room at Nøstet and up the stairs of the white house, and I've done a little nearly every evening since, with the radio on, or the rain.

::media[Not Nana's quilt, which stays off the internet, but stars on cream like hers]{file="quilt-3"}

## The last stitch

At four o'clock, with the light going over the fjord, I tied the last knot the way she taught me, small and hard, and cut the thread with the orange-handled scissors from her sewing box.

## The label

A label from a scrap of the backing, stitched on in the bottom corner where she always put hers.

:::private
It says who began it and who finished it, and the three places it was sewn in, and under that her four words, in my best backstitch: *make do and mend*.
:::

## After

I rang Mum. Neither of us said very much, and that was right. Then I spread it on the bed and Biscuit walked straight into the middle of it and lay down, as if he'd been waiting a year.
