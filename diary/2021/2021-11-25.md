The Christmas lights went up in town: strings of white over Torgallmenningen and a tree on the square that [last week's storm](ref:event:first-bergen-storm#:~:text=wind) would have had over in a minute. Everyone is suddenly saying *jul* about everything: *julebord*, *julegaver*, *juleøl*, *julestress*. New word, my favourite so far: *julestria*, the Christmas rush, which sounds like an illness and is one.
