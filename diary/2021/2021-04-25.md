Read Mum letter seven on the phone tonight, the star letter. She was quiet for so long I thought we'd been cut off. Then she said, "I never knew he said that," and then, "She told me it was her idea," and then she laughed, properly, for the first time since November. "You'd better keep that one," she said. "It's yours."
