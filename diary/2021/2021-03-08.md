Started a pen plotter on Saturday and haven't written it down yet, so: a pen plotter. A machine that holds a pen and draws whatever the computer tells it, line by line, slowly. I want it to draw contours with a real fountain pen on real paper, so that a map comes out looking drawn rather than printed. And I want something to do with my hands that isn't waiting for an email.

The parts so far:

- two stepper motors
  - out of a dead office printer Dougie let me collect from the empty office
  - one of them squeaks, and I've grown fond of it
- a small servo, to lift the pen
- two aluminium rails and a pair of drawer runners
- the fountain pen Mum gave me at graduation, fine nib
- a Raspberry Pi from the weather station's spares box
