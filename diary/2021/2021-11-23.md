The flat has been very quiet since Sunday. I sleep under the quilt now, and twice I've woken thinking I was at the Bay, in the little back bedroom with the rowan tapping on the window. I keep thinking of things to tell her: that Bergen has a gingerbread town, that Sverre ties his bins to the railing in a storm, that I can say *takk for maten* without thinking. I'm writing them here instead. Maybe that's what this is for.
