Norway's national day. Our teacher came to the evening class in her :hint[*bunad*]{note="the folk costume, different for every valley; hers is from Hardanger, where her mother's family grew apples"}: a black skirt, a red bodice, a white apron with white embroidery, beads across the front. She made us sing the national anthem from a sheet, badly, microphones off, and then said *hurra for syttende mai* and meant it. Next year I'll see it for real.

Rules eased here today too. Six of us from three households can sit in a kitchen together again, a sentence that would have made no sense two years ago.
