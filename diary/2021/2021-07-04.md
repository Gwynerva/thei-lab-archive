Legs like wet rope. The train home through rain, Marta asleep on my shoulder from Crianlarich to Queen Street. In the evening I opened the Munro map and coloured Ben Nevis, the last grey triangle that was ever going to turn green. Thirty-four of 282. It can stay like that now: a map of five years, not of a list.

Mum rang at seven and asked if I'd *done the big one*. I said yes. She said *well, that's that then*, which is exactly what she said when I handed in the dissertation.
