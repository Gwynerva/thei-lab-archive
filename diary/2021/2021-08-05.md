A bad day. Nothing happened, which was the problem. I counted the floorboards (nineteen across, twenty-six long), read the same page of the textbook four times, and cried in the shower because the shower is the only place in here with a door. What have I done: a new job in a language I can't speak, in a city I've seen from one window, and the cat in another country.

Then the cattery sent a video of Biscuit asleep in a cardboard box with all four legs in the air, and I laughed so hard someone knocked on the wall. Day five of ten. Halfway.
