Biscuit's travel crate arrived: hard plastic, air holes, a metal grille for a door, and a sheet of stickers for the day itself, LIVE ANIMAL and THIS WAY UP. I left it open on the living-room floor as the vet suggested. He walked in, turned round twice and went to sleep, which the internet says never happens.
