*Dugnad*. Eleven people from the white house and the two next door, one ladder, four rakes and a roll of black bags. I cleared the gutters of what looked like a whole year of the mountain: needles, moss, a tennis ball, one very surprised frog. Sverre supervised from a kitchen chair on the steps and at twelve made waffles with brown cheese for everyone. Nobody would let me bring anything. I think I'm a neighbour now.
