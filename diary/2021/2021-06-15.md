## Biscuit's papers

The vet read his microchip (it works; he objected), gave him his rabies jab (he objected more) and started a folder she called his passport, though since January it isn't officially a passport at all but a certificate with a longer name. To me it's a passport. He's emigrating.

## Nana's birthday

It's Nana's birthday too. She'd have been ninety-two. Mum went up to the churchyard above the Bay with sweet peas from the garden and rang me from the bench there, and we didn't say much, and I could hear the sea behind her the whole time.

I told her about the rabies jab instead. She said Nana would have wanted to know what the vet charged, and she was right, and I told her, and she said "well!" exactly the way Nana did.
