## Out

First day out of the city since October. Marta and I took the bus to Bonaly at eight with sandwiches and a flask, and it felt like going abroad. The driver said good morning as if he meant it. Everyone on the bus had boots on.

## Up

Past the reservoir, up onto Capelaw and along to Allermuir, then over to Caerketton with the whole city laid out below us like a map somebody had dropped: the castle, Arthur's Seat, the Forth going silver behind, Fife on the far side. Skylarks going up all round us, one after another, as if someone were winding them. A wind on the top that took the words out of your mouth before you'd finished them.

## The knee

The knee was fine. On the steep bit off Caerketton I came down sideways, the way Morag taught me, and a family overtook us and the smallest child copied me all the way to the bottom, very seriously, one step at a time.

## Down

Lambs at Swanston, the first ice cream of the year from a van at the end of the road, and the bus home with sunburn on the left side of my face only.

---

In the evening I drew the route in the margin of my notebook, a dotted line from the bus stop up and over and down, and then sat looking at it for longer than it deserved. I'll miss these hills. I haven't even gone and I'm already making the list.
