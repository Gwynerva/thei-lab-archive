Rain: day 3. What I can see from the window, which is my whole country for now:

- a slice of Puddefjorden, grey, with a little ferry crossing it every forty minutes
- the green hump of Nordnes with its trees
- the backs of four wooden houses, one yellow, one white and two the red of old tomatoes
- a gull on the chimney opposite who has been there since Sunday and whom I've named Dougie
- a crane, not moving
