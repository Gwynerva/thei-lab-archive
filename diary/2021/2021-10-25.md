Homesick today, properly, for the first time since the room at Nøstet. A woman in the shop said something to me in Norwegian and I didn't understand a single word, and she said it again in English, kindly, about the bags, and I nearly cried at the kindness and had to go and look hard at the yoghurts.

I wanted Nana. I wanted to ring her and tell her about the spoon and hear her say *well, that's a spoon, then*. That's the thing nobody tells you about moving: you leave the living behind on purpose, and the dead come with you whether you like it or not.
