Day two: the bowl, with the hook knife, which is a curved blade you pull towards yourself with every instinct shouting *no*. By three o'clock I had a spoon. It's lumpy, a bit lopsided, the bowl is shallow and the handle has a kink where I lost the grain, and it's the most satisfying thing I've made since the mug.

::media[Not mine, mine is lumpier, but this is the long-handled shape I was aiming for]{file="wooden-spoon-2"}

Brought it home in my coat pocket and stirred my tea with it.
