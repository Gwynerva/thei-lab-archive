Sunday walk along Fjellveien, the road that runs along the side of the mountain above the city, with half of Bergen and all of its dogs. Everything on the slope is green and wet: spruce with moss up to the elbows, moss on the walls, moss on the moss. In Leith the autumn goes yellow first. Here it goes greener.
