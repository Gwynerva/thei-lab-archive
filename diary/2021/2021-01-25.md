Burns Night in lockdown. Callum couldn't come through from Glasgow, so he posted two haggis from the butcher near The Wee Oven, packed in ice packs, with a note: one for now, one for the freezer, neither for the cat. Then he did the Address on the laptop from his own kitchen with a bread knife, stabbed at the camera on "an' cut you up wi' ready slight", and froze mid-lunge for a whole minute while the connection thought about it.
