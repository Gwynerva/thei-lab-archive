A new word tonight that I love more than is reasonable: *pålegg*. Guess what it means before you look.

:::spoiler
Anything you put on a slice of bread. Cheese, ham, jam, brown cheese, a fried egg, cucumber, the lot: one word. The packed lunch it all goes into is *matpakke*, and from the way the teacher said it, that's a whole way of life.
:::
