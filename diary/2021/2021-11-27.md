*Pepperkakebyen*: the gingerbread town, hundreds and hundreds of little houses made by every nursery, school and office in the city, laid out in a dark hall under blue light, with a train going round, Bryggen in gingerbread, the funicular, and a coastal ship with icing portholes. The whole room smells of cinnamon and cloves and children.

::media[The gingerbread town: houses round a blue water channel, with gingerbread boats on it]{file="pepperkakebyen-1"}

The boys' house was by the station, slightly collapsed on one side, with three windows of red boiled sweets, and they were right: it was the best one. I'm going to make one next year. A lighthouse, possibly.
