Started packing books, one box a night, which feels too early and too late at once. The shipping company's limit is 25 kilos a box. Atlases are heavier than they look, and I have eleven. Norwegian has one word for what I'm filling: *flytteeske*, a moving box. I've written it on the side of every one.
