Rain: day 4. A Saturday with nowhere to go and nobody to go there with, so I made popcorn in a pan with a lid that doesn't fit and watched *Sintel* on the laptop: a girl, a baby dragon, a long walk through the snow. I cried at the end again, though I know exactly how it ends.

::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eRsGyueVLvQ"}

The rain on the window kept time with the music. Dougie the gull didn't move from his chimney all evening.
