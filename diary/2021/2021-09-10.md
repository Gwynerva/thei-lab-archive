Anna asked if I'd like to walk Vidden with her next Saturday: from Ulriken to Fløyen across the top, five or six hours, *if the weather lets us*. I said yes before she'd finished asking. Then I looked it up, and it's fifteen kilometres over a plateau with no trees on it, and I said yes again, to myself, more quietly.
