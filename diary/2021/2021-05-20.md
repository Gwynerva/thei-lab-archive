First jab. A conference centre full of folding chairs and arrows on the floor and volunteers in high-vis being kind in shifts, and a queue that moved like a well-run river. Needle in, plaster on, fifteen minutes on a plastic chair in case I fainted, which I didn't, though I thought about it. *Vaksine*, *sprøyte*, *stikk*: I made it a vocabulary lesson so I didn't have to think about my arm.

Evening: arm ~~sore~~ very sore, and a temperature that lasted exactly as long as one episode of something Marta chose.
