Two months since the plane came down over the islands. Lists, because that's how I think.

## What I miss

- the Shore at six in the morning, before anyone
- Marta practising scales through the wall
- Hamish, and the smell of a loaf on a Saturday
- people saying *aye*
- the driver on the 22 who called everyone *pal*
- shops open on a Sunday

## What I don't miss

- the 22
- the flat's boiler, which had moods
- not being able to see the sea from the kitchen

## What I have instead

- a slot of fjord in the kitchen window
- Sverre, his barometer and his rules
- Biscuit on the windowsill, watching gulls like a job
- a job where my lines go on the sea
- the light, which changes every ten minutes, so that I can't stop looking at it

## Fårikål

Today is *fårikålens festdag*, the feast day of mutton and cabbage, which falls on the last Thursday of September and is a real thing, and the canteen at work served it: lamb and cabbage and whole peppercorns, cooked for hours in nothing but itself. I had seconds. It tastes like something Nana would have made, if Nana had been Norwegian.
