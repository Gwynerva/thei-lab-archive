Took the balcony garden apart. The tomatoes, still green and heavy, went across the landing to the family with the south-facing kitchen and two small boys who swore to water them. The basil went to Marta. The chives went in the compost, which felt like a crime. The balcony is just a balcony now, with a ring of soil where the pots were.
