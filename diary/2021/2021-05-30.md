Up Arthur's Seat at five with Priya before her train, which was her idea and her alarm. The city asleep below us, the Forth going silver, Fife just waking up. On the top she told me something about the last year that's hers to tell and not mine, and I'll only write that I'm glad she told me up there, with nothing but the wind to overhear.

::media[North-east from the top, the Forth going silver]{file="arthurs-seat-2"}

She caught the ten past nine. I came home and slept until two.
