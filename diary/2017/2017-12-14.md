Forthline Christmas lunch: twelve of us round one long table in a pub on the Shore, paper hats on, and Dougie made a speech about the year that included the words "and Mira, who has brought us a tide machine, God help us". Ines had told him. Of course Ines had told him. Afterwards he said that from now on I'm to have a catchment of my own: a small one in Fife, with one stubborn underpass. My own catchment!
