Printed it! Two copies at the copy shop on Nicolson Street, the pages still warm; they'll bind them on Friday morning. I asked the woman behind the counter whether I could bind one myself, coptic stitch, and she looked at me the way you'd look at someone who asked to bake their own passport. Fair enough.

Then this week's bribe, show three: two men doing the entire history of the Royal Navy with a cardboard boat and a bucket of water. The front row got wet. I was in the front row.
