## McEwan Hall

Graduated! 🎓 MSc in Geographic Information Science, **with distinction**, which I still can't write down without grinning.

Met Mum and Dad off the early train at Waverley at ten, Dad in the suit he wears for funerals and the regatta dinner, Mum with a cake tin "for later". Clare came straight from Leeds with her camera round her neck.

The gown was, as feared, not regular: the sleeves came down past my fingertips, the hem dragged along the floor, and the hood slid off backwards every time I bowed my head. I crossed the stage holding it all up like a lady in an old painting stepping over a puddle. You bow, they tap you on the head with a cap that's supposed to have something very old sewn into it, somebody hands you a scroll, and it's over in about four seconds. Four seconds for a whole year! But they're a very good four seconds.

Mum cried from the moment we sat down. Clare took a hundred and forty photos.

## Afterwards

Dr Grieve found us in the crowd outside the hall, and Dad asked her about the drone for twenty minutes, and at the end she told him my dissertation was "the clearest thing I read this summer". Dad has been repeating this to strangers ever since.

Lunch somewhere with tablecloths, where Dad asked the waiter whether the fish was local (it wasn't) and then made a speech of about eleven words (it runs in the family), and everyone at our table clapped, and so did the next table. 🎉

Then a walk round Old College in a bitter wind so that Mum could take more photos, all of us squinting.

::media[Old College quad, where Mum made us stand in the wind for one more photo]{file="old-college-1"}

Mum and Dad went home on the five o'clock train with my certificate in a cardboard tube, to be framed "for the shop".

Nana phoned in the evening and said something about Grandad that I'm going to keep for myself.

## Three good things

1. Mum's face
2. "The clearest thing I read this summer"
3. Not falling over the gown
