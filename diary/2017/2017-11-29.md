## McEwan Hall

Graduated! 🎓 MSc in Geographic Information Science, **with distinction**, which I still can't write down without grinning.

The gown was, as feared, not regular: the sleeves came down past my fingertips and the hem dragged along the floor, and I crossed the stage holding it up like a lady in an old painting stepping over a puddle. You go up, you bow your head, they tap you on it with the cap, somebody hands you a scroll, and it's over in about four seconds. Four seconds for a whole year! But they're a very good four seconds.

Mum cried before my name was even read out. Dad took seventy-two photos. Sixty of them are of the wrong person.

## Afterwards

Photos in the Old College quad in a bitter wind, all of us squinting. Dr Grieve found us in the crowd, shook Dad's hand and said my dissertation was "the clearest thing I read this summer". Dad has been repeating this to strangers ever since.

::media[Old College quad, where we stood in the wind for the photos]{file="old-college-1"}

Then dinner on the Shore, all of us, Priya and Callum too, and Dad made a speech of about eleven words (it runs in the family) and everyone clapped. 🎉

## Three good things

1. Mum's face
2. "The clearest thing I read this summer"
3. Not falling over the gown
