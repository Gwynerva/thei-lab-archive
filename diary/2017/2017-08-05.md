Spoke Room. Every visitor in Edinburgh seems to have hired a bike, ridden it up the Royal Mile and broken it. We did nine repairs before lunch. In the quiet half hour at three the Frankenbike got a bell: the one off the child's bike from July, whose owner wanted a louder one. So now my bike plays half a tune too.
