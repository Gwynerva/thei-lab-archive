Catching up on this week's shows before I forget them:

5. A magician in a church hall, on Sunday, whose card trick I still can't work out. I'm not saying what happened, in case I go back and catch him at it.
6. A string quartet in a church in the dark on Tuesday, one candle each. The candles went out one by one and they kept on playing. Goosebumps.
7. A mime, on Wednesday, after the interview. I left at the interval. Quietly.
8. Tonight: an opera in a swimming pool, the audience round the edge in flip-flops. The tenor sang the last aria floating on his back. I can't tell you whether it was a good opera, but it was certainly an opera.

Eight down. Three to go, and three days of the Fringe to do them in.
