Started :hint[georeferencing]{note="pinning a scanned old map onto real-world coordinates, by finding the same points on the old map and on a modern one"} the 1850s map of the coast. You pick points that exist on both, a church corner, a field gate, the end of a pier, and the computer stretches the old map to fit. The abbey is very helpful. It hasn't moved since 1853. Everything else has, a bit.
