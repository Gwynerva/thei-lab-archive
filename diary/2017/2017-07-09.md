Spent Sunday with the 1853 map, tracing the cliff top at Saltwick with the mouse a millimetre at a time. The surveyors drew the cliff in little hachures like eyelashes, and my line has to go somewhere through them. I put it along the top edge and wrote down why, so that future me can disagree with present me properly.

::media[An old draught of the coast, older than my 1853 map, which I keep open for company]{file="old-map-yorkshire-2"}

Drew a tiny map of the flat in the margin afterwards, kettle and all, just to draw something that isn't falling into the sea.
