Bank holiday, and the start of the point clouds. You feed the software all the drone photos, it finds the same bits of rock in all of them and works out where the camera was for each one, and from that it builds the cliff in three dimensions, millions of points, every one with a colour. It takes hours. My laptop sounds like it's trying to take off.
