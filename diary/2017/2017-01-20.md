THE PROPOSAL IS ACCEPTED!

## The email

It came at 4.12 in the afternoon in the middle of a spatial databases lab, and I read it under the desk like a teenager. Dr Grieve:

> The proposal is approved. It's a good question, and a better one for being small. Go and measure your cliff.

I may have made a noise. The boy next to me asked if I was OK. I said "my cliff is approved". He didn't ask anything else.
