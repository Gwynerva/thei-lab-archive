Fieldwork, day one!

## Getting there

Sam drove us down in the department's van, the drone in its case strapped in on the back seat like a passenger. Down the A1, across to the coast, over the moors with the heather still brown, and then the moment at the top of Blue Bank where Whitby appears below with the abbey on the other side. Sam said "oh, that's good". I felt like I'd built it.

## Setting up

Base station on the cliff top above Saltwick, on a spot I'll be able to find again (a fence post, Dad's favourite fence post). Then the twelve targets pegged out along the top and down on the shelf at the foot of the cliff, and every one surveyed with the rover, standing very still with the pole level. The caravan park man came out to see what we were doing and stayed an hour.

::media[Saltwick Nab from the north, the cliff we're measuring, in surf]{file="saltwick-nab-1"}

Mum fed Sam a tea of a size he wasn't ready for. He's staying at a B&B on the West Cliff and said he'll be walking it off till Thursday.
