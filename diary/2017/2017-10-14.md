## Commit number one

A wet Saturday, rain on the skylight all day, and I spent it at the kitchen table. At twenty to eleven at night I typed the first message — *tides, roughly* — and pressed enter. **tideline** exists, and it's on the internet, where anybody could look at it (nobody will). It's one file, forty lines long. It takes a list of waves, each with a size, a speed and a lateness, and adds them up for any hour you ask.

So far it knows two waves, M2 and S2, the moon's and the sun's, for Whitby, with numbers out of an old paper. Its high water comes out about forty minutes off Dad's booklet. That's either a bug or the other hundred waves. Probably both. It's a very small, very wrong thing, and I love it.

## The name

It's called tideline after the line of weed and shells that the sea leaves along the beach at Whitby to show where it got to and no further. Dad used to walk me along it looking for jet. Every tide writes its own line.

The other candidates, for the record: Priya suggested *Tidy McTideface*, Callum suggested *Moon Sums*, and I briefly considered something with Greek in it before remembering I don't know any Greek. Lower case, because it's small and I want it to stay modest.
