[Storm Caroline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Caroline "named this week, here by Thursday") arrived in the night and spent the whole day trying to get into the flat through the windows. The kitchen sash rattled like teeth. At about four the big communal bins on our street decided to leave, and three of them went rolling down the hill towards Leith Walk with Callum and a man from the next stair chasing them in the rain. They caught two.
