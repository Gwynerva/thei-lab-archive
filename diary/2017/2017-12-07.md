[Storm Caroline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Caroline "named this week, here by Thursday") came in the night. The windows rattled in their frames for hours and the trees in the back green roared, and I was up at the kitchen window recording them when, at two in the morning, there was an almighty crash: every wheelie bin on the street going over at once and setting off down the hill towards Leith Walk like a herd.

::audio[The back green at two in the morning, from the kitchen window, just before the crash]{file="sound-wind-1" title="Storm Caroline from the kitchen"}

Callum and I went out in our coats over our pyjamas and chased three of them down. Ours had lost its lid. All day at work the office windows hummed, and Dougie kept glancing at the flood maps on the wall as if they might need him.
