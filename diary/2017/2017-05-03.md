The point cloud finished at four this morning. Thirty-one million points. I turned it round on the screen with the mouse and there it was: my cliff, every ledge and crack, the caravans on top, the targets on the shelf like tiny chessboards. And the fall from the 12th, a fresh scar in the face with its heap at the bottom, in three dimensions.

I sat in the kitchen in my dressing gown and turned it round and round for an hour. Priya came in for breakfast and said "is that the cliff?" and I said "it's *my* cliff".
