Thirty-one million points of Saltwick Bay on my laptop again, and the fan sounding like it wants to emigrate. Sam found a typo in one of the :hint[ground control points]{note="marks on the ground measured with GPS, so the drone's pictures can be pinned to the real world"} from April — a 7 that should have been a 1 — so I ran the whole cloud again with the corrected list, and it took all day, and the error came down under four centimetres. Stupidly proud of that. Four centimetres! On a cliff that's been falling into the sea since before the first map of it was drawn!
