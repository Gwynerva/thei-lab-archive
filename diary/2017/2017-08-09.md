Show four: a murder mystery on a moving double-decker bus. You buy a ticket, you get on, the bus drives round the New Town, and somebody "dies" on the top deck by Charlotte Square. We had to guess the killer before the last stop. I was wrong, and so was everybody else.

:::spoiler
It was the conductor — the woman in the peaked cap who checked our tickets at the start and then sat at the back the whole time doing a crossword. Nobody ever looks at the person checking the tickets.
:::
