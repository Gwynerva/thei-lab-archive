Burns Night!

## The supper

Ten people round our table, which seats four. Callum in his whites and a borrowed kilt, the haggis on the good plate, and Priya standing on a chair to address it. She did all eight verses, with the knife, and the voice, and at "his knife see rustic Labour dight" she stabbed it so hard it went across the table. Huge applause.

::media[Two haggis in the pot before the main event, Callum guarding them]{file="haggis-2"}

Then haggis, :hint[neeps and tatties]{note="swede and potatoes, both mashed, always on the plate with a haggis"}, a whisky sauce that Callum says is "a crime against Burns but a delicious one", and cranachan for pudding.

## The pipes

There was no piper, so Callum played the pipes on his phone through the little speaker, propped in a mug to make it louder. It sounded like a piper a long way off in the fog.

::audio[The pipes, from a phone in a mug, our Burns Night]{file="music-bagpipes-1" title="The Skye Boat Song"}

## After

Everybody did a toast. I did the reply to the toast to the lassies, badly, with jokes about maps, and one of them landed, the one about scale. Then we pushed the table against the wall and tried to do Strip the Willow in a living room the size of a lift, six people spinning and everybody else flattened against the bookcase. Priya's lab-mate went into the radiator twice. The downstairs neighbour came up to see what the thumping was and joined in.

Bed at two, with the smell of haggis in my hair and my ears still full of the pipes.
