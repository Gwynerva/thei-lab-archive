Away at six. Stirling, Crianlarich, across Rannoch Moor in the rain, down through Glen Coe in more rain, and a rainbow over the glen that lasted just long enough for Priya to pull into a lay-by and both of us to run out without our coats. Then Fort William, the long road west, the bridge to Skye.

Straight up to the Old Man of Storr, in a mist so thick we were nearly underneath him before he appeared: enormous, grey, leaning, like somebody's grandfather looming out of a fog. Spooky and wonderful. We stood and looked at him for ages and he looked back.

Pitched the tent at Sligachan in a downpour in eleven minutes, which is a record for us. The Cuillin were somewhere behind the cloud; we'll take the guidebook's word for it. Dinner was pasta in the tent porch and a lot of laughing.
