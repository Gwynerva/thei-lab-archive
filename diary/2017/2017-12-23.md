Home for Christmas, with a rucksack full of notebooks and robins! Edinburgh to Darlington, Darlington to Middlesbrough, then the little train across the moors to Whitby: an hour and a half of heather and sheep and stations with one bench each. Dad was on the platform in his shop jumper. The town smells exactly the same: chips, the sea, and coal smoke from somewhere.
