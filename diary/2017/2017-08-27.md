Shows nine and ten, on stiff Chonzie legs. Nine: a one-man *Hamlet* in a portable toilet, for an audience of one at a time. Seventeen minutes. I don't know what I saw, and I'll never forget it. Ten: a late-night comedy in a tent so cold that the comedian handed out blankets at the door, and then made jokes about the blankets for twenty minutes.
