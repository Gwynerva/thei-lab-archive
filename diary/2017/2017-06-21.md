The longest day. Walked up Calton Hill at half nine in the evening, the same hill as on the shortest day, and the sun was still up, just, low in the north-west over Fife. It set at about three minutes past ten. Never properly dark tonight. The sky stayed blue-green over the Forth until I went to bed.

::media[From the top of Calton Hill: the roofs, the Crags, and the Pentlands far off]{file="calton-hill-4"}

On the way down a blackbird was singing on the top of a chimney like it had a lot to say.
