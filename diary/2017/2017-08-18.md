## Handed in

Handed in at 11.20 at the School office on Drummond Street. The woman at the desk stamped it, said "Congratulations," and put it on top of a pile of about forty others, all the same blue. That's it. A year of my life, on top of a pile.

Then I stood on the pavement outside not knowing what to do with my hands.

## What's in it

The cliff at Saltwick has gone back 0.3 metres a year since 1853, on average, and nine metres in one winter. Six old surveys, one drone, thirty-one million points, and two old American reports whose layout I copied shamelessly for my results chapter:

## After

Priya and Callum were waiting at the flat with a cake in the shape of a cliff (Callum) with a toy caravan on the edge (Priya). We ate the caravan first.

Then we took the rest of the cake up Calton Hill and sat on the wall above the Fringe crowds, and I told them the whole thing, chapter by chapter, and they let me. Dr Grieve emailed at six: "Well done. Now rest." I'm going to do exactly as I'm told.
