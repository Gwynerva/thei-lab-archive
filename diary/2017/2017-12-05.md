The Met Office has named a storm Caroline and says she'll be here on Thursday: gusts of ninety miles an hour in the far north and, for us, according to Callum, "a bit blowy". Dougie read the warning out loud in the office like a radio announcer, and then everybody went quiet and looked at the flood maps on the wall, which is what flood people do when it's going to be windy.
