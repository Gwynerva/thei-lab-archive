## Bikes

We hired bikes for the day and rode everywhere, and I understand now why everybody goes on about it. The cycle lanes are proper lanes, wide and kerbed, with their own traffic lights, and in the rush hour there are *hundreds* of bikes moving together like a shoal. Nobody wears Lycra. A man rode past us in a suit with a Christmas tree on his cargo bike. In February. A woman rode past with a cello on her back and I nearly crashed into a bin watching her.

It's like a city somebody designed on paper first, with a good pencil.

## Up the tower

The Round Tower has no stairs, just a ramp that spirals up inside, wide enough for a horse and cart, and from the top you see the whole city laid out flat: copper roofs gone green, the harbour, cranes, and the bridge to Sweden faint in the haze. I'd have drawn it if my hands hadn't been frozen.
