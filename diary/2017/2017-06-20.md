Someone took my bike from the stair.

Came down at eight to go to the lab and the cable lock was lying on the floor of the stair, cut clean through, and no bike. My bike! The red one, the frame I've had since I was eighteen, with the bell that doesn't work and the mudguard I fixed with a cable tie. It was locked to the railing at the bottom of the stair like always. Someone came in through the main door, which never closes properly, and walked out with it.

::media[The last photo of my bike, the faded red frame, the brake I fixed in October]{file="vintage-bicycle-2"}

Rang the police, who gave me a crime number and their sympathy, in that order. Then I wrote a notice and stuck it on the inside of the main door:

STOLEN FROM THIS STAIR, 20 JUNE:\
one red bike, steel, a bell that doesn't work.\
If you see it, top floor. There are biscuits in it for you.

Walked to the lab, which took forty minutes, and was in a mood all day. But I know where the next one's coming from. [The Spoke Room](ref:project:spoke-room "where the next bike will come from") has a pile of frames in the back marked PARTS, and I know my way round a bottom bracket now.
