Learned today what a "1 in 200 year" flood really means: not that it happens every two hundred years, but that every single year there's a one-in-two-hundred chance. So it could happen twice next week. Dougie explained this cheerfully while eating a banana. Drew a little river with a flood on it in my work notebook, to remember.
