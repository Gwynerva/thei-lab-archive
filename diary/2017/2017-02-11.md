Started the wallet after the Spoke Room, at the kitchen table, with the radio on.

- [x] cut the pieces out with a craft knife and a steel ruler (and not my thumb)
- [x] mark the stitch line with a fork, which is what the internet said
- [x] punch the holes, one by one, with an awl and a hammer, on a chopping board on the floor so as not to wake the downstairs neighbour
- [ ] stitch
- [ ] edges
- [ ] cry, probably

The stitch is a :hint[saddle stitch]{note="one thread with a needle at each end, both needles going through every hole from opposite sides, so if one stitch breaks the rest hold"}: a needle on each end of the thread, both through every hole from opposite sides. Did eleven holes in an hour. There are a hundred and forty. Nana would say a stitch in time is still a stitch. Nana is very patient.
