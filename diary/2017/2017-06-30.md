## The number

The first proper number. I ran the comparison along the whole bay, from the 1853 edge to April's point cloud, point by point, every metre of the cliff: a line every metre, from the old edge to the new, measured, and then all of them averaged. Averaged over 164 years, the cliff top at Saltwick is going back about a third of a metre a year. Some years less, some places much more. And April's fall, in one go, took more than all of an ordinary year.

A third of a metre doesn't sound like much. It's the length of my forearm. But it's every year, and it's the field, and the fence posts, and one day it'll be the caravans, and the path, again.

If it holds. I'll check it all again next week, with the aerial photos in between, to see if it's speeding up. I haven't told Dad yet, because he'll say "I told you", and he didn't, but he did.
