Overtides! Whitby's harbour is shallow, so the tide trips over itself coming in and needs a few extra, faster waves in the sum to catch it. Gave tideline three of them tonight, and Whitby's high water is now eleven minutes out instead of twenty. Texted Dad next week's times. He texted back "Will check against the booklet." An hour later: "Eleven minutes is a long time on the bar. Getting there." From Dad, that's a medal.
