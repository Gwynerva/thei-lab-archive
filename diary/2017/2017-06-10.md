Leith Festival gala day!

The whole of Leith Links full of stalls and tents and flags strung between the trees, a pipe band, a dog show, a bouncy castle shaped like a pirate ship, and the smell of burgers and candyfloss and cut grass. And us, under a gazebo that kept trying to blow away, with three workstands and the tool roll and the sign.

## What we did

- thirty-eight bikes checked, from a balance bike to a tandem
- nineteen sets of brakes adjusted
- nine punctures
- one bike declared "not a bike any more, sorry" (it was mostly rust and stickers)

A small boy brought his bike for a check, stood next to me the whole time, and asked a question about every single tool on the roll. By the end he could name the tyre levers and the chain tool. His mum said he'd never sat still that long in his life.

The woman who runs the Spoke Room said it was our best day ever, and gave everybody a burger and a can. Sunburnt on one arm only, the arm that was out of the gazebo.
