Best Saturday at the Spoke Room yet. A girl of about ten came in with her dad and a flat tyre and I showed her how to do it herself: levers, the tube out, find the hole in a bowl of water, patch, chalk, back in. She did the whole thing. She said "is that it?" and her dad laughed so much he had to sit on a crate.
