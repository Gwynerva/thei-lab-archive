Production line on the kitchen table: holes punched in forty signatures with an awl and a card template, covers cut from board, then Nana's fabric and some marbled offcuts Fergus gave me glued over them. The press came home in a taxi; the driver asked if it was a printing press and I said yes because it was easier. Nine notebooks sewn by midnight. Fingers like sausages.
