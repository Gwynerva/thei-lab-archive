Last night at the bindery Fergus taught us the [coptic stitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coptic_binding "the one where the sewing shows along the spine, like a chain"). There's no glue on the spine at all: you sew each signature to the next with a little chain stitch, with a curved needle, and the book opens perfectly flat. It's the most beautiful thing I've ever been taught. I'm going to make every notebook I own like this for ever.
