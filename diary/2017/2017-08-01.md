Chapter five, day one. Made eleven maps of the same piece of cliff, one per survey, all at the same scale, and laid them out on the living-room floor in date order. If you walk along the row it's like a flick-book of the coast going backwards into the land. Callum walked along it in his socks, which wasn't the idea.
