Ben Lomond, my first Munro!

## Up

Priya's lab-mate dropped us at Rowardennan at eight and drove off to go fishing. We went up the Ptarmigan way, which is the quiet way: through the trees by the loch, then out onto the open hill, then a long ridge with the loch below on the left getting bigger and bluer all the time. Hazy, warm, not a breath of wind. Priya talked the whole way up. I couldn't, I needed the breath.

::media[Loch Lomond from the Ptarmigan path, hazy and long]{file="loch-lomond-2"}

## The top

The last bit is a steep rocky pull up to the summit, and then suddenly you're on it, 974 metres, with a trig point and a crowd of people eating sandwiches and a black dog going round them all. And the view! North, the whole of the Highlands, ridge behind ridge, going blue into the distance.

::media[North from the top of Ben Lomond, the whole Highlands at once]{file="ben-lomond-3" layout="stretch"}

I touched the trig point. Priya took a photo of me touching the trig point. I look like I've won something, and I have.

## Down

The tourist path down, which is long and stony and full of people coming up in trainers asking "is it much further?" Yes. Knees like jelly at the bottom. Ice cream at Rowardennan. Asleep in the car before we got to the end of the loch.

There are 282 Munros. I've done one. Priya has started saying "next".
