The wallet is finished!

::media[The wallet, open: three pockets, a card slot, all those stitches]{file="leather-wallet-1"}

A hundred and forty holes, two needles, one long thread waxed with a candle end, and a seam that's only a bit wobbly at the corner where I was watching the telly. It closes with a brass button I took off a coat from the charity shop. It smells amazing. I've put my library card in it and a fiver and taken it out to look at it about twelve times.
