Read back through the year tonight: the end of the old notebook, then the two I made myself, the bindery one and this one. January me was so worried about everything: the proposal, the money, whether I'd ever have a job. Most of it worked out. Some of it worked out better than I'd have dared to write down.

The thing I haven't written down all year, so I'll write it here: I'd like to stop being the person who's always about to start something and be the person who keeps going with it. The cello, maybe. We'll see.

Priya came back this afternoon with a streaming cold and went straight to bed, so tomorrow will be quiet.
