The last exam I'll ever sit. ~~Ever.~~ Probably.

## The exam

Spatial databases, nine in the morning, the big hall again, the loud clock. Didn't sleep much, after last night, but I'd had porridge, which Mum says is "a foundation". There was a question on joining tables that I'd practised the night before last, so I may have been revising after all. Wrote till the last minute, put the pen down, and the man at the front said "that's it, everybody", and two hundred people breathed out at once.

That's the taught part of [the course](ref:project:msc-gis "one year: the taught part ends today") done. All of it. Just the dissertation left, all summer, which is the bit I actually like.

## After

Outside on the steps everybody did the same thing: stood about in the sun blinking, not knowing what to do with their hands. Then the whole course went to the pub on Teviot Place and sat outside till it got cold. Somebody's dog drank somebody's pint. The boy who sits next to me in databases told me he'd been terrified of me all year "because you always know where things are". I said that's just maps. He said that's what he meant.

Walked home down the Bridges and Leith Walk at ten, still light, the sky green over the Forth.
