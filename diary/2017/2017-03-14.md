Twenty-five! On [Pi Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi_Day "3.14, my birthday, for which I will never apologise"), as I remind everyone, every year.

## Breakfast

Callum's secret project was a pie. A steak pie, for breakfast, with a π cut into the lid and a candle stuck in the middle. "Because you won't shut up about it." Best breakfast of my life.

## Post

- from Priya: a waterproof field notebook and a pencil with a cliff drawn on it
- from Clare and Dev: a book of old maps of Yorkshire (they know me)
- from Mum and Dad: a card with a robin on it, and a note in Dad's capitals: HAPPY BIRTHDAY. PROUD OF YOU. DAD. That's the most words he has ever written in a card.
- from Nana: a letter, two pages, and a sprig of rowan from the garden, the berries dried dark red

::media[Rowan berries like the ones Nana sent, still bright]{file="rowan-berries-2"}

:::private
Mum also put fifty pounds in the card, which they can't afford, not this year. I'm going to put it back in her purse at Easter when she isn't looking. She'll know it was me. She won't say.
:::

## The evening

It's Tuesday, so I spent my twenty-fifth birthday evening at the bindery, sewing signatures, which is exactly what I wanted to do. Somebody told Fergus and the class sang Happy Birthday in a very quiet, embarrassed way, all looking at their books. Fergus didn't sing, but he gave me a sheet of marbled paper "for an endpaper, when you're good enough". I will be.

Three good things:

1. a pie with a π on it
2. PROUD OF YOU. DAD.
3. the marbled paper, in a cardboard tube, by my bed
