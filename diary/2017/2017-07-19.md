Rode the Frankenbike to the lab every day this week. The front wheel came off an old racer and the back off a shopping bike, so it leans forward slightly, eager, like a dog on a lead. At night I carry it up all seventy-two stairs and it sleeps in the hall, where everyone bangs their shins on it. Nobody is cutting through anything ever again.
