Spoke Room. The woman who runs it took me to the back and said "go on, then: PARTS". Found a steel frame in good nick, an old blue one with a dent in the top tube that doesn't matter. Wheels from one bike, handlebars from another, a saddle that's seen things. By four it was a bike, mostly. It has no brakes yet. It's not going out of the basement until it has brakes. But it's mine, and I built it, and nobody can cut through that.
