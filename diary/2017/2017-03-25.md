## Getting there

Straight from the Spoke Room to Waverley with oil on my hands, the train to Queen Street, chips in the rain on George Square, and then the walk east to the Barrowland, with its big neon sign and a queue right round the corner of people in damp jackets, all talking at once.

::media[Red sandstone in Glasgow on the way to the gig, blue sky for once]{file="glasgow-2"}

## The gig

Inside there are stars on the ceiling and the floor bounces when everybody jumps. And everybody jumped. I jumped. I don't jump. The band were brilliant, loud, sweaty, the singer climbing up onto the speakers. Callum knew every word and sang them all directly at us. Priya lost a shoe in the crowd for one song and somehow got it back, passed hand to hand over everyone's heads like a trophy.

## The last train

Then we *ran* through the streets for the last train, made it with a minute to go, and sat on the floor of the end bit of the carriage because the seats were full of other people who'd also run. Somebody had a bag of doughnuts and shared them all the way to Linlithgow. Got in at half one. The clocks go forward tonight, so it's really half two. I'm knackered and very happy, and my ears are ringing like a fire alarm.
