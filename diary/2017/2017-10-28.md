## Stob Binnein, in the first snow

Up from Glen Dochart (Priya driving the borrowed car; I've started paying in flapjack) to Stob Binnein, 1,165 metres, the higher of the two big hills above Crianlarich. The first snow of the winter was on the top, not much, a dusting on the north side that crunched, and a wind that made my eyes run so hard I came down with salt lines on my face.

From the top you look straight across the bealach at Ben More, which is a bit bigger and a lot steeper and smug about it. Next year.

On the way down we met a man coming up in shorts, in October, in the snow, who asked if it was "nice up there". Priya said "Bracing." He said "Grand," and carried on, knees pink, whistling.

## Six

That's the last Munro of the year, and the list at the back of the notebook now reads:

1. Ben Lomond
2. Ben Vorlich (Loch Earn)
3. Meall nan Tarmachan
4. Ben Chonzie
5. Bla Bheinn
6. Stob Binnein

Six in a year, from somebody who couldn't see the point of hills eighteen months ago. Two hundred and seventy-six to go. I'm not going to do them all. (Am I?)

## Home

Fell asleep in the car before Callander with a flapjack in my hand. Priya says I snore like a small outboard motor.
