Rainy Sunday at home with tideline. It can find the high and low waters now instead of just drawing the curve: it walks along the sum looking for the turning points. Printed a whole year of Whitby high waters for Dad, right to within ten minutes or so, folded it into a little booklet and sewed it with a single stitch, like a pamphlet. My Christmas present to him. Don't tell him.
