The 1913 map and the 1853 map disagree about the cliff by more than any cliff can move in sixty years. Two hours of panic, then the answer: the 1913 sheet was printed on paper that has shrunk, unevenly, a bit more north to south than east to west. **Paper shrinks.** Nobody told me paper shrinks. Stretched it back with eight control points and a lot of swearing.
