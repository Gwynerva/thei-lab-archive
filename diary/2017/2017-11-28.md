Mum and Dad came up on the train this afternoon, Mum with a cake tin and Dad in a new tie he says he's had for years (the label was still on it). They're in a B&B on Pilrig Street. Tea at the flat: Callum cooked, Dad told Priya everything there is to know about the tides at Whitby, and Priya, bless her, asked follow-up questions.

:::private
Dad was out of breath at the top of our stairs. Seventy-two steps, and he had to stand on the landing for a minute pretending to admire the view from the stair window. He's fifty-four. I didn't say anything. Mum saw me not saying anything.
:::

Gown collected. It is not regular. We'll see tomorrow.
