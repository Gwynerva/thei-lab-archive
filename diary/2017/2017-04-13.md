Last day.

## The second flight

Sam did a second set of flights over the fall, so now we have the cliff the day before and the day after, which Dr Grieve will either love or think I arranged. The heap from yesterday was already smaller: the morning tide had been at it, and the clay at the edges had gone soft and grey and was sliding off into the pools.

::media[Black Nab in a rock pool on the grey last morning]{file="saltwick-bay-3"}

## Packing up

Then we surveyed all twelve targets again, in case anything had moved (target nine had, again; the same dog), packed up the base station, and pulled up the pegs. The week, counted:

- 1,840 photos
- 31 GNSS points
- 12 targets, all back in the van
- 1 cliff fall
- 2 very tired people
- 0 gulls hit, which Sam says is a record for him at the coast

Sam drove the van back to Edinburgh in the afternoon with the drone in the passenger seat, and Mum gave him a bag of sandwiches for the A1 that would have fed the whole department. I'm staying for Easter. Hard drive on the desk in the sweet pea room, with a tea towel over it, like a cage with a bird in it.
