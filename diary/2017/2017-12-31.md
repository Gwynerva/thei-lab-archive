## A quiet one

No Princes Street this year. Priya's at her parents', and Callum is cooking Hogmanay dinner for two hundred at the restaurant until one in the morning. So it's me, the cello in the corner, a pot of soup and the window.

Walked up Calton Hill at four to watch the last light of the year go, and the Old Town turned pink, then grey, then lit up.

## 2017, in a list

- handed in a dissertation about a cliff
- climbed six Munros
- saw eleven shows in one August
- got a job, and kept it so far
- graduated, in a gown three sizes too big
- made twenty-three notebooks and sold fourteen
- started a tide machine for Dad
- printed fifty-two robins
- brought a cello home

The whole year is on one page somewhere, eventually:

::card[The year on one page, written later, with hindsight]{to="ref:page:year-2017"}

and every day of it is in [the diary](/diary/).

At midnight the fireworks went up over the castle and I watched from the window with a mug of tea. Callum came in at half one smelling of roast potatoes, with a bag of leftovers, and we ate them sitting on the stairs. Happy new year.
