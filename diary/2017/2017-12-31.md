## A quiet one

No street party this year. Callum is working, Priya has a cold, and I have no money left after Christmas. So I walked up Calton Hill on my own at four to watch the last light of the year go, and the Old Town turned pink, then grey, then lit up.

::media[The Old Town at dusk on the last day of the year, from the hill]{file="edinburgh-skyline-2" layout="stretch"}

Then home to Priya, and tea, toast and a hot water bottle each at the kitchen table, going through the year. Her idea. The resolutions and the three good things went in the back of the notebook; this is the rest.

## 2017, in a list

- handed in a dissertation about a cliff, with two minutes to spare
- climbed six Munros
- saw eleven shows in one August
- got a job, and kept it so far
- graduated, in a gown made for somebody a foot taller
- sewed forty-odd notebooks and sold fourteen
- started a tide machine for Dad
- printed fifty-two robins
- got my cello back (unplayed, so far)

The whole year is on one page somewhere, eventually:

::card[The year on one page, written later, with hindsight]{to="ref:page:year-2017"}

and every day of it is in [the diary](/diary/).

At midnight we opened the kitchen window to hear the fireworks over the castle arrive, a second late, small and far away. Then bed. Happy new year.
