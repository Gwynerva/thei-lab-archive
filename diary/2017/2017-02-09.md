My wallet has finally fallen apart, after nine years, outside the chippy, in the rain, coins everywhere. So I'm going to make a new one. Ordered a bag of leather offcuts, two blunt saddler's needles and some waxed thread. Drew a pattern on squared paper in the spatial databases lecture. It has three pockets and a slot for my library card.
