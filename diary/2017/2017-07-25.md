The number came out tonight. Average retreat of the cliff top at Saltwick since 1853: **0.3 metres a year.** A metre every three years. A double-decker bus in a lifetime.

But the number I keep looking at is the other one: nine metres gone in the single winter of the 2013 surge. Nine metres! Thirty years of the average in one winter. Rang Dad. He said "Told you," and then asked if the caravans were safe, and I said not forever.
