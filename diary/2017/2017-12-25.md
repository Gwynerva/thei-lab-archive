## Christmas Day

Dad fetched Nana from the Bay at ten, in her good coat and the brooch Grandad brought back from somewhere hot. She inspected the kitchen, approved of the turkey, disapproved of the sprouts ("too early on") and sat by the fire directing.

Clare and Dev arrived at noon, straight off Clare's night shift, Clare still wearing her hospital lanyard, and by three they were asleep on the sofa together like a pair of cats.

## Presents

- Dad got the tide booklet. He turned every page, checked three dates against the printed book from the shop, and said "Close enough." Then he put it in his top pocket and kept it there all day.
- Mum got a big marbled notebook for her garden plans.
- Nana got a small checked one, and a jar of her own jelly back as a joke. She laughed for a full minute.
- I got wellies (from Mum, who'd heard), a head torch (from Dad, obviously), and from Nana a tin of :hint[parkin]{note="Yorkshire gingerbread made with oats and black treacle; better after a week in the tin"} and a needle book she'd sewn, with my initials on it in red.

## The news

Over the pudding Clare tapped her glass and said that she and Dev have set a date: September, in York, and I'm doing the invitations, she's decided, because "you're the one with the paper". Mum cried (twice in one month!). Nana said "About time." Dev went pink to the ears.

---

At dusk Dad and I walked down to the harbour, the two of us, to look at the water. High tide at 16.48, he said, reading it out of his new booklet. It was.
