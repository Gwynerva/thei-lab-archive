A big brown envelope from Dad: the old photos from the shop drawer, of the caravan park and the cliff at Saltwick, from 1987, 1994 and 2003. On the back of each one, in his capitals, the date and *WHERE THE FENCE WAS*. He's been measuring it in his head for thirty years.
