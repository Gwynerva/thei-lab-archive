Copenhagen! The flight was an hour and three-quarters, which is shorter than going home to Whitby. The metro from the airport has no driver and you can sit right at the front and pretend you're driving it, so obviously we did, both of us, like children.

Walked down to Nyhavn in the dark: the coloured houses, the old boats with their masts lit up, everybody in big coats drinking outside under blankets as if it were normal. Hot dogs from a stand for tea. The hostel has a cat that sits on the reception desk and checks your passport.
