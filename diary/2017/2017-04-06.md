Sam showed me his flight plan: a grid of lines over the cliff, back and forth like mowing a lawn, a photo every two seconds, each one overlapping the last by most of itself. He says the only things that can stop us are wind, rain, and "a gull with an attitude". The forecast says light winds. I'm not looking at the forecast again. I've looked at it eleven times today.

He did a test flight on the Meadows at lunchtime, and half the students on the grass stopped eating to watch.
