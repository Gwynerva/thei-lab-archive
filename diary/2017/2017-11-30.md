Tired, happy, and back at my desk as if nothing had happened. Took Mum and Dad to the station at eight, Dad carrying my scroll in its cardboard tube as if it might go off, and was at the office by nine. Dougie had stuck a paper mortarboard on my monitor, and everybody wanted to hear about [the graduation](ref:event:graduation-2017), so I told the gown story four times before lunch.
