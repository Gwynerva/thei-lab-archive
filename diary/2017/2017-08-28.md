Eleven! The last show on the last day: a choir of forty singing sea shanties in a church, and everyone joined in the choruses whether they knew them or not. Then up Calton Hill with half of Edinburgh for the fireworks over the castle, the music coming up from the gardens a fraction late, so every bang landed just before its note.

::media[The cannon on Calton Hill, where we sat with a bag of chips each and waited for dark]{file="calton-hill-2"}

Eleven shows in one August, and a dissertation. I'm counting both.
