## Bla Bheinn, in the rain

The plan was Bla Bheinn, the one Cuillin Munro you can walk up without a rope, from Loch Slapin. The weather's plan was rain. Both plans went ahead.

It rained on the walk in, on the burn crossing, up a path that was really a stream, up the long steep corrie, and in the gully of scree at the top, where you go up two steps and slide back one. Priya counted the waterfalls on the way up (thirty-one) to take her mind off it. At the top, 928 metres: a cairn, a wall of white, and a view of exactly nothing. Priya wrote VIEW on a page of my notebook and photographed me holding it up against the white. We touched the cairn, shouted "Five!", ate a squashed flapjack each, and came straight down.

## The tent

By the time we got back to Sligachan, Callum's tent was leaking at every seam and had a small lake under the groundsheet. We patched the worst of it with a bin bag. Nana would have done it with a needle and candle wax. Then we sat in the car with the heater on full and our socks on the dashboard, steaming, and I recorded the rain on the roof because it was the loudest thing I'd ever heard:

::audio[Rain on the roof of the hire car, both of us too tired to talk]{file="sound-rain-3" title="Rain at Sligachan"}

At nine we gave up on the tent altogether and took two bunks in the hostel, and lay there listening to it rain on a different roof.

## Margins

Drew Bla Bheinn in the margin tonight, from the map, since I never actually saw it. It's a very good drawing of a mountain I can only vouch for by the state of my legs.

Number five. One more this year, if the weather lets me.
