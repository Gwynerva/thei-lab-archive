Nobody reads this but me, so: I'm scared about September. Everyone on the course seems to have interviews lined up, and I have two rejections and seven silences, and one of the silences is the company in Leith I want most. The money lasts till the end of August if I keep eating pasta, and then the rent is £425 on the first of September whatever happens. Mum would send money if I asked, which is exactly why I won't.

Walked past the Leith one on the way home, on purpose: two rooms up a stair on the Shore, a plant in one window and a map pinned up in the other. I stood on the other side of the river looking at it like a lovesick teenager.
