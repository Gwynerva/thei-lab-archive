New Year's Day. Walked to Portobello with Priya to blow the cobwebs off, and there were people *swimming*, in January, on purpose, screaming. We watched from the prom with our hands round coffees like sensible people.

::media[The old groyne at Portobello in the first light of the year]{file="portobello-beach-1"}

Clare rang at teatime: they told Mum and Dad last night. So now I can write it in here properly. **Clare and Dev are getting married!** Mum cried, Dad shook Dev's hand for a very long time and then went out to "check the boat".

A new thing for 2017: at the end of a day, three good things. Here are today's:

1. people swimming in the sea in January (not me)
2. Clare's voice on the phone
3. the sofa is finally inside the flat (Callum and the neighbour, a saw, don't ask)
