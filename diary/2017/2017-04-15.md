Walked to Robin Hood's Bay along the old railway line, the Cinder Track, seven miles, with the sea on my left most of the way. Nana was in the garden with the rowan just coming into leaf. She's slower on the lane than at Christmas. She says it's her knees. She made me take the arm on the way down.
