Site visit in Fife, in the new wellies: a burn the width of a pavement that, three times a winter, turns into a river and goes straight through a primary school's playground. Stood in it with Dougie, measuring, while thirty children watched from the classroom windows as if we were the most interesting thing that had ever happened. Maybe we were.

Home to a letter from Nana: two pages about the jelly ("did yours set?"), the weather at the Bay, the neighbour's cat, and then at the bottom, out of nowhere:

> Your grandad would have liked your tide machine. He never trusted the books either.

I've read it ten times.
