Boiled the juice with the sugar this morning, Nana timing it on the kitchen clock and doing the wrinkle test on a cold saucer out of the freezer. Set at eleven minutes. Eight jars, the colour of a sunset through a window.

::media[The jars in a row on Nana's worktop, the spent pulp and the stalks still beside them]{file="jam-jars-1"}

Four came home with me on the train, wrapped in a jumper, with the card. [Nana's jelly](ref:event:rowan-jelly "eight jars, four for me") will be on everything I eat until Christmas.
