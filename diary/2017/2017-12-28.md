Opened the cello's case tonight for the first time since Dad brought it in October. Didn't play it. Just looked, and wiped the dust off with a sock, and put the music from the loft on the shelf beside it. Then I walked to the music shop on the Southside for no reason at all, and on the board by the till there was a card: *Cello lessons, adults welcome, beginners and returners*. I took a photo of it. I'm not saying anything yet.
