First Saturday of July, and the Spoke Room smelled of wet tyres and fabric softener, as ever. Four punctures, one buckled wheel (trued badly, then less badly) and a child's bike whose bell plays half a tune and stops, as if it's forgotten the rest.

In the quiet hour I gave the new bike mudguards out of the parts pile, one black and one silver. Callum has named it **the Frankenbike**, because it's made of the bits of other bikes, and the name has stuck. It's a very good name. I'm annoyed I didn't think of it first.
