First Saturday of July, and the Spoke Room smelled of wet tyres and fabric softener, as ever. Four punctures, one buckled wheel (trued badly, then less badly) and a child's bike whose bell plays half a tune and stops, as if it's forgotten the rest.

Then the good bit: the woman who runs it says I can have the pick of the donation pile and build something to replace the bike that walked off from our stair last month. Found a steel frame, faded red under the rust, with a seat tube straight enough to trust. It's mine now. **The Frankenbike begins!**
