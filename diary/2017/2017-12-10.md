## Printing

The kitchen table is too small and too wobbly, so the printing happened on the kitchen floor, on newspaper, on my knees. The block on a chopping board, the ink rolled out on an old baking tray until it hissed, then onto the block, then a card face down on top, then rub, rub, rub with the back of Callum's wooden spoon, because I don't have the proper tool and Nana says make do.

Then you peel the card back. That's the moment. The first eight were blotchy. The ninth was perfect, and after that I had the knack.

## Numbers

- cards printed: 52
- blotchy ones, before the knack: 8
- ones with thumbprints: 4 (three of them Callum's)
- cards good enough to send: 40
- clothes pegs borrowed from the whole stair: 40

## The hall

The forty good ones are drying on a string across the hall, pegged up like washing. There is ink on my elbows, my left ear and somehow the back of my neck. Callum stood in the doorway, looked at forty robins hanging in rows, and said they looked like a very small, very angry army. They do.
