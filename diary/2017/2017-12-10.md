## Printing

The kitchen table is too small and too wobbly, so the printing happened on the kitchen floor, on newspaper, on my knees. Ink rolled out on a glass chopping board, the roller back and forth until the ink hissed, then onto the block, then a card face down on top, then rub, rub, rub with the back of a wooden spoon, because I don't have the proper tool and Nana says make do.

Then you peel the card back. That's the moment. The first one was too pale. The second had a thumbprint where the robin's tail should be. The third was perfect.

## Numbers

- cards printed: 52
- cards good enough to send: 40

- cards with Callum's thumbprint specifically: 3
- robins drying on every flat surface in the flat, including the bread bin: all of them

## The floor

There is ink on the lino (the floor kind, which feels like a joke). There is ink on my elbows, my left ear and somehow the back of my neck. Priya came in at eleven, looked at forty robins drying in rows on the floor and said they looked like a very small, very angry army. They do.
