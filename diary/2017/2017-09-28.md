**Pay day!** My first pay from a real job, for two and a half weeks of work. I looked at it on my phone in the office loo for a long time.

Filled in the spreadsheet the minute I got home: the rent column, the food column, and the savings column, which has a number in it that isn't zero for the first time since [the day I started counting](ref:diary:2017-09-01#:~:text=spreadsheet).

:::private
And the overdraft is gone. Cleared. The bank has been lending me money since February and I've been pretending it hasn't, even in here. Gone tonight, all of it, with a bit left over. I sat on the bed and laughed like an idiot.
:::

Bought a bag of good coffee on the way home. Small celebrations.
