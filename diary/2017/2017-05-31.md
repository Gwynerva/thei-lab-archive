The plan for the summer, on the wall where the revision timetable was:

1. line up the point clouds, the before and the after
2. compare them with the 1853 edge and the aerial photos
3. work out how fast, at every point along the bay
4. write it all down (ten thousand words, which sounds a lot until you think about cliffs)
5. figures, maps, the drone photo of the fall
6. hand in by the 18th of August

And, on the side, the notebooks: five by the end of the summer, properly, counting the one I made at the bindery.
