Catching up on the weekend's shows before I forget them:

5. A string quartet playing in a church in the dark, one candle each. The candles went out one by one and they kept on playing. Goosebumps.
6. A comedian who did forty minutes on her nan. Thought of Nana June the whole time and laughed till I cried.

Seven down, four to go, and four days to the deadline. One of those numbers is more frightening than the other.
