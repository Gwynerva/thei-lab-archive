The Fringe through the library window: a man on stilts went past at first-floor height twice this afternoon, and the second time he waved at me. I waved back. Then I formatted forty-one figure captions until midnight.

Two shows down, nine to go, and four days to the deadline. One of those numbers is more frightening than the other.
