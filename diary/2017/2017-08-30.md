**THEY OFFERED ME THE JOB.** Junior GIS analyst at Forthline Mapping, starting Monday the 11th. Dougie rang at half nine this morning while I was still in my pyjamas, and I tried very hard to sound like somebody who wears clothes. I said yes before he got to the salary. (The salary is fine. It's more than nothing, which is what I earn now.)

Rang Mum, who cried. Rang Dad, who said "Good. Leith's got a decent harbour." Rang Nana, who said "Well, of course they did."

Then ran down Leith Walk to the Shore just to look at the building. It looked exactly the same. I didn't.
