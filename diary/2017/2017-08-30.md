**THEY OFFERED ME THE JOB.** Junior GIS analyst at Forthline Mapping, starting Monday the 11th. Dougie rang at ten past eleven while I was in the bath, and I answered dripping and tried very hard to sound like somebody wearing clothes. I said yes before he got to the salary. (The salary is fine. It's more than nothing, which is what I earn now.)

Rang Mum, who cried. Rang Dad, who said I was a good lass and that Leith was handy. Rang Nana, who wanted to know whether it was a proper job, with a pension.

Then ran down Leith Walk to the Shore just to look at the building. It looked exactly the same. I didn't. Priya came home with prosecco, and Callum made chips at midnight, and I sat at the kitchen table and started a spreadsheet, because that's apparently who I am now.
