Bank holiday. Walked out to Cramond and across the causeway to the island at low tide, after checking the tide times twice, the way Dad taught me, and then a third time on the board at the slipway. Ate a sandwich on top of the old gun battery and watched the water start to come back over the sand. Came back across with an hour to spare. Dad would have said forty minutes was plenty.
