Christmas cards this year are going to be **linocuts**, because I've decided, and because the shop ones are £3 each. Bought a block of lino, three gouges, a roller and a tube of black ink. Drew a robin on the block in pencil, head on one side, the way they look at you in Nana's garden. Then spent four hours cutting away everything that isn't a robin.

::media[Cutting along the pencil lines with the smallest gouge]{file="linocut-1"}

Cut myself twice. The robin has a slightly bigger beak than intended. He looks determined. I like him.
