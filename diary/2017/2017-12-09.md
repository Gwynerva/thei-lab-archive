Christmas cards this year are going to be **linocuts**, because I'm skint, and because the shop ones are £3 each. Last weekend I bought a block of lino, a set of cheap gouges, a roller and a tube of black ink, and drew a robin on the block from a photo I took in the Botanic Garden in October, head on one side the way they look at you. I've been cutting every evening since. Tonight I finished cutting away everything that isn't a robin.

::media[Cutting along the pencil lines with the smallest gouge]{file="linocut-1"}

Cut myself twice, and the robin once: his tail is a bit shorter than I planned. He looks determined. I like him. Printing tomorrow.
