The [Fringe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh_Festival_Fringe "the biggest arts festival in the world, and it's on my bus route") started today and the city went from normal to absolutely rammed in about an hour. The Royal Mile is wall-to-wall flyers. I've been handed eleven already and I only went out for milk.

This week's bribe, at four, in a room above a pub: a woman alone on a stage telling the story of her grandmother's allotment, with a watering can and a bag of soil. By the end the whole room was crying, including a man in a kilt at the back who pretended he had something in his eye. I thought about Nana's garden all the way home.
