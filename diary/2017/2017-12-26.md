Boxing Day walk to Sandsend along the beach at low tide — Mum, Clare, Dev and me — the sea flat and silver on one side, the cliffs on the other, and the abbey getting smaller behind us. Nana and Dad came round by road in the van and met us at the far end with a flask of tea and a tin of parkin, and Dad named every fishing boat pulled up on the slipway, and who owned it, and what was wrong with its engine.
