Boxing Day walk, the whole family minus Nana (who stayed by the fire with the crossword): along the West Cliff, down to the sands, out along the pier to the lighthouse, back over the bridge and up to the abbey, where the wind nearly took Dev's hat into the North Sea. Then Saltwick, for me, alone, for half an hour. My cliff is still there. Slightly less of it.
