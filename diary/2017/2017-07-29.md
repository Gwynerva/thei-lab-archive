## Number three

Meall nan Tarmachan, 1,044 metres, the hill of the ptarmigan. Priya drove the borrowed car up to the car park above Loch Tay (I owe her lab-mate a car's worth of petrol), and the path goes up through the grass so gently it feels like cheating. Then the top, and the cairn, and a band of cloud sitting over Ben Lawers that never came any closer.

::media[The cairn, and that band of cloud keeping its distance]{file="munro-cairn-2"}

## The ridge

The Munro is only the start. The ridge goes on west over three more tops, up and down little rocky steps, with lochans in the dips like dropped coins. Priya said "Shall we?" and I said "Obviously" and my legs said nothing, because nobody had asked them.

Lunch on the third top, out of the wind behind a rock, looking down at Loch Tay, which from up there is long and silver and looks like somebody dropped a knife in the glen. Saw no ptarmigan at all. The hill of the ptarmigan, and not one ptarmigan. I want a refund.
