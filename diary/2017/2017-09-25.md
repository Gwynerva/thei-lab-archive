Monday, of course, the sun came out. "Brightening later," said the forecast, and it brightened at eight in the morning, exactly as we were stuffing a soaked tent into the boot. So we went north instead of home: up to the Old Man of Storr in a lifting mist, then round to the Quiraing, where the land has slipped and broken into pinnacles and green steps all the way down to the sea.

Drove home in the dark, gently steaming. Back in Leith at midnight. Work in eight hours. Worth it.
