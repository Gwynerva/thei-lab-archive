"Brightening later," said the forecast for Monday. It didn't. We stuffed a soaked tent into the boot and went north instead of home, to the Quiraing, where the land has slipped and broken into pinnacles and green steps all the way down to the sea. We walked in through cloud. Then, for exactly ten minutes, it lifted: green cliffs, the pinnacles, the fields, the sea, all at once, and both of us standing there with our mouths open. Then it closed again, as if Skye had decided we'd earned that much and no more.

Drove home in the dark, gently steaming, the midges' revenge itching on every bit of skin. Back in Leith at midnight. Work in eight hours. Worth it.
