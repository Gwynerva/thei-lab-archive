*Written on 27 December 2018, on the train back to Edinburgh from Whitby, with a tin of Mum's mince pies on the seat beside me as a deterrent.*

## Music

The year began with a cello. On 16 January, twelve years after I'd put my school cello in a cupboard, I had [my first lesson](ref:event:first-cello-lesson) with Mrs Ogilvie, who found the scratch on the front that I say is shaped like Norway, agreed with me about it, and then had me play an open C string for ten minutes. My first notes in twelve years sounded like a fridge. By the end, a slightly better fridge. The cello itself [went to a luthier](ref:event:cello-to-luthier) in February for a new bridge and a seam glued, and came back sounding like a different, better-behaved instrument.

In March I joined the Leith Scratch Orchestra, which rehearses in a church hall that has never once been warm, and on 8 December I played in [my first concert in twelve years](ref:event:first-concert-2018). Back desk of the cellos. I came in a bar early once, and nobody died.

## Work and code

At Forthline I stopped being entirely new. [The first flood map](ref:event:first-flood-map) with my name in the corner went to a council at the end of June. In the evenings, [tideline reached 1.0](ref:event:tideline-1-0) on 2 April, and in October I gave [my first talk](ref:event:first-talk), about tides, to forty people at a mapping meetup, with my knees knocking so hard I could hear them through the microphone. Somebody asked a question I couldn't answer, and I said "I don't know", and the world carried on. I'm writing that down because I'll need it again.

## Iceland

Eleven days on the ring road with Priya in May, in a small hired car whose heater only worked on the passenger side. Waterfalls you can walk behind, icebergs in a lagoon, a fog in Seyðisfjörður so thick we heard the town before we saw it, and the midges at Mývatn, which I'm still not ready to talk about. [The whole trip](ref:project:iceland) is written up, at length.

## Hills, and a shelf

Seven more Munros, thirteen in all now, with [Schiehallion](ref:event:schiehallion), number ten, in August, inside a cloud. Camping in Glencoe in July, with a rainbow up the glen on the Saturday morning. And in July I finished [the bookshelf for the hall](ref:project:hall-bookshelf), with dovetails I cut myself. Three of the eight are good.

## Family and friends

[Clare married Dev](ref:event:clare-wedding) in York on 8 September, and I made the paper things: invitations, place cards, and a seating plan in the shape of the Ouse. Callum left for Glasgow at the end of August, taking his knives and his good pans, and opened [his café](ref:event:wee-oven-opens) in November. Marta, a violinist from the orchestra, moved into his room two days after he left, and the flat is quieter now, and more musical.

:::private
Nana fell in November and broke her hip. She was in hospital for a fortnight and furious about the food. By Christmas she was back in her own chair, ordering us all about, but she's smaller than she was, and slower on the lane, and I noticed Mum noticing.
:::
