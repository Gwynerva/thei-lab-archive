*Written on 31 December 2019 in Edinburgh, before we go up Calton Hill for the last Hogmanay of the decade.*

## Biscuit

On 9 February [Biscuit came home](ref:event:biscuit-comes-home) from the rescue centre: a ginger tom of about three, with a torn ear and an opinion on everything. He slept on my maps the first night and has slept on every map since. Marta says the flat smells of cat now. Marta is right.

::media[Biscuit in his first week, on the kitchen floor, already in charge]{file="ginger-cat-1" layout="centered"}

## Japan

Two weeks in April, alone, which I'd never done. Kyoto in cherry blossom; [Fushimi Inari at dawn](ref:event:fushimi-inari-dawn) with nobody else on the path; the deer of Nara, who bow if you bow and then mug you for crackers; four days on [the Kumano Kodo](ref:event:kumano-kodo) through cedar forest, sleeping in inns where dinner was twelve small dishes and I could name three. Tokyo at the end, far too big to understand in two days, so I didn't try.

::media[Fushimi Inari at dawn, the gates going on for ever]{file="fushimi-inari-1" layout="stretch"}

## Work

[Promoted](ref:event:promoted "apparently") to GIS developer on 1 May, which mostly means I now get asked the questions I used to ask. A fortnight later [a stranger fixed a bug](ref:event:stranger-fixed-bug#:~:text=bug) in tideline: somebody in another hemisphere had read my code closely enough to find a mistake in the nodal corrections, and was kind about it. In September I talked about floods at [a conference in Amsterdam](ref:event:amsterdam-conference) and ate my own weight in apple cake.

## Walking, and running

[Six days on the Cleveland Way with Dad](ref:event:cleveland-way-walk) in July, from Saltburn to Filey: fifty-odd miles of cliff path, most of it in companionable silence, which is how Dad and I talk best. On the third day Nana was waiting at the top of the lane at Robin Hood's Bay with a pot of tea and opinions about our boots. Nine more Munros, twenty-two in all, with [Ben Lawers](ref:event:ben-lawers), number twenty, in August. And on 6 October I ran [a half marathon in Glasgow](ref:event:half-marathon-race) in one hour, fifty-eight minutes and forty-one seconds, which is under two hours, which was the whole point.

## The rest

- Nana was [ninety](ref:event:nana-90) in June: twelve of us in a cottage built for four, nine candles, one for each decade, and Nana dancing with Dev in the kitchen.
- Priya passed her viva in March and [moved to Plymouth](ref:event:priya-leaves) in September. The flat has been one person too quiet since.
- I started [pottery](ref:project:pottery) on Tuesday evenings and made [one lopsided mug](ref:event:lopsided-mug), which I drink from every day.
- I started learning [Bach's first prelude](ref:project:bach-prelude) on the cello. Twenty-two bars so far. There are forty-two.
- The power went off for a whole night in November, and Marta played Irish tunes by the light of a head torch while I joined in underneath, badly, and it was the best evening of the autumn.

---

*Added in 2021, typing this up:* it was the last Christmas with Nana. We didn't know. She made the mince pies and told me off about my posture, and I'm glad of both.
