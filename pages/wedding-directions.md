We're getting married on Saturday 22 August 2026 at Øvre Lid, our house above Utne in Hardanger: in the orchard if it's dry, in the barn if it isn't. Here's how to find us. If anything here goes wrong on the day, ring Clare, not me.

## From Bergen, by car

1. Take the road east out of Bergen towards Norheimsund, about an hour and a quarter, through a great many tunnels and past Steinsdalsfossen, which you can walk behind if you have ten minutes.
2. Carry on along the fjord to Kvanndal, another forty minutes or so.
3. Take the ferry from Kvanndal to Utne. It runs about every hour and takes twenty minutes. Pay on board.
4. From the ferry quay in Utne, go up the hill past the folk museum and take the second track on the left after the old school. Look for the blue gate with balloons on it.

## Without a car

Take the bus from Bergen to Kvanndal (on Saturdays they're thin, so check), then the ferry as a foot passenger. We'll meet every ferry from eight until half past ten with Jonas's van and Eirik Nesheim's car, and there's a lift back for the evening ferries too.

## From further away

Fly to Bergen and follow the above. Clare has made a spreadsheet of every flight from the UK that weekend, and she will send it to anyone who asks, and to some people who don't.

## Where to sleep

- **The hotel by the quay in Utne**, white, wooden and very old; we've booked most of it, so tell us and we'll put you in.
- **Cabins at the campsite in Kinsarvik**, across the water, a short ferry ride away.
- **Our spare rooms**, for Mum and Dad, Clare and Isla, and Priya. Sorry, everyone else.
- **Tents at the top of the orchard**, if you're brave and own a good mat.

## On the day

- [ ] Shoes you can stand in on grass and gravel. Not heels. Really, not heels.
- [ ] A jumper, even in August: the fjord makes its own weather at about six in the evening.
- [ ] Something for the rain, just in case. The barn is swept, and full of lights.
- [ ] Nothing else. Please, no presents. If you insist, there's a jar for the barn roof.

## Timings

- **11.00** under the old Gravenstein by the road, the big tree you'll pass on the way up
- **12.00** cider, cake and photographs
- **15.00** long tables in the orchard (in the barn, if it rains), and speeches, which Jonas promises will be short
- **after that** music from three friends from the Strykeorkester, dancing on the grass, and the last ferry back to Kvanndal for those who need it
