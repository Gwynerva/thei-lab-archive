*Thirty Walks Around the Fjord* went to the printer on 15 September 2026, after a summer of proofs on the kitchen table, and I still found these afterwards, mostly in bed at two in the morning, one at a time. If you have a copy, here's what to write in the margin. If you find another, please tell me: there's a free print for anyone who does.

## Found after the proofs

1. **Page 14, walk 3**: the gate at the top of the track is green now, not red. Somebody painted it in August.
2. **Page 22, walk 5**: the stretch from the ferry quay to the boathouses is ~~4.6 km~~ 6.4 km. I swapped two digits. The total is right.
3. **Page 31, walk 8**: the church was built in the 1870s, not the 1890s, as the parish has kindly pointed out.
4. **Page 40**: the scale bar on map 11 is upside down. It still measures correctly, if you turn the book over.
5. **Page 57**: Lofthus is spelt with two *f*s, once, which it never has been.
6. **Page 63, walk 22**: the car park has moved down below the bridge.

## From readers of the advance copies

7. **Walk 17**: at the junction above the second gate the path goes left, not straight on. I knew it was wrong before it went, and I'm sorry.
8. **Walk 22**, again: the stream is on the other side of the path, and has been since a flood in 2024. The farmer told me so, firmly.
9. **Walk 26**: "about two hours" is about three hours, unless you are Jonas.
