*written in january 2024. not for anyone else.*

## spring

deck on in january. seven coats of varnish by april. an accordion from a flea market in march that i never learned to play. work getting louder all spring.

20 may: [*Nana June*](ref:event:kayak-launch) went in the water at the slipway by the workshop, at nine in the morning, in sun. she floated. she went straight. Jonas cried. so did i, later, in the van.

::media[the first evening out in her. flat calm.]{file="sea-kayak-1" layout="centered"}

the [Bergenstest](ref:event:bergenstest) in may, results in june: passed. didn't celebrate. no time.

## summer

[Dad, sixty](ref:event:dad-60), 8 july. i'd painted a new board for over the shop door, the old letters in new gold, and brought it over on the plane in a ski bag. he looked at it for a long time and said aye, that'll do. which from Dad is a speech.

Mum retired two weeks later. thirty-four years of other people's children. i watched it on Clare's phone, at my desk.

1 august: [Jonas moved in](ref:event:jonas-moves-in). a bigger flat, still Sandviken. his tools in the hall. i like his tools in the hall.

26 august: put *Nana June* on [a rock at Herdla](ref:event:herdla-rock). my fault. we mended her.

## autumn

the release, september. evenings. weekends.

9 october: couldn't read the screen.

[sick leave](ref:event:sick-leave) from that day until january. *utbrent*, the doctor said. someone to talk to from the 24th. clay from november, [tuesday evenings](ref:event:pottery-again), nothing that mattered, which was the point.

some days the most i did was feed Biscuit and stand at the window watching the rain come in over Askøy. some days that was a lot.

the first bowl that held water: Jonas put it on the table and filled it with clementines as if it were nothing. it wasn't nothing.

## winter

3 december: [the sea at Nordnes](ref:event:first-winter-swim), seven degrees, sleet. forty seconds of not thinking.

christmas in Whitby, quiet. no linocut cards this year. i never cut the block.
