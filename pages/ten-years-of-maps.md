Ten years of the diary is also ten years of maps: the margins, the field notebooks, the routes pencilled onto somebody else's base, the commissions, the atlas. These are the ones I'd carry out if the house were on fire, roughly in order. Most of them started on another map, as all maps do, and became mine in pencil. The rest live with [their projects](/projects/), or under [the maps tag](ref:tag:maps).
