*Written on 31 December 2025 at Øvre Lid, snowed in, happily, with the stove going and Biscuit on the warm end of the bench.*

## The move

We packed the flat in Sandviken all through March, two people, one cat and three and a half years of Bergen into sixty-one boxes, and [moved to Øvre Lid](ref:event:move-to-ovre-lid) on 29 March, in a hired van filled twice, with the cello upright between my knees and Biscuit at my feet, on the ferry from Kvanndal. The house had a roof that leaked, a well we weren't sure of, and no kitchen to speak of. We had a kettle, a camping stove and the biggest view I've ever had from a window.

## Summer

On 10 June I signed [the contract](ref:event:atlas-contract) for *Thirty Walks Around the Fjord*, a walking atlas of Hardanger for Forlaget Fjellstue, and spent the summer and autumn walking all thirty with a notebook and a pencil. Mum and Dad came for a week in July and fell in love with the place, and then with the ferry. At the end of the month we walked the rim of [Vøringsfossen](ref:event:voringsfossen) for the atlas, before the coaches came, and it was the loudest thing I've ever stood next to.

## Svaneti

Eleven days in Georgia in September: Tbilisi, with its balconies and its sulphur baths, then a long road north to Mestia, and [four days on foot](ref:project:svaneti) from village to village to Ushguli, under the towers and the glaciers. [Crossing the river below Adishi glacier](ref:event:adishi-crossing) on a horse, with the water up to its belly, was the most frightened I've been since the knee, and the most alive.

## Autumn

The roof [leaked over the stairs](ref:event:roof-leak) on 28 September, the same week the roofer was booked, which we took as a sign. On 4 October we [pressed the first cider](ref:event:cider-pressing-2025) with the Nesheims and their press: 212 litres, and every one of them sticky. On 18 October [the stove was lit](ref:event:stove-lit) for the first time, and the whole house changed its mind about us.

::media[The first fire in the stove. Biscuit was in front of it within the minute.]{file="video-fire-1" layout="natural"}

The kitchen came out on 3 November, and the new one isn't in yet. [First snow](ref:event:first-snow-2025) on the 15th. We're cooking on the top of the stove and washing up in the bath, and it's fine. Make do and mend.

## Christmas

The first Christmas at Øvre Lid, with Sigrid, and far too much food.

:::private
On Christmas Eve, after the dishes, Jonas said come and look at the stars, and I said it was minus six, and he said come anyway. At the bottom of the orchard, in the snow, with the star I'm named after low in the south (he'd checked), he asked me to marry him, with a ring he'd twisted from the copper wire we stitched *Nana June* with. He got as far as *vil du* before I said yes.
:::

## Three good things

1. The stove.
2. The far bank of the river at Adishi.
3. Whatever this year was, it was ours.
