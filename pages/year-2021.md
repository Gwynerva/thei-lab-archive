*Written on 31 December 2021 in Sandviken, with the fireworks starting over the city and Biscuit under the bed.*

## Leaving

The year started in lockdown again, and with a Norwegian lesson on a laptop on 11 January: *jeg heter Mira*, to a teacher in Trondheim who laughed kindly at my vowels. In February I decided. In March [Nordvåg Kart interviewed me](ref:event:nordvag-interview) on a video call from Bergen, and on the 19th [they offered](ref:event:nordvag-offer). I said yes before they'd finished the sentence.

Then everything was lasts. The last Munro, [Ben Nevis](ref:event:ben-nevis), saved for the end on purpose, in fog so thick at the top that I could see the cairn and nothing else. The last day at Forthline, in the yard behind the office, with Dougie making a speech from an upturned crate. The last night in the flat, on a mattress on the floor, talking to Marta until it got light. Hamish stayed in Scotland. On 31 July I [left Leith](ref:event:leaving-leith) with two suitcases and a sleeping bag; everything else, the cello included, was already on a ship.

## Arriving

Ten days of [quarantine](ref:event:quarantine) in a studio flat on Nordnes, with a view of a slice of fjord between two white houses. Then the door opened, and it was Bergen: wet cobbles, white wooden houses, the smell of the fish market, gulls the size of small dogs. On 13 August [Biscuit landed](ref:event:biscuit-lands) at Flesland in a crate covered in stickers, walked round every room of the new flat with his belly low to the floor, and then lay down in the one patch of sun as if he'd paid for it. First day at Nordvåg on the 16th: nine people, good coffee, and everyone speaking English to me out of kindness and Norwegian to each other out of habit.

## New words

- *regnvær*: rain weather
- *pålegg*: whatever goes on the bread
- *dugnad*: the stairwell, Saturday, everybody
- *kos*: the reason for all the candles
- *takk for sist*: thanks for last time

[More of them](<ref:page:norwegian-words#h:Food> "and the food ones are the best ones"), as I collect them.

Rain: day 1 of the current spell, as I write. It's falling on the fireworks.

## The quilt

On 21 November, a year to the day, I [finished Nana's quilt](ref:event:quilt-finished). Afterwards I sat on the floor with it over my knees for a long time, listening to the rain on the roof, and it was all right.

## And

[Pinnekjøtt at Anna's](ref:event:christmas-2021) on Christmas Eve, my first Christmas away from Whitby that wasn't a lockdown. [The gingerbread town](ref:event:gingerbread-town). [Fløyen](ref:event:floyen), the first of seven mountains. A weekend of [spoon carving](ref:project:spoon-carving) in October: eleven spoons by Christmas, and one of them a spoon.
