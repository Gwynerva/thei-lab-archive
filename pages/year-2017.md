*Written on 31 December 2017 in Leith, on a quiet Hogmanay, with a mince pie from Mum's tin.*

What. A. Year. I started it as a student with a dissertation idea and a bike, and I'm ending it with a degree, a job, a craft-fair stall, six Munros and no bike (RIP, June). Here it is on one page, because Priya says I can't do it, and I like a challenge.

## The degree

The [proposal was accepted](ref:event:proposal-accepted) on 20 January, and from then on everything was the cliffs. In April I spent [a week at Saltwick](ref:event:whitby-fieldwork) with Sam from the department and his drone, and on the Wednesday, 12 April, [I watched the cliff fall](ref:event:saltwick-cliff-fall "only a small one, but still"): a slab of clay the size of a car, at seven minutes past four, with a crack like a plank dropped in an empty hall. I've never been so excited by anything going wrong.

::media[Saltwick Bay from the top, the week of the fieldwork]{file="saltwick-bay-1" layout="stretch"}

Last exam on 26 May. Dissertation in on 18 August. [Graduation](ref:event:graduation-2017) on 29 November, **with distinction**, in a gown made for somebody a foot taller. Mum cried. Dad said he had something in his eye. Nana sent a card with a fiver in it, as if I were nine, and it was the best thing anyone gave me all year.

## Things I made

- a leather wallet, saddle-stitched, in February. Wonky. Love it.
- my first coptic notebooks, after [the Tuesday course at Lowe Bindery](ref:event:first-bindery-evening), and then a great many more
- fourteen notebooks sold at [my first craft fair](ref:event:craft-fair-2017) on 2 December! Fourteen!
- linocut Christmas cards: [a robin](ref:section:linocut-cards/robin), printed on the kitchen floor, ink on everything
- rowan jelly with Nana in September. Mostly she made it. I stirred.

::media[The first coptic notebook, standing up on its own, which is the test]{file="coptic-binding-1" layout="natural"}

## Hills

[Ben Lomond](ref:event:ben-lomond) on 6 May, my first Munro, in jeans (never again). Then five more, ending on Stob Binnein in October. Also Skye in the rain, St Abb's Head and its seabirds, and the Orionids from the Pentlands at one in the morning with a flask. Six Munros. Only 276 to go!

## Work

The [offer from Forthline](ref:event:forthline-offer) came on 30 August, twelve days after I handed in the dissertation, which is the fastest anything has ever happened to me. [First day](ref:event:forthline-first-day) on 11 September. I'm a junior GIS analyst. I have a lanyard! And one Saturday in October I typed the first line of [a tide library](ref:project:tideline), for no reason except Dad's tide table. It doesn't work yet. It will.

## Three good things

1. The cliff falling. Sorry, cliff.
2. Fourteen notebooks.
3. Nana on the phone after graduation, telling me she'd always known I'd be "a great one for maps".

---

Brilliant year. Knackered. Bring on 2018.
