Every map here started at my kitchen table: pencil, then pen, then watercolour, then a scan, then a print on heavy cotton paper by a small printer in Bergen who is more patient with me than I deserve. The [first one sold](ref:event:first-print-sold "a Saturday in September 2024, to a stranger") on 14 September 2024, and I still can't quite believe people want them on their walls.

## The maps

- **Sandviken.** [The first one](ref:section:illustrated-maps/sandviken): the white wooden houses, the steep lanes, our old flat, the sea at the bottom of every street.
- **Bryggen and Vågen.** The wharf, the fish market and every boat that was in the harbour on one cold morning in March.
- **The seven mountains.** All seven of Bergen's on one sheet, with the route I walked [in a single day](ref:event:seven-in-a-day) in June 2024, and the places I sat down.
- **Utne and the fjord.** The village, the ferry quay, the folk museum, and Øvre Lid halfway up the hill.
- **Whitby.** Drawn for the wall behind the till in Dad's shop, and then, after enough customers asked, for everyone else.

## Sizes and prices

:::list{counter="upper-alpha"}
1. A4, unframed, 450 kr
2. A3, unframed, 650 kr
3. A2, unframed, 950 kr: Sandviken and the seven mountains only, because they're the only ones with enough in them
:::

Postage is at cost, which in Norway is more than you'd think, and to the UK and the rest of Europe about 120 kr more. Everything goes flat between two boards, never rolled. ~~Framed prints~~ I tried once, and the glass arrived in Stavanger in eleven pieces.

## Commissions

A map of your street, your walk, your wedding, the place you grew up. They start at 4,500 kr for an A3 and take about six weeks, most of which is me walking the place, or asking you very detailed questions about it. I take four a season; [this is what they're like](ref:section:illustrated-maps/commissions).
