*Last updated on 15 October 2026, at the kitchen table, with the stove going.*\
If you asked me over a cup of tea what I'm up to, this is what I'd say.

## The atlas

*Thirty Walks Around the Fjord* went to the printer on 15 September: thirty walks, thirty maps, a year and a half of walking and drawing. The printed sheets are due [tomorrow](ref:diary:today+1 "the kitchen table is cleared for them"), for me to check before they're bound, and I'm not sleeping especially well. The mistakes that turned up too late are on [the errata page](ref:page:atlas-errata), which I'm told every book has and which I'm trying not to take personally.

## Teaching

The autumn term of [my QGIS course](ref:project:qgis-course), Monday evenings, online: twelve students and one finished map each by December. This group runs from a hydrologist in Tromsø to a farmer near Voss who wants a map of his own drainage. We're three weeks in, and nobody has given up yet.

## The house and the barn

The kitchen is done, the windows are done, and now [the barn is next](ref:section:farmhouse/now "the north half of the roof first"). The roofer comes [the day after tomorrow](ref:diary:today+2) to measure up. Jonas has drawn the roof twice and I've drawn it once, badly, for luck.

## The orchard

We pressed the second year's apples with the Nesheims on [3 October](ref:event:cider-pressing-2026): 257 litres, forty-five more than last year, thanks to Sigrid's pruning, which she has mentioned. The [first frost](ref:event:first-frost-2026) came on the twelfth and took the last of the nasturtiums.

## The rest

- **Swimming:** in the fjord again since 1 October, which makes [this winter](ref:section:cold-water/this-winter) my fourth. Eleven degrees and falling.
- **The cello:** the Elgar serenade with the Strykeorkester, when I can get the ferry and the bus to Bergen for the big rehearsals.
- **The loom:** still on the first weave, the view from the landing in five bands of wool: orchard, fjord, far shore, mountain, ice. It has drawn in at the middle like a waist, and I am unreasonably proud of it.
- **Biscuit:** about ten, slower on the stairs, faster than ever when the fridge opens. He has claimed the warm end of the bench by the stove.
