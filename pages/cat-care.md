Thank you for looking after him. Biscuit is a ginger tom of about ten, adopted from a rescue centre in Edinburgh in 2019, flown to Norway in 2021, and now in charge of an old farmhouse in Hardanger. He is friendly, greedy, and a good deal less fragile than he'd like you to think.

## Every day

- [ ] Breakfast early (he will tell you exactly when) and dinner at about five (see below)
- [ ] Check he has eaten it: if he leaves two meals running, ring the vet
- [ ] Fresh water in the bowl in the kitchen **and** the one by the stove; he won't drink from the same one twice running
- [ ] The litter tray in the back porch, once a day
- [ ] Count him in at night, then lock the cat flap with the little red switch

## Food

Half a pouch of the wet food morning and evening, and a small handful of the dry, no more, whatever he tells you. The full list of [what he eats and what he pretends to eat](<ref:project:biscuit#h:Things he eats> "and what he steals") is in his project. He is not allowed brown cheese, however he looks at you. He knows where it's kept.

## Habits

- He sleeps on the warm end of the bench by the stove, or on whatever map is on the kitchen table.
- He talks a great deal at about four in the morning. It means nothing. Ignore him.
- At dusk he sits by the barn door and stares at the swallows. He hasn't caught one in two summers and won't start now.

## If he goes missing

Don't panic. He [stayed out all night once](ref:event:biscuit-missing "and came back at breakfast as if nothing had happened"), in June 2025. Walk round the barn and the orchard calling him, shake the box of dry food, and ring us. Eirik and Kari Nesheim, down the road, know him and will help look.

## The vet

The clinic in Odda, forty-five minutes along the fjord. His papers are in the blue folder on the shelf by the door, and the carrier is on top of the wardrobe in the back bedroom.

The spare key is in the old coffee tin on the beam inside the barn door, behind the paint. Please put it back.
