Nothing is going to happen. But Nana June left everything in order, in a tin, with a list on top, and it made the worst week of Mum's life a little less bad. So this is my list. It's private: only I can see it here, and Jonas and Clare each have a printed copy in an envelope.

## Ring first

1. Jonas.
2. Clare, who will ring Mum and Dad, gently, and in the right order.
3. Anna in Bergen, who will tell the people from Nordvåg and the orchestra.
4. Priya, who will tell everyone else.

The numbers are in my phone under *ICE*, and on the card pinned behind the kitchen door.

## Papers

- **The will** is with the solicitor in Odda. A copy is in the fireproof box in the barn office, bottom shelf, under the atlas contract.
- **The house**: the rent-to-own agreement with Ragnhild's family, and the deeds as they stand, are in the same box.
- **Passwords**: there's a password manager, and Jonas knows how to get into it. Its recovery sheet is in the fireproof box too. No password is written here, and none ever will be.
- **Money**: the business account and the house account are at the same bank. The accountant's details are on my profile, under a private link.
- **Pensions**: a small one in the UK from Forthline and a small one here from Nordvåg; the letters are in the green folder.

## Biscuit

[Everything about Biscuit](ref:page:cat-care) is on his page. He goes with Jonas. If Jonas can't have him, he goes to the Nesheims, who have already said yes.

## What I'd like

- Something small, in the orchard if the snow allows, and a cello if anybody can face playing it.
- My drawings to Clare, to keep for Isla when she's older.
- Nana's quilt to Isla, when she's old enough not to spill things on it.
- [This site](ref:page:about) kept online for as long as it's cheap, the public things public and the private things private. Don't tidy it up.
