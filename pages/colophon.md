This site is a personal archive: a small program I host myself, which keeps the diary, the projects, the events and these pages, and the links between them. It runs on a rented server in a data centre somewhere near Oslo and costs me, each month, about the same as two cinnamon buns.

## How things get in

- **[The diary](/diary/)** I write most evenings, straight in, on the laptop at the kitchen table. The years 2016 to 2020 were [typed up from paper](ref:section:this-archive/moving-in) in 2021, about one notebook a month.
- **Projects and events** I add when something starts, and fill in as it goes. Some are never finished, which is honest, at least.
- **Pictures** are mine, mostly, taken on a phone that has been dropped into two fjords. A few old maps and paintings come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), and each of those says where it came from and under which licence.
- **Sounds** come from a small recorder in my coat pocket; there's [a page about the kit](ref:page:recording-kit).

## Rules I keep

1. Nothing is deleted to make me look better. Typos stay. Bad ideas stay.
2. Private things are marked private, and stay private.
3. Dates are written as days. Where I don't know the day, I say how sure I am.
4. Links are checked once a year. Some still die in between, and I'm sorry.
