A Munro is a Scottish mountain over 3,000 feet. There are 282 of them, and people who have climbed every one are called compleators, with that spelling. I am not one. I climbed thirty-four between May 2017 and July 2021, and then moved to a country where nobody counts mountains like this, which is either very sensible or very sad. The rules I kept are in [the project](ref:project:munros); [the map I coloured in](ref:project:munro-map) as I went is still online, frozen at thirty-four.

The dates are the days I stood on top, except in 2020, when I lost count and took them from the photos afterwards.

### 2017

1. Ben Lomond, 6 May: [the first](ref:event:ben-lomond "with Priya, in jeans and a borrowed waterproof")
2. Ben Vorlich (Loch Earn), 17 June
3. Meall nan Tarmachan, 29 July
4. Ben Chonzie, 26 August, *the dullest hill in Scotland, and I'd do it again*
5. Bla Bheinn, 24 September, on Skye, in the rain
6. Stob Binnein, 28 October

### 2018

7. Ben More, 21 April
8. Stob Dubh of Buachaille Etive Beag, 16 June
9. Bidean nam Bian, 7 July, the Glencoe weekend
10. Schiehallion, 11 August: **[number ten](ref:event:schiehallion)**
11. Cairn Gorm, 15 September
12. Ben Macdui, 16 September, *the second highest in Britain, and the first time I was properly cold in September*
13. Ben Vane, 13 October

### 2019

14. Beinn Ghlas, 11 May
15. Ben Ledi, 25 May
16. Stuc a' Chroin, 8 June
17. Beinn Dorain, 27 July
18. Beinn an Dothaidh, 3 August
19. Ben Challum, 17 August
20. Ben Lawers, 31 August: **[number twenty](ref:event:ben-lawers)**
21. Lochnagar, 14 September
22. Mount Keen, 28 September

### 2020

23. Ben Oss, 27 June, the first hill after lockdown
24. Ben Vorlich (Loch Lomond), 11 July
25. Beinn Ime, 8 August
26. Ben Narnain, 29 August
27. Carn Liath, 5 September, a week before [the knee](ref:project:knee "which ended the year's hills")

### 2021

28. Driesh, 24 April
29. Mayar, 25 April
30. Ben Lui, 15 May
31. Braigh Coire Chruinn-bhalgain, on Beinn a' Ghlo, 29 May
32. Sgurr nan Gillean, 12 June, with a guide, and I'm not ashamed
33. Aonach Mòr, 19 June
34. Ben Nevis, 3 July: **[the one I saved for last](ref:event:ben-nevis "in fog, as it turned out")**

Thirty-four down, 248 to go, from Hardanger, by ferry and plane. I'm not holding my breath, but I've kept the boots.
