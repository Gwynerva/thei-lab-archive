I'm Mira Testwell. I make maps for a living, mostly by hand these days, and I make most other things by hand too: notebooks, a kayak, a quilt that was mostly my grandmother's, cider, a kitchen (with help). I grew up in Whitby, on the Yorkshire coast, spent five years in Edinburgh and Leith and four in Bergen, and since the spring of 2025 I've lived in an old farmhouse above the Hardangerfjord with Jonas, a ginger cat called Biscuit and forty apple trees.

This site is where all of it is kept. On the first of October 2016 I decided to keep a diary "properly, in one place, for once", and for once I did. It was paper for the first few years; in 2021 I typed the lot in here, starting in the second lockdown in Leith and finishing on winter evenings in Bergen, with the rain going sideways past the window. There are about two thousand diary entries now, seventy-odd projects, a couple of hundred events, and these pages.

## How to read this site

There's no right way in. A few doors:

- **[The diary](/diary/)** is the spine of it: one entry a day, most of them short, some of them very much not. Open it anywhere, or start at [the very first entry](ref:diary:2016-10-01 "a Saturday, in a flat off Leith Walk").
- **[All projects](/projects/)** are the things I made, learned or did on purpose, each with its stages, from a dissertation about falling cliffs to [a five-metre kayak](<ref:project:kayak#h:The launch> "and the day it floated") and [the house](ref:project:farmhouse).
- **Events** are the days that mattered, large and small. They sit beside the diary, and all of them together make [my life on one line](/life/).
- **[Tags](/tags/)** cut across everything. Try [Biscuit](ref:tag:biscuit), or [rain](ref:tag:rain), of which there is a great deal.

### Some things worth knowing

- Where I'm not sure of a date, I've said so, and roughly how unsure.
- The spelling is British. The Norwegian is mine, which is to say not always right.
- Some things are private and will stay that way. If a page seems to stop halfway through a thought, that's usually why.
- Old entries sound young. I've left them as they were, exclamation marks and all.

## Why

Because I forget. Because Nana June kept every letter anyone ever sent her, and when she died [they turned out to be the best thing she left](ref:project:nana-letters "a toffee tin of forty-one letters"). Because a map is only any use if somebody drew it down, and I've come to think a life is the same.
