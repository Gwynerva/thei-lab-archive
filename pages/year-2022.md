*Written on 28 December 2022, on the last night in Whitby, in my old room, with Jonas asleep in the box room because Mum has standards.*

## Jonas

On 15 January I went on [a kayak course](ref:event:kayak-course), because I'd decided, out of nowhere, to build a kayak, and wanted to know what one felt like first. The instructor was a boatbuilder called Jonas, who explained a wet exit as if it were the most interesting thing in the world, and then showed me, upside down, in a fjord, in January. Four weeks later we [had waffles](ref:event:first-date). By March I'd learned the word *forelsket*, and used it on everyone.

## Lofoten

Nine days in March with Priya, who flew up from Plymouth with a suitcase full of thermals. Reine under snow. Henningsvær, with its football pitch on a rock in the sea. After two nights of cloud, on 8 March, at about ten at night, [the sky moved](ref:event:northern-lights): green, then brighter green, then a pink edge right overhead, moving so fast that I ducked. We lay on the bonnet of the hire car for two hours and said "oh" about forty times each. I forgot to take a single photograph, so these are from other nights.

## The kayak

[The strongback](ref:event:strongback) in February, copper wire through the spring, and glass in the autumn, in [Jonas's workshop](<ref:project:kayak#h:Plans> "plans, plywood, and how it started"), three evenings a week after work. It's beginning to look like a boat. Jonas says it has always looked like a boat. I'm learning a great many [new words](ref:page:boat-words).

## Dad

On 5 September Mum rang at ten to six in the morning, which she never does. [Dad's heart](ref:event:dad-heart): a blocked artery, a stent, five days in hospital in Middlesbrough complaining about the tea and the parking and the lack of anything to fix. I was at his bedside by the evening, via Amsterdam. He asked who was minding the cat. He's fine. He's walking up the 199 steps again, slowly, pretending it's for the view.

## Rain

[Twenty-one days in a row](ref:event:rain-streak), 3 to 23 October. I wrote [an app to count them](ref:project:rain-counter) as a joke, recorded the rain on the roof on the sixth day, and by the twentieth had stopped finding any of it funny. *Rain: day 21.* Then on the 24th the sun came out, and the whole of Sandviken stood in the street with their faces turned up like plants. [Why it does this](ref:page:why-bergen-rains) took me a page.

## And

- [Thirty](ref:event:birthday-30), on 14 March, two days after Lofoten, with a cake Jonas made, π piped on top, and a reel of copper wire for a present, which I've decided is romantic.
- Hardanger in blossom in May, and Jonas's mother Sigrid, who looked at me hard and then took me out to show me which of her apple trees were lazy.
- [My first 17 May](ref:event:first-17-mai): national costumes, flags, children, and hot dogs at nine in the morning.
- [An audition](ref:event:orchestra-audition) in August, at thirty, for Sandviken Strykeorkester. I got in. Back desk again. I like the back desk.
- [Christmas in Whitby](ref:event:christmas-2022), and Jonas meets the family. Dad took him down to the shop and they talked about rope for two hours.
