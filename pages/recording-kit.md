In October 2022, on the sixth day of [twenty-one days of rain](ref:event:rain-streak), I put a recorder on the windowsill of the flat in Sandviken and [recorded the rain on the roof](ref:event:rain-recorded). That became [Sounds of Bergen](ref:project:field-recordings), and this is what I carried about for the two years after.

## The kit

- **A small handheld recorder** with two microphones crossed on top, the kind journalists use, bought second-hand from a man in Åsane who'd used it for one wedding.
- **Two small omnidirectional microphones** as well, about the size of the end of a pencil, on short cables, so I can clip them to my collar or put them either side of a window.
- **A furry windshield**, known to everyone as the dead cat, which Biscuit hated on sight and attacked twice. Without it, wind is all you hear.
- **Spare AA batteries**, always, in an inside pocket where they stay warm. Cold batteries die in minutes on a winter quay.
- **Closed headphones**, to hear what the microphones hear, which is never what you thought you were hearing.

## What I'd buy again

All of it except the cheap tripod, which blew into Vågen. ~~A better recorder~~: the small one was fine. What makes a recording good is standing still for long enough, in the right place, at the right time, and not sneezing.
