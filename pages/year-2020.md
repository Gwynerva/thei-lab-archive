*Written on 31 December 2020 in Leith. It isn't going to be a long one.*

## Spring

Day 1 of lockdown was 23 March. On day 2 I went for a walk, and then I went for one every day for the rest of the year. On day 7 [Hamish](ref:event:hamish-born) was born, in a jam jar on the kitchen windowsill. By day 30 I was baking [bread for the whole stair](ref:event:bread-for-the-stair), and the orchestra was meeting [on a video call](ref:event:orchestra-call), forty cellos out of sync, laughing.

::media[One of about a hundred loaves. They got better.]{file="sourdough-1" layout="centered"}

## Summer

[The prelude, recorded](ref:event:prelude-recorded) on 20 June: all forty-two bars, take thirty-eight of forty-one, with a wobble in bar 22. [The hundredth walk](ref:event:walk-100) on 1 July, the same way as the first: down Leith Walk, along the Shore, up the river. The balcony grew tomatoes. The Raleigh from the skip [rode again](ref:event:raleigh-first-ride). On 22 August I swam in the sea at Portobello for the first time, and screamed, and went back the next week.

::audio[The prelude, 20 June, take thirty-eight]{file="music-bach-cello-1" title="Prelude, first suite"}

## Autumn

[The knee](ref:event:knee-goes) went on 12 September, on a run, on a kerb, with a noise. Physio from the 24th, with Morag, twice a week, and exercises on the kitchen floor with Biscuit lying on whichever leg I needed. In October Biscuit got out of the skylight and strolled along the ridge of the roof between the chimney pots for an hour, four floors up, and I have never been so cross with anyone I love. The walks got shorter. They didn't stop.

## November

Nana went into hospital on the 10th.

[She died on the 21st](ref:event:nana-dies), aged ninety-one.

[The funeral](ref:event:nana-funeral) was on 3 December. Twenty people in a cold chapel.

---

I finished the sweater on 19 December. We couldn't go to Whitby, so I had Christmas in Leith with Marta, and we roasted a chicken, because a turkey felt like lying.

> Make do and mend.

Two hundred and eighty-three walks. I'm going out for the last one now.
