Woke at seven because the radiator clanked, saw the roofs white, and thought: right. Arthur's Seat before anyone else gets there.

Out of the door by half seven with my hat, my gloves and a cereal bar. The grass on the lower slopes crunched, every blade furry with frost, and my breath hung in front of me like a speech bubble with nothing in it. Up past the ruined chapel and the last steep bit to the top, where it was just me, one man, and his very cold dog.

The city below was all chimney smoke and pale light, the Forth flat as tin, Fife still half in shadow. I counted the hills I could name (eight) and the ones I couldn't (lots).

::media[The one leaf I stopped to photograph, with my glove in the way]{file="frost-2"}

Back down by nine for porridge. First frost of the winter, officially logged.
