My first Hogmanay in Edinburgh, and Callum got us tickets for the street party on Princes Street. Tens of thousands of people, a band I couldn't see, a hot dog that cost five pounds, and at midnight the fireworks came off the castle rock all at once and the whole street said "ooh" together like one big animal.

Then Auld Lang Syne, arms crossed, with a couple from Ohio on one side and a man in a kilt on the other who knew all the verses and sang every one of them.

Walked home down Leith Walk at two with half of Leith, everyone wishing everyone a good new year.

Resolutions, written on the back of my ticket:

1. Climb a Munro.
2. Learn to bind a book properly.
3. Finish the dissertation. On time.
4. Fewer biscuits.

I'll report back.
