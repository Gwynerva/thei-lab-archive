Our first field trip! Twenty of us in a minibus to Glenmore, by Loch Morlich, with boxes of kit that cost more than my entire life.

The idea was to learn :hint[GNSS]{note="satellite positioning: GPS, and its Russian, European and Chinese cousins"} surveying properly. You set up a base station on a known point, walk about with a receiver on a pole, and the two of them argue with the satellites until you know where you are to about two centimetres. Two centimetres! In a forest! My phone thinks I'm in the loch half the time.

::media[The receiver on its pole, looking very pleased with itself]{file="surveying-instrument-1"}

Saturday we mapped a stretch of path and a burn. Sunday it rained sideways and the receiver lost its fix every time we went under the pines, so we learned about multipath, which is a posh word for "trees". Back on the minibus at four, steaming gently.

What I'll remember: the moment the numbers settled and the dot on the screen stopped wandering. It felt like the world had agreed to stand still for me.
