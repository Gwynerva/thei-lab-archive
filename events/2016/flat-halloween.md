Halloween party at ours, two days early, because everyone has a deadline on the actual day.

Priya came as a jellyfish (an umbrella, a lot of ribbon, her PhD in marine biology finally paying off). Callum made a soup and served it *in the pumpkin*, which he says is a thing chefs do and I say is showing off. I went as a contour line. Nobody got it. I had brown wool pinned round me in loops with little numbers on, 100, 110, 120, and I explained it eleven times. One person said "oh, a snail". Fine.

When everyone had gone home the three of us curled up on the sofa under one blanket and watched *Sintel*, which Priya had never seen, and she cried at the end, which I had warned her about.

::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eRsGyueVLvQ"}

Best Halloween yet. Next year: a costume people understand.
