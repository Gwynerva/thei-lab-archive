It's GIS Day! (Every year, the Wednesday of Geography Awareness Week. I didn't know this either until last month.)

The department had posters up all afternoon, a quiz with a terrible prize, and a cake shaped like a globe that someone had iced with the continents slightly wrong. Australia was enormous. I said so. Nobody thanked me.

The best part was a talk by a man from a small company in Leith called Forthline Mapping, about surface-water flooding: what happens when rain falls faster than the drains can take it, and how you work out where it goes. He had maps of streets I walk down every day, coloured blue where the water would sit. I asked a question about rain gauges and went bright red, but he said it was a good one.

Note to self: Forthline. Leith. Flood maps. Twelve people. Remember them.
