First Saturday at the Spoke Room! It's a bike co-op in a basement off Leith Walk, down eleven steps that are always wet, and it smells of chain oil and digestive biscuits. You bring your broken bike, someone shows you how to fix it, and you pay what you can. I'm on the other side of the counter now, which is terrifying, because I've only ever fixed my own bike, and badly.

What I did today:

- two punctures (one of them mine, from cycling there)
- a brake cable on a lovely old Dawes with a wicker basket
- watched the man who runs it true a wheel, and pretended I understood

They pay me in tea and twenty pounds a day, which is a fortune when you're a student. The best thing is [the tool wall](ref:section:spoke-room/tools): every spanner has its outline painted behind it, so you can see at a glance what's missing. I want one for my whole life.

Knackered, oily, brilliant. Same again next Saturday.
