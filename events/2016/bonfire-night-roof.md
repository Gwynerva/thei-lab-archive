Bonfire night. Callum discovered that the skylight above the top landing opens all the way, and that if you're careful (we were not very careful) you can sit in the gully between two slopes of the roof with your back against the chimney.

So we did. Three of us, two blankets, a flask of Callum's mulled cider, and the whole city going off around us: Calton Hill, somebody's garden in Pilrig, a big display out towards Portobello, and the odd rocket from the Links that went sideways.

::media[What we could see from the chimney, more or less]{file="edinburgh-skyline-2"}

The best bit was the smell: gunpowder, cold slate, cinnamon.

Not telling Mum about this one. Or the landlord. Which is why it isn't public.
