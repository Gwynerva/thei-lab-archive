Never again. (I say this every time.)

Three deadlines, all at nine on Friday morning, all of which I had "basically started". At ten last night I made a list on the back of an envelope and stuck it to the laptop:

- [x] Spatial databases report, 2,000 words, the one about bus stops
- [x] Remote sensing lab: land cover of the Lothians from Landsat
- [x] Cartography poster, A1, the Water of Leith from source to sea
- [ ] Sleep

Callum came in at two from a late shift and made me cheese on toast without being asked. Priya left a note on the kettle saying COURAGE. The poster printed at 8.40 at the print shop on Nicolson Street with a big smudge across Balerno, which I have decided is artistic.

::media[The map for the poster, halfway through the night, before the smudge]{file="qgis-screenshot-3"}

Handed in all ~~two~~ three by five to nine. Slept on the bus home and missed my stop by four.

Knackered. Proud. Mostly knackered.
