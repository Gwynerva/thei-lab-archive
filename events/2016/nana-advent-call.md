Nana rang at six, as she does on the first of December every single year, to ask whether I'd opened the first window of my advent calendar. I'm twenty-four. I had. (Mum posts me one. Chocolate. Every year.)

We talked for forty minutes. Her knees, the rowan tree, which neighbour's cat is digging up her bulbs. Then, because it was dark and she was in that mood, she told me the star story again: Grandad Arthur on the deck of his ship, pointing up at the Whale and finding Mira for her.

> The wonderful one. It comes and goes, but it always comes back.

She says that's why I'm called what I'm called, and why I always come home for Christmas. I said that's because of her mince pies. She said that too.

I didn't tell her I'd been crying over my coursework before she rang. I think she knew anyway.

Private, because it's hers as much as mine.
