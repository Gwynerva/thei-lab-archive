Released tideline 1.0 this afternoon, Easter Monday, at the kitchen table, with Priya counting down from ten for reasons of drama.

Since [the first commit](ref:event:tideline-first-commit) in October it has grown from two constituents to thirty-seven, learned nodal corrections (the moon's orbit wobbles over 18.6 years and the tide wobbles with it), and stopped being forty minutes out. Against the official Whitby predictions for April it is now within four minutes and six centimetres at every high water. I checked every one, with Dad on the phone reading the yellow booklet aloud.

What 1.0 means, to me:

- the numbers are right, or right enough, for the harbours I've tried
- the code is tidy enough that I'm not embarrassed by it
- there is a README with a large warning in capitals

That warning matters. tideline is not for navigation. If you take a boat out on my numbers, that's on you.
