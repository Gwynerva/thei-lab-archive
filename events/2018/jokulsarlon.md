I have been trying for an hour to describe the blue.

Jökulsárlón is a lagoon at the foot of Breiðamerkurjökull, a tongue of the biggest glacier in Iceland, and the glacier calves into it, so the whole lagoon is full of icebergs drifting slowly towards a short river and the sea. Some are white and dull like old snow. Some are striped black with ash from eruptions centuries ago. And some, the ones that have just rolled over and shown their undersides, are a blue I don't have a word for: deep and clear and glowing, as if there were a lamp inside.

::media[The whole lagoon, end to end, from the shingle bank]{file="jokulsarlon-5" layout="stretch"}

A seal came up beside the bank and looked at us for a long time with an expression of mild disappointment, then sank.

Across the road, on the black beach, pieces of ice that had made it out to sea were washed back up on the sand, glittering like broken glass. Priya called it the diamond beach before we found out that everyone else calls it that too.

We stayed till eleven at night. It was still light. The ice went pink.
