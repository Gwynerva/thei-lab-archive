Marta Kowalczyk moved in today. She plays second violin in the Scratch Orchestra (I sit behind her on Tuesdays and know her shoulders better than her face), she's a data analyst at a bank, and when Priya and I put a card up at rehearsal saying ROOM, LEITH, NO SMOKERS, NO DRUMMERS, she rang within the hour.

She arrived with a violin case, two suitcases, a box of books in three languages, and a large plant called Grażyna, which she carried up all five flights herself because "Grażyna doesn't trust movers".

To christen the kitchen she made pierogi, the three of us round the table pinching the edges, Marta correcting our technique without mercy. Mine leaked. Priya's were perfect. Marta's were art.

Tonight, from her room, scales on the violin, slow and careful. From mine, scales on the cello. Priya says she's moving into the bathroom.

I think this is going to be good.
