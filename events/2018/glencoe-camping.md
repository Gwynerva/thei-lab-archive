A long weekend camping in Glencoe with Priya, at the campsite by the river where you can see the Pap from the tent door. Still the heatwave, mostly: clear sky, warm rock, and midges in numbers I can only describe as geological.

Saturday, Bidean nam Bian, the highest hill in Argyll and Munro number nine, up through the Lost Valley where the MacDonalds hid their stolen cattle, then a long scramble to the top. Hot and enormous and worth it. Sunday, a lazy walk to the lochan and a swim in water so cold it made me shout.

What I packed, for future me, who always forgets something:

- the tent (Priya's, with the poles this time)
- kitchen
  - stove and two gas canisters
  - one pan, two sporks, a lighter, and a spare lighter
- clothes
  - one layer more than seems sensible
  - the good socks
- midge net (essential)
- midge spray (useless)
- Nana's tartan blanket
