## Wednesday

The snow came in sideways off the Forth, the warning went from amber to red, and at eleven Dougie sent everyone home "while the buses still run". They did not still run. I walked up Leith Walk through snow to the shins with a stream of other office people in the wrong shoes, all of us laughing slightly hysterically.

## Thursday

Nothing moved. No buses, no trains, no bin lorries; the shop on the corner sold out of bread by ten and milk by twelve. The whole street was out in the middle of the road, because there was no reason not to be. Children built a snowman at the bus stop and gave it a timetable.

Priya, Callum and I went down the slope behind Calton Hill on a baking tray and a bin bag. The baking tray is faster. The baking tray is also now a different shape.

## Friday

Snowed in again. In the evening, under every blanket in the flat, we watched a short film about llamas and a penguin in the snow, which felt appropriate.

::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SkVqJ1SGeL0"}

By Saturday it had turned to rain and grey slush, as if it had never happened. Edinburgh does not hold a grudge.
