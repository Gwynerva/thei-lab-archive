Schiehallion today: Munro number ten. Double figures! This year's, so far:

7. Ben More, in April, in snow
8. Stob Dubh of Buachaille Etive Beag, in June
9. Bidean nam Bian, in Glencoe, in midges
10. Schiehallion, today, in cloud

Schiehallion is a beautiful lonely cone in the middle of Perthshire, and for a cartographer it's holy ground. In 1774 astronomers came here to weigh the Earth, by measuring how far the mountain pulled a plumb line sideways. To work out the mountain's mass they had to know its shape, and the mathematician doing the sums, faced with thousands of surveyed heights, joined up the points of equal height with lines. :hint[Contour lines]{note="lines joining places of the same height; the closer together they are, the steeper the ground"}, on this hill. I was embarrassingly moved.

The path is a long boulder field and the top was in cloud, so I saw nothing at all from the summit. It didn't matter. I ate my sandwich on the rocks and drew contours round my boot in the grit.
