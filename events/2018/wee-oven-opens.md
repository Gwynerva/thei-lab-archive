The Wee Oven is open! Callum's café, in a little shop in the West End of Glasgow, with a blue door, six tables, a counter of bread and a big old oven at the back that he talks to.

Marta and I got the train through first thing to be there when the doors opened. There was a queue. A real queue, down the street, in the rain, for my old flatmate's scones! I've never been so proud of a scone.

Inside: the smell of his kitchen in Leith, exactly, which nearly finished me off. Sourdough, cheese scones, a cardamom bun I'll be thinking about for the rest of my life, soup with a slab of bread, and coffee in mismatched cups from charity shops. Callum behind the counter in a black apron, flour on his eyebrows, not stopping for a second.

He came out at two and sat with us for exactly four minutes, and said "I can't believe it's real". It's real. It was full all day.

We took a loaf home on the train, and it's on the kitchen table now, half gone. A café of his own, at last, and somewhere for the rest of us to go.
