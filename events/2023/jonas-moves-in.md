new flat. Same hill, three streets up, a white house with a blue door and a kitchen you can turn round in. Jonas's van, two trips, his tools in the hall until further notice.

we are :hint[*samboere*]{note="people who live together without being married"} now. There is a form for it. Of course there is.

what arrived:

- one sofa (his), one armchair (mine)
- four frying pans, which is three too many
- a framed chart of the Hjeltefjord
- Biscuit, who inspected every room, then sat in the empty bath and would not leave it

The barometer Sverre gave me went on the wall by the door. Jonas tapped it, the way you're meant to, and it went down.

:::private
I'm glad. I am. And I lay awake listening to someone else breathing and thought: what if he sees how tired I am, all the time. And what if he doesn't.
:::
