two dates for one thing. The test on a Saturday in May. The result on a Thursday in June.

the test: four hours in a room with forty others, a pencil, a sandwich in my bag I never ate. Reading, listening, writing. Then a conversation with two examiners who were kind, and did not laugh when I said *hyggelig* where I meant *viktig*.

the writing task was about whether towns should close their centres to cars. I wrote about Leith Walk. They will have been confused.

::file[Two years of lists. The first year's, typed up]{file="norwegian-words" title="Words, first year"}

then five weeks of not thinking about it, very hard.

:::spoiler
Passed. B2, all four parts. I read it on my phone in the stairwell and then sat down on the stairs and cried a bit, which was not about the test.
:::
