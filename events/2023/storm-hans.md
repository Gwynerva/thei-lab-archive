the storm has a name, Hans, and a red warning for the east. We watched it on the news from the sofa that is now ours: rivers in the valleys out over the roads, whole fields gone brown, the railway over the mountains shut.

here, only rain. Ordinary Bergen rain, sideways, two days of it. Sverre's barometer dropped and stayed down. [Storm Hans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Hans "the name the forecasters gave it") on the screen, Hans at the window.

Jonas rang his mother. The apples in Hardanger were fine. Sigrid was more worried about the wasps.

the lights flickered at eight and came back. We watched a short film, the one with the shepherd girl and the dog and the mountain, and I fell asleep before the end, which I do now.
