the release. Forty-one harbour charts of the west coast, new survey data, a new symbol set, all due the last Friday in September, because in March everyone agreed on that date while it was far away.

four weeks of it:

1. checking soundings against the survey, harbour by harbour
2. fixing what the new symbols broke
3. fixing the fixes
4. a launch party I left after twenty minutes

it went out on the 29th. All forty-one. Anna sent a message with a lot of exclamation marks. I read it in bed at nine at night and didn't answer.

:::private
I cried in the toilets on the Tuesday of the second week and couldn't have said why. On the Wednesday of the third I read the same paragraph of a spec for forty minutes. I'm putting it here because I won't say it to anyone. Not yet.
:::
