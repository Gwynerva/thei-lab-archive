the deck went on today. Two panels of 3 mm okoume bent over the beams and held down with tape, clamps and every last bit of copper wire on the reel. Jonas did the bow, I did the stern, and neither of us said a word for an hour.

then it was a boat. Not a hull on a strongback, not a project: a closed thing with a cockpit hole in it, a shape you could put in water.

cold in the workshop. Minus four outside, and the epoxy wouldn't kick until Jonas dragged the heater over and pointed it at the stern like a hairdryer.

sat on an upturned bucket afterwards and drew her in the margin of the cut list. Eight weeks since [the first deck beam](ref:section:kayak/deck). Varnish next, he says. Seven coats, he says.

We'll see.
