Tove asked what I used to do with my hands that wasn't for anyone. I said pottery, once, in Edinburgh. She said: well, then.

so. Tuesday evenings, a studio in an old warehouse by the water, eight of us, and a teacher who centres clay without looking at it.

my hands remembered more than I did. Wedge, cone up, cone down, open. The first one collapsed. The second collapsed more slowly. The third is a small bowl, thick-walled, a little off-centre, the size of two cupped hands.

::media[The wheel by the window, which I've claimed]{file="video-pottery-1"}

for two hours I didn't think about anything but the wall of the pot between my fingers.

Four years without clay. It was still there.
