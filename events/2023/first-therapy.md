a room above a bakery in the centre. Two chairs, a plant, a box of tissues placed exactly where you'd need it. Tove is older than I expected, reading glasses on a cord, and she asked me to tell her about the last six months.

I told her about the release, in order, like a report. Then about the kayak. Then about Nana, which I hadn't meant to.

she said very little. At the end she said that I talk about everything I've made as if it were a debt I'm still paying off.

walked home the long way, past the fish market, up the hill. Slept two hours in the afternoon.

next Tuesday, same time.
