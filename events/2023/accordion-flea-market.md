flea market in a school gym in Fana. Waffles, old skis, a box of cassettes, a man selling nothing but lamps. And on a table at the back, a red accordion with cream keys and a chrome grille, 1950s, one strap missing.

600 kroner. The man said it had been his uncle's, who played at weddings in Voss and "never the same tune twice".

I don't know why I bought it. Yes I do. It was the first thing in weeks I *wanted*.

Carried it home on the bus on my knees, like a cat.

::audio[One octave, five ways. The bellows wheeze a little.]{file="music-accordion-3" title="Registers"}

It weighs eleven kilos. My left hand has no idea what the buttons are for. Biscuit left the room and has not come back.
