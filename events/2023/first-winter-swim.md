a woman from the pottery class swims at Nordnes every morning, all winter. She said: come on Sunday. I went on Sunday.

seven degrees in the water, two in the air, sleet. Six women and one old man in a woolly hat, all completely calm about it. :hint[*Vinterbading*]{note="winter bathing; said as if it were normal"}, they call it. It is not normal.

in to the waist, gasping. Then the shoulders. Breathe out, long, the way the old man said. Count. I got to forty and got out, and my skin was bright red, and I laughed out loud for the first time in I don't know how long.

[Nordnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordnes "the tip of the peninsula, where the sea bath is") at nine on a Sunday, the fjord black, the city behind still asleep.
