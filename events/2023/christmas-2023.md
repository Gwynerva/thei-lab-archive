Whitby, the two of us, a quiet one. Mum and Dad had been told the short version. Nobody asked about work. Dad asked about the kayak instead, and then about the repair, in detail, for an hour, which was the kindest thing anyone did all week.

no linocut cards this year. I never cut the block. Mum put last year's kayak on the mantelpiece instead, "so it doesn't look bare".

walks:

- the West Cliff to Sandsend and back, in a gale
- the 199 steps, twice, the second time for the view
- Saltwick at low tide with Dad, to look at the cliff, which is still falling, slowly, the way it should

---

[the diary](/diary/) for this autumn is mostly gaps. This is one of the few full pages: the abbey on a grey afternoon, mince pies, Jonas asleep in Dad's chair with Biscuit's absence on his lap.

:::private
On Christmas night I told Mum properly. All of it. She held my hand across the kitchen table and said "Oh, love" and nothing else, and that was right.
:::
