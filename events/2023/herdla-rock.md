paddled out from the beach at Herdla on a grey, still morning, Jonas beside me in his old plastic boat. Low tide. I was looking at an eagle, or at a cloud I'd decided was an eagle, and there was a rock just under the surface where the chart said there wasn't one.

I make charts. Not that one. I checked.

a crunch. That's all. A sound I felt in my teeth.

on the beach: a crack in the bow, two hands long, through the glass into the wood. No water in. She'd held.

I sat on the stones and was not sensible about it. Three months old. Sixteen months of work. Jonas said boats are for using and scratches are their diary. I said this is not a scratch. He agreed.

::card[Launch day, three months before. Flat calm, no rocks]{to="ref:event:kayak-launch#:~:text=calm"}

so: sand it back, new cloth, epoxy, fair it, varnish. Three weeks. [The launch](ref:event:kayak-launch#:~:text=calm "the day she was perfect") was the day she was perfect. Now she's ours.
