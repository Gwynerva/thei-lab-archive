Mum retired today. Thirty-four years at the same primary school, the last nineteen of them in Year 2.

I wasn't there. I flew back after Dad's birthday because of the release, and watched the assembly on Clare's phone, propped against two monitors full of harbour charts. Shaky video. The hall, a hundred children singing something with actions, Mum in the front row trying very hard not to.

They gave her a quilt with a square from every class. She'll hate that it's on display and love that it exists.

she rang at seven, Sunday-call voice on a Friday. "What do I do on Monday?"

I said: nothing. Nothing for a week. Then see.

I should take my own advice. I wrote that and deleted it and wrote it again.
