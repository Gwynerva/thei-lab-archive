seventh coat. Done.

Sand with 320, wipe, tack cloth, varnish, wait two days, again. Since February. The workshop smells of it and so do I. Jonas says he can tell which evenings I've been in from the stairwell.

On the last coat I could see the strip lights in the deck, and my own face, upside down, looking tired.

It's the most *finished* thing I've ever made. More than [the deck](ref:event:deck-on "January, the cold day"), more than the bookshelf, more than anything with stitches in it. You can't hide a run in varnish. I tried. You can see where.

work is loud. The release, everyone counting the weeks to September. Six hours of harbour charts, two hours of sanding, sleep. The sanding is the good part.
