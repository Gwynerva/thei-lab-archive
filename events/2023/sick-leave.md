the doctor said it on the Monday. *Utbrent.* Burnt out. She signed me off for a month, and then another, and then another.

what it was like, as far as I can say it:

- sleeping eleven hours and waking tired
- not being able to choose between two kinds of bread in the shop
- a video call in the flat downstairs making my heart race
- crying at an advert for a ferry company
- walking. That still worked. Down to the harbour and back, every day, slowly

> You don't have to earn rest. You're allowed to be tired before you've finished.

That was the doctor too. I wrote it on a card and put it on the fridge, where Jonas reads it every morning and I don't.

[the diary](/diary/) is nearly empty for these weeks. That's the record. Nothing happened, and it took everything.
