Jonas signed us both up in March, when I still said yes to things.

a Saturday at the sailing club. Six of us, two small keelboats, an instructor who is possibly nineteen. The morning on the pontoon learning words: *skjøte*, *fall*, *baug*, *akter*. The afternoon out on the fjord.

I steered. The instructor said "bear away" and I did the opposite. Twice. The boom came across and missed my head by the width of my hat.

then the wind settled, and the boat leaned and went, and it was quiet in a way a kayak never is. No paddle. Just the water talking along the hull.

Jonas, in the other boat, waved like an idiot.

knots for homework. A bowline, behind my back, ten times.
