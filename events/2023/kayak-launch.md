sixteen months, from [the plans](<ref:project:kayak#h:Plans> "the very first sheet") on the kitchen table to this.

We carried her down to the slipway at nine, Jonas at the bow, me at the stern, Anna and two people from the workshop behind us like a procession. Flat calm. The fjord grey and silver, gulls on the posts.

no champagne: champagne is for ships. A jar of rowan jelly instead, Mum's, from Nana's recipe. I said her name and put a spoonful on the bow, and Jonas said the fish would be pleased.

***Nana June***, in white on both sides of the bow, lettered by hand last week. Squint on the left.

:::quote[Nana June, every time Dad took a boat out]{align="center"}
Never trust a calm sea.
:::

So I didn't. Spraydeck, buoyancy aid, pump, spare paddle. Then I got in and pushed off, and she floated, level, dry, and turned when I asked her to.

Twenty minutes out and back along the shore. That's all. My hands shook for an hour afterwards.
