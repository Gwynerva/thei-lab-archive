Posted it today, tracked and insured and wrapped three times, at the post office counter in the supermarket, with a queue of people sending Christmas presents behind me.

The book: twelve pages, drawn by hand, the story of a ginger cat who paddles a kayak from Bergen to Whitby to visit a baby, with a map on every spread so she can follow him across the North Sea. Coptic-bound, the way Fergus taught me, the boards covered in a scrap of blue cotton from Nana's sewing box.

Inside the front cover:

*For Isla, who lives on the other side of the map.*\
*From Auntie Mira, Bergen, Christmas 2024.*

She's nine months old and she'll chew it. That's allowed. That's what board covers are for.
