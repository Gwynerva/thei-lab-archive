Dev rang at half six our time with his voice gone strange. **Isla Mistry**, born at four this morning in Leeds, seven pounds and an ounce, dark hair, lungs "in excellent working order".

Clare sent one photo an hour later: a crumpled red face in a hospital blanket, eyes shut, one fist up by her ear as if she were thinking hard about something. I've looked at it about two hundred times.

I'm an aunt. *Tante*, says Jonas, who is already planning a small wooden boat for her.

The hat I've been knitting since January is finished except for the pompom and is, I now see, about three sizes too big. She'll grow into it. That's what hats are for.

Mum was there already, of course. She drove down from Whitby at two in the morning when the first message came. I booked flights for Easter before breakfast.
