Back today. Four hours, nine till one, on :hint[*gradert sykmelding*]{note="graded sick leave: you work part of the week and the doctor signs off the rest"}, with a plan agreed between Anna, the occupational health nurse and me, written down and signed by all three of us.

My desk was as I'd left it, except that someone had watered the plant and someone else had propped a postcard of Whitby Abbey against the monitor. Nobody made a fuss. Anna brought coffee at ten and talked about her daughter's ski race.

What I did: read emails for an hour and deleted most of them. Fixed one symbol on one chart. Went home at one, as agreed, even though I wanted to stay and prove something.

That last part is the whole exercise, Tove says. Going home when I said I would.

Three good things:

1. the postcard
2. the walk home in daylight, such as it is in January
3. Biscuit on the stairs, as if he'd been waiting all morning
