I wrote it by hand first, at the kitchen table, then typed it. Six lines. I'd been drafting it for a month.

The plan in January was a different one: ~~half time till the summer, then full time, then see~~. Then I saw. The work itself was fine. It was who I turned into when I did it for long enough.

Anna read it in the small meeting room and was quiet for a while, and then said she'd hoped and she'd guessed. She said she'd miss my hand-lettered labels on the office fridge. Then she asked what I'd do, and I said maps. Just maps. Mine, for people who want them.

The last day is the 31st of May. Seven weeks of handing over.

Sigrid, when Jonas told her on the phone, said: "Good. She was getting too thin." Which wasn't the point, and was also exactly the point.
