I found it a week ago, stroking him on the sofa: a lump on his left side the size of a marble, under the fur. He didn't mind me touching it. I minded.

The vet in Sandviken took a sample with a needle while Biscuit glared at her with the whole of his face. Then a week of waiting, of watching him eat (normally), sleep (normally, on my maps) and shout for breakfast at five (normally).

She rang this afternoon. A fatty lump, a lipoma, nothing at all. "Old cats get them," she said. "He's an old cat now."

He's about eight and a half. That isn't old. I've decided.

We celebrated with a tin of the expensive fish. He ate half of it and left the rest, on principle.
