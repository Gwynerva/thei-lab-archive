The last day. Cake at two, the whole office round the long table, Anna making a short speech and then a longer one.

They gave me a chart: the harbour at Skuteviken, the first one I drew, in my first autumn, framed and signed on the back by everyone. Somebody had added a tiny kayak in pencil by the guest pontoon, with *Nana June* on it in very small letters. I didn't cry until I got outside.

What I'm taking with me:

- the habit of checking every sounding twice
- a lot of Norwegian I'd never have learned anywhere else, most of it about depth contours
- Anna, who is a friend now and not a boss, and who has already invited herself to whatever I do next

What I'm leaving: the feeling of a date in the calendar like the edge of a cliff.

Walked home along Vågen at five with the chart under my arm, the fishing boats in, the sun out for once. Three good things, and all of them today.
