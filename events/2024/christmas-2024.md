Their first Christmas in Norway. They came on the 23rd with a suitcase that was mostly Christmas cake, and a jar of Mum's rowan jelly for Jonas, who now asks for it by name.

Christmas Eve the Norwegian way, since we're here. The bells rang Christmas in at five, from every church in the city at once, and Mum stood at the open window to hear them. *Pinnekjøtt* at six, which Dad approached like a man defusing a bomb and then had thirds of. Presents after dinner, not in the morning, which Mum found "very continental". Rain, of course.

On the 25th we walked up Fløyen in the drizzle, all four of us, Dad slow on the steep bits since his heart but not stopping. At the top, the city below in the grey, the fjord, the islands, he stood looking at it for a long time and said: "So this is where you went."

It wasn't a question. I said yes. He nodded, the way he does at a good splice.

Biscuit sat on Mum's lap for three days and has not forgiven her for leaving.
