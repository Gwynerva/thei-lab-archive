Easter in Leeds, in Clare and Dev's small terraced house, with everybody in it: Mum and Dad down from Whitby, me from Bergen, Dev's parents on the Sunday with enough food for a regiment.

Isla is four weeks old. She is mostly asleep, and when she's awake she looks at the ceiling light with an expression of deep suspicion. I held her on the first evening, sitting very still on the sofa while Clare showed me how to keep her head up, and she fell asleep on my chest, and I didn't move for an hour and forty minutes. Dad timed it.

::card[The morning she arrived, as it reached Bergen]{to="ref:event:isla-born"}

Clare looks tired and fierce and happy. On the last night, washing up, she said "You look better than at Christmas", and I said I was, and it was true.

I want to make her something. Not a hat. A book, maybe. With maps in it.
