The Sandviken map took six weeks: every street on the hill, the white houses, the old battery, the steps, the bakery, our door, Biscuit in the window if you know where to look. Pen first, then watercolour, then scanned and printed on thick paper by a man in Møhlenpris who takes printing more seriously than I take anything.

I put it in the shop on Monday. Today, an email: *Order 0001*. Somebody in Oslo who grew up on the hill and wanted to see the street again.

Not Mum. Not Anna. Not Sigrid (she'll buy one at Christmas, she says, and pay full price, she says). A stranger, with money.

::link[The shop: three prints so far and a lot of white space]{url="https://example.org/mira-maps"}

I packed it in a tube with too much tissue paper and a handwritten card, and walked it to the post office as if it were going to the other side of the world.
