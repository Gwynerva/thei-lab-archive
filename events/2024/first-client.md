The first client is a hiking club in Voss with forty kilometres of waymarked trails and a map from 1996 that still shows a footbridge the river took in 2005.

They found me through Anna, of course. Their chairman rang on Monday and talked for fifty minutes, mostly about stiles.

What they want: one sheet, folded to fit a jacket pocket, the trails in red, the huts, the car parks, only the bridges that exist. What I want: to draw it properly, by hand, contours from the open data, in the lettering I learned this spring, and then make it into something a printer can use.

Six weeks. A price I'm already embarrassed by. I said yes before he'd finished the sentence.

Afterwards I walked to the post office just to be walking somewhere. *Voss trails, sheet 1*, in pencil, at the top of a clean page.
