Ines met me at the airport last night with a sign that said PEREGRINA in her best capitals, which is how she does everything.

The first stamp: the cathedral, a small office off the cloister, a man with a rubber stamp and an ink pad who did it carefully, square in its box, and wished me *bom caminho*. My *credencial* has thirty-two boxes. I mean to fill them.

The rest of the day we did nothing a pilgrim should. The Ribeira in the sun, *pastéis de nata* eaten standing at a counter, the iron bridge on foot, the top deck and then the bottom one and then the top again. Ines talked about her mother and her new job. I talked, for the first time, about the autumn, without it turning into a speech.

Tomorrow the coast, north, with the sea on our left for days, and Ines for the first of them. My boots are new. Ines says that's a mistake. Ines is usually right.
