Two summers since the boom and my hat. Today the test: an examiner from the club, a 26-foot keelboat, a south-westerly of about twelve knots, and me in charge of the whole thing for an hour.

What she asked for:

1. leave the pontoon under sail, no engine
2. tack, gybe, heave to
3. a man-overboard drill, for a fender called Kåre
4. reef the main in a gust
5. come back alongside without touching anything

I touched something. Lightly. She wrote it down and said it was "a kiss, not a collision".

Passed. A certificate on thin card with my name spelled right, which is more than the Compostela managed.

The knots went into the margins of my notebook a year ago; now they're in my hands. Jonas has started looking at the chart of the North Sea the way other people look at holiday brochures. I've told him: one summer at a time.
