A form on the Brønnøysund registers' website, twenty minutes, and an email back with a number. I'm an *enkeltpersonforetak*: a sole trader. A business with one employee, who is also the board, the accountant and the person who makes the coffee.

The name is just my name, with *kart* after it. I spent a week thinking of something cleverer and then didn't.

The list on the fridge, under the doctor's card:

- [x] register the business
- [x] a separate bank account
- [x] find out about VAT (not yet, not until I've earned fifty thousand kroner)
- [x] tell Mum (she asked about my pension)
- [ ] find an accountant who explains things slowly
- [ ] a website that is more than three lines of text
- [ ] the first client

In the evening Jonas brought up a bottle of cider from the Nesheims' press and we drank it on the front steps. *Skål*, for a company of one.
