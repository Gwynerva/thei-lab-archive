The Nordvåg summer party: forty of us on an old wooden passenger boat, out of Vågen at five and round the islands until well after midnight, in the kind of light that doesn't end.

On deck: buckets of pink shrimps, bread, mayonnaise, lemon, and the rule that you peel your own. A *rekefest*, a shrimp party. My fingers smelled of shrimp until Tuesday.

The cartographers stood at the rail arguing about which skerries were on which chart, which is apparently what cartographers do at parties in every country. Anna made a speech about the year and mentioned the harbour I charted last autumn by name, and the rock in the right place, and people clapped, and I went red to my ears.

::media[Byfjorden on the way back, nearly midnight, nearly light]{file="byfjorden-1"}

At midnight the sun was only just below the hills to the north, the sky over the sea was orange and the water pale gold, and nobody wanted to go home. Someone had a guitar. Someone always has a guitar.
