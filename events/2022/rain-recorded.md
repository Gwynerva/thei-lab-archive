Rain: day 6. Last week I bought a small recorder, the kind journalists use, with two little microphones crossed at the top and a fluffy windshield that Biscuit thinks is a rival cat.

Tonight I put it on the sill under the skylight, pressed the red button, and lay on the floor for twenty minutes in the dark, not moving, listening to the rain on the roof. Not the sound of rain: *this* rain, on *this* roof, in Sandviken, on the 8th of October. The drops on the glass, close and hard. The gutter running over. Under it, further off, the whole city dripping. A car on the wet road. Once, a gull, complaining.

::audio[The rain on the skylight, the best of twenty minutes]{file="sound-rain-3" title="Rain on the roof, Sandviken"}

Playing it back on headphones, I heard things I hadn't heard lying there: the tick of the radiator, my own breathing, Biscuit's tail on the floorboards.

I think I've started something. I want to record the bells, the harbour, the gulls, the fish market at six in the morning. A map of Bergen made of sound.
