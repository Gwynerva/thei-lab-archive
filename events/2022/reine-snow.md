Lofoten with Priya. We flew to Bodø, took the ferry across in a swell that sent half the passengers to the rail, and drove down to Reine in the dark. This morning we opened the curtains and there it was.

Every peak white, all the way down to the water. Red fishermen's cabins on stilts on black rock, snow on their roofs. The sea a colour I don't have a word for, somewhere between green and steel. Not a breath of wind. The kind of view you've seen on a thousand calendars and still can't believe when it's outside your window.

Priya, who has done fieldwork on three oceans and is not easily impressed, stood on the cold step in her socks with her coffee and said nothing for a full minute. That's a record.

::media[Sakrisøy in the evening, ochre cabins and white mountains]{file="lofoten-winter-1"}

We walked out to Hamnøy and back. My fingers stopped working after twenty minutes. I took a hundred photos with my thumbs.
