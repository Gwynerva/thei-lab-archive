Henningsvær: a fishing village spread over a scatter of little islands joined by bridges, and at the far end of it, on a rock with the sea on three sides, a football pitch. Bright green artificial grass, floodlights, goals, and then nothing but rock and the Norwegian Sea. Kick the ball too hard and it goes to the Arctic.

Priya and I walked out and stood in the middle of it in the wind. Somebody had cleared the snow off. Somebody always does.

Everywhere else: *tørrfisk*, stockfish, thousands of cod hung in pairs on wooden racks to dry in the wind, the way they've been dried here for a thousand years. The smell is something. Priya the marine biologist gave me a lecture on cod. I listened to all of it.

We had fish soup in a warm room by the harbour and watched the light go pink on the snow. Two more days, then home to the rain.
