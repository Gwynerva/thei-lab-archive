The B1 exam. Reading and listening in the morning, on a computer in a hall full of nervous people, then an essay: write to a friend about a journey you've made. I wrote about the ferry to Lofoten and spelled *ferje* three different ways.

The oral was in the afternoon, two of us at a time with an examiner, and the topic was, I swear, the weather. I talked about rain in Bergen for four minutes without stopping. The examiner smiled at *det regner ikke, det bare øser*, which Mr Haldorsen taught me (it doesn't rain, it just pours), and wrote something down.

Afterwards, a beer in the sun outside with two people from the class: an *utepils*, a beer drunk outdoors because it's finally warm enough. It wasn't, quite. We drank it anyway.

Results in a few weeks. I think I passed. I think I talked too much about rain.
