Thirty, on Pi Day, as always. ~~Twenty-nine and a bit~~ Thirty. I've decided it suits me.

Back from Lofoten on Saturday night, knackered, washing everywhere. Today was a normal Monday at work, except that Anna had put a candle in my *matpakke* and the team sang the Norwegian birthday song, which is long, it turns out, with a lot of *hurra* in it.

Mum rang at seven in the morning, as she has every birthday since I left home, to tell me the time I was born. Dad came on, said "Thirty, eh," and handed me back.

In the evening Jonas came round with a cake he'd made himself, round, with the candles set in a circle and a little π piped on top in icing. And a present, wrapped in a page of a boat magazine: a reel of copper wire, for stitching the kayak's panels together.

It is either the most romantic present I've ever had or not romantic at all. I've decided it's the first. We start stitching tomorrow.
