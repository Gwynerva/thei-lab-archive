Two lines. Two years of avoiding it, and I got it in the end from someone at Norwegian class who "only had a cold".

Days one to three: fever, aching everywhere, a cough like a seal. Days four and five: the fever went and the tiredness didn't. Day six: I could taste coffee again, which made me cry, which tells you how day six went.

What helped:

1. Biscuit, who lay on my feet all week and didn't care that I was horrible
2. Mr Haldorsen, who left a bag of oranges on the step with a note in capitals, *GOD BEDRING*, get well soon
3. a pot of soup that appeared on the doorstep on Tuesday with a note signed *J.*, from someone I've spent precisely one weekend with, on a kayak course, who apparently lives two streets away
4. films, in bed, on the laptop

The best of them was a short one about a girl searching for a baby dragon through snow and ruins. I watched it twice and cried both times, which may have been the fever.

Negative on Thursday. I'm out. I'm going to go and stand in the rain on purpose.
