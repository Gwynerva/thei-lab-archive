Lyderhorn, the fifth: out on the west side of the city, a bare rocky top above the islands, where the witches of Bergen are supposed to have met on midsummer night. No witches today. A family with a dog, and a lot of heather.

Hot, for Bergen: twenty-four degrees, which made the whole city slightly hysterical. From the top the sea was every shade of blue, and the islands went on to the horizon like stepping stones.

On the way down Jonas knew a little rocky bay, and we swam. The water was fourteen degrees. I lasted three minutes and screamed for two of them. Jonas floated on his back and laughed at me.

Five down. Two to go.
