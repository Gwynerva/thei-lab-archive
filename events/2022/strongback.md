He meant it. Jonas's workshop is an old boathouse by the water at the edge of town: a long room that smells of cedar and epoxy, a wood stove, half a rowing boat on trestles, and a radio playing the coastal forecast in Norwegian.

Before there can be a boat there has to be a :hint[strongback]{note="a long beam or box on legs, dead straight and perfectly level, with forms fixed along it, on which a hull is put together"}. Ours is two long spruce planks screwed into a box beam, five and a half metres long, on trestles. All day we checked it for level and twist with a string line, a spirit level, and a laser that Jonas uses as a cat toy when nobody is looking. If the strongback twists, the boat twists. If the boat twists, it goes round in circles forever.

By four it was dead straight. Jonas sighted along it with one eye shut and said "*Fint*", which from him is a speech.

The plans are pinned to the wall now. [The plans](<ref:project:kayak#h:Plans> "how I chose them") are for a five-metre sea kayak in okoume plywood, and the cut list is long:
