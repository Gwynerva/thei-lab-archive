Damsgårdsfjellet, the fourth of the seven: the low wooded one across the water from the centre, the one everybody forgets. An hour up from the bus stop through birch woods just coming into leaf, past a family pushing a pram up a path that didn't look pram-shaped.

From the top, the whole city to the north: Nordnes, the harbour, the mountains behind. I could trace my walk to work. I could see Fløyen, the first one, and the line of the cable car up Ulriken, and the white house where I live, if I squinted.

::media[From the other side, in winter light: Damsgårdsfjellet between the city and Løvstakken]{file="damsgardsfjellet"}

Four down, three to go. My legs have mostly stopped complaining.
