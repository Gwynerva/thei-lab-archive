Rundemanen: the seventh mountain, the last one, the one with the big mast on top, right behind Fløyen. I've looked at that mast from my kitchen window every day for fourteen months.

Of course it sleeted. *Sludd*, horizontal, coming off the plateau straight into our faces, while Jonas walked ahead of me saying "nearly there" cheerfully for an hour. The path past the lakes was half stream. The last part was a scramble over wet rock, with the mast humming in the wind above us like a giant tuning fork.

At the top I put my hand on the cairn and said "seven" out loud, the way I said "thirty-four" on Ben Nevis. Nobody heard me over the wind.

All seven, one at a time: Fløyen, Ulriken, Løvstakken, Damsgårdsfjellet, Lyderhorn, Sandviksfjellet, Rundemanen. Some people do all seven in one day. Jonas says I should. Jonas says many things.
