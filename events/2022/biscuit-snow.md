It snowed in the night, properly, twenty centimetres of it, which in Bergen usually means rain by lunchtime, so I opened the door to the steps at eight while it was still there.

Biscuit has seen snow before, the Leith kind: a dusting that melts on the balcony while you watch. He has not seen this. He put one front paw in it, took it out again and shook it, very slowly, as if it had insulted him personally. Then the other paw. Then he sat down on the doorstep and looked at the white lane for a long time.

Then something clicked, and he launched himself into it like a ginger torpedo, sank to his ears, came up with snow on his whiskers, and ran in circles round the garden making noises I've never heard from him.

::media[Afterwards, fluffed up to twice his size, refusing to come in]{file="ginger-cat-13" layout="natural"}

He ate some, too, which puts it on [the list of things he eats](<ref:project:biscuit#h:Things he eats> "a long and worrying list"), between Christmas ribbon and my hair ties.

By two it was raining and the snow was slush. He sat at the window and watched it go with real sorrow.
