Priya's viva today. Four years of seagrass meadows in the Firth of Forth, a hundred and ninety pages, and two examiners in a room with her for three hours and ten minutes.

Marta and I waited in the corridor with a bottle of fizz in a rucksack, and every time the door moved we stood up like dogs hearing the lead.

She came out pale, then saw us and burst out laughing. Minor corrections. *Doctor* Nair. She made me call her that all the way down the Mound.

We drank the fizz on the steps in the drizzle, out of the paper cups from the coffee machine, and then had chips on the wall at the Shore, which is where we've celebrated everything since 2016.

She's applying for postdocs now. One of them is in Plymouth. I'm not thinking about that yet.
