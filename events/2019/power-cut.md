title: A night without power
summary: The whole street goes dark at six; two candles, a violin by head torch, and a cat in his element

---

At ten past six the lights went, the fridge stopped humming, and the whole street went black, every window. A cable fault somewhere under Leith Walk, according to the man in the flat below, who knows these things.

What we did, in order:

- found the candles (two, both birthday)
- found the torch (no batteries)
- found the head torch in the hill bag (working, thank you, first aid course)
- ate everything in the fridge that wouldn't last, which was mostly cheese
- put on every jumper we own

Then Marta got her violin out and played by the light of the head torch, Irish tunes, then Bach, then whatever we shouted, and I fetched the cello and joined in badly underneath, and the people across the street opened their window to listen. Biscuit wandered the dark flat completely unbothered, a cat in his element, and stepped on my face twice.

The power came back at twenty past one. We'd gone to bed by then, under a heap of coats. Warmest I've been in weeks.
