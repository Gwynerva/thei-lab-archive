The mugs came out of the glaze firing tonight. Ten of us crowded round the kiln shelves like children round a Christmas stocking.

Mine leans. Eight degrees to the left, by the spirit level on my phone; you can see it from across a room. The handle, which I attached very carefully, sits a little low, so your knuckles touch the side. The glaze was meant to be a speckled oatmeal and has come out the colour of milky tea, with a dark run down one side where I held it in the bucket too long.

::media[Not mine (mine is uglier), but the colours I was hoping for]{file="mug-2" layout="natural"}

I love it completely. It holds exactly one mug of tea, and it's the first thing I've ever made out of mud.

:::spoiler
On the bottom, scratched in with a pin before the firing: *M.T. 2019, wonky on purpose*. It was not on purpose.
:::

The teacher picked it up, turned it round, and said, "It has character." Everyone knows what that means. Biscuit has already knocked it off the table once. It survived. It's that sort of mug.
