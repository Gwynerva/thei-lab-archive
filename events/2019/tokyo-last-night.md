Last night of the trip. Tokyo is too big to have a view of, so I went up a tower to get one anyway. From up there it doesn't end: lights to every edge of the window, and somewhere off to the right the orange stalk of Tokyo Tower, like a lit match.

::media[From the thirty-ninth floor, the city going on and on]{file="tokyo-skyline-1"}

Dinner was ramen from a vending-machine shop: you buy a ticket from a machine, hand it over, sit at a counter facing a wall, and a bowl appears. No talking required, which suited my Japanese. Pork broth, an egg with a soft middle, and a fried cutlet I hadn't meant to order (wrong button).

::media[Ramen, and the cutlet I ordered by mistake]{file="ramen-bowl"}

Two weeks. Kyoto, Nara, four days in the forest, two in the city. I came on my own because nobody could come with me, and I was a bit scared of it. I'm going home a bit braver.

Flight at eleven tomorrow, and Biscuit at the end of it, who will pretend not to have noticed I was gone.
