Twenty-seven. The fourteenth of March again, 3.14, and Marta, who has lived here long enough to know, made a pie: apple, with a π cut into the lid. Priya brought candles in the shape of a 2 and a 7, then lost the 7, so for ten minutes I was two.

Nana's card came a day early, as always, with a pound coin taped inside "for sweets". She's done that since I was five, and I'd be heartbroken if she stopped.

[Biscuit](ref:project:biscuit "who did not sing") sat in the empty cake box for the rest of the evening and wouldn't be moved. The best present I got this year arrived in February and now owns the box the presents came in.

Twenty-seven: a cat, a job I like, a cello I can almost play. Not bad going.
