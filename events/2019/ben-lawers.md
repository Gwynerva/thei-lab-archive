Number twenty. Ben Lawers, 1,214 metres, the highest hill in the southern Highlands. Marta drove; I navigated; the car park was full of people in better boots than ours.

We went up into cloud from the start, thick and wet, and I was already composing the line for the map ("cloud, again") when, about two hundred metres below the top, we walked straight out of it. Everything below us was white. Every glen was filled, and the tops stuck up out of it like islands: Schiehallion, the Tarmachan ridge, and far off something that might have been Ben Nevis. The sky was a ridiculous blue.

::media[South-west from the top, the morning still on the hills]{file="ben-lawers-1" layout="stretch"}

We sat by the summit cairn for an hour eating squashed sandwiches. Marta, who does not get excited about mountains, took eighty photos.

Tonight I changed one cell in a spreadsheet and [the Munro map](ref:project:munro-map "coloured in as I go") turned dot number twenty green. Twenty of 282. At this rate I'll finish at about Nana's age.
