Card number three. 2017 was a robin, 2018 the lighthouse, and this year there was never really a question: Biscuit, asleep on a map.

I drew him from life, which was easy, because he didn't move for two hours. The hard part was the map under him. Contour lines in lino are a nightmare, every one a separate thin cut, and if the gouge slips you've moved a hill. I moved two. Nobody will know.

::media[Cutting the block, Saturday morning]{file="linocut-1"}

Two colours this year: pale blue for the map, then black for the cat and the contours. That means lining up two blocks, which means that of forty prints, twelve have the cat floating slightly off the map like a ghost. Marta says the ghost ones are better. Marta is being kind.

Forty printed, twenty-eight good, all drying on the clothes horse and the backs of chairs. Biscuit has walked across three of them. Those three are going to Nana, who will think the paw prints are the best part.
