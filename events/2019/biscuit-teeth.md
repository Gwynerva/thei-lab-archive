The bad tooth the rescue centre were "keeping an eye on" turned out to be two bad teeth, both at the back, both now in a little plastic pot the vet handed me as if I'd want them. (I kept them. Of course I did.)

He went in at eight, starved and furious, and came out at four, groggy and furious, with the tip of his tongue sticking out, which it did all evening and which I am not allowed to find funny.

Soft food for two weeks, then back to normal. Normal is the list of [what he eats](<ref:project:biscuit#h:Things he eats> "a long list, and a fussy one"), which now has a footnote: not the hard treats, not for a while.

::card[The day he came home, three months ago]{to="ref:event:biscuit-comes-home"}

The bill came to more than my bike cost. He's worth it. He's asleep on the radiator shelf now, tongue still out.
