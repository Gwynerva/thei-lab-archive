Second full day in Kyoto, and I'd timed it, more by luck than planning, for the blossom.

Maruyama Park in the afternoon is one enormous picnic. Blue tarpaulins everywhere, laid out at dawn by someone junior from each office to save the spot; by four the tarps had filled with colleagues, beer, little boxes of food, and a level of happiness I've never seen at a work do in Leith. This is :hint[hanami]{note="looking at the blossom, which in practice means a picnic under it"}, and it is the best idea any country has had.

The weeping cherry in the middle is old and propped up on wooden crutches, and so covered in blossom that the branches look poured rather than grown. I sat on a wall with a rice ball and a can of something peach-flavoured and watched people photograph it for an hour. A man of about eighty stood under it with his hands behind his back the whole hour and never took a single picture.

::media[The old tree, propped up and in full flower]{file="maruyama-park-1"}

At dusk they lit lanterns under the branches and it all went pink and gold. I walked back along the river to the hostel feeling ridiculously lucky.
