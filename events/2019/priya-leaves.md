Priya's gone. The postdoc in Plymouth starts tomorrow: seagrass again, but in Devon, where she says the water is warmer and the people are slower.

We loaded the van at eight. Forty boxes, mostly papers, a surfboard nobody has ever seen her use, and the green armchair from the front room, which we'd always assumed came with the flat and turns out to have been hers all along. The room looks wrong without it.

Marta and I stood on the pavement and waved as if it were a ship leaving. Biscuit watched from the window. When I came back up he was asleep on the patch of carpet where her chair had been, which is either very sweet or very territorial.

Three years in this flat together. She was the first person I met in Edinburgh, and I don't know who I'd be here without her. The fridge whiteboard still says [the bet](ref:event:the-long-day): *one fish supper, with a pickled egg*. I'm not rubbing it off.
