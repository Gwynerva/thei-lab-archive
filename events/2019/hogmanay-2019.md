Up Calton Hill at half eleven with Marta and four people from the orchestra, a flask of something hot and spiced, and what felt like half of Edinburgh. The whole hill was packed and facing the castle.

At midnight the fireworks went up off the castle rock and the city lit up, the Old Town red and gold, smoke drifting over Princes Street. People sang Auld Lang Syne in four keys at once, and a man I'd never met hugged me and wished me "a braw new decade".

::media[The Old Town from the hill, just after midnight]{file="edinburgh-skyline-2"}

Walked home down Leith Walk at two, with half the city, everyone loud and kind.

Resolutions for 2020, written on the back of a bus ticket:

1. Finish the prelude.
2. Ten new Munros.
3. Visit Nana more.
4. Write more in [the diary](/diary/), and less on the back of my hand.

It feels like a big year coming. I don't know why I think that. I just do.
