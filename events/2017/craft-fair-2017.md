My first ever stall! Half a trestle table at the winter craft fair in a church hall in Leith, shared with a woman who makes earrings out of old spoons.

I brought thirty-one coptic notebooks, A6 and A5, with covers of marbled card and old maps, the stitching showing on the spine because that's the whole point. Twelve pounds for small, eighteen for big. Priya said I was underpricing. Fergus, when I told him, said "aye, well, everyone does the first time".

How it went:

1. First hour: nothing. I rearranged the table four times.
2. Second hour: a woman bought two and said they were "too nice to write in". Please write in them.
3. Lunchtime: a run on the map covers. It turns out people love seeing their own street on a notebook.
4. Afternoon: sold out of A6, sat down, ate a mince pie, nearly cried.

Fourteen sold. £192 in a biscuit tin. I spent eight pounds of it on earrings made from a spoon.
