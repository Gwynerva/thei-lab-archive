Bus to St Abbs with Priya, who has wanted to show me the seabird cliffs since the day we met. It's a nature reserve on a headland: grassy tops, a lighthouse, and cliffs that drop straight into the sea and are covered, every ledge of them, in birds.

Mostly guillemots. Thousands of guillemots, standing shoulder to shoulder on ledges the width of a bookshelf, in neat brown-and-white suits, all facing the rock like they're in trouble. Priya says they don't build nests at all, and the eggs are pointed so they roll in a circle instead of off the edge. I watched one wobble and didn't breathe.

Also kittiwakes, razorbills, fulmars, a shag drying its wings, and the noise. The noise!
