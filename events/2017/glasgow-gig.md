A friend of Priya's from the lab plays bass in a band, and they had a gig in Glasgow, in a basement off Sauchiehall Street that held maybe eighty people and smelled of spilled lager and hairspray. Priya, Callum and I got the train through after tea.

::media[Glasgow on the walk from the station, before it got dark]{file="glasgow-2"}

The band was good! Loud, fast, a singer who kept apologising between songs. Callum danced like a man being attacked by bees. I stood near the back with my fingers half in my ears and loved it anyway.

Then we realised it was 23.15 and the last train to Edinburgh left Queen Street at 23.30. We ran. Callum carried Priya's jacket like a relay baton. We made it with about forty seconds to spare and sat on the floor of the vestibule all the way to Waverley, ears ringing, laughing at nothing at all.

Glasgow, I'll be back. Maybe in flatter shoes.
