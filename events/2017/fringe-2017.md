August in Edinburgh: the whole city turns into a theatre and you can't walk down the Royal Mile without being handed eleven flyers and a free sample of something. I had a dissertation to finish. I went to eleven shows anyway. In my defence, most of them were free.

The best, in order:

1. A one-woman show about her grandmother's allotment, in a room above a pub, that made the whole audience cry.
2. Two men doing the entire history of the Royal Navy with a cardboard boat.
3. A late-night show in a tent so cold the comedian handed out blankets.
4. A magician in a church hall whose trick I still can't work out.

The magician's trick, for anyone who isn't going and wants to know:

:::spoiler
The card you signed turns up inside an orange. Not the orange on the table: the orange in your own coat pocket, which was empty when you came in. I checked my pocket twice on the way out. I'm still checking.
:::

The rest were fine, two were awful, and one was a mime I left early, quietly, as a mime would want.
