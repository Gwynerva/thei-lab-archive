Last exam of the MSc! Spatial statistics, half nine, in a hall that smelled of floor polish and fear.

Three hours. Kriging, autocorrelation, and a question about Moran's I that I'm fairly sure I answered with a question of my own. By the end my hand had gone into a claw and I'd drawn a small map of the exam hall in the margin, exits marked, which I think says something about me.

Priya was waiting outside with two ice creams that had started to melt, and we lay on the Meadows in the sun until four doing absolutely nothing.

This was ~~the last exam I will ever sit~~ the last exam I will sit *this year*. I know myself.

Now: the dissertation summer.
