No street party this year. Callum was working, Priya had a cold, and I had no money left after Christmas. So the two of us sat at the kitchen table with tea, toast and a hot water bottle each, and went through the year.

Last year's resolutions:

- [x] Climb a Munro (six!)
- [x] Learn to bind a book properly
- [x] Finish the dissertation, on time
- [ ] Fewer biscuits

Three out of four. The biscuits were never going to happen.

Three good things about 2017:

1. Fergus's Tuesday evenings.
2. Watching the cliff fall (terrible and wonderful).
3. A job, a real one, in Leith.

At midnight we watched the fireworks over the castle from the window, small and far away, and opened it to hear them arrive a second late. Then bed.

I've been keeping [the diary](/diary/) for fifteen months now, the longest I've ever kept anything. Here's to the next fifteen.
