Burns Night at the flat! Callum is a chef and takes this very seriously. He made the haggis himself (do not ask), plus neeps, tatties and a whisky sauce that could strip paint.

We had eight people round the kitchen table, which seats four. Callum piped the haggis in from the hall with his phone held high playing the pipes, walking very slowly, and we all stood up, and it was ridiculous and also lovely.

::audio[What Callum's phone played, at full volume, three times]{file="music-bagpipes-1" title="The Skye Boat Song"}

Priya did the Address to a Haggis in an accent that started in Aberdeen and ended somewhere near Cardiff, and stabbed it at the right line with the bread knife. Everyone cheered. I did the Selkirk Grace, because it's short.

Things I learned:

- haggis is much nicer than its reputation
- whisky sauce is not a drink
- the smoke alarm in our kitchen works
