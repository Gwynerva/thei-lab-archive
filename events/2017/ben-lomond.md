My first :hint[Munro]{note="a Scottish mountain over 3,000 feet; there are 282 of them, and people tick them off"}! Ben Lomond, 974 metres, with Priya, on a bright cold Saturday with half of Glasgow.

We got the bus to Balloch and a lift from a very kind man to Rowardennan, then up the tourist path: wide, steady, relentless. Priya chatted the whole way up. I could not chat. I could breathe or chat. I chose breathing.

The last bit is a proper ridge with a drop on one side, and then suddenly the top, and the whole of the Highlands laid out north of us like a crumpled map.

::media[North from the top, every hill I'll never manage]{file="ben-lomond-3" layout="stretch"}

Sandwiches at the trig point. A man with a flask of soup offered us some, which is how I know hill people are the best people.

One down, 281 to go. That's a resolution ticked off, Hogmanay me. Next time I'm bringing better socks.
