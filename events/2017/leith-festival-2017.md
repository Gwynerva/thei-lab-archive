Leith Festival's gala day on the Links! Every club, choir and charity in Leith with a stall and a gazebo, a pipe band marching round in circles, a fun run, a dog show, and an ice-cream van with a queue to Pilrig.

The Spoke Room had a stall: free bike checks, a track pump, and me with my sleeves rolled up. We did thirty-one bikes. Tyres, brakes, one chain that had clearly never met oil, and a child's bike with a bell shaped like a ladybird, which I fixed with enormous care.

The dog show was the highlight. "Waggiest tail" went to a greyhound who didn't wag once, which nobody could explain.

Then a long walk down to the Shore, strings of little flags across the street, everyone out in the sun, and chips on the quay.

Leith on a sunny day is the best place in the world. Leith on a rainy day is also fine.
