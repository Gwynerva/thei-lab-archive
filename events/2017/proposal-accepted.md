It's official! Dr Grieve read my proposal over the holidays and called me into her office this afternoon. I thought she'd say it was too big. She said it was too big, *and* that I should do it anyway, on one condition: pick one bay and do it properly, not the whole coast badly.

So: Saltwick Bay. Old Ordnance Survey maps from 1853 onwards, aerial photos, and a drone survey in April with Sam Okafor, who flies the department's drone and apparently once landed it in a pond on purpose.

The title, for now: *How fast is Whitby falling into the sea?* She made me promise to give it a proper academic subtitle. I promised. I lied.

Rang Dad. He said "good" and then talked about the tide for ten minutes, which is how Dad says he's proud.
