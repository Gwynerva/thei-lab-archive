Priya and I borrowed Callum's tent and drove to Skye in a hire car for a long weekend. It rained. I want to be precise about this, as a scientist: it rained for 61 of the 72 hours we were there.

Saturday: the Old Man of Storr, in mist so thick we were standing almost underneath it before it appeared, enormous and grey and leaning. Spooky and wonderful.

Sunday: Bla Bheinn, Munro number five, up a path that was more of a stream, to a summit that was more of a cloud. We saw nothing at all from the top. Priya took a photo of me holding up a sign saying VIEW against the white.

Monday: the Quiraing, where the cloud lifted for exactly ten minutes and showed us green cliffs and pinnacles and the sea, then closed again, as if Skye had decided we'd earned that much and no more.

The tent leaked at the seams. Nana would have mended it with a needle and some candle wax. We mended it with a bin bag.

Midge count: thousands. Dry socks remaining: none. Would go back tomorrow.
