Storm Caroline! A storm with a name, which makes it feel like a visitor. The forecast said gusts of ninety miles an hour in the far north and "a bit blowy" for us. (The forecast didn't say "a bit blowy". That was Callum.)

It was more than a bit blowy. All night the windows rattled in their frames and the trees in the back green roared, and at two in the morning there was an almighty crash, which turned out to be every wheelie bin on the street going over at once and setting off down Leith Walk towards the sea like a herd.

::audio[The back green at two in the morning, recorded out of the kitchen window]{file="sound-wind-1" title="Storm Caroline"}

Callum and I went out in our coats over our pyjamas and chased three of them down. Ours had lost its lid. We never found the lid. Somewhere out in the Forth there's a green lid with our flat number on it, sailing to Norway.
