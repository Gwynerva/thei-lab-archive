Home for Christmas with a rucksack full of presents I'd made: a coptic notebook each, a robin card each, and a jar of Nana's own rowan jelly back for Nana, which she found very funny.

On Christmas Eve Clare and Dev came up from Leeds and, over the trifle, told us they've set a date: the 8th of September next year, in York. Mum cried (she does). Dad shook Dev's hand for a very long time. Nana asked Dev whether he could cook. He said a bit. She said that'll do.

Then Clare asked me whether I'd "do the paper things" for the wedding: invitations, place cards, maybe a map. I said yes before she'd finished the sentence.

::media[The abbey on Boxing Day morning, under a very big sky]{file="whitby-abbey-2"}

On Boxing Day we walked to Sandsend along the beach at low tide, the sea flat and silver and the abbey behind us, and Nana came round in the van with Dad and met us at the far end with a flask. Dad named every boat.

Best Christmas in years. And I already have a project for 2018.
