Came down the stair this morning and my bike was gone. The lock was still there, on the railing at the bottom, neatly cut through, as if to say: thanks, we only wanted the bike.

It was a red Peugeot, older than me, faded nearly pink, with a basket I'd fixed on with cable ties and a bell that went *tunk*. I'd done the brakes ten days ago. Someone is out there right now enjoying my excellent brakes.

::media[The nearest thing I have to a photo of it: the same faded red, the same tired saddle]{file="vintage-bicycle-2"}

Rang the police, got a reference number, and was told kindly but clearly not to hope. Rang the insurance, who said no.

At the Spoke Room on Saturday everyone was outraged on my behalf, which helped, and somebody said there's a frame in the back that just needs "a bit of love". I've seen it. It needs a lot of love. But it's free.

Walking everywhere till then. Leith Walk is longer than I thought.
