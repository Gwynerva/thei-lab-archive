title: Twenty-five on Pi Day
summary: A quarter of a century, a pie with π on it, and a present from Callum I'm not allowed to spoil
periods:

tags: [birthdays]
---

Twenty-five today! On Pi Day, as always, which means I've had pie for my birthday every year since I was eleven and found out. This year Priya baked an actual apple pie and cut a π out of the pastry on top. I may have cried a bit. 3.14159 candles would have been a fire hazard, so there were three.

Mum rang at seven in the morning to sing. Dad came on the line and said "twenty-five, eh". Nana's card came with a pressed rowan leaf inside and a five-pound note, which she has sent every birthday since I was born and which I will never spend.

Callum gave me a present wrapped in a map of Glasgow, and said I wasn't to say what it was until I'd used it. All right. For those who want to know:

:::spoiler
A 1953 Ordnance Survey one-inch map of Whitby and the moors, second-hand, with someone's walks pencilled on it in faint dotted lines. I want to follow every single one.
:::

A quarter of a century. I don't feel any wiser. I do feel full.
