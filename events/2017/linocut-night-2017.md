Christmas cards this year are home-made, because I'm skint and because I've wanted to try linocut since I saw some prints in a shop window in Stockbridge. This year's is [a robin](ref:section:linocut-cards/robin "2017, the first card").

I bought a block of lino, a set of cheap gouges, black ink and a roller, and spent last week cutting a robin from a photo I'd taken in the Botanic Garden. I cut myself twice and the robin once (his tail is shorter now; he's fine).

::media[The robin I drew him from, head on one side]{file="robin-bird-1"}

Tonight, printing. Newspaper all over the kitchen floor, the block on a chopping board, ink rolled out on an old baking tray, and the back of Callum's wooden spoon to rub the paper down. It works! Not every time. The first eight were blotchy, the ninth was perfect, and after that I had the knack.

Forty cards hung to dry on a string across the hall with clothes pegs. Priya ducked under them on her way to bed and said it looked like a migration.

Ink under my fingernails till Christmas, probably.
