The Orionids peaked tonight and the forecast said clear, so I took the last bus out to Flotterstone with a sleeping mat, a flask and two jumpers, and walked up the hill in the dark by head-torch.

Then I lay on my back in the heather for two hours and counted. Twenty-three meteors! Most were quick little scratches at the edge of my eye. Three were long and bright and left trails, and one broke into two pieces and I said "oh!" out loud to nobody.

They're bits of [Halley's Comet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halley%27s_Comet "it comes back every seventy-six years or so; next in 2061"), dust it shed centuries ago, burning up as the Earth drives through it. Nana would like that: it comes and goes, but it always comes back.
