Handed it in! 14,812 words, forty-one figures, two bound copies and one PDF, at 15.58, two minutes before the deadline, because the copy shop's binding machine jammed and I nearly lay down on the floor.

The answer to the question I asked on the train back from Whitby last November: the cliff at Saltwick has gone back about 0.3 metres a year on average since 1853, but nine metres went in one bad winter, and [the slab I watched come down](ref:event:saltwick-cliff-fall#:~:text=crack) in April is in there too, with its date and its time.

::card[What I found, on one page, without the forty-one figures]{to="ref:section:whitby-erosion/results"}

Thank you, Dr Grieve, Sam, Dad's fence post, and the coffee machine on the third floor.

Then I went home, slept for eleven hours, and woke up with no idea what to do with myself. A Fringe show tonight, I think. Then a job.
