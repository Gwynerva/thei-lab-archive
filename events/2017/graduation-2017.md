I graduated! MSc in Geographic Information Science, *with distinction*, which I'm going to say casually in conversation for at least a year.

The gown was hired and built for someone a foot taller, so I walked across the stage in the McEwan Hall holding it up like a Victorian lady crossing a puddle. They tap you on the head with a cap that's supposed to have a bit of an old reformer's trousers sewn into it. I'm choosing to believe this.

Mum cried from the moment we sat down. Dad wore his good jacket and a tie, which I've seen twice before (Clare's graduation and a funeral), and asked Dr Grieve about the drone for twenty minutes. Clare came up from Leeds on her day off and took 140 photos, nine of them in focus.

::media[Old College, where Mum made me stand for the ninth photo]{file="old-college-1"}

Then lunch, then a walk round Old College so Mum could take more photos, then Mum and Dad on the train home with my certificate in a cardboard tube, to frame "for the shop".

[The MSc](ref:project:msc-gis), done. That gown, never again.
