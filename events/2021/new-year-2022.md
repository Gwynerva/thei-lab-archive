*Nyttårsaften*. In Edinburgh, Hogmanay is a street party with a stage and a ticket. In Bergen, it seems, half the city lets off its own fireworks from its own garden, all at once, at midnight.

I went up the lane behind the house to the bend where you can see the whole harbour. Mr Haldorsen came too, in his good coat, with a thermos of coffee with something in it. At midnight the whole bowl of the city lit up: fireworks from every hill and garden and balcony, the bangs coming back off the mountains a second late, smoke hanging over Vågen in the orange light. It went on for half an hour. Biscuit watched it from under the bed.

Then I went in and had midnight again an hour later, on a video call with Priya and Marta in Scotland, with "Auld Lang Syne" sung badly over a bad connection.

Resolutions, to be checked in a year:

:::spoiler
Speak Norwegian at the shop even when they answer in English. Climb all seven mountains. Make something with my hands that floats, or flies, or at least holds water.
:::

It's been five months. I came here on my own. I'm still on my own, but less. Tomorrow I'll read back through [the diary](/diary/) and see how much of it I'd do again. Most of it, I think. *Godt nytt år.*
