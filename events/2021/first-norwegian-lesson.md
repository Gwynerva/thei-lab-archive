Twelve of us in little boxes on a screen, and a teacher in Trondheim with a candle lit on her desk at four in the afternoon. She said that was *kos*. I wrote it down.

We said our names. *Hei, jeg heter Mira. Jeg bor i Edinburgh.* I said it like a robot reading out a bus timetable, and she said "Fint!" anyway, which I am choosing to believe.

Why Norwegian, everyone asks (Mum asks). Honest answer: I don't quite know yet. There's a job advert I keep reading. There's a sea I've only ever seen from the other side. And I needed something that wasn't the quilt or the knee or missing Nana.

Tonight's words:

- hello and thanks
  - *hei*: hello, and also bye, confusingly
  - *takk*: thanks; *tusen takk*, a thousand thanks
- the important ones
  - *kaffe*: you can guess
  - *kos*: cosiness, candles, a blanket, being together; there's no English for it
  - *regn*: rain, which I suspect I'll need

Homework: count to twenty. I get as far as *tolv* before it goes wobbly.
