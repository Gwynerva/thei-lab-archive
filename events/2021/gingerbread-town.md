*Pepperkakebyen*: the gingerbread town. Every November Bergen builds a whole city out of gingerbread, houses and churches and the funicular and boats on a blue canal, and everyone queues in the dark to look at it. Children make most of it. Companies make some of it. This year, for the first time, a lighthouse by Nordvåg Kart.

We built it over three lunchtimes in the office kitchen: a tower of gingerbread rings stacked like the light at the harbour mouth, cemented with icing, with a strip of yellow icing for the beam. The colleague who does our depth soundings did the maths for the tower, and it still leaned. We called it the Leaning Lighthouse of Nordvåg, and put a little gingerbread chart in front of it with the rock in the right place.

Tonight I went to see it. There it was, by a canal, between a gingerbread school and a gingerbread ferry, leaning gently towards the sea.
