Back to the walk, the window and the kettle. Last spring it was almost an adventure: bread, a balcony, [a hundred walks](ref:event:walk-100). This time nobody is clapping and nobody is baking. It gets light at half eight and dark by four.

What I do, mostly:

- the knee exercises, twice a day, on the kitchen floor
- an hour of the quilt every evening, Nana's thimble on my finger
- a Norwegian lesson on Mondays and Thursdays, which is a story for another day
- the walk along the Water of Leith, the same one every day, the river high and brown

Marta plays scales through the wall. Biscuit sleeps on the radiator. The flat smells of wet coats.

I'm not sad all the time any more, which is new. Some days I even notice things: the heron at Canonmills, standing in the white water like it's waiting for a bus.
