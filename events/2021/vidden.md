Vidden: the walk across the high plateau from Ulriken to Fløyen. The cable car up Ulriken, then five hours across the top, over bare rock and bog and little lakes, following cairns and red T's painted on stones. Everyone in Bergen has done it. Anna has done it fourteen times.

We did it in a cloud. "We" being me and a group from work who go every September, who said this was normal and handed me a spare hat.

What I saw: rock, wet; cairns coming out of the grey one at a time, like at the top of Ben Nevis; a sheep, offended; the red T's; my own boots. What I heard: wind, my breathing, someone's flask lid, and somewhere a waterfall we never found.

Twice the cloud tore open for half a minute, and there were lakes below us, dark and still, and then it closed again.

New word: *fjellvett*, mountain sense. There's a list of nine rules. Rule one is plan your trip. Rule nine is turn back in time, there's no shame in it. Nobody turned back.

Two down. My legs are the shape of question marks.
