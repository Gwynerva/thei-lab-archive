The email came at ten past four, in the middle of a flood model for a council in Fife. *Vi er glade for å kunne tilby deg stillingen.* I read it with the dictionary open, just to be sure that *tilby* means offer. It does.

I rang Mum first. She went quiet, and then she said, "Your Nana would have packed your bag for you." Then she cried a bit and so did I, and then she asked whether they have proper tea in Norway.

Then Marta, who came out of her room with the last of the wine. Then Priya, who screamed down the phone from Plymouth.

I haven't said yes yet. That's a formality. I said yes in February, walking along the prom at Portobello in the sleet, when I decided to stop waiting for life to go back to normal and go and find a different normal instead.

Three good things:

1. *tilby*
2. Mum's voice
3. Biscuit asleep on the printout of the offer
