Ten days of *karantene* in a studio flat on Nordnes that Anna found for me: one room, one window, one kettle, and a view of a slice of Puddefjorden between two white houses.

Rain: day 1. Rain: day 2. Sun on day 3, for an hour, and I stood at the window like a cat. (The actual cat is in a cattery in Scotland, being fed salmon. He'll be fine.)

What I can see from the window:

- a boat that goes past at eight and comes back at four
- a man in shorts who walks a very small dog in every weather
- gulls, who are louder here and have opinions
- a corner of sky that is never the same grey twice

Anna leaves shopping bags on the doorstep and texts "*Levert!*", delivered, and I've learned the word for every vegetable by reading the receipts. *Gulrot*, carrot. *Løk*, onion. *Agurk*, which I refuse to believe is a cucumber.

::media[The view, most days]{file="rain-window-2"}

I read the Nordvåg style guide twice. I sleep. I do the knee exercises with my feet against the radiator. I have watched this bit of fjord more closely than any water since Saltwick. On the last evening the cloud lifted off the hills across the water and the whole thing turned gold, and I thought: all right, Bergen. I see you. Tomorrow I'm coming out.
