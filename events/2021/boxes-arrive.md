Forty-three boxes, carried up our lane by two men from the removal firm who called it *bratt*, steep, in the tone of people who once carried a piano up it.

The cello came out of its hard case in tune, nearly. The bookshelf came in pieces, as it left. The framed map from Forthline has a crack across one corner of the glass. Biscuit inspected every box and claimed the biggest one, empty.

And [the lopsided mug](ref:event:lopsided-mug "it has survived worse"), wrapped by Marta in four pages of newspaper, came out whole. It's still lopsided. It's on the table with tea in it.

Unpacked so far: six boxes. The rest can wait. I sat on the floor among them eating bread and brown cheese, the first meal in the flat that didn't come out of a paper bag, and for about ten minutes it felt like home. That's a start.
