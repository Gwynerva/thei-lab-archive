Bergen has seven mountains, more or less (people argue about which seven), and today I climbed the first and easiest. Fløyen is right behind the city. The funicular goes up every quarter of an hour, but I walked, because I'm stubborn, and because Mr Haldorsen downstairs, a retired ship's mate who has lived in this house since before I was born, told me a proper *bergenser* walks.

The path zigzags up through the trees, every bend numbered, and every few bends the funicular slides past through the leaves, full of tourists looking down at me with pity. Forty minutes to the top, sweating.

And then the whole city at once.

::media[From the top: the harbour, Nordnes, the islands, and the sea going on to the edge of everything]{file="floyen-1" layout="stretch"}

I could see Sandviken, and roughly my roof. I could see the office. I could see the harbour mouth, and a ferry going out. The weather came in from the sea in grey sheets while I watched, went round the city, and went away again.

Waffles at the top, with brown cheese. I'm learning. One down, six to go.
