First day at Nordvåg Kart. My desk is by a window that looks across the roofs to Vågen, and if I lean, I can see a sliver of Bryggen. I will be leaning a lot.

Things I learned today:

- Everyone says *hei* to everyone, including the boss, and nobody is Mr or Ms anything. Anna is Anna.
- Lunch is at half eleven, and it's a *matpakke*: slices of bread wrapped in greaseproof paper, with *pålegg* on top. *Pålegg* is anything you put on bread. Cheese is *pålegg*. Brown cheese is very much *pålegg*. Jam, ham, fish paste out of a tube: all *pålegg*. I mentioned beans on toast and got a look.
- Coffee is taken seriously and often.
- The charts are of small harbours up and down the west coast, the kind of place with a quay, a shop and a church, and a skerry that the old chart puts in the wrong place.

Anna gave me my first one: a little harbour north of the city whose survey dates from the 1930s. My job is to bring it into this century without moving anything a fishing boat relies on.

I said *takk* about two hundred times. On the way home it rained, of course. Rain: day 9 of the 16 since I landed.
