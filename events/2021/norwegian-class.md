A2, in a real room, with a whiteboard and a radiator that clanks. Eight months of Norwegian through a laptop, and it turns out people in a room are much harder to understand than people on a screen, because they don't wait their turn.

There are eleven of us from nine countries: a nurse from the Philippines, two engineers from Chennai, a Polish carpenter who already speaks better than the rest of us and is only here for the grammar, a dentist from Aleppo, me. Our teacher writes everything twice, once in *bokmål* and once the way people in Bergen actually say it, and then shrugs.

We did the past tense. *Jeg flyttet til Bergen.* I moved to Bergen. I said it and the room nodded, and something about saying it out loud to strangers who had done the same thing made it true in a way the permit hadn't.

In the break we compared rain. The engineers from Chennai said the rain here is "too thin". I'm adding that to my list.
