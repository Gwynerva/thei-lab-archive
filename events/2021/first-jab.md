First jab, in a conference centre where I once sat through a very long talk about drainage. Now there were arrows on the floor, a man in a lanyard who called everyone "my love", and a nurse who asked if I was nervous and then did it while I was still answering.

Fifteen minutes on a plastic chair afterwards, in case. I texted Clare a photo of the plaster. She replied with a thumbs up from the middle of a night shift.

Arm sore by teatime. Heavy-headed all evening. Didn't care. It felt like a door had opened a crack, and on the other side of it, somewhere, there's Bergen.
