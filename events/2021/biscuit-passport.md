Biscuit has more paperwork than I do. Today the vet scanned his microchip (it beeped; he didn't), gave him his rabies jab and filled in the first of the forms in green ink. He sat on the scales, five kilos and a bit, and glared at me the whole time as if I'd personally invented rabies.

The list, stuck to the fridge:

- [x] microchip read and written down, all fifteen digits
- [x] rabies vaccination
- [x] wait at least 21 days after the jab before he flies
- [x] book the pet transport (he flies as cargo, in a heated hold, which sounds worse than it is)
- [x] buy a crate he can stand up and turn round in
- [ ] the health certificate from the vet, in the last days before he flies
- [ ] leave the crate open in the living room for a month so he thinks it's furniture

He's already sitting in the crate. He's also already been sick in it. Progress.
