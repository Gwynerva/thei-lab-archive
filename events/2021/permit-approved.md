The permit is approved. Two months of forms, a folder of certified copies, a degree certificate I had to dig out of a box under the bed, a morning in an office in Edinburgh having my fingerprints taken. And then, today, an email that said, more or less, *you may come*.

I read it on the bus and missed my stop.

*Oppholdstillatelse*: a residence permit. Eighteen letters. I can say it now, slowly, in three goes. *Opp-holds-tillatelse.* Literally something like "permission to stay up", which is what I'll need for the first winter.

Still to do: Biscuit's papers, a flat, the movers, telling the orchestra, and telling the knee it's going to have to climb seven mountains.
