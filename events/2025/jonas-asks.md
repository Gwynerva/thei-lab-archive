After the dishes he said come and look at the stars, and I said it was minus six, and he said come anyway.

We stood at the bottom of the orchard in the snow. The fjord was black, Folgefonna a pale shape across it, and there, low in the south, was Mira, the star, the wonderful one. He had checked. He had a ring in his pocket, twisted from the copper wire we stitched *Nana June* together with, a stand-in until a real one, he said, and he got as far as *vil du* before I said yes.

We went in and did not tell anyone until the morning, and sat by the stove with it on my finger, gold and green in the firelight. It is still the only ring I want.
