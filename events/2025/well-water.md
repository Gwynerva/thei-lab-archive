The letter from the lab came this morning: no bacteria worth the name, a little iron, a little hard. *Drikkevann*, it says at the bottom, drinking water, with a stamp. Six weeks of carrying bottles up from the shop in Utne are over.

The well is up the slope behind the barn under a wooden lid, fed by a spring that comes out of the hillside and never, Ragnhild says, ran dry. I went up and recorded it, because I wanted to keep the sound of the day we were allowed to drink it.

::audio[The spring above the well, on the day the letter came]{file="sound-stream-1" title="The spring"}

Jonas poured two glasses from the kitchen tap, the only part of the kitchen that does anything, and we drank them standing up. It tastes of stone. It tastes, Jonas says, like the water he drank here as a boy, visiting Ragnhild in the summers, which it is.
