I woke because the light was wrong: too bright, too quiet. Fifteen centimetres overnight and still coming, big slow flakes falling straight down onto forty bare trees. The fjord was black against it. Folgefonna had vanished into the white across the water as if it had never been there.

Biscuit went out, stood in it to his elbows, looked at me with real betrayal and came back in. He did the same in Bergen in 2022. Some things he does not learn, and I love him for it.

The road down was not ploughed until two, so there was nothing to do but be snowed in, and we were. Porridge. A walk to the bottom gate and back, which took an hour because I kept stopping to look. Then the stove, and blankets, and on the laptop a short film Jonas had never seen, about a shepherd girl and her dog and the spirits of a mountain, called, of all things, *Spring*.

::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WhWc3b3KhnY"}

Either very fitting or very silly. Both. Afterwards I drew the orchard with the trees as small grey crosses on white paper, the simplest map I have ever made.
