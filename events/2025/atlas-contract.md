Signed today, at the kitchen table that is not yet in a kitchen, with the fountain pen Mum gave me when I graduated. *Thirty Walks Around the Fjord*, for Forlaget Fjellstue, a publisher of four people above a bike shop in Bergen.

What I have agreed to, in plainer words than the contract's:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. thirty walks around the Hardangerfjord, each walked by me at least once
2. thirty maps, drawn by hand, to the same scale and with the same symbols
3. a page of words for each, saying where to park, where to turn and where to sit
4. drawings in by the end of April, proofs in May, the printer in September
5. no walk anyone could fall off without trying quite hard
:::

The last one is the editor's, and the one I like best.

::link[Their list, which I have read cover to cover twice]{url="https://example.org/forlaget-fjellstue/books"}

I have walked eleven of them already without knowing they were for a book. Nineteen to go: a summer of boots. Jonas has offered to carry the sandwiches on all of them, and to drive me to the start of the ones that begin on the other side of the water.
