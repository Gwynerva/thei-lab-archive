Three and a half years in Sandviken, the last nineteen months of them in this bigger flat with Jonas, and all of it fits into sixty-one boxes. I counted. Every box has a little map of the room it is going to drawn on the lid, which Jonas says is the most Mira thing he has ever seen. He has watched me plan a Munro, so that is saying something.

Roughly where everything went:

- The kitchen
  - the pottery from my second go at clay, wrapped twice
  - the waffle iron, which is not negotiable
- The workroom
  - the drawing board, the paints, the Sandviken map that paid for the paints
  - the cello, which travels on my lap in the car and nowhere else
- Biscuit
  - one carrier, one blanket, one very offended cat

What does not go in a box: the view down to Byfjorden from the kitchen window, the rain, and the woman at the bakery on the corner who started saying :hint[*det vanlige?*]{note="the usual? The best two words a bakery can say to you"} in our second month.

I wrapped Sverre's barometer in a tea towel last of all. The needle still says rain. In Bergen it has never once been wrong.

Make do and mend, Nana would say. I say pack the mending first.
