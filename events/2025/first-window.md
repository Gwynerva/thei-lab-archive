The first of [the windows](<ref:section:farmhouse/the-house#h:Fourteen windows> "all fourteen, by name") came out this morning: the small one in the pantry, chosen because if we ruined it nobody would see.

We did not ruin it. Jonas took the sash out the way he takes a plank off a boat, as if it might still be useful to someone, and laid it on the trestles in the barn. The putty came away in grey crumbs. Under four coats of paint there is pine as tight-grained as anything you can buy now, and the glass is the old drawn kind, wavy, with a bubble in the corner pane like a fish's eye. We are keeping every pane.

The plan, for each of them:

1. Out, and the hole covered with a board and a prayer
2. Old paint off, slowly, with the heat gun and patience
3. Rot cut out, new wood scarfed in where it must be
4. New putty, linseed oil paint, three coats, a week between
5. Back in, and the board taken down

Fourteen to go. ~~Fourteen~~ Thirteen and a half, Jonas says, if we count the one on the trestles as half done, and he does, so I do.
