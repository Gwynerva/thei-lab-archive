My fourth 17 May, and the first in the village. [My first, in Bergen](ref:event:first-17-mai "2022, and half the city in the street") was drums and crowds and *buekorps* boys marching in step; Utne's is forty children, a brass band of eleven, the fire engine and a tractor with a flag tied to the exhaust.

We stood by the road outside the hotel and shouted *hurra* when everyone else did. Jonas wore his good shirt. Sigrid wore her :hint[*bunad*]{note="the folk costume; every valley has its own, and hers is Hardanger's, with the beaded breastplate"}, which she has worn every 17 May since she was confirmed and which still fits, she says, because she has never once had a second helping of anything.

Afterwards: hot dogs in *lompe* at the school, ice cream for the children, coffee for the rest of us, and a speech by a girl of about twelve about what freedom means, which was better than most speeches I have heard from grown men.

An old man next to me asked where I was from, and when I said Whitby, by way of Bergen, he said *velkommen heim*. Welcome home. I had to go and look at the fjord for a minute.
