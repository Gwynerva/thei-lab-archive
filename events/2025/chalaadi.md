The jeep from Ushguli left at eight and took three hours to undo what we had walked in four days, which felt like an insult to our legs. Back in Mestia by lunch, and because neither of us can sit still, out again in the afternoon for Chalaadi.

It is the gentle one. A taxi to the bridge, then a path through tall pines with sunlight in stripes across it and boulders the size of cars, the river beside you all the way.

::media[The path in, before the trees gave out]{file="chalaadi-2"}

Then the trees end and there is the glacier, grey and dirty at its snout, pouring down from a peak that was in cloud and then, for about a minute, was not.

::media[The ice, and the minute the peak came out]{file="chalaadi-1"}

I touched it. Jonas told me not to stand under it, and I did not stand under it for long. We walked back down in the evening light and ate khachapuri for the fourth day running, and nobody complained.
