Seven months of hats indoors are over. The chimney man came on Thursday, lowered a camera down the flue, looked at the screen for a long time and said *det går fint*, it'll do, which is the most anyone in Hardanger has said about anything in this house.

Jonas laid the fire this evening: birch from the pile behind the barn that Eirik cut for us in the spring, kindling split thin, one firelighter as a concession to the century. I lit it. The paper caught, then the kindling, then the stove began to tick as the iron warmed, and the whole room changed. The windows went dark and the room went gold, and Biscuit arrived from wherever he had been and lay down in front of it as if he had never been anywhere else.

We sat on the floor and did not talk. Outside it was raining and four degrees. Inside it was twenty-one, by the thermometer on Sverre's barometer, which has never looked so pleased with itself.
