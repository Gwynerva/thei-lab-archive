The second pressing, and everybody knew what to do this time, which made it faster and much less funny.

The Nesheims' press up on its trailer at nine. Sigrid with her crates and slightly fewer opinions than last year, because the Aroma was right. Anna and her husband on the early ferry again. Jonas on the screw again. Me on the clipboard, writing down every crate and every carboy, because that is who I am.

::card[Last year's number, to beat]{to="ref:event:cider-pressing-2025#:~:text=212"}

The trees were heavier this year after Sigrid's pruning, and the two nameless ones gave more than they ever have, as if to make a point. By five, in the dusk and the wasps: **257 litres**, in eleven carboys, and a cellar that smells like the inside of an apple.

Last year I did not know what I was doing and was grateful to everyone. This year I knew a little, and was grateful to everyone anyway. That part does not seem to wear off.
