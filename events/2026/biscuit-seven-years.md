Seven years ago today [he came home](ref:event:biscuit-comes-home), about three years old and furious about the carrier. Two countries, four homes, one roof incident and a great many maps slept on since.

He is about ten now, and this winter he has started to be an old cat. He sleeps more and longer, mostly on the bench by the stove, mostly on whatever atlas sheet I have just left to dry. He takes the stairs one at a time. He has stopped going down to the bottom of the orchard and patrols the porch instead, which he considers sufficient. The vet in Odda says his heart is good and his kidneys are a ten-year-old cat's kidneys, and to keep an eye.

I keep an eye.
