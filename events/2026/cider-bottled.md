Three and a half months in the cellar, and today [the juice we pressed in October](ref:event:cider-pressing-2025#:~:text=212 "all two hundred and twelve litres of it") came up the stairs in carboys and went into bottles.

The bottles were the hard part. We have been collecting since October: from the Nesheims, from Sigrid, from the recycling at the shop in Utne when nobody was looking. Two hundred and eighty-one of them, washed in the bath. I drew the label in the evenings after the atlas: the house, the slope of trees, the fjord, and *Øvre Lid 2025* in the hand I learned on the lettering course.

Siphon, fill, cap, wipe, box. Jonas siphoned, I capped, Biscuit supervised from the top of the freezer. By four the cellar was full of boxes and the kitchen that is not yet a kitchen was sticky to the elbow.

Sigrid came at five to taste. She held the glass up to the window, smelled it, drank, and was quiet for a long time.

*Tørr. God.* Dry. Good. Then: *the Aroma was right*, which was my idea, and which I will be reminding Jonas of until the next pressing.
