Clare flew into Bergen with Isla on the Saturday and I fetched them on the bus and the ferry, Isla asleep on my lap for the whole crossing with a fist in my hair. Dev had to work. He sent a long list of instructions, which Clare read once and put in the bin.

Isla is two and a third and has just discovered the word *why*. Why is the water down there. Why is the snow up there in the summer. Why is Biscuit old. Why does Jonas have a beard. I answered all of them, more or less truthfully, and found I did not know why the snow stays on Folgefonna in July, except that it does.

She was frightened of the cows for a day and then not. She picked up every fallen green apple in the orchard and brought them to me one by one, as presents. She and Biscuit came to an understanding on the second evening: she would not chase him, and he would lie within reach and allow one finger, carefully.

Clare and I sat up on the porch every night with a bottle of the cider and talked the way we have not since we shared a room in Whitby.\
She said: *you look like you live here.*\
I said: I do.

On the last morning Isla stood at the window with her nose on the glass and said *bye, fjord*. I will be hearing it for a week.
