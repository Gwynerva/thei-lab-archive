Two days earlier than last year, and whiter, because Sigrid made us prune in March and stood over us while we did it. The two nameless trees are covered. They do not care what anyone calls them.

The difference this year is that I know what I am looking at. Last May it was a white slope. This May it is forty trees, each with a name or a nickname and a crate count from October in a column in my notebook.

::media[The Nesheims' grandson flew his drone low along the rows for us]{file="video-orchard" layout="natural"}

We had coffee on the bench at the bottom of the orchard, with the bees, and I remembered standing on the porch last year with my coffee going cold, not knowing what to do. This year I knew. I sat down.
