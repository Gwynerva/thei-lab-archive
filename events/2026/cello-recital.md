Once a year the orchestra gives a spring evening where anyone who wants to plays something alone. Eight years ago I would have laughed. Four years ago I would have found a reason to be ill. This year I put my name down in January, before I could think.

The prelude and the two minuets from the first suite: the prelude because I learned it bar by bar in lockdown and it has been mine since, the minuets because they are kind. I practised in the parlour every evening after the atlas. Biscuit left the room for a week, then stopped bothering.

I was fourth, after a violin, a viola and a very good bass. I sat down, set the spike, looked once at Jonas in the third row, and played.

::audio[The prelude as it should go; mine went nearly like this]{file="music-bach-cello-1" title="Prelude, first suite"}

:::spoiler
On the way into the pedal point, about bar twenty-nine, I lost it. For two beats there was nothing in my head at all. Then my left hand found the note on its own, the way it has ten thousand times in the parlour, and nobody noticed but me and the bass, who smiled.
:::
