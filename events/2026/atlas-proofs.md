The parcel came on the morning ferry and the post van brought it up the hill: a heavy brown envelope from Fjellstue, and inside it the whole book, every page, on proper paper, the maps in colour for the first time. *Thirty Walks Around the Fjord*. My name on the front, small, which is how I wanted it.

I laid it out on the new kitchen table, page by page, and walked round the table looking at it for half an hour before I could pick up the red pen.

Then the red pen. So far:

- a footbridge on walk nine that I drew on the wrong side of the stream
- walk twenty-two says *turn left at the barn*, and there are two barns
- a contour on the Vøringsfossen map that goes uphill in a way no contour ever has
- Utne spelled correctly everywhere except on the map of Utne

I am keeping a list, as I always do, and anything that gets past me will go on [the errata page](ref:page:atlas-errata "for the mistakes I miss anyway"). Something will.

Jonas read the words for walk thirty, the one up behind our house, out loud while I made supper, and stopped in the middle and said, *you wrote this*. As if he had not watched me do it all winter.
