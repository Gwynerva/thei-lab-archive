## The first entry

Ten years ago today, in the top-floor flat off Leith Walk, I wrote at the top of a page that I was going to keep a diary *properly, in one place, for once*. I was twenty-four, had been in Edinburgh a fortnight, and thought GIS was going to be about maps. It was about maps. It was about everything else too.

I did not keep it in one place. It was notebooks until 2019, then files, then this. But I kept it.

## What is in it

I went back through [the diary](/diary/) this week, not all of it, nobody could, but the edges. Some things I had forgotten entirely. Some I had remembered wrong. A few I had remembered exactly, and those were mostly small: a bun on a bench in Stockbridge, the smell of resin in Jonas's workshop, the night the power went in Leith and Marta played by candlelight.

- Two countries, four homes, one cat.
- A dissertation, two jobs, one left well and one left badly, and now work that is mine.
- Thirty-four Munros, seven mountains in a day, sixty kilometres in Svaneti.
- A kayak, a house, an orchard, a book with my name on it at the printer.
- Nana, and then not Nana.
- Jonas.

The voice changes, which I had not expected. Exclamation marks everywhere in 2017. Headings by 2019, as if I thought someone was marking it. Whole weeks of single lines in 2023. And lately something slower, which I think is just what I sound like now.

---

## What I think it is for

Nana kept letters, a tin of them, which I read after she died and which told me who she was at twenty-four, and at forty, and at seventy, in her own words, and not who I had decided she was. I think that is what this is. Not a record for anyone else. A way of not making myself up afterwards.

There are hard years in here. 2020. The autumn of 2023, which is mostly private and will stay so. I have not tidied them. They were true.

## Three good things, today

1. A card from Mum, posted a week ago in Whitby, that just says *ten years, love, keep going*.
2. Biscuit asleep on the first notebook, which I got down from the shelf, as if he knew.
3. Jonas bringing coffee to the desk, reading over my shoulder, and saying nothing about it.

Ten years.\
Here is to the next ten, written in one place, for once.
