Minus two at seven, and the orchard grass white and crisp under my boots on the way to the well. The last apples on the high branches had frost on their shoulders. The boathouse roof at the bottom of the slope was white, and the fjord below it perfectly still and steaming a little, the way warm water does under cold air.

[The forecast](https://www.met.no "it said minus one; it was minus two") had promised it for three days, and I had not believed it, because it was fourteen degrees on Friday.

I went back in for the camera and then did not use it. I stood with my hands round my coffee and watched the sun come down the mountain across the water, field by field, until it reached the orchard and the white went to wet and the frost was over.

The second winter in the house. Firewood stacked in the barn, a kitchen, fourteen windows that close, and a cat who has already claimed the bench by the stove. I am not afraid of this one.
