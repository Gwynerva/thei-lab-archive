A day off the boat. We hired bicycles in Lerwick and rode out on the small roads, up over the hill with the islands spread round us, brown and green and grey, the sea on both sides at once.

Halfway up the hill road there were ponies: eleven of them, standing in the road as if they had been put there to stop traffic, chestnut and black and one the colour of cream, all about the height of my hip and all mane. They looked at us. We looked at them. Nobody moved for some time.

::media[The one with the most hair, and the least interest in us]{file="shetland-pony-2"}

Eventually the cream one came over, inspected the bag on Jonas's handlebars, found nothing in it worth having, and the herd parted round us like water round a rock. I have wanted to see a Shetland pony in Shetland since I was seven. They were exactly as advertised.
