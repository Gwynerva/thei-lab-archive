The fifth cohort. Eight Monday evenings, twelve students, one map each by the end, which is the only rule I have kept unchanged since the first term.

This lot: a hydrologist in Tromsø, two planners from Stavanger, a farmer near Voss who wants to map his own drainage, a retired ship's engineer who signed up "to keep the brain in gear", and seven others I am still learning, faces in little boxes with bookshelves and cats behind them. Biscuit appears in mine most weeks. He has a following.

What I teach has hardly changed: layers, projections, symbology, labels, the print layout, patience. What I have changed is the order. Projections used to be week two, and broke people. Now they are week five, when everyone already has something they care about on the screen and is willing to suffer for it.

Week three as I write, styling, which is the week everyone either falls in love with the software or gives up. Nobody has given up yet.
