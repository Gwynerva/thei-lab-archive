Finished the sweater tonight. Cast on in April, Day 21, in front of the news. The body took all summer. The first pair of sleeves came out long enough for a gorilla, so I pulled them back in October and knitted them again.

Raspberry red, cables up the front. It fits. It's warm. One cable crosses the wrong way, near the left hip, and I'm leaving it.

Nana taught me to knit when I was eight, on her settee, with two pencils and a ball of string, before she'd let me near her needles. I kept thinking of that, casting off.

Wearing it now. Biscuit is lying on the sleeve.
