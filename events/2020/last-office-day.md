Dougie came out of the glass box at eleven and said, "Right. Home, all of you, from tomorrow. Take what you need." Twelve people stood up and looked at their desks like people packing for a long trip without knowing the weather.

I took my monitor, the good keyboard, my chair cushion, the spider plant (Gerald, three years old), and the mug that says WORLD'S OKAYEST MAPPER. Ines took the other plant. Somebody took the kettle and nobody stopped them.

I carried the monitor home down Leith Walk in a bag for life, with Gerald on top. The street was quiet in a way I've never seen it at lunchtime. A man coming the other way with a printer under his arm nodded to me like a fellow soldier.

Set it all up on the kitchen table. Biscuit was sitting on the keyboard within four minutes. Colleague of the year.
