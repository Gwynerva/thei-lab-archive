The peak night of [the Perseids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perseids "dust from a comet, every August"), a clear sky forecast, and the city lights hidden behind a hill. So Marta and I took the last bus out to the edge of the Pentlands with sleeping bags, a flask of tea and a packet of custard creams, and walked up in the dusk to a slope above the reservoir.

It doesn't get properly dark here in August until nearly midnight. Then it did, and we lay on our backs on the bags and counted. The first one went right over us, long and green, and Marta shouted. After that we got calmer and more competitive. Thirty-one between midnight and two, by my count; twenty-nine by hers, but she fell asleep for a bit.

The Milky Way came out over the hills, faint, like breath on a window. Sheep coughed in the dark, which is a horrible noise if you're not expecting it.

Walked down at first light and caught the first bus home, frozen and stiff and very happy. Best night of the year.
