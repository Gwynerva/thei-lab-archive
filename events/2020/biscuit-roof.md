I opened the skylight to let the paint smell out, turned round for one minute, and Biscuit was gone.

He was on the roof. Four floors up, on the slates, strolling along the ridge between the chimney pots like a tightrope walker in no hurry at all, looking down into the street with great interest. I stood on the stepladder with my head out of the skylight, saying his name in a voice I was trying to keep calm. He looked at me as if I were the one in the wrong place.

An hour. Treats rattled: ignored. Tuna: considered, then ignored. Marta on the pavement opposite with binoculars, reporting his movements. A small crowd. One man suggested the fire brigade; another said the fire brigade don't do cats any more; they had a long and friendly argument about it.

In the end it started to rain, and he came back in through the skylight as if he'd always meant to, dripping, and sat on the stepladder and washed. The skylight now has a screw in it. He's sulking on the map drawer.
