Day 7. There's no yeast in Leith. Not in the Co-op, not in the corner shop, not in the big supermarket by the Shore, where there's no flour either. Marta found a bag of strong white at the back of the cupboard from before Christmas, I've read four websites, and so: a starter.

Fifty grams of flour and fifty of water in a jam jar with a loose lid. Stir. Leave it on top of the fridge where it's warm. Do it again tomorrow, and the day after, for about a week, until it bubbles and smells of apples and beer and faintly of nail varnish, and is alive.

Marta said that if we're going to feed it twice a day it needs a name. Hamish. Obviously Hamish.

At the moment he's a jar of beige paste with an elastic band round it to mark how high he's risen (not at all, yet). In a week, if he lives, part of him becomes the :hint[levain]{note="the bit of starter that goes into the dough and raises the loaf"} for a loaf. I've never been so invested in a jar.
