Since October: the prelude from the first cello suite. Everyone knows it, even people who don't think they do: the rolling broken chords, and the bit near the end where it climbs and climbs over the open D. Forty-two bars. I started with the first four, at half speed, and Mrs Ogilvie said, "Again." She has said it for eight months, by email since March.

This morning, with Marta out on her walk and the flat quiet, I propped my phone on the music stand and pressed record. Forty-one takes. In take nine I stopped to sneeze. In take twenty-six Biscuit walked across the bow. Take thirty-eight was the one.

::audio[Take thirty-eight]{file="music-bach-cello-1" title="The prelude, first suite"}

It isn't perfect. There's a wobble in bar 22, and the pedal at the end is too fast because I could see the finish line. But it's all there, beginning to end, and it's mine.

I sent it to Mrs Ogilvie, who wrote back an hour later: *Good. Now the allemande.* I sent it to Nana, who rang straight away and said, "Play it to me down the phone," so I did, the whole thing, with the phone on the stand. She said it was very nice and very sad. That's twice she's said so. She's right.
