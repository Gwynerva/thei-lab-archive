Day 13. Nobody has told the spring. Sunny, nine degrees, and the balcony at the back is the size of a single bed, but it gets the sun from eleven till four, which in Leith is a fortune.

What went in today, into tins, yoghurt pots and the bottom halves of milk bottles:

- Tomatoes
  - Tigerella, striped, from a packet Mum posted
  - Gardener's Delight, the safe one
- Herbs
  - basil, which will die
  - parsley, which won't
  - chives
- Nasturtiums, because Nana always had them and they'll grow in anything
- Sweet peas, for the railings

Labels made from lolly sticks, in pencil, which Biscuit has already pulled out of two pots and taken under the sofa. I no longer know which pot is the chives.

Watering can: the old teapot. Greenhouse: a clear plastic bag over each tin, held on with a rubber band. Hopes: high, for a balcony the size of a bed.
