Mum rang at seven. Nana fell in her kitchen in the night and wasn't found till morning, when the neighbour saw the curtains still shut. Ambulance to Scarborough. A broken wrist, they think, and her chest isn't good.

No visitors on the ward. Mum is allowed in for half an hour on Thursday. The rest of us ring, and a nurse holds the phone to Nana's ear.

The fifteenth. She sounded like herself. Asked after Biscuit. Told me to wear a vest.

The eighteenth. She didn't know it was me at first.

The twentieth. Mum says come if I can.
