Came down for the weekend, early for Mum's birthday, and walked straight into [Storm Dennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Dennis "the second named storm in a week"). Warnings out for half the country. Whitby got the wind and the sea.

Saturday afternoon Dad and I went down to the chandlery to double the lines on the boats he looks after. The Esk was brown and fast and full of branches, and at the harbour mouth the sea was coming over both piers, green and then white, throwing spray as high as the lighthouses. They'd shut the West Pier. People stood by the bandstand with their hoods up to watch, leaning into the wind at an angle that would have been funny if it hadn't been so loud.

Dad stood watching the harbour with his hands in his pockets and said, "Never trust a calm sea." Then, after a while: "Your Nana says that." I know, Dad.

By Sunday afternoon it had blown itself out. Nothing of his was lost but a fender and a bin lid.
