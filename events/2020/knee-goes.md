Out for a run on the river path this morning, the first in weeks, trying to get back something like my half-marathon legs. Coming down the steps by the weir I put my foot on a step that wasn't where I thought it was, and my left knee went sideways and made a noise like a knuckle cracking, only on the inside.

I sat on the step for ten minutes. A man with a spaniel asked if I was all right; I said yes, which was a lie. Walked home very slowly, three kilometres, holding on to railings, and that was walk 173, the hardest yet.

It's twice the size of the right one now and it won't straighten. Frozen peas, the sofa, Biscuit on the good leg. The doctor on the phone says a sprain, maybe a ligament: physio, rest, no running.

A hundred and seventy-three walks in a row. I'm not stopping now. Tomorrow's will be to the end of the street and back.
