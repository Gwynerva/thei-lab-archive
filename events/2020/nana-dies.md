Nana died this evening, in the hospital at Scarborough, with Mum beside her. Mum rang at half nine and said, "She just went quiet, love, as if she was listening for something."

June Dunn.\
Born 15 June 1929.\
Seamstress. Wife of Arthur. Mother of Helen. My Nana.

She made my first winter coat and every Christmas stocking I ever had. She taught me to sew on a button and never to trust a calm sea.

I don't have anything else to write.
