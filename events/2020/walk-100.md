Walk 100. A hundred days in a row since 24 March, rain or shine, at least half an hour out of the door. Not one missed, though walk 31 was once round the block in a hailstorm and only counts because I say it does.

The last three, for the record:

98. The Water of Leith from the Shore up to Dean Village and back; a heron at the weir, and a man feeding him sardines from a tin.
99. Calton Hill at half nine at night, still light, with Marta. We sat on the steps and said nothing for a quarter of an hour.
100. The first one again: down Leith Walk, along the Shore, up the river path to Canonmills, home past the Botanics. An hour and ten minutes. I counted what wasn't there on walk 1: the cherry has leaves now, the bakery on the corner has a hatch and a queue, and the heron has a friend.

::media[Dean Village from the water, walk 98]{file="leith-river-1"}

I've marked every walk in pencil on a map of the city on the wall by the door, one line each. The lines along the river path are so thick now that you can't see the river. It's the best map I've made this year, and I make maps for a living.
