Day 34. Finished: the Dunn family tree, Nana's side and Grandad Arthur's, on one sheet of A2, in ink.

Six months of evenings since November: the census returns online, Mum's box of certificates, and two long phone calls with Nana in which she corrected nearly everything. ("No, love, that was my aunt's husband, and he was a rogue.")

It starts with a fisherman born at Staithes in 1868, the first Dunn anyone can find, and comes down through five generations to Clare and me. I drew it like a map: the oldest at the top like hills, the family running downhill towards the sea, the names set along the way like villages. Grandad Arthur, the merchant seaman, has a little ship beside him.

Rang Nana and read the whole thing to her. She was quiet for a while, and then she said, "You've put my aunt's husband in." I said he's family. She said, "More's the pity."

Posting her a copy on Monday, rolled in a tube, with *do not fold* on it twice.
