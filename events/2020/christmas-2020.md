Christmas in Leith. The rules changed again on the 19th, and I wasn't going to carry anything to Mum and Dad's this year of all years. Marta's family are in Poland and she couldn't go either. So: the two of us, a chicken (no point in a turkey), roast potatoes, crackers from the corner shop, and the paper hats.

Walked along the Shore in the afternoon, walk 277, frosty and still and pink.

Rang Whitby at four. Mum and Dad and Clare and Dev on the speaker, all at the table, and a gap at the end of it that everyone was trying not to look at. Mum said Nana's chair was there. Dad said, "Never trust a calm sea," and then nobody said anything for a bit.

In the evening Marta and I watched short films under a blanket. This one made us both cry, which is fair enough, this year.
