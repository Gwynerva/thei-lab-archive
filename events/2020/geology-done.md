Final assignment in tonight, and that's the course done: *Rocks for beginners*, sixteen weeks online, two evenings a week, started on Day 15 of lockdown because I needed something that wasn't the news.

I chose the Whitby coast for the final project, of course. Three years ago I measured how fast those cliffs fall into the sea; now I know what's falling. The shales at the bottom, grey and flaky, laid down as mud in a warm Jurassic sea. The ammonites in them, which Whitby people call snakestones. The jet, which is wood squashed for a hundred and eighty million years. And the sandstones on top, harder, holding the whole lot up until the shale underneath gives way. That's the entire erosion story in two layers. I could have done with this for the dissertation.
