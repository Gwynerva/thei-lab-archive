Marta's recital, on a day that only comes round every four years, which she said meant that if it went badly nobody would remember it till 2024.

It didn't go badly. A church hall off Great Junction Street, about sixty people on folding chairs, a borrowed piano with one sticky key, and Marta in a red dress playing the Franck sonata with a pianist from the orchestra. She'd been shaking in our kitchen at five. She stopped the moment she lifted the violin.

In the last movement the piano chases the violin round and round, the same tune a bar behind, and by the end I'd forgotten I knew her and was just listening.

Afterwards: wine in plastic cups, and a cake I'd made in the shape of a violin which looked, everyone agreed, like a guitar. She hugged me so hard I spilled the wine down both of us.

Walking home I thought: we should do more of this. Go out more, hear more music, see more people. Note to self, 29 February 2020.
