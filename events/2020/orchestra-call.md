Day 30. Agnes set up a video call for Tuesday rehearsal, for whoever could manage it, and forty-one of us managed, which is more than ever turn up at the church hall.

We tried to play the Elgar together. It cannot be done. Each of us is a fraction of a second behind someone and ahead of someone else, so what you hear is the same piece forty times over, slightly smeared, like a choir in a cathedral with far too much echo. The six cellos, in our six little boxes, sounded like forty. Agnes conducted for two minutes, then put her baton down and laughed until she had to take her glasses off.

So she muted everyone but herself and played the piano, and we all played along at home, unheard, alone together, which is daft and which I loved.

Afterwards everyone unmuted and talked at once for half an hour. Mrs Ogilvie, who had never used a computer for anything before, was there with her camera pointing at her ceiling. She said "better", to someone.
