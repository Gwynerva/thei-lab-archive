Day 27. Hamish is three weeks old and finally strong enough to raise more than his own jar, so: a batch of eight rolls, one for every flat on the stair.

They're not good. They're dense, a bit flat, and one has a burnt bottom (that's ours). But they're bread, and nobody in Leith has seen yeast for a month.

At ten this morning I left one on every doormat, wrapped in a tea towel or a paper bag, with a note: *From the top floor, made with Hamish. Stay well.* Then I ran back upstairs like a child who's rung all the doorbells.

By the afternoon there was a bar of chocolate outside our door from the second floor, a jar of chutney from the old woman on the ground floor, and a note from the student flat that said *who is Hamish*. Marta wrote back *a jar*.

That's the most we've talked to our neighbours in three years.
