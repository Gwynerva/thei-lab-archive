First physio, with Morag, in a little room above a shop on Easter Road, both of us in masks and the window open.

She bent my knee every way it bends, and a few ways I'd have said it didn't, and said "mm" a lot. Then she drew it for me on the back of a leaflet: the ligament on the inside, which I've stretched, and the cartilage, which is "probably fine, probably". No running till Christmas. Possibly no running till spring.

::media[The knee as Morag drew it, only neater]{file="knee-diagram-1"}

Homework, twice a day:

1. Straight-leg raises, ten, slowly.
2. Squeezing a rolled towel under the knee, ten seconds, ten times.
3. Standing on the bad leg while I brush my teeth, which sounds easy and is not.

She says the walking is good, as long as I go slowly and stop when it aches. So walk 185 was to the end of Easter Road and back, slowly, and I stopped twice. That counts.
