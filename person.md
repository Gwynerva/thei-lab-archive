I'm Mira, a cartographer from Whitby who lives above a fjord in Hardanger with Jonas, a ginger cat called Biscuit and an orchard that needs more of us than there are. I draw maps for a living and for the pleasure of it, teach other people to make their own, and make most other things by hand: notebooks, a kayak, cider, a kitchen.

I've kept a diary since October 2016, in Edinburgh, in Bergen and here, and this is where it all lives: ten years of days, the projects that filled them, and a few pages of things I wanted written down properly. It's a life, not a portfolio, and there is a great deal in it about rain.

I play the cello, not well and very happily. I swim in the fjord in winter, which everyone agrees is a strange thing to do on purpose. I'm named after a star that comes and goes but always comes back, and I like to think that's the general idea.
