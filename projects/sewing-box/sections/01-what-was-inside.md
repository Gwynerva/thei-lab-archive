---
title: What was inside
summary: Seventy years of thread, sorted on the kitchen table
---

I emptied it onto the kitchen table on a Saturday in February and sorted it all into piles. It took all day. Marta came in at teatime, looked at the table, and backed out again.

- **thread**: 112 reels, wooden ones and plastic ones
  - every shade of navy that has ever existed
  - a reel of real silk, gold, never used, still in its paper
- **buttons**: a biscuit tin full, the old Coronation tin
  - the brass ones off Grandad's merchant navy jacket
  - six pearl ones from somebody's wedding dress
- **needles**: in a felt book with my initials embroidered on the cover, which I'd forgotten I gave her when I was nine
- **pins**: hundreds, in a pincushion shaped like a strawberry
- **a thimble**: the ring kind, worn smooth on one side
- **notes**: a shopping list from 1987, a bus ticket from Scarborough, and a slip of paper in her handwriting that says only *Mira, sleeves*

I think the last one is from September, when I rang her about the sleeve that came out too long. She was going to tell me something about sleeves. I've kept the slip in the felt book.

> Make do and mend. And if you can't mend it, make do.

She said that about her hip, too.
