  - 2021-02-13..2021-03-20

Six weekends, five jobs, in order:

1. **Out.** Lifted the trays off their arms, unscrewed the brass, peeled out the old lining in shreds. Kept a scrap of the old green paper in the felt book.
2. **Glue.** Worked hide glue into the split along the side with a palette knife, and clamped it overnight with every clamp I own and two I borrowed from Dougie, collected from his doorstep.
3. **Brass.** Cleaned the arms and hinges with a paste of vinegar and salt, then polished them, not too bright. Replaced Nana's fuse wire with a proper rivet, and kept the fuse wire too.
4. **Lining.** Green baize, cut to fit each tray from a paper template, glued down with wheat paste, which I learned to make at the bindery four years ago.
5. **Back.** Screwed it all back together, waxed the outside with beeswax, and put the thread in.

The arms swing out like they always did. There's a faint squeak from the left one that wasn't there before, or that I'd forgotten.
