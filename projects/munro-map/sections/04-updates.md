Every Munro I climbed after the map went online got an update the same week. The pattern of three years:

- **2019**: ten updates, from the spring to Mount Keen at the end of September
  - number twenty, Ben Lawers, with a sea of cloud in the glens below
  - one update fixing a summit I'd put in the wrong glen entirely
- **2020**: six updates, five of them in the summer after lockdown
  - one of them, in January, taking back a hill I had marked as climbed by mistake
- **2021**: seven updates, the last of them
  - Ben Nevis, 3 July, in fog, number thirty-four

After that I moved the map off the box under my desk to a small paid host, put a line at the bottom saying *Stopped at 34 when I moved to Norway*, and left it. It still loads in under a second.
