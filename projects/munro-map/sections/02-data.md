### The hills

The list of Munros is public, and maintained with great care by people who love hills more than I do. I took the names, heights and summit positions from an open hill database, then checked every one against the Ordnance Survey's own maps, because two summits had moved a few metres in a recent resurvey and I wanted to be right.

### The base map

A plain, quiet map of Scotland, coastline and lochs only, so that the triangles were the loudest thing on it. I drew the style myself: pale land, pale blue water, no roads.

### My days

The dates, the weather and the one-liners came from [the diary](/diary/), copied by hand. The routes came from my phone's GPS when I'd remembered to switch it on, which was about half the time.

All of it went into one :hint[GeoJSON]{note="A plain text format for map features: points, lines and shapes, with properties attached, that a browser can read directly."} file, which is in the source download on the project page. It is about forty kilobytes.
