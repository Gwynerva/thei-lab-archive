---
title: The idea
---

November 2018. Ten Munros done, Schiehallion the tenth, and a spreadsheet with ten rows in it. Priya said "you should make it a map, you make everything a map", and she was right, and it was raining for a week, so I did.

What I wanted:

1. every Munro on a map of Scotland, from the start, so I could see the gaps
2. the ones I'd climbed in a different colour, the colour of rowan berries
3. one line for each day, so the map was a diary as well
4. nothing else: no login, no likes, no app

It would be a single page, fast on a phone in a car park at the bottom of a glen with one bar of signal. That was the hardest requirement, and the only one I really cared about.
