title: Building it
periods:
  - 2018-11-03..2019-01-20
---

Two and a half months of evenings, mostly the dark ones before Christmas.

1. A web page with a map on it. One evening.
2. All 282 triangles on the map. One evening, then three more evenings making them look right at every zoom level.
3. Clicking a triangle shows its card. A weekend, because the cards wouldn't close on a phone.
4. The red. I tried eleven reds. The one I chose is the colour of the rowan jelly Nana and I made in 2017, held up against the kitchen window.
5. Making it fast. I cut the page down until it loaded in under a second on one bar of signal, standing in the car park at Glen Lyon with my phone held up like a torch.

It went online on 20 January 2019. The first person to look at it, apart from me, was Dad, who wanted to know why so much of it was still grey.
