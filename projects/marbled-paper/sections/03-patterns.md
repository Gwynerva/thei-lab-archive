Marbling patterns have lovely names, and most of them are just one thing done after another.

- **Stone**: drop the colours one after another, each pushing the last into veins. My most reliable.
- **Combed**: drag a rake through the stone pattern, one way and back.
- **Nonpareil**: comb it again with a fine comb until it looks like woven silk.
- **Feathered**: draw a single stylus through the nonpareil in waves.
- **Bouquet**: weave the comb as you pull, which I have managed exactly once.
