After the move to Bergen, tideline became something I looked after rather than something I made. A few issues a year. A new Python version that broke the file reader. A question from a student about the Norwegian ports, which I could suddenly answer from the window.

In 2021 I fixed things the same week. In 2022 I fixed them the same month. In 2023 I didn't fix them.

the issue list in spring 2023: eleven open. one of them a year old.

not because it was hard. because everything was.

I don't know exactly when I stopped. That is the honest answer, and why this stage has no proper ending.
