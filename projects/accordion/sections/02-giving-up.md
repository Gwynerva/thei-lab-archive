---
title: Giving up
summary: Three tunes, eight weeks, and the cello in the corner
---

may.

three tunes. *Kjerringa med staven*, which is a children's song and which I still can't play without the bellows hiccupping in the middle. a waltz from the library book. the first half of something Irish.

the left hand won't. it knows a fingerboard, not buttons.

:::private
it isn't the accordion. nothing's going in at the moment. work takes all of it and there's nothing left at the end of the day for learning anything. tired in a way sleep doesn't touch.
:::

put it back in its case. got the cello out. played the prelude, slowly, badly, the whole way through. cried a bit, not about the accordion.

sold it in june to a girl who'll play it properly. that's the right ending for it.
