saturday. a [flea market](ref:event:accordion-flea-market) in a school gym, in the rain. went for a lamp. came home with an accordion.

six hundred kroner. the man said it was his father's. he played a few bars to show me it worked, a waltz, and the whole gym went quiet for a moment, and that was that.

red, cream keys, chrome. heavy. the case smells of an old house and peppermints.

carried it home on the bus on my knees. jonas looked at it, and at me, and said "ok," in the voice he uses for weather warnings.
