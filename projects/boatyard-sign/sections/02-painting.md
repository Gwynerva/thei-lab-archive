saturdays in the workshop, starting the week after [the launch](<ref:project:kayak#h:The launch> "Nana June, 20 May").

the board first. three larch planks, tongued and grooved, glued, planed flat. jonas helped with the planing. the kayak on its trestles beside us, finished, which is a strange feeling, a boat just sitting there.

then the ground: three coats of navy enamel.

then the letters. mum's photos printed at full size, taped together on the workshop floor, the outlines pricked through with a needle and pounced onto the board with a bag of chalk dust. the old way. the book explains it better than I can.

then paint. a long sable brush, a mahl stick to rest my hand on, white enamel, no hurrying. a letter an evening, some evenings. the R took three.

the red shadows last. then two coats of the varnish left over from the kayak, which was just enough.

:::private
the only thing I finished that summer that I'm proud of. work was eating everything else. painting letters was the one place nobody could reach me.
:::
