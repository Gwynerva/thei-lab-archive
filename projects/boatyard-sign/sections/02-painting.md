evenings in the workshop, starting the week after [the launch](<ref:project:kayak#h:The launch> "Nana June, 20 May").

the board first. stripped back to the wood: under the orange a green, under the green a white, and under the white somebody's initials cut with a knife, and *1971*. sanded round them. jonas helped with the sanding. the kayak on its trestles beside us, finished, which is a strange feeling, a boat just sitting there.

then the ground: two coats of orange enamel.

then the letters. the old ones traced off before the stripping, taped together on the workshop floor, and put back through carbon paper, so they're his letters and not mine. the book explains a better way, pouncing, for next time.

then paint. a long sable brush, a mahl stick to rest my hand on, black enamel, no hurrying. a letter an evening, some evenings. the R took three.

then three coats of the varnish left over from the kayak, which was just enough.

:::private
the only thing I finished that summer that I'm proud of. work was eating everything else. painting letters was the one place nobody could reach me.
:::
