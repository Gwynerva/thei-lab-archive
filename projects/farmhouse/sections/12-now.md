The snow went from the yard in the first week of April, and the barn started looking at us.

What we have done so far: emptied the loft of seventy years of things (two cartloads to the tip, one to the museum in Utne, which was delighted with a hay sledge), propped the north end with two new posts, and drawn the roof three times, once by me and twice by Jonas, who drew it properly. What we have not done is pay for it.

The plan for the autumn:

1. The roofer comes [the day after tomorrow](ref:diary:today+2) to measure.
2. New roof on the north half before the snow, if the money and the weather agree.
3. The loft floor in the spring.
4. Then, perhaps, the house gets its paint.

Biscuit has views on all of this. He wants the barn to stay exactly as it is, with the swallows in it.
