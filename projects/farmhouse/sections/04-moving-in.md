Five days, one hired van filled twice, one cello and one cat. Jonas drove. I sat beside him with Biscuit's carrier between my feet and the cello between my knees, from Sandviken to the ferry at Kvanndal and across the fjord.

What I remember of each day, which is not what the [move itself](ref:event:move-to-ovre-lid "the five days, in order") remembers:

- **Saturday.** The track. The van could not get round the last bend, so the first load came the last hundred metres on our backs and in Ragnhild's wheelbarrow.
- **Sunday.** Rain sideways, which showed us exactly where the roof was weakest. I put a saucepan on the landing and wrote *roof* at the top of a new page.
- **Monday.** Water off, water on, water off. Eirik walked up from below with a spanner and a thermos and fixed the stopcock in four minutes. Biscuit came out from under the bed.
- **Tuesday.** I unpacked the books before the plates, which tells you everything about me.
- **Wednesday.** The van back to Bergen, and home on the bus and the ferry. *Home*, I said, and only noticed afterwards.

There was no kitchen, in any sense that counts, for most of a year. There was a two-ring camping stove on the porch, a kettle, and the tap in the yard.
