We took the old kitchen out on the third of November, all of it except the range, down to the boards: cupboard doors held shut with a bent nail each, a cooker that had not cooked since about 2009, the sink and its bucket, and three layers of lino, the bottom one orange and brown squares that Ragnhild chose in 1974 and was proud of. It filled a skip. Under the last layer was the floor of 1891, wide pine boards, grey and dry and sound, and we stood and looked at it for a long time.

The electrician came back on the twenty-first of March to sign it all off, whistled the whole day, and at five o'clock switched everything on. That evening Sigrid and Eirik and Kari, who had fed us all winter, came up for fish soup at the new table under the west window.

[A kitchen at last](ref:event:kitchen-done) is how the event puts it. I just put "A kitchen!" and meant every letter.
