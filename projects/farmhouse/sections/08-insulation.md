The north wall had nothing in it but air, mouse nests and newspaper. Jonas took the inside panels off before I went to Georgia, and I came home to a house with its ribs showing.

Inside the wall we found:

- newspaper from 1911, which we kept one page of
- a clay pipe, broken
- a child's leather shoe, very small
- more mice than I am prepared to describe

The shoe went back in, wrapped in a cloth, with a note of the date. Jonas said that is what you do. I do not know whether that is a custom or just Jonas, and I did not want to find out by not doing it.

The wood fibre boards came on the nineteenth, the week after I got home, and went in over two weekends: soft, brown, dusty, and warm to the touch even before they were up. The north bedroom, which had been a fridge, is now only a cold room.
