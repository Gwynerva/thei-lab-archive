Our water comes from a well up the slope behind the barn: a spring out of the hillside, a stone-lined shaft with a wooden lid, and a pipe that runs down to the cellar. Ragnhild says it never once ran dry. Nobody had tested it since 2009, so for six weeks we carried bottles up from the shop in Utne, and I sent a sample to the laboratory in Bergen in a cool bag on the bus, feeling like somebody in a detective novel.

The answer came on the fourteenth of May: no bacteria worth the name, a little iron, a little hard. *Drikkevann*, said the letter, drinking water, and I pinned it on the pantry door beside Ragnhild's snow dates.

Jonas says it tastes of rock. I say that is what water is supposed to taste of.
