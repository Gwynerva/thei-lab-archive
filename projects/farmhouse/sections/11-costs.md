What the house has cost us so far, apart from the rent to Ragnhild, which I keep elsewhere.

::file[Every receipt, April 2025 to March 2026]{file="house-costs-2025" title="What the house cost"}

The big ones, rounded:

1. The stove and the chimney liner: thirty-two thousand kroner. The liner was most of it.
2. Wood fibre for the north wall: twenty-three and a half thousand.
3. The oak worktop: fourteen thousand.
4. The electrician: thirteen thousand.
5. Scaffolding for two weeks: ten thousand.

Altogether a hundred and fifteen thousand kroner and some change, which is more than I earned in the first half of 2025 and less than I feared. Nothing on the list was a luxury. The roofer's second visit, the one for the leak, is the only line I resent.

What it has not cost: a loan. We paid as we went, job by job, and when there was no money the job waited. The barn is waiting now.
