---
title: The rules
summary: Made up on the way down from my second Munro, and kept, mostly.
---

Made up on the way down Ben Vorlich in June 2017, out loud, to Priya, who agreed to all of them to make me stop talking!

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. It counts when I touch the top cairn. Not near it. *On* it.
2. A bad forecast is a reason not to go. Bad weather on the day is just weather.
3. Turning back is allowed. Turning back is clever. (I turned back twice. I'm still annoyed.)
4. No running. Not that I could!
5. Every one gets a line in the notebook, a colour on the map, and a piece of tablet at the top.
6. Ben Nevis is last. Whatever happens.
:::

I broke rule five once: on Bla Bheinn, in Skye, in the rain, when the tablet had turned to porridge in my pocket. I kept rule six for four years, which is the longest I have ever kept a rule about anything.

The packing list came later, after Ben More, when I got to the top without a hat in April and learned something.
