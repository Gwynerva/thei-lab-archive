Five, after lockdown. Short notes, because that was how I wrote that year.

Ben Oss. End of June, I think. The first hill in four months. Cried a bit at the top. Wind.

Ben Vorlich, the Loch Lomond one. July. Rain. Didn't care.

Beinn Ime. August. The first time I saw other people on a hill and was glad.

Ben Narnain. Late August. The Cobbler from above, all its knuckles.

Carn Liath. The fifth of September. Heather, a hare, a long grey day.

A week after Carn Liath the knee went, and that was the end of the hills for that year. I have the dates of all five in the list, but I could not have told you them in October. That summer every day was the same day with a different mountain on it.
