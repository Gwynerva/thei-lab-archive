Seven in 2018, numbers seven to thirteen, fitted round the first year at Forthline and the cello.

- **Ben More**, 21 April. Got to the top without a hat in a wind straight from the Arctic and learned about hats.
- **Stob Dubh**, the summit of Buachaille Etive Beag, 16 June. The small shepherd, they call it. It did not feel small.
- **Bidean nam Bian**, 7 July, on the Glencoe camping weekend, up through the Lost Valley in the heatwave, in midges.
- **Schiehallion**, 11 August, [number ten](ref:event:schiehallion), the fairy hill, the one they used to weigh the Earth. A long boulder field at the top, crossed like an old lady crossing a cobbled street, and a summit in cloud.
- **Cairn Gorm** and **Ben Macdui**, 15 and 16 September, a night in a bothy between them. Macdui is the second highest in Scotland and on top it feels like the surface of the moon.
- **Ben Vane**, 13 October, the smallest Munro, and steep enough to make the point.

I have started reading the map the night before like a book. Contour lines close together: that's a morning. A flat bit: that's lunch.
