The best year: nine, numbers fourteen to twenty-two, between Japan and the half marathon, on legs that had never been fitter and will never be again.

Beinn Ghlas in May, to start. Cruach Ardrain, Stuc a' Chroin. Then Beinn Dorain and Beinn an Dothaidh on two Saturdays in a row, above the railway line at Bridge of Orchy, where the train to Fort William goes round the horseshoe curve far below you like a toy. Ben Challum in August, wet.

[Number twenty](ref:event:ben-lawers) was Ben Lawers, on the last day of August, with Marta driving: the highest of the year and the best day of the four years, cloud all the way up and then, two hundred metres below the top, out of it into blue, with every glen full of white and the tops standing up out of it like islands. We stayed too long on top, which is how we came to be by the lochan in the dusk on the way down (the story is on [the main page](ref:project:munros#:~:text=lochan "under a spoiler")).

Lochnagar and Mount Keen in September, from the east, with the heather going over and the first frost.

By September I was going up hills faster than my training plan said I should be able to run. I did not tell the plan.
