Six! In one summer! I did not own proper boots in May and by October I owned proper boots, proper socks, a map case and opinions.

1. **Ben Lomond**, 6 May, with Priya, in a wind. My first ever! My legs did not work for two days.
2. **Ben Vorlich**, by Loch Earn, 17 June, where I made up [the rules](ref:section:munros/the-rules).
3. **Meall nan Tarmachan**, 29 July, a ridge like a dragon's back, cloud all the way.
4. **Ben Chonzie**, 26 August, which everyone says is the most boring Munro in Scotland, and everyone is right, and I loved it anyway because there were mountain hares.
5. **Bla Bheinn**, on Skye, 24 September, in rain so heavy we could hear it inside our hoods. Saw nothing. Counted it.
6. **Stob Binnein**, 28 October, with snow on the top already, the day the clocks went back. Priya fell over in it on purpose.

Three good things about this year of hills:

1. Priya.
2. The way my legs feel the day after, which is terrible, and also great.
3. Tablet tastes better at 1,000 metres. This is science.
