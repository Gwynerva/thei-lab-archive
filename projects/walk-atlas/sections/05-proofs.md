The [proofs arrived](ref:event:atlas-proofs) on 22 May in a box the size of a suitcase, and covered the new kitchen table for most of the summer.

The checklist I worked through, walk by walk:

- [x] every path matches the notebook
- [x] every name spelt right, in both languages (mostly)
- [x] the colours have survived the printing (the greens hadn't; a second proof in July)
- [x] page numbers on the fold-out map match the pages
- [x] the scale bar on each map is the right length, measured with a ruler, thirty times
- [ ] walk 17's junction, which I never resolved and which is now in the errata

In the middle of it, on 22 August, we got married in the orchard. The proofs went into a cupboard for a week. When I took them out again I found three mistakes in an hour, which proves something about weddings.
