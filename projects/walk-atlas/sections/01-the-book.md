---
title: The book
---

What the book is, in its own terms:

- **Thirty walks**, numbered round the fjord, from the shore at Utne to the hill behind our house
- **Thirty maps**, one per walk, each on a right-hand page, drawn by hand at about 1 to 25,000
- **Directions** on the facing page, short, with a line on what to look out for
- **Two languages**, Norwegian above, English below, in the same paragraph's space
- **A fold-out map** at the back with all thirty on it, for planning
- **Size:** narrow enough for a jacket pocket, which was the only thing I insisted on from the start

What it isn't: a book of summits. Some walks have one. Most go somewhere quieter: a waterfall, an old farm, a beach where the fjord is warm enough in August, the place where the ferry looks smallest from above.

The walk list as data, with lengths and climbs, is in the files on the project page. I made it first, before anything else, and it is the only part of the book that never changed.
