The files went to the printer on 15 September. Since then the book has been somewhere I can't see it, which is harder than I expected.

What happens now, as the publisher explained it to me twice:

- the printed sheets, any day now, for one last look at the colours
- folding and binding, about three weeks
- the boxes, to the publisher's office, then to the shops
- a small launch at the publisher's, with apple juice from the orchard

I have a printed sheet's date on the calendar, and I've walked down to the post box twice this week to check for nothing. The book is finished. I am not finished waiting for it.
