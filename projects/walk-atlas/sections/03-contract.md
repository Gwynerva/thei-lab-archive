---
title: The contract
periods:
  - 2025-06-10
---

Signed today, at the publisher's office in Bergen, three floors up above a bookshop, with the cat on the table and coffee in mugs that didn't match.

Thirty walks, thirty maps, delivered by the end of April 2026, for an advance that will pay for the stove and most of a window, and royalties after. My name on the spine, and the maps credited as mine, which I asked for and they had already written in.

I took the ferry home to Utne and sat outside on the deck the whole way, though it was raining, and read the contract again from start to finish, as if it might have changed since the office. It hadn't. Jonas met me at the quay with Biscuit's carrier, empty, which he said was for the contract, and he carried it up the hill like that, for a joke, the whole way.
