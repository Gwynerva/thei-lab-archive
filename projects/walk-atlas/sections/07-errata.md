title: Errata
summary: The mistakes, found and still to be found.
---

Every map book has mistakes, and this one is no exception. The corrections are kept on [the errata page](ref:page:atlas-errata) and in a plain text file on the project page, and will go into a second printing if there is one.

The first two were found before the book was even printed: a misspelt farm name and a car park that closed in 2025. Since then, from readers of the advance copies:

3. Walk 17: the junction above the second gate. The path goes left, not straight on. I knew it was wrong, and I'm sorry.
4. Walk 22: the stream is on the other side of the path. It has been since a flood in 2024. The farmer told me so, firmly.
5. Walk 26: "about two hours" is about three hours, unless you are Jonas.

:::spoiler
The worst one isn't on any page yet. On the fold-out map, the house at Øvre Lid is drawn with thirteen windows. Nobody has noticed. I'm leaving it, for now.
:::
