title: Before
summary: The year that led up to it, and the signs I walked past
---

What 2023 was, before October, in the order it happened:

- January: the kayak's deck on, after a winter of evenings in Jonas's workshop
- March to September: the big release at work, forty-one harbour charts, a date that moved twice while the work did not
- May: the Bergenstest, and the kayak launched, a week apart
- May to July: Dad's sign for his sixtieth, painted in the evenings
- August: Jonas moving in, a storm, the kayak on a rock at Herdla, and repairs
- September: the release month itself

Every one of those was a good thing, or a thing I chose. That was the trap. Nobody made me do any of it. I said yes to each one, happily, and each one was the one more thing that I am, as everyone who has ever known me will tell you, prone to taking on.

And under all of it, two things I had not looked at: Nana, who had died in a lockdown when I could not go to her, and whom I had not cried for properly; and the move, the whole life in Scotland packed into boxes, which I had treated as a project to be done well rather than a thing to be sad about.

The signs, which I can see now and could not then: sleeping eleven hours and waking tired. Reading the same paragraph for forty minutes. Crying in the toilets at work, and once at an advert for a ferry company. Not recording anything after June, not drawing in the margins after July. Saying I was ~~fine~~ tired, when people asked, and then, by September, not even that.
