a Monday. The doctor, the word, the form. I walked home through the fish market and bought nothing.

what the days were, in October:

- sleep. Ten, eleven hours, and then a nap
- one walk, slowly, down to the harbour and back
- Biscuit on my legs on the sofa all afternoon, as if he had been prescribed too
- not answering the phone, even to Mum, who rang anyway and talked to Jonas instead

november: [therapy](ref:section:burnout/therapy) on Tuesdays. Pottery again on Tuesday evenings, because clay does not need you to think. I made a bowl that was entirely the wrong shape and kept it.

a Sunday in November, rain against the window from breakfast to dark, and I could not read and could not sleep any more, and Jonas found a short film on his laptop, an old animated one about an enormous gentle rabbit and three bullies, and we watched it on the sofa with Biscuit between us. I laughed. Ten minutes of not being tired.

::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ"}

december: [the first swim](ref:event:first-winter-swim), at Nordnes, in sleet. Forty seconds. I laughed on the steps, the second time in a month.

christmas in Whitby, quiet. Mum did not ask anything. Dad took me down to the shop on Boxing Day and let me sort rope for three hours without speaking, which is the kindest thing he has ever done for me, and he has done a lot.

no cards this year. I never cut the block.
