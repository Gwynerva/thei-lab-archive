---
title: What helps
summary: A list, for the next dark October
---

For me, next time. Read this when the heaviness comes back and every email looks like a cliff. It does come back, now and then, for a day; it has never stayed.

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. One thing at a time. Write the one thing on a card. Do it. Then the next card.
2. Outside before noon, whatever the weather. Down to the water and back.
3. Cold water, if there is any. In to the shoulders, breathe out, count.
4. Hands in something: clay, wood, soil, a cat.
5. No decisions after eight in the evening.
6. Sleep, and do not apologise for it.
7. Tell Jonas. Tell him early. He would rather know.
8. Ring Mum on Sunday even if there is nothing to say. Especially then.
9. Put the phone in the drawer with the money cards, face down.
10. Remember that it lifts. It always has. Write down the day it does.
:::

And a sentence from Nana, which I did not understand until 2023:

> A stitch in time is still a stitch.

I used to think it meant: do it now, before it gets worse. I think now it means: one stitch is still a stitch. One walk, one swim, one card on the table, done, is still something done. You do not have to mend the whole thing today.
