I am writing this in June 2024, back from Santiago, a week before I start working for myself. It is private. Perhaps one day I will make it public, or turn it into something; there is already [an essay](ref:page:on-burnout "the version I can show people") for the people I can show. This is the version that is just for me.

## What happened

In the autumn of 2023 I stopped. Not on purpose. After a year of doing too much, and then a month at work of doing far too much, my body and my head simply declined to go on, the way a car declines to start on a cold morning: no drama, no breakdown at the side of the road, just a click and nothing.

The doctor said *utbrent*. Burnt out. She signed me off for a month, then another, then another. I was off work from the ninth of October to the fourteenth of January.

> You don't have to earn rest.

She said that too. I wrote it on a card and put it on the fridge, and for a long time Jonas read it every morning and I didn't.

## What I want to remember

That it was not weakness, or not only. That a lot of people go through it and most of them do not say so. That the things that helped were small and dull and physical: walking, cold water, clay, sleep, a cat. That Jonas did not try to fix it, which was the most helpful thing anybody did. And that I am, a year later, not the same as before. Not better, not worse. Different, and more careful with myself, and, oddly, happier.

The diary for those months is nearly empty. That is the record. If you are me, reading this in some future October, go and look at [the diary](/diary/) for the autumn of 2023 and see how little is there. Then look at [everything else](/projects/), and see how much came after.
