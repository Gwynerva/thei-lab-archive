---
title: What it is
---

A bike co-operative in a basement on a side street off Leith Walk, down eleven stone steps with a rope for a handrail. Three workstands, a tool wall, a pile of donated frames at the back and a kettle that has seen things.

You can bring a bike in and pay a small fixed price for a job, or pay a few pounds for an hour with the tools and do it yourself with someone watching. Donated bikes are fixed up and sold cheap, mostly to students, sometimes given away to whoever needs one most.

It runs on volunteers, a grant that gets renewed every spring at the last possible minute, and tea.
