My [first Saturday](ref:event:spoke-room-first-saturday) was 8 October 2016, and I spent most of it sweeping. By November I had my own stand by the window.

A Saturday went like this: unlock at ten, kettle on, the queue already on the steps. Brakes and gears until lunch. Lunch on an upturned crate. Then the do-it-yourself hour, which was always three hours, then sweeping, then the tin.

My tally, kept in the back of a notebook because of course I kept one:

- punctures: 61
- brake cables: 34
- gear cables: 22
- wheels trued: 19
- bottom brackets: 7, each one a fight
- bikes rebuilt from the donated pile: 5

My last Saturday was 26 August 2017, two weeks before I started at Forthline. They gave me a spoke key with a ribbon on it. It is in the drawer under the plotter now, still with the ribbon.
