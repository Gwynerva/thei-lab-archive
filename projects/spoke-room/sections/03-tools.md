---
title: The tool wall
---

The best thing in the Spoke Room was the wall. Every tool had its place on a board, with its outline painted behind it in white, so you could see at a glance what was missing and who had walked off with it.

- **Top row: the everyday tools**
  - tyre levers, three sets
  - hex keys from two to ten millimetres
  - the good adjustable spanner, which nobody was allowed to take home
- **Middle row: the fussy ones**
  - cone spanners, thin as biscuits
  - a chain whip and lockring tools
  - the spoke keys, in a jar
- **Bottom row: the heavy ones**
  - a headset press nobody really knew how to use
  - the bottom bracket tools, greasy forever
