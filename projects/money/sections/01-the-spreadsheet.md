---
title: The spreadsheet
summary: Two columns called FOOD and OTHER FOOD, and a ritual
---

I made the first one on the fourth of January 2017, in the flat in Leith, and called it the most grown-up thing I had ever done. It was mostly a column called FOOD and a column called OTHER FOOD. The other columns were rent, bills, a bus pass I never used because I walked everywhere, the Spoke Room's tea fund, and a line called *fieldwork* that was a very hopeful number.

The spreadsheet has been rebuilt four times since, for a salary, for kroner, for freelance, for the house, but the ritual has stayed the same:

1. On the first of the month, with a coffee, at the kitchen table.
2. Put in everything from the month before, from the bank, by hand. No apps. I want to type every number, because typing it is how I see it.
3. Look at the two lines that matter: what came in and what went out.
4. Write one sentence in the notes column about the month. *Fine.* *Tight.* *Christmas.* *The roof.*
5. Close it, and do not open it again until the next first of the month.

The fifth step is the hardest and the most important. For most of 2023 I opened it every day and it did not help. Tove, when I told her, asked what I was hoping it would say. I did not have an answer.
