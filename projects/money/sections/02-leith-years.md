The first salary came on the last Friday of September 2017, from Forthline, and I looked at the bank on my phone on the bus and laughed out loud, which made a woman move seats. It was not a large salary. It was larger than anything I had had before, which was the student loan and Saturdays at the Spoke Room for tea and a little money.

What the Leith years were, in money:

- a third of the rent, then a half when Priya left and Marta and I decided not to replace her
- the cello lessons, every Tuesday, the best money I have ever spent
- trips, carefully: Iceland on a budget with Priya, Japan alone on a bigger one, which I saved for for a year
- the promotion in 2019, which went straight into a savings account I called *one day*, without knowing what one day was
- the lockdown, when there was nothing to spend money on, and *one day* grew

In March 2021 *one day* got a name. It was Bergen. It paid for the flights, the shipping of forty-three boxes, a deposit in kroner, Biscuit's papers, his flight and his crate, and it was very nearly enough.
