title: Glazes
---

Glazing is the part where you find out what you've made, and then the kiln tells you that you were wrong.

### What I used

- **Oatmeal**: speckled, forgiving, the colour of porridge. The lopsided mug.
- **Tenmoku**: dark brown going to rust where it's thin, glossy where it pools. Beautiful on everyone else's pots.
- **Celadon**: pale green, like the sea at Saltwick on a grey day. Mine came out the colour of a hospital wall.
- **A blue the Bergen studio mixed itself**, which they called *fjord*, and which ran down every pot and glued two of mine to the kiln shelf.

### What I learned

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. A test tile is never the same as a pot.
2. Glaze too thick runs; too thin, it disappears.
3. Wax the foot, or the pot becomes part of the shelf.
4. Nothing comes out the colour on the bucket.
:::
