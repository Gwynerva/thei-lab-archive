Out of the kiln tonight: one mug, in a speckled oatmeal glaze, leaning about eight degrees east, with a handle that I pulled myself and that is slightly too small for more than two fingers.

I love it so much it's embarrassing.

It holds one cup of tea exactly. If you fill it to the top it dribbles out of the low side, so you don't fill it to the top. Biscuit has already tried to knock it off the table twice and failed, because it's heavier than it looks: the base is about a centimetre thick, which the teacher said "is one way to do it".

Took it home on the bus wrapped in my scarf. Made tea in it before I'd taken my coat off.
