first class. couldn't centre anything. didn't care, which is new.

the studio is warm and smells of wet earth. nobody asks what you do for a living. you do the clay.

:::private
"something for the hands that isn't a keyboard," tove said. I said I'd try one class. that was in october. still going.

some tuesdays I just wedge clay for two hours and don't throw anything. the teacher lets me.
:::

december. a bowl. small, thick, wobbly rim, dark blue running down the outside like rain on a window. it's on the windowsill. jonas puts his keys in it.

january. back at work half time. kept the tuesdays.

spring: cups, five of them, for coffee, none the same size. The last class was on the 11th of June and I was in Viana do Castelo in the rain, so Jonas went and fetched my last pots off the shelf and sent me a photo of them in a row on the kitchen table, like ducks.
