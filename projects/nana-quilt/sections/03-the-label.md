Every quilt should have a label, Nana said, so that "whoever finds it in a hundred years knows who to blame". So I made one from a scrap of the cream pillowcase, embroidered it in navy thread from her sewing box, and sewed it on the back, bottom right, with the last of her own thread.

:::quote[The label on the back]{align="center"}
Pieced by June Dunn, Robin Hood's Bay, 2019–2020\
Quilted by June Dunn and Mira Testwell\
Robin Hood's Bay, Leith and Bergen\
Finished 21 November 2021
:::

Nobody sees it unless they turn the quilt over. That's right. It's for her, and for whoever finds it in a hundred years.
