---
title: The quilt she left
summary: Log cabin, twelve rows, sixty years of scraps
---

A log cabin quilt is built block by block from a small square in the middle, the hearth, with strips laid round it like logs round a fire, light on one side and dark on the other. Nana's has a red square at the centre of every block, which is traditional, and which she said was "so the house is always warm".

What the strips are, as far as Mum and I can tell:

- the pale blue: a summer dress of Mum's, about 1975
- the pink with white flowers: Clare's christening dress, cut down years ago
- the cream: pillowcases from the cottage, the good ones
- the dark blue stripe: one of Grandad Arthur's shirts, from his ship
- the red centres: nobody knows; Nana wouldn't say

There are 144 blocks in twelve rows of twelve, and every one of them is a little bit crooked, because she was piecing it at nearly ninety with her eyes going, and I love every crooked one.
