January, Leith. Started where she stopped. Her needle was still in it, threaded, parked in the fourth row. I quilted on with the same thread until it ran out.

How it goes: a short needle, a single thread with a knot you pop through the top so it hides in the wadding, and small running stitches through all three layers, rocking the needle up and down with the thimble behind it. Five or six stitches on the needle at once, then pull. Nana did twelve to the inch. I do eight, on a good night.

March: row six. The thimble has made a dent in my finger.

August, Bergen: the quilt came over in the shipping boxes, and I was so glad to see it that I sat on the floor of the half-empty flat with it in my lap for an hour.

October: rain, day 19. Row eleven. The light goes at five now and I quilt under the lamp.

November: the last row.
