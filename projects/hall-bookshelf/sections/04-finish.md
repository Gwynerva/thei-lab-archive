---
title: Oil and the hall
periods:
  - 2018-07-21
---

Three coats of Danish oil over a week, rubbed in with an old T-shirt and left to dry with the windows open, so the whole stair smelled of linseed for days. Today it went up against the wall between the radiator and the switch, and the books went in.

It stands. It isn't square, but it stands.

1. Callum's cookbooks on the bottom shelf, the heaviest, where they've always been, on the floor, except now the floor is oak.
2. Maps and atlases in the middle.
3. Priya's journals at the top, where she can't reach them without the step.

Three good things: the smell of the oil, the clunk the shelf made when it touched the wall, and Callum saying, after a long look, "That's a proper thing, that."
