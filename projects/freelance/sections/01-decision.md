---
title: The decision
summary: A spring of half days, an apple tree, and a notice handed in on a Friday.
---

The decision didn't come in one moment, though I can point to a few.

In February 2024 I was back at Nordvåg four hours a day, and I noticed I was happiest in the hour after I left: walking home along the harbour, thinking about maps I wanted to make for nobody. Later that month I drew a little map of Sandviken for Isla, who had just been born, and spent a whole Sunday on it without once looking at the clock. In early March, on a weekend at the farm in Hardanger, I helped Sigrid prune and told her I was thinking of leaving my job with nothing to go to.

She went on cutting for a while. Then:

> An apple tree doesn't fruit every year either, and nobody calls it lazy.

I handed in my notice on Friday 12 April. Then I walked the Camino from Porto to Santiago in June, which gave me fifteen days of putting one foot in front of the other and not deciding anything at all, and I came home and registered the company.
