Written in October 2026, at the kitchen table at Øvre Lid, which is a real kitchen table now, in a real kitchen.

What the work is this autumn:

- the atlas, at the printer since September, the sheets due any day
- the QGIS autumn term, Monday evenings, fourteen students this time
- two map clients, both patient, both from the island tourist office's recommendation
- prints for the Christmas market, drawn in the evenings by the stove

How a day goes, when it goes well: Biscuit at six, coffee at the window, work from eight to two with the fjord changing colour behind the screen, a walk or a swim, the stove, the course on Mondays. On Fridays I do the accounts and nothing else, because I learned that a Friday afternoon with numbers is the price of every other day being free.

:::spoiler
I earn less than I did at Nordvåg. I have never been so sure of anything as I am that it was the right trade.
:::

Gratitude, written down because Nana would have: for the work, for the people who send it, for Jonas, who never once asked when I'd get a proper job again.
