The real numbers, for me, so I stop guessing.

- **July to December 2024:** about 212,000 kroner invoiced, before tax. Nordvåg paid me roughly that in four months.
- **2025:** about 540,000, a third of it the atlas advance and the course. Booked solid January to April, almost nothing in August, which I'd planned for and still panicked about.
- **2026 so far:** steadier. The course is full every term. The atlas royalties won't start until next year.

What frightens me: one bad winter, a broken arm, the course losing its students to a cheaper one. What helps: a buffer of three months' costs in a separate account that I don't look at, and Jonas's salary from the workshop, which we agreed would pay for the house and not for me.

The house is rent-to-own from Ragnhild, which makes the numbers kinder than they'd otherwise be. The rest of it lives in the spreadsheet, where it belongs.
