---
title: Why walk it
summary: Because of the autumn, and because of Nana's boots.
---

I walked it because the year before had been the worst of my life, and I had come out of the other side of it with a notice handed in, a last day at work, and no idea what to do with a body that had spent seven months being careful.

:::private
In October 2023 I stopped. I could not go to work, and then I could not do much else. Three months of sick leave, a therapist on Tuesdays, a lot of lying on the floor of the flat with Biscuit on my chest. I went back half time in January and handed in my notice in April. By June I was better, not well. I wanted something long and simple: walk, eat, sleep, walk. Something with an end I could see on a map.
:::

I chose the Camino because Ines lives in Porto and said she would walk the first days with me, and because she said it in the voice she used at Forthline when a deadline was impossible and she had decided we would meet it anyway.

I chose the coast because I wanted the sea on my left.

And I chose it because of Nana, who never went further than Scarborough on foot in her life and who always said that a long walk was the best way to sort out a short temper. I did not have a short temper. I had something worse. A long walk was still the best idea I had.
