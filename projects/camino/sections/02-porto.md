Ines met me at the airport with a sign that said PEREGRINA in her best capitals, which is how she does everything. Then dinner by the river, and a bed in her spare room under a picture of a saint I did not recognise.

On the eighth, the first stamp: a small office off the cathedral cloister and a man with a rubber stamp and an ink pad who did it carefully, square in its box, and wished me *bom caminho*. My *credencial* has thirty-two boxes. I meant to fill them, and did.

The rest of the day we did nothing a pilgrim should. The Ribeira in the sun. *Pastéis de nata*, eaten standing up at a counter. The iron bridge on foot, the top deck and then the bottom one and then the top again. The blue tiles on the side of the Carmo church, which I could have looked at all day.
