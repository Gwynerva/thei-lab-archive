---
title: Eleven spoons
summary: One a fortnight, more or less, through the first dark winter
---

Eleven spoons between October 2021 and February 2022, carved at the kitchen table on newspaper in the evenings, with the radio on and the rain on the window.

:::list{counter="upper-alpha"}
1. The course spoon. Lumpy. Mine forever.
2. A coffee scoop, short and deep, which lives in the coffee.
3. A butter knife, which is technically not a spoon.
4. A long-handled stirring spoon for Anna, for Christmas.
5. A spoon for Sverre downstairs, who inspected it like a ship's mate and said, "Ja."
6. A failure, split along a knot.
7. Another failure, split along the same knot, because I didn't learn.
8. A cooking spoon for Mum, posted to Whitby in a padded envelope.
9. A tiny salt spoon, the best one, for Priya.
10. A ladle that was too ambitious and is now a very deep spoon.
11. A spoon for Jonas, in February, from a branch of birch he brought from the workshop, which I'm not going to say any more about.
:::
