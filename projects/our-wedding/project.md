We were married on the twenty-second of August 2026, in the orchard below the house, at eleven in the morning, under the old Gravenstein by the road that Ragnhild's father planted. A woman from the council did it, in Norwegian and then again in English for Dad, who had asked her to. There were forty-one of us on borrowed chairs on the slope, three strings from the orchestra, forty bottles of our own cider, and Ragnhild at the top of the orchard in the shade with a blanket over her knees.

This page is for the people who were there, and for us. It is not public, and it will not be.

## How it came about

:::private
He asked on Christmas Eve, at the bottom of the orchard, in the snow, at minus six, with the star I am named after low in the south over the fjord; he had checked. The ring was twisted from the copper wire we stitched *Nana June* together with, and he got as far as *vil du* before I said yes.
:::

We decided on the orchard the same night, by the stove, and on August, because that is when the Gravensteins are nearly ripe and the evenings are still long. Everything after that was lists.

## What we said

We wrote our own promises and kept them short, one line each, his in Norwegian and mine in English. Sigrid said the last words before the officiant's, and they were not on any list:

:::quote[Sigrid, standing up from her chair, before anyone could stop her]{align="center"}
Look after each other the way you look after the trees. Slowly, and every year.
:::
