title: Invitations
summary: Twenty-four invitations, hand-set and printed, with a map on the back.
periods:
  - 2026-03-07..2026-04-18
---

I wanted to make them myself, of course, and I wanted them printed, not drawn. A letterpress studio in Bergen let me have their small press for three Saturdays, and a woman there who did not want to be named in anyone's anything taught me to set type.

The words took longer than the printing. In the end, in grey ink on thick cream card:

Mira Testwell and Jonas Brekke\
are getting married in the orchard at Øvre Lid\
on Saturday the twenty-second of August 2026\
at eleven o'clock\
Come as you are. Bring a coat.

On the back of each card I drew the map: the ferry, the road from Utne, the turn by the white post, the track, the barn, an apple tree with an X under it. Twenty-four maps, all a little different, because I drew them all by hand, and by the twentieth the apple tree had become a sort of joke between me and myself.

They went in the post on the eighteenth of April, the English ones first because of the post. Clare rang the day hers arrived to say she had cried at the map, not the words, which is the right way round.
