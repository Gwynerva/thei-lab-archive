title: Guests
private: true
---

Forty-one, in the end, which was ten more than we meant and twenty fewer than Sigrid wanted.

Who we could not leave out: our families, all of them, both sides, including the cousins Jonas had not seen since he was twelve. Priya. Anna and her husband. Eirik and Kari, and their dog, who was not invited and came. The three from the orchestra, who were guests as well as players. The neighbours along the road. Ragnhild, for three hours, which was all she would allow.

Who we left out and minded: people from Nordvåg. I did not want the day to have anything of that year in it, and Anna understood, which is one of the reasons she is Anna.

Who said no: Mrs Ogilvie, from Edinburgh, who wrote a letter in fountain pen to say that at her age she did not fly, that she was very glad about the cello, and that I was to play the prelude at my own wedding. I did not. I will write and tell her that I meant to.

Seating took four evenings and one argument, about Jonas's uncles. The uncles were fine.
