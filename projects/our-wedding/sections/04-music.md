---
title: Music
summary: Three players from Sandviken Strykeorkester in the long grass, and what they played.
---

I did not want to play at my own wedding. I wanted to listen. So three of my friends from Sandviken Strykeorkester, two violins and a viola, came over on the ferry with their cases in the back of a car and played in the orchard, sitting on kitchen chairs on a rug on the grass, with clothes pegs on their music against the wind.

What they played:

1. Elgar's serenade for strings, the slow movement, arranged by the viola player for three, while Dad walked me down through the trees
2. A wedding tune from Hardanger, very quietly, during the signing
3. In the evening, at the tables, waltzes and reels, until the viola player said her fingers had stopped, and then two more

::audio[The serenade, as a whole orchestra plays it; ours was three people in a field, and better]{file="music-strings-1" title="Serenade for Strings"}

The viola player sits behind me in the orchestra. Before they started she handed me her bow and told me to tighten it for luck, and I did, and she played the whole morning without once looking at the music. I owe each of the three a bottle of cider every year for the rest of our lives, which they have already pointed out.
