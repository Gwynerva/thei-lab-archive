title: Five boards
periods:
  - 2018-11-17..2018-12-15
---

Rough-cut the five blanks on the back green on a cold Saturday, then took everything inside, because November.

1. **Mum**: long, with a handle, for the jam kitchen.
2. **Dad**: short and thick, to go by the cooker. (It went to the shop.)
3. **Clare and Dev**: their first married Christmas, so their initials burnt into the corner with a soldering iron, badly.
4. **Nana June**: small enough for one hand, with a hole to hang it by the back door.
5. **Callum**: end grain, for the café. Thirty-six little blocks of oak glued in a chequerboard and flattened with the plane until my arm went numb.

Planing on the landing in the evenings, with the door open for the light. The woman from the ground floor came up to complain about the noise and stayed to have a go. She was better at it than me.

Oiled them all on the 15th, five coats, and stood them on the bookshelf to dry like a row of very thick books. Marta said they looked like a xylophone and hit one with a spoon to check. It was an F.
