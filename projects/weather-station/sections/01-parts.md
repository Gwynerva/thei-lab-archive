The shopping list, as it stood on the fridge in April 2020, ticked off as the parcels came:

- [x] a Raspberry Pi, the spare one from the drawer
- [x] one small board for temperature, humidity and pressure
- [x] a tipping-bucket rain gauge, second-hand, from a man in Fife who'd upgraded
- [x] anemometer cups and a wind vane on one arm
- [x] a radiation shield
  - six white plant saucers, stacked with spacers, a classic
  - one long bolt through the middle
- [x] a waterproof box for the Pi, plus far too many cable glands
- [ ] a proper mast
  - never bought; a broom handle cable-tied to the railing did the job for two years

Total cost about £95, most of it the rain gauge. The most expensive single part was, as Priya pointed out on a video call, my time, which was free in 2020 because nobody else wanted it.
