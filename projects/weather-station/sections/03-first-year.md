A year in numbers, read off the balcony:

1. warmest reading: 27.4 degrees, a still afternoon in August
2. coldest: minus 4.1, the night of 10 February 2021, with the tomatoes long gone
3. wettest day: 31 millimetres, in October, most of it in three hours
4. windiest gust: 71 km/h, from the west, the night Biscuit refused to come out from under the bed

That last night I recorded the wind from the balcony door, on my phone, because the numbers didn't feel like enough.

In December the readings went on without me. I looked at them again in the spring and found that I could tell which hours I'd been on the phone to Mum by the radiator going on and off under the kitchen window, two metres from the shield.
