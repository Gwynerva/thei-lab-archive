I took it down in July 2021, a week before leaving Leith. The broom handle went in the communal bin; the cups, the shield and the Pi went into a shortbread tin, wrapped in tea towels, and the tin went into the hand luggage, because I didn't trust anyone else with it.

The rain gauge got a full security search at Edinburgh airport. The woman at the scanner tipped the bucket, heard it click, and asked what it was for. "Counting rain." She looked at the screen and then at me and said, "You're going to Bergen?" I said yes. She laughed for quite a long time.

It went up again in September on the kitchen window ledge in Sandviken, bolted to a plank wedged in the frame. New words that month: *regn* (rain), *yr* (drizzle, and the name of the forecast everyone uses), *styrtregn* (pouring), *skodde* (sea fog, which came up the fjord most mornings and swallowed the readings).
