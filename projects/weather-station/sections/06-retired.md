Took the station off the window today, a week after the twenty-one days of rain finally stopped.

The board inside the shield had gone green at every joint. The rain bucket was full of moss, actual moss, growing. The cups still turned, though nobody had oiled them in a year. I washed it all in the sink and dried it on the radiator and put it in the shortbread tin, where it still is.

Last reading: 9.8 degrees, 93 per cent humidity, 1003 hectopascals, falling. Raining, of course.

By then I'd stopped needing it. I had [a counter for the rain](ref:project:rain-counter) instead, which only did one thing and did it loudly, and I had Mr Haldorsen at the bottom of the stairs, who was better than both.
