**Day 55.** Soldered the sensor board on the kitchen table, which now has a small burn shaped like Fife. Biscuit supervised from the chair.

**Day 62.** The shield. Drilled six plant saucers and stacked them on a bolt. It looks like a wedding cake for a very small, very sad wedding. Works, though.

**Day 70.** First readings from the balcony. Temperature 31 degrees, which was wrong: the sun was on the box. Moved everything to the other end of the railing.

**Day 77.** The rain gauge, finally. It hasn't rained since it arrived.

**Day 84.** Everything talking to everything. Readings every ten minutes into the database, a chart every morning. I sat on the balcony with tea and watched the pressure fall a tenth of a hectopascal at a time, which is a perfectly normal thing to do on a Sunday.\
It rained at four. Fourteen clicks. I counted.
