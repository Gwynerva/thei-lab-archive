The first term taught entirely from the farmhouse. The stove was lit on 18 October, the second Saturday of term; the kitchen came out on 3 November, the fifth Monday, so for the second half I taught from the bedroom with a plank for a desk and a sheet pinned up behind me to hide the plaster.

Two things I changed this term:

1. Smaller groups for the critique evening, three or four at a time, so the quiet ones talk.
2. Elevation moved earlier, because it is the evening that makes people fall in love with the thing.

One of the students was a fruit farmer from across the fjord, mapping frost pockets. In May she sent me a photograph of her blossom, and a note: *the low corner, just where your map said.* I printed it and pinned it by the window.
