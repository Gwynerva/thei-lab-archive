The second cohort, taught around the move. I packed boxes in the day and taught on Monday evenings with the flat getting emptier behind me on camera; by March the students could see bare walls and one lamp, and by week seven a stack of boxes labelled *KITCHEN, FRAGILE, PROBABLY*.

We moved to Øvre Lid on 29 March. The last session was on the 31st, from the farmhouse, from a box in a room with no kitchen, on a phone hotspot that worked only when I sat by the window that faces the ferry. Eleven students showed eleven maps. One of them asked what the noise was behind me. It was the fjord. I turned the laptop round to show them, and nobody said anything for a while.
