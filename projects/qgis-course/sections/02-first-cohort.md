Twelve students from five countries, Monday evenings from the flat in Sandviken, with Biscuit walking across the keyboard in week one and becoming, by week three, the course mascot.

What they mapped, in the end:

- **Water**
  - flood risk to a sports ground in Yorkshire, which felt like coming home
  - the ponds of a nature reserve in Denmark, counted by size
- **People**
  - every bus stop in a Scottish town and how far it is from a bench
  - the old parish boundaries of a valley in Cumbria
- **Land**
  - frost pockets in an orchard on the Hardangerfjord
  - every bench in one man's town, still for no reason he would admit to

There were two Mondays off, one for the autumn holiday and one when my voice had gone. On the last evening, 9 December, everyone held their printed map up to the camera at once, and I took a screenshot of twelve small rectangles holding twelve smaller ones, and it is still the background on my laptop.
