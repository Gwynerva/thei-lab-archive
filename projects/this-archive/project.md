On the first of October 2016, in the little room with the skylight in Leith, I decided to keep a diary *properly, in one place, for once*. I had started about nine before. This one I kept.

It was paper first: six notebooks, from October 2016 to the end of 2019, black, with elastic bands, filled in pencil and then in fountain pen. From 2020, in the lockdown, when I was writing every day, it went into files on the laptop. In 2021, in the next lockdown, I typed the notebooks in, all seven hundred and twenty days of them, and since then everything has been in one place, the place you are reading it now.

## What is here

- [the diary](/diary/), about two thousand days of it, from a sentence to a few pages each
- [projects](/projects/), the things I made and did that were bigger than a day
- the events, the days that stood out
- pages, for the things that are not about a time at all
- and [tags](/tags/), which hold it all together, mostly

If you are new here, start with [how to read this site](<ref:page:about#h:How to read this site> "the short version, on the about page").

## What I do and don't do

I write most days, never all of them. I write the weather, always. I draw a little map in the margin when something happens somewhere, which on this site means a picture. I keep lists: three good things at the end of a day, rain counted in Bergen, new words, cello minutes. I do not go back and tidy. Spelling stays; exclamation marks stay. The hard years are as they were.

Some of it is private, which means only I can read it. Some of it is for whoever has the link. Most of it is for anyone, which still surprises me every time I think about it, and which I decided in 2021 because I was tired of being someone who kept things in a drawer.

:::private
The drawer in question still exists. It has the money cards in it, face down, and the letter I wrote to Nana on the night of the funeral and never posted, because there was nowhere to post it to.
:::
