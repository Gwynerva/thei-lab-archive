Six notebooks, all black, all with an elastic band, all bought from the same stationer in Stockbridge until it closed:

1. **October 2016 to June 2017.** A coffee ring on the cover. Pencil. Exclamation marks on every page. The MSc, the Spoke Room, Copenhagen, the cliffs.
2. **June to December 2017.** The dissertation summer, Forthline, graduation, the first craft fair. The first entry in fountain pen, which I was very pleased with and smudged at once.
3. **January to August 2018.** The cello, Iceland, the bookshelf. A pressed harebell from Glencoe in July.
4. **August 2018 to April 2019.** Clare's wedding, Callum leaving, Nana's fall, Biscuit. Several pages chewed at the corner.
5. **April to October 2019.** Japan, the Cleveland Way, the half marathon. A cherry petal from Maruyama Park, now brown.
6. **October to December 2019.** Short, because I had a new idea about typing. The last Hogmanay in Edinburgh, written on the first of January in a hand I would not call steady.

::media[A page from the fourth notebook: a box I drew in the margin and never built]{file="notebook-paper" layout="centered"}

They live on the shelf above my desk at Øvre Lid, in order. Biscuit sleeps on them sometimes, which seems right; he is in three of them.
