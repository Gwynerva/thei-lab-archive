The first of October 2026. [Ten years to the day](ref:event:ten-years "the day the whole thing is counted from") since the first page.

I began it in the fjord: down through the orchard at seven, into the water off the rock, twelve degrees, the first swim of the winter. Then a coffee, and the reminder I had left myself on the very first entry, ten years ago, to read it again on this day. So I did. A girl of twenty-four, seventy-two steps up, a sofa stuck on the landing, pasta on the floor at eleven at night, and the weather: dry, blue, a bit of wind off the sea. A good omen, she wrote. She was right.

Then I read the edges of the ten years, not all of it, nobody could: the first day of everything. The first lesson. The first Munro. The first day in Bergen. The first window out at Øvre Lid. The first swim, forty seconds, in sleet.

In the evening, a card from Mum, posted in Whitby a week before, and Biscuit asleep on the first notebook, and Jonas reading over my shoulder and saying nothing about it.

What next: the same. One line is still a line.
