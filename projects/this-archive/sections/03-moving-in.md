In the second lockdown, in the spring of 2021, with Norwegian on Mondays and letters at weekends and nothing at all to do in the evenings, I started typing the notebooks in. One a month, more or less. Notebook four was done by the beginning of May; the last of them, the sixth, in the last week of December, in Bergen, with the rain going and Biscuit on the keyboard.

The rules for typing:

- every word as written, spelling and all, exclamation marks and all
- the little maps in the margins became pictures, where I had one that fitted
- nothing added, except, here and there, a note of what I knew later and did not know then
- nothing taken out, though some of it I made private

It was strange reading myself at twenty-four. I was louder than I remembered, and more frightened, and funnier, and I had completely forgotten about a boy from the Fringe whose name I still cannot remember. I found the day I decided on the dissertation, the day I bought the cello strings, the day I first wrote down *Bergen* (a Tuesday in 2019, about a chart I liked). It was like reading letters from a younger sister I was fond of and slightly embarrassed by.

By the end of the year everything was in one place: the paper years, the lockdown files, the first months in Norway. Seven hundred and twenty days typed in, and from then on I have written straight into it.
