---
title: What I ate
summary: A list of the meals of two weeks, by someone who had never had a proper breakfast before.
---

### Breakfast

At the inns on the Kumano Kodo: rice, miso soup, grilled fish, pickles, an egg, green tea, seven small dishes on a tray, at seven o'clock. I have never been so well fed so early. I came home and tried to make it in Leith and managed the rice.

### Noodles

Soba at a counter in Kyoto, alone, facing the wall. Cold soba on the Kumano Kodo, in a village shop, out of a bamboo box. Udon at a station in the rain, standing up, in four minutes, which was exactly the time between two trains. Ramen on the last night in Tokyo, pork broth and a soft egg, from a machine.

### The convenience shop

The rice triangles wrapped in seaweed that you unwrap in a clever three-step way I never learned. Egg sandwiches, soft as clouds. Hot tea in a bottle out of a machine in the middle of nowhere.

### Things I could not identify

- something sweet and green on a stick, in Nara
- something on a skewer under the railway bridge in Tokyo, which the man in the grey suit would not tell me the name of, and which was delicious
- a pickle at Takahara the colour of a sunset

### What I miss

The breakfasts. The breakfasts. The breakfasts.
