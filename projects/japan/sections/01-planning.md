I planned this one like a work project, because I was frightened of it.

- [x] Flights, booked in January
- [x] A rail pass, which was cheaper than I thought and more useful than I could have imagined
- [x] All the accomodation, booked in one evening in February, which is not like me
- [x] The luggage-sending service on the Kumano Kodo, so I could walk with a small pack
- [x] Phrases, written in a notebook in English, in Japanese, and in my own rough way of saying them
- [ ] Learning to read anything at all (I learned to read *exit* and *noodles*, which turned out to be enough)
- [x] Somebody for Biscuit: Marta, who said yes before I had finished asking

The route went on my wall in Leith as a strip map, the way old road books show a route: one long line from Kyoto to Tokyo with the Kii peninsula bent into it like a hook.

My mum asked whether I was sure. I said no, and went.
