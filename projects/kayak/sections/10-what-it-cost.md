Written in 2024, because I'd been avoiding adding it up.

- plans: 1 100 kr
- okoume, four sheets: 6 400 kr
- epoxy, all of it: 5 900 kr
- glass cloth: 2 300 kr
- copper wire: 450 kr
- varnish, brushes and sandpaper (so much sandpaper): 2 700 kr
- hatches, seat, footrests, deck lines: 3 800 kr
- the workshop: nothing, which is not nothing

About 22 650 kr. More than a second-hand plastic kayak. Less than a new one. And sixteen months of evenings, which I'd spend again.

I never told Mum the total. She'd have said, "You could have bought a nice one." I could have. That wasn't the point.
