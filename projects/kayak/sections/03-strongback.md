Saturday in the workshop. Built the :hint[strongback]{note="a long, dead-straight, rigid beam on legs that the boat is built on, so the hull can't twist while it's going together"}: two lengths of spruce made into a box beam six metres long, on four trestles, levelled with a laser and a great many shims. If it isn't straight, the boat won't be straight. If the boat isn't straight, you paddle in circles forever.

Jonas: "Spend a week on the strongback and you save a month on the boat."

I spent three. On it go the forms, plywood cross-sections of the hull every half metre, taken straight off [the plans](<ref:project:kayak#h:Plans> "what I built it from"), and the panels will be stitched round them. Cutting the forms was the first time I'd used a band saw. Jonas stood very close the whole time and pretended he wasn't.

Rain: day 8. The workshop has a woodstove, a radio, and a kettle older than me. I could live here.

Then, on the 12th, waffles, after a Saturday in the workshop, which was not about the strongback, and that's all I'm saying. More on [the first day of it](ref:event:strongback "5 February").
