The deck went on in two halves: two more panels each side, curved over the deck beams and stitched and glued like the hull, with a hole for the cockpit and two for the hatches.

December: deck panels cut and fitted, the Christmas card printed on the bench beside them, then Whitby for Christmas with Jonas, who met the family and whom the family met. Dad took him round the shop and showed him every rope and every repair in the place, and at the end said, "You'll do." From Dad that's a speech.

January: back in the workshop. Glassed the deck. Fitted the coaming round the cockpit, laminated from thin strips of ash and bent round a form, the prettiest part of the whole boat.

On the 28th we glued the deck down for good, and suddenly it was a closed thing, a boat, not a shape. Jonas put his hand flat on the foredeck and left it there for a while and didn't say anything, which from Jonas is also a speech.
