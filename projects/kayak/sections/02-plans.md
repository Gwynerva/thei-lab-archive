The plans came as a roll of full-size drawings and a booklet, and I spread them across the floor of the flat, and Biscuit lay on the bow.

The wood is okoume, a :hint[marine plywood]{note="plywood made with waterproof glue and no gaps inside the plies, so it doesn't rot or come apart when it gets wet; okoume is a light African hardwood"} from West Africa, light and pinkish brown, 4 mm thick, five plies. Four sheets, two and a half metres each, scarfed end to end to make panels long enough for the hull. Jonas showed me how to cut a scarf: a long shallow slope on each end, so the glue line is twelve times the thickness of the wood. Mine were the worst scarfs he'd seen "this year", he said, which was encouraging, as it was only February.
