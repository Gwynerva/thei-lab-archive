coat one, 11 february. thin. the wood goes dark and gold and suddenly you can see every mistake.

coat two. sanded between. dust everywhere.

work is all about the release already. evenings in the workshop are the only quiet hours.

then one a fortnight, more or less:

3. 4 march: a run on the starboard side. sanded it out.
4. 18 march: good.
5. 1 april: good. jonas says "nearly".
6. 15 april: tired. skipped the sanding on the stern. regretted it.
7. 22 april: the last. done. [the seventh coat](ref:diary:2023-04-22 "the evening it was finished").

:::spoiler
the name goes on the bow in gold letters on the day: Nana June. nobody knows except jonas. not even mum.
:::

stood in the workshop tonight with the lights off and the varnish catching the light from the street. she looks like a violin.
