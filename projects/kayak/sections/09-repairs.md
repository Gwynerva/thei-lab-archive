[herdla](ref:event:herdla-rock), 26 august. a rock under the surface at low water, just off the beach. heard it before I felt it. a long scrape along the keel, and a crack in the glass you could put a fingernail in.

got her to the beach. sat on the stones for a while.

:::private
it wasn't the rock. or it was, but it was everything else as well. the release, the hours, waking at four. I sat on the beach at herdla and couldn't stop shaking, over a scratch on a boat. jonas sat with me and didn't say anything for an hour. that was right.
:::

three weekends in the workshop:

- sand back to the wood round the crack
- fill with epoxy and a little wood flour
- a patch of glass cloth, feathered in
- varnish, three coats this time, not seven

it doesn't show unless you know. I know.
