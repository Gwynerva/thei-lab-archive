summary: Four days on the cliffs with a drone, and the afternoon the cliff came down.
periods:
  - 2017-04-10..2017-04-13

---

**Monday.** Wind far too strong to fly. Sam and I laid out ground control points along the cliff top instead: thirty-eight orange squares pegged into the grass, each one measured to the centimetre. A dog walker asked whether we were marking out a crop circle.

**Tuesday.** Flew! Four batteries, six flights, about nine hundred photographs. The drone dipped once over the edge in a gust and my heart stopped. Sam was completely calm.

**Wednesday.** More flights in the morning. In the afternoon, while I was writing up numbers on the cliff top, a piece of the cliff about the size of a bus came away to the south of the Nab and slid onto the beach with a noise like a long sigh. Nobody was underneath. I didn't take a single photo; I just stood there.

::card[The fall, written up the same evening]{to="ref:event:saltwick-cliff-fall#:~:text=fall"}

**Thursday.** Re-measured every control point near the fall. Packed up. Fish and chips on the pier with Sam, who said it was the best fieldwork he'd ever had, "because something actually happened".
