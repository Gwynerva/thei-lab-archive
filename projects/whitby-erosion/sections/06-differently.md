---
title: What I'd do differently
---

A list I wrote the week after handing it in, while it still stung, and added to later:

1. Fly the drone in April *and* in October, so I could see a winter's loss directly instead of guessing it from photographs two years apart.
2. Put the control points further back from the edge. Four of the thirty-eight are on the beach now.
3. ~~Read everything first, then start.~~ Start measuring in week one and read alongside. I lost a month to reading.

And the ones I only understood once I'd started work:

4. Write the code so that someone else can run it. Nobody could, including me, by Christmas.
5. Ask the people who live on the cliff. The caravan park had thirty years of photographs of its own edge in a filing cabinet, and I found out a week after submitting.
6. Take a photo of the thing you came to see happen, even when you are too stunned to move.
