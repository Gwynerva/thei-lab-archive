---
title: The question
summary: A weekend on the cliffs with Dad, and a fence post hanging on its wire.
---

It started on [a weekend at home](ref:event:whitby-cliffs-weekend), 18 to 20 November 2016. I'd come down from Edinburgh to get away from coursework and Dad wanted company on a walk. We went along the West Cliff, over the bridge, up the steps and out past the abbey to Saltwick, the way we used to on Sundays when I was small.

At the fence post he stopped. It was hanging over the drop on its wire, the post itself in the air.

> That was in the field when I was a lad. Well. Near enough.

On the train back I wrote in the margin of my notebook: *how fast? since when? is it faster now?* Three questions, which is too many for a dissertation, Dr Grieve said, and then let me keep all three as long as I answered the first one properly.

The proposal went in at Christmas and was accepted on 20 January. The fence post is gone now, but I have a photo of it on the cliff, and one of Dad pointing at it, slightly out of focus because he wouldn't stand still.
