After two nights of cloud at Reine we drove east along the islands all day, on the forecast's word, to a guesthouse above the beach at Uttakleiv. What the third night was, by the clock, as I wrote it down at two in the morning so I would not lose it:

- **Six.** Dusk going on for ever, blue on the snow. Cloud moving off to the east. Soup.
- **Half past nine.** Priya at the guesthouse window: a pale stripe over the mountains across the bay, like a cloud lit from inside.
- **Ten.** Every layer we owned, down to the beach. The stripe was green.
- **Quarter past ten.** Moving.
- **Eleven.** All over the sky, folding and pouring, pink at the edges, so fast overhead that I ducked.
- **Midnight.** Fading to a soft green haze. I told Priya about my name.

We lay in the snow on the beach until we could not feel our backs, and then on the bonnet of the hire car, and said "oh" about forty times each.
