The last days, at the other end of the islands, in [Henningsvær](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henningsv%C3%A6r), a fishing village spread over a handful of little islands joined by bridges.

- **The ninth.** On from Uttakleiv to Henningsvær, in sun, stopping at every bridge.
- **The tenth.** All of the village on foot. The football pitch on a rock at the far end, with the sea on three sides, cleared of snow by somebody. Stockfish on the racks. Priya's lecture on cod, all of it. Fish soup, of course.
- **The eleventh.** Nothing at all, on purpose: the harbour, a bakery, a long sleep, the light going pink on the snow.
- **The twelfth.** The frozen lake above Svolvær first thing, Priya on a borrowed pair of skis, going backwards. Then the little plane from Svolvær, Bodø, Bergen, and rain, which felt almost friendly.

::media[Birches by the frozen lake above Svolvær]{file="lofoten-winter-3" layout="natural"}

On the plane home Priya said, "So. The boat man." I said there was nothing to tell. She said, "Yet." She was right about that too.
