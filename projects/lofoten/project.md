In March 2022 Priya came over from Plymouth and we went north, to Lofoten, in winter, because she had always wanted to see the northern lights and I had been in Norway for seven months and had seen nothing of it but Bergen and rain.

Lofoten in March is a line of black-and-white mountains standing straight up out of the sea, snow down to the waterline, red fishermen's cabins on stilts on the rocks, and a light that changes every ten minutes. It is cold in a dry, clean way that Bergen never is. The wind on the ridges lifts :hint[long white plumes off the tops]{note="spindrift: powder snow blown off the summits and ridges, streaming like smoke"} that look, from the road, as if the mountains were smoking.

## The days

1. [Getting there](ref:section:lofoten/getting-there): a flight to Bodø and a ferry in a swell.
2. [Reine](ref:section:lofoten/reine): snow, red cabins, and two nights of cloud.
3. [The lights](ref:section:lofoten/the-lights): the third night.
4. [Henningsvær](ref:section:lofoten/henningsvaer): stockfish, a football pitch on a rock, and home.

## Priya

Priya is the person I have travelled best with in my life. We drove round Iceland together in 2018 and found that we could be quiet in a car for three hours and then sing for three more, and Lofoten was the same. She stood on the cold step at Reine in her socks with her coffee and said nothing for a whole minute, which I had never once seen her do. She explained the aurora to me twice, lying in the snow, and I understood it both times, and it did not help at all.

## Someone else

I had been on exactly one date with Jonas, three weeks before, waffles in a café, and I sent him a photograph from the top of the ferry with the mountains coming up out of the sea. He sent back one word, *misunnelig*, envious, and I looked at it far more often than one word deserves. Priya noticed. Priya notices everything.

[The sky moved](ref:event:northern-lights#:~:text=moves "that is the thing nobody tells you") is the night of the lights, in the events; the story of my name is in it too.
