---
title: Repertoire
summary: What I have played since 2018, more or less badly
---

Everything I have played in an orchestra or in front of anyone since the cello came down from the loft, by where I played it. Not a boast: a list. Some of these I could barely play.

:::list{counter="upper-roman"}
1. Leith, 2018–2021
   - Elgar, the Serenade for Strings, first movement (my first rehearsal, and my last)
   - Dvořák, a serenade, a whole winter of it
   - the slow movement of the New World, which everyone's grandmother cried at
   - Handel, every December
2. Alone, 2019–2020
   - Bach, the prelude of the first suite
   - Bach, the allemande, half learned
3. Sandviken, 2022 until now
   - Grieg, the Holberg Suite, in Grieg's own town
   - Elgar, the Serenade again, Advent 2024, and this time I got through the second page
   - Mozart, the little night music, which is harder than it sounds
   - Warlock, the Capriol Suite, for the joy of it
4. The recital, 2026
   - Bach, the prelude and the two minuets of the first suite
:::

And one I play for nobody, on the last evening of every year, because Nana liked it: *The Swan*, very slowly, with all the vibrato Mrs Ogilvie ever let me have.
