title: Commissions
---

About half the maps now are made to order. How it works:

- [x] you write and tell me the place, and why it matters
- [x] we talk, by email or on a call, and I ask a lot of questions about doors
- [x] I walk it if I can, or work from your photographs and an old map if I can't
- [x] a pencil draft, which you can change as much as you like
- [x] ink, colour and names, which you can change very little
- [ ] waiting, which is the part people find hardest: four to six weeks

Things I've drawn so far: two weddings, a grandmother's island, a school's sixtieth, a farm for an eightieth birthday, a cabin and its lake, the route of a first date, a dog's daily walk (with the dog in seven places), and a street in Whitby for Mum's retirement, with the school and the harbour and the steps.

:::private
I said no once, to a commission for a map of a man's ex-wife's house with "the places it all went wrong" marked on it. He offered a lot of money. I suggested he try a therapist, and then I went for a swim.
:::
