Six weeks of walking every street, lane and stair in Sandviken with a sketchbook, a pencil and a flask, in the summer after leaving Nordvåg.

The routine: walk in the morning, two or three streets, drawing doors and corners and the shapes of roofs. Ink in the afternoon at the table by the window. Colour on Sundays. By the end of August the map had 410 houses on it, 23 stairs, one bakery, three cats on walls and the white house we lived in, with Biscuit in the window, very small.

I printed twenty copies at a print shop by the university. On 14 September somebody I'd never met bought one from the shop page and wrote in the order notes: *I grew up in the house with the cat on the wall.* I cried at my desk, which was by then my own.
