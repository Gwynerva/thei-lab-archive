title: Prices
---

The current prices are on [Prints and prices](ref:page:prints). Here is how they got there.

- **A4 print, unframed:** ~~250 kr~~ 350 kr. I undercharged for a year because I was embarrassed.
- **A3 print, unframed:** ~~400 kr~~ 550 kr.
- **Commission, one map, A3:** from 4,500 kr, more for a big place or a hurry.
- **Postage:** at cost, which in Norway is more than you'd think.

How I worked them out, in the end: the hours, honestly counted, times what I'd charge for map work, plus the paper and the printing, then rounded down a little, because they are for people, not for offices.

Sigrid, when I told her the new prices, said:

> If they buy it at that price, it was too cheap. If they don't, it was right.

I raised them again the next spring. People still buy them. Sigrid was right about that, as she is about apples.
