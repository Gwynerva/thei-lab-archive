title: The style
---

### Rules I keep

1. Draw from walking, never from a screen alone. Software for the bones, feet for everything else.
2. Ink first, then colour, then names. Never the other way round.
3. Leave out more than you put in. A map is a list of what mattered.
4. Five colours at most, mixed from three tubes.

### Rules I break

- that north must be at the top (it is, but sometimes I want to turn the fjord)
- that buildings should be drawn in plan; I draw the ones I love in :hint[elevation]{note="From the side, as if you were standing in the street looking at them, the way the old town views drew churches."}, so you can see their doors

### Where it came from

From the old topographical drawing manuals, where a hill was shaded by hand and every tree was a tiny round tuft. From the 1853 survey of the coast at home. From Nana's embroidered map of Robin Hood's Bay, which hung over her sewing machine and had the church in the wrong place and was right about everything else.
