On the twenty-third of March 2020 we were told to stay at home and allowed out once a day for exercise. On the twenty-fourth I went out for mine and wrote it down, and then I did it again the next day, and the next, until it was a thing I did, and then it was the thing that held the days together.

There were [rules](ref:section:daily-walk/rules). There was a notebook, then a spreadsheet, then [a map](ref:section:daily-walk/the-map). There were 283 days between the twenty-fourth of March and the end of the year, and I walked on every one of them, even the ones I do not remember.

::media[The Water of Leith, on one of the first mornings]{file="leith-river-2" layout="stretch"}

## What the walks were

Mostly the same few miles, over and over: down Leith Walk, along the Shore, up the Water of Leith path to Canonmills or as far as Dean Village, home by the back streets. In the first weeks the streets were so empty I could hear the river from the bridge. In May the blossom came out along the path and nobody was there to see it but us, the people out for our one walk, nodding to each other from two metres away like members of a club.

## What they were for

I did not know at the time. I know now: they were for keeping a shape on the day when nothing else had one. Work on a laptop in the kitchen, bread, the cello, the walk, Biscuit, bed. The walk was the only part that went anywhere.

On a wet Sunday in April the walk was round the block three times in the rain, to make up the half hour, and afterwards Marta and I watched a short film about a rabbit on her laptop under a blanket and laughed more than it deserved.

## The end of it

The year got worse in the autumn. The knee went in September; Nana died in November. The walks went on anyway, shorter, slower, one of them only to the corner shop on Marta's arm. Five of them, in Whitby in November, I do not remember at all; Dad says we went to the end of the pier and back every morning, and I believe him. They are not on the big map. They are on a scrap of paper pinned to its corner, five short lines, all the same.

The full list of every walk with the weather is in [the diary](/diary/), day by day; the [projects](/projects/) of that year, the sweater and the bread and the bike, were all done between walks.
