From the notebook, copied as written.

Day 1. Down to the Shore and back. Nobody about. Sun. A swan on the river, looking as surprised as me.

Day 12. Round by Pilrig Park. Rain. A man playing the bagpipes out of his window to nobody, or to all of us.

Day 41. Up the river to Dean Village. Blossom on every tree on the path. Three people in two miles.

Day 67. First day out of the city: the Pentlands, the bus, the hills. Sat on the top of Allermuir and could not stop smiling.

Day 100. A hundred. The first one again: Leith Walk, the Shore, the river path to Canonmills, home past the Botanics. An hour and ten minutes. The heron has a friend.

::audio[The blackbird on the Links, recorded on day 30]{file="sound-blackbird" title="Blackbird"}

Day 152. Portobello, into the sea for the first time, at last. Cold. Wonderful. Will go back.

Day 173. The knee, on the steps by the weir, on a run. Three kilometres home holding on to railings. The hardest walk yet.

Day 174. Down three flights on my bottom, to the corner shop on Marta's arm, and back. Forty minutes for two hundred metres. Counts, by rule five.

Day 208. Round the block, slowly, to look for Biscuit, who was on the roof. He was on the roof.

Day 243. Whitby. To the end of the pier and back with Dad, he says. I do not remember it.

Day 255. In Robin Hood's Bay, after the funeral, down the lane to the beach and back up past the rowan.

Day 283. The last one of the year. Down Leith Walk to the Shore in the dark, and back. Not looking forward to anything in particular. Not looking back. Just walking.
