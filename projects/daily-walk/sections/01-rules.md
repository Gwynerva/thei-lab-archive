---
title: The rules
summary: Written on the back of an envelope on day three.
---

Written on the back of an envelope on day three, stuck to the fridge until we left the flat:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. Once a day, at least half an hour, out of the door and back on my own legs.
2. Not the same way twice in a row, if it can be helped.
3. Write it down: how far, the weather, and one thing I saw.
4. Rain is not a reason. Sadness is not a reason.
5. A bad day still counts, even if the walk is to the corner.
:::

Rule four I kept until the autumn. Rule five I made for myself in April, on a day I did not want to go out, and it turned out to be the only one that mattered.

Marta added a sixth in pencil in May: *say hello to the heron*. There was a heron on the Water of Leith below the bridge at Bonnington, most mornings, standing in the weir. I said hello to it every time.
