Pencil first, for every page, on cheap paper, until the cat looked like Biscuit and not like a fox. Then the real thing, on heavy watercolour paper: pencil, ink line, then colour, one page at a time.

Biscuit modelled, in the sense that he slept on everything I was trying to draw.

April and May at the desk in Sandviken, in the evenings after half days at work. June on the Camino, two pages painted on my knee in hostels, from memory. July and August at the desk again, between the first freelance jobs. September, the sky pages, which took longer than anything, because a night sky in watercolour is mostly waiting for the paper to dry.
