---
title: Why
---

I could say it was for my health, and it was, but not in the way the articles mean. I did not do it to boost my immune system, whatever that means, or to burn anything. I did it because I could not do anything else, and this was a thing a person could do in forty seconds and then go home.

The woman from the pottery class, whom I will not name because she would hate it, had swum at Nordnes every morning for twelve winters. She did not tell me it would help. She said only that the water was seven degrees and that most people screamed the first time and that screaming was allowed.

I did not scream. I gasped, and then I breathed out, long, the way the old man in the woolly hat told me to, and counted, and got to forty, and got out. And on the steps, wrapped in a towel with my teeth going, I laughed, and I had not laughed for so long that I did not recognise the sound.

That is the why. The rest, the rules and the count and the notebook, came later, because I am who I am.
