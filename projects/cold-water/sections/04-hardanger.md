The first summer at Øvre Lid the fjord was too warm to count and I was too busy with windows to care. Then in October 2025 the water dropped under fifteen, and I walked down through the orchard in my dressing gown and boots and got in off the flat rock at the bottom of the field, and that was the beginning of the second place.

It is different here. At Nordnes there were steps and a ladder and a crowd; here there is a rock with a crack in it where I put my foot, and a heron who watches from the next one and does not approve. The water is deep almost at once, dark green going black. And in spring it gets colder, not warmer, because the snow on Folgefonna melts and the rivers bring it down, and it lies on top of the salt water like a cold sheet.

::media[The rocks in February, ice on everything above the tide]{file="icicles-2" layout="natural"}

Jonas comes down with a torch when it is dark or under five, which from December is always. He does not swim. He stands on the rock in his felt hat from Svaneti with a flask and says *du er gal*, you are mad, and holds out the towel.

The count, from October to the end of April: fifty-eight swims from the rock. The coldest was two degrees in the water and minus nine in the air, in February, ninety seconds, and I have never felt so alive or so stupid. Then the stove, and the kettle, and an hour of being very warm and very kind to everyone.
