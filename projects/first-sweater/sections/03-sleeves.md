title: Sleeves, twice
periods:
  - start: 2020-09-01
    end: 2020-12-31

---

September. First sleeve done. Tried it on. It came down past my fingertips like a straitjacket. I'd misread the decreases: every *eighth* round, not every *tenth*. Ripped it back to the cuff in one long pull, which was weirdly satisfying, and wound the wool back into a ball with Biscuit's help, which was not.

The knee went on the 12th, so the second go at the first sleeve was knitted with my leg up on a cushion and a bag of frozen peas on it. Good for knitting, bad for everything else.

October. First sleeve, properly. Second sleeve, started.

November. Halfway up the second sleeve when Mum rang about Nana.

Didn't knit.

---

December. Picked it up again on the 6th, after the funeral. Knitted every evening. It was the only thing I wanted to do.
