Cast off the second sleeve at four this afternoon. Sewed the underarms. Wove in the ends: twenty-three of them, I counted.

Put it on.

It fits. The cable is a bit tight at the top and the neck is a bit loose, and it's the best thing I have ever made.

Wore it to the shop for milk. Wore it on the walk, round the Shore, in the dark. Wore it on the phone to Mum. She asked if it was the red one Nana had been asking about. I said yes. She was quiet for a bit, and then she said, "She'd have said the neck's loose."

She would.
