Day 21. Cast on 212 stitches with Nana's needles, which still smell faintly of her cupboard. Took an hour. Joined in the round and twisted it on the first try, so cast on again. Two hours.

Day 35. Ten centimetres of ribbing. My hands know what they're doing now, mostly.

Day 52. The cable. Every eighth row you slip four stitches onto the bent stick, hold them in front, knit the next four, then knit the four off the stick. It looks like magic. It feels like defusing a bomb.

Day 85. The weather station went online yesterday and beeps every ten minutes. Knitting on the balcony beside it. Biscuit lies on the ball of wool as if he's hatching it.

Day 101. The body's done, to the armpits, on the same day as the hundredth walk. Put it on the floor and looked at it for a long time. It's a sweater with no arms, and I made it.

Most of the body was knitted through films, one a night, the laptop on a cushion. My favourite was a short one about a sheep, which felt only right:
