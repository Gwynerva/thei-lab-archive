---
title: The pattern
summary: A raglan, from the hem up
---

Chosen from a free pattern online after three evenings of looking at every sweater on the internet. A plain raglan, worked in the round from the hem up, the sleeves knitted separately and joined at the yoke. It looked simple. Nana's verdict on the phone: "Simple's hard. Do it anyway."

What I needed:

- wool: seven skeins of aran-weight in raspberry red, plus one for luck
- needles
  - Nana's 5 mm, the old grey ones, for the body
  - a 4.5 mm circular for the ribbing, bought new, which felt like cheating
- a cable needle, which is a bent stick
- stitch markers, which in my case were paper clips
- a tape measure and the patience of a saint

And a tension swatch, which the pattern said to knit before you begin, in capital letters, and which I knitted, sulkily, and was glad of, because my tension is tighter than the pattern's and I'd have made a sweater for Biscuit.
