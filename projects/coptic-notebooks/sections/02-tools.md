title: Tools
summary: Almost nothing, which was the point
---

The whole kit fits in a shoebox, which is what sold me on it. A top-floor flat in Leith has no room for a press.

- **a bone folder**, Fergus's spare, sold to me for a fiver
- an awl, really a bradawl from Dad's shop with the price still on it
- needles
  - curved ones for the coptic stitch
  - straight ones for everything else, and for lending
- thread
  - waxed linen in black, red and a blue the shop called *harbour*
  - a lump of beeswax for when it isn't waxed enough
- a cutting mat, a steel rule and a scalpel
- bulldog clips, as many as there are in the flat
- two bricks wrapped in brown paper, which are a book press if you don't own a book press
