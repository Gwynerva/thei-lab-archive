The dissertation summer, and the only thing keeping me sane was a stack of paper at the other end of the kitchen table. Five finished notebooks by the end of August, all coptic, every one of them a bit different, because I couldn't stop fiddling:

1. **The map one**, covers wrapped in an old OS sheet of Edinburgh with Arthur's Seat on the front. To Priya, for lab notes.
2. **The bingo card**, a red and white card from a charity shop on Leith Walk. To Mum, who has never played bingo in her life.
3. **The square one**, square on purpose after two square by accident. Kept.
4. **The tree**, a kraft cover printed with a tree and a twig tied on for a pencil. To Nana June, for her recipes, which she won't write in because "they're in my head, pet".
5. **The gingham**, blue and white, sewn in red thread. Kept. No idea what for yet.
