My first stall! A church hall in Leith, half a trestle table, a tablecloth from Mum and thirty-one notebooks laid out in rows like biscuits. There's more about [the day itself](ref:event:craft-fair-2017) elsewhere; this is the money side.

Prices, after an argument with myself:

- A6, any cover: £12
- A5, any cover: £18
- the square one: not for sale (nobody asked)

Sold **fourteen**. Fourteen strangers handed me money for something I'd sewn at the kitchen table, and one woman came back after an hour to buy a second one for her sister. I nearly hugged her. Priya ran the float and ate most of the tablet she'd made to sell.

---

Three good things:

1. fourteen notebooks!
2. the woman who came back
3. chips on the walk home, in the cold, with £192 in a biscuit tin
