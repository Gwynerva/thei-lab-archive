  - 2019-10-06

Up at five in Leith, the first train to Glasgow, full of people in shorts pretending not to be nervous. Marta came with me, with a flask and a sign she had made that said GO MIRA, I THINK.

The start in George Square, ten thousand people and a man with a microphone. Over the river, out west through two parks with trees going yellow, and back along the Clyde. At mile twelve, where it hurt in a dull way and then in a sharp way and then in no way at all, Callum was standing on the pavement outside the café he had opened a year before, in his apron, holding a tray of scones and shouting my name, and I nearly stopped to take one, and didn't, and have regretted it since.

The finish on Glasgow Green. The clock said:

**1\:58\:41**\
Under two hours, by a minute and nineteen seconds.

I sat on the grass and cried, which I had not expected, and ate a banana, which I had. Then Marta and I walked very slowly to Callum's café and he gave me the scone I had missed, and two more, and would not take any money.

[The day itself](ref:event:half-marathon-race) has the photograph Marta took of me at the finish, which I have asked her to destroy.
