title: The plan
---

Copied from a book in the library into my notebook, then into a spreadsheet, then onto the fridge, where Marta added comments in red pen.

Four runs a week, sixteen weeks:

- **Tuesday**, intervals: run hard for two minutes, walk for one, over and over, on the path round Leith Links, where the dog walkers learned to step aside
- **Thursday**, a steady run at a pace you could talk at, if you were someone who talked while running, which I was not
- **Saturday**, short and easy, or the Spoke Room's old crowd's park run if I could face people
- **Sunday**, the long run, a mile longer every week or two, ending on the prom at Portobello with an ice cream, which was the real reason I did it

And three rules of my own, in capitals on the fridge:

1. NEVER TWO HARD DAYS TOGETHER
2. IF IT HURTS IN A SHARP WAY, STOP. IF IT HURTS IN A DULL WAY, IT'S RUNNING
3. THE LAST WEEK IS FOR RESTING, NOT FOR PANIC

I kept the first and the third. The second I kept in 2019. In 2020 I forgot it, once, and that is [another page](ref:project:knee).
