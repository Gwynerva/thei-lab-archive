In May 2024 I was working out my notice at Nordvåg and starting to feel like myself in the mornings, and the club rang to ask if I wanted to finish what I had started. I said yes before I had thought about it, which I am told is a sign of recovery.

This time I remember all of it. Evenings on the fjord after work, out past Askøy and back in the long light. Jonas sitting on his hands in the cockpit while I did every tack, which for Jonas is a kind of love letter. A fender he named Gunnar, whom I rescued six times in a man-overboard drill and ran over the seventh.

Then the test, on the seventeenth of August, in a south-westerly of about twelve knots: leave the pontoon under sail, tack, gybe, heave to, rescue another fender, reef in a gust and come back alongside without touching anything. I touched something. Lightly. [The rest is here](ref:event:sailing-certificate).

That evening Jonas spread the chart of the North Sea on the kitchen table and put a mug on Shetland to hold it flat. I said, one summer at a time. He waited two.
