eight Saturdays. Six of us, two small keelboats, an instructor of about nineteen who calls everyone *du* and means it kindly.

[the first one](ref:event:first-sailing-lesson): the boom, my hat, Jonas waving like an idiot from the other boat.

then mornings on the pontoon learning the parts of a boat, and afternoons out by Askøy learning what they do. I steered badly and then less badly. I liked it best when nobody spoke and the boat leaned and went.

some Saturdays I nearly didn't go. I went. That was the whole achievement of the summer, and I knew it at the time.

the last lesson was on the twenty-sixth of August, a sail round the bay and waffles on the terrace. In the afternoon we took the kayak out to Herdla, which is [another story](ref:event:herdla-rock).
