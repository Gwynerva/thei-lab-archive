---
title: Knots
summary: Seven knots, tied behind my back, in the dark, and once in the North Sea
---

The instructor gave us knots for homework on the first Saturday, and I have been tying them ever since: on the train, in meetings, round the leg of the kitchen table, once round Biscuit's tail, briefly. Seven of them earned their place.

- **The bowline**, the king of knots. A loop that will not slip and will not jam.
  - Tied behind my back, ten times a night, for a week.
  - Tied with my eyes shut, for the test.
  - Tied for real at two in the morning off Shetland, in the dark, with cold hands.
- **The clove hitch**, quick to a post, quicker to come undone if you trust it too much.
- **The round turn and two half hitches**, for a fender, or for tying anything to anything.
- **The cleat hitch**, the one everyone on the pontoon watches you do.
- **The reef knot**, for reefing, and for nothing else, the instructor said, twice.
- **The figure of eight**, at the end of every sheet so it cannot run out through the block.
- **The rolling hitch**, which I never needed and like the most.
