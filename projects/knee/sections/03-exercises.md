title: The exercises
summary: Morag's list, by stage, which I still do now and then
---

Morag's list, as she wrote it out for me, by stage. I still do the last ones, in the kitchen while the kettle boils, every other day when I remember and every day before a long walk.

- **Stage one**, the first fortnight
  - Tightening the thigh with the leg straight, ten seconds, ten times
  - Sliding the heel up and down the bed, to get the bend back
  - Ice, after, always
- **Stage two**, to Christmas
  - Straight leg raises, lying down, three sets of ten
  - Mini squats against the wall, as far as it would go without hurting
  - Standing on the bad leg for thirty seconds, then with my eyes shut, which is much harder than it sounds
- **Stage three**, to the end
  - Step-ups on to a stair, slowly, three sets of fifteen
  - Stepping down, slower, which is where the knee really works
  - Hopping, small, then bigger
  - Walking, further every week

What I still do, in Hardanger, in 2026:

1. step-ups on the bottom stair, which creaks
2. balancing on one leg while I brush my teeth
3. stepping down off the woodshed step, slowly, thirty times

Jonas calls it my knee ballet. It is less elegant than that.
