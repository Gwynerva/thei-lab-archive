Tuesday evenings, six till nine, at a print workshop near the old harbour. I was back at work four hours a day; Tuesdays were the evenings I looked forward to.

- **February.** Pencils only. Straight lines, circles, the same letter eighty times. I nearly gave up in week two and didn't.
- **March.** Pens. Isla was born on the 2nd; on the 5th I lettered her name forty times in a row until it looked like her, then sent Clare the best one.
- **April.** Brushes. Paint everywhere. Handed in my notice on Friday the 12th, and lettered *ferdig* (finished) on a scrap of paper and pinned it over my desk.
- **May.** Our own alphabets. Mine was for maps.

On the last evening, 14 May, the teacher asked us each to letter one word that mattered. I wrote *Sandviken*, small, the way it would go on a map, and she said it was the first thing I'd lettered all term without looking worried.\
I hadn't known it showed.
