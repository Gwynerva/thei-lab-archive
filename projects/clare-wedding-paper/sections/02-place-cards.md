Eighty-four names in a fortnight, in a looser hand than the invitations, with a sprig of rosemary drawn on each, because rosemary is for remembrance and Clare wanted Grandad Arthur at the table somehow.

The table plan was harder than the cards. Nobody tells you a table plan is a diplomatic document. Two cousins who don't speak, an aunt who must be near the door, Dev's grandmother who must be nowhere near the speakers, and Nana June, eighty-nine, who must be next to the dance floor because "I'm not going all the way to York to look at a wall". The aunt went from ~~table 3~~ to ~~table 2~~ to table 7 in one evening of phone calls.

Callum moved to Glasgow at the end of the month in the middle of all this. His leaving tea was eaten off a table covered in name cards, and he found his own in the pile and asked if he'd been invited. He had.
