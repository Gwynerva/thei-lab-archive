Eighty-four invitations, one at a time, in the evenings, with the window open and the heatwave starting.

The wording, after four drafts and one argument about whether "request the pleasure" is pompous (it is; Mum won):

Helen and Martin Testwell\
request the pleasure of your company\
at the marriage of their daughter\
Clare\
to\
Dev Mistry\
on Saturday the eighth of September\
in York

The list on the fridge:

- [x] buy card (cream, heavy, from the print shop)
- [x] test nibs (the 2 mm italic, dark green ink)
- [x] rule guidelines in pencil on a light box (a picture frame on a lamp)
- [x] letter 84 (actually 97, with the spoils)
- [x] rub out the pencil without smudging the ink (didn't always)
- [x] post them to Clare in Leeds, recorded, insured and prayed over

About four an hour, on a good night. Callum kept bringing me toast and asking to see, and then not looking because he was frightened of breathing on them.
