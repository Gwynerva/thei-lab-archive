[Jonas moved in](ref:event:jonas-moves-in) on the first of August 2023, into a bigger flat three streets up the same hill: a white house with a blue door, a kitchen you could turn round in, a view of Byfjorden from the kitchen window, and room for his tools in the hall, until further notice, which turned out to be never.

We arrived with one sofa (his), one armchair (mine), four frying pans, a framed chart of the Hjeltefjord and a cat who sat in the empty bath for the whole of the first day.

Two months later I stopped working, and for three months that flat was most of my world. Jonas did not try to fix anything. He made fish soup as if he were cross with it, and left for the workshop at seven, and came back at five, and in between the flat was quiet and grey and kind, and Biscuit lay on my legs. It is a good flat to be ill in, which is not something you look for in a listing.

Then the better things: the spring of 2024 and the walk to Santiago; my desk in the corner of the living room when I went freelance; the first map I sold, drawn at the kitchen table; Mum and Dad for Christmas 2024, Dad in his good coat on the steps of the sea bath.

And then the house. In March 2025 we [packed it](ref:event:packing) into sixty-one boxes, each with a little map of where it was going on the lid, and on the twenty-ninth we drove out of Sandviken for the last time with Biscuit complaining in his carrier between my feet, the cello upright between my knees and the barometer, wrapped in a tea towel, in the last box.
