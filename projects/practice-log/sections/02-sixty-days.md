Day one, the fifth of February: fifty minutes on a rented cello, all bowing, *sore fingers*. My own was at the luthier's having its insides put right, and for a month I practised on an instrument that was better than mine in every way and that I never once liked.

Day sixty, the fifth of April: forty-four minutes of open strings, *tired*, which felt right, because you end where you began.

In between, the sums:

- **1,883 minutes** in all, thirty-one hours and a bit
- fifty days played, ten not: three for snow (the Beast from the East, when the whole city stopped), one for my birthday, two for Easter, two for Callum's family, two for no reason I wrote down
- most minutes on bowing, 453 of them, which Mrs Ogilvie said was the only correct answer
- the most common verdict *better*, ten times; *awful* seven times; *nearly music* once, on day fifty-two

::file[The whole log, sixty lines of it, zeros and all]{file="practice-log-2018" title="Sixty days of practice"}

The best day was the thirteenth of March, the first rehearsal with the Leith Scratch Orchestra, which I counted as forty-four minutes of orchestra part, *lovely*. The worst was the twenty-second of February, eleven minutes, *awful*, *the A string broke and so did I*.

I stopped the script on day sixty and did not stop practising. That was the point.
