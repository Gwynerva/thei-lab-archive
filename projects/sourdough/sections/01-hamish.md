Day 7. A jam jar, 50 g of wholemeal flour, 50 g of water, a stir, a tea towel over the top. That's it. That's the whole of a sourdough starter, apparently. You make him and then you keep him alive.

The plan, written on the fridge:

1. Days one to three: feed once a day, the same again.
2. Days four to seven: throw half away, feed the rest. (Throwing half away feels wrong. Nana would never.)
3. After that: twice a day, until he doubles in four to six hours.
4. Then bread.

Marta has asked whether "he" lives on the windowsill permanently. Yes. He's got the best view in the flat.
