Day 62. Loaf fifteen.

It rose in the oven. It *rose*. Split open along the score in a ridge, which is called the ear, which I knew from the internet but had never seen in my own kitchen. The crust went dark and crackled as it cooled, which is called singing, which I also knew from the internet and didn't believe until I stood in the kitchen with my ear to a loaf and heard it.

Waited the hour. Cut it.

Holes. Proper holes, all through it, uneven and glossy. Butter went into the holes.

Rang Mum and held the phone up so she could hear it crackle. She said, "That's lovely, love, what am I listening to."
