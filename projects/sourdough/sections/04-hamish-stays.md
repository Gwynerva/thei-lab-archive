title: Hamish stays behind
periods:
  - 2021-07-30
---

Last full day in Leith. The flat is boxes, Marta is packing her own boxes in the next room, and Biscuit is in disgrace for climbing into the crate that's going to Bergen and refusing to come out.

This morning I handed Hamish over to Marta, in his jar, with a label on the lid already written in her handwriting: *HAMISH. FEED ME.* We had a last loaf together on the back step, and then she carried the jar into her room like a woman carrying a baby.

He'll live with her across town now. Marta says he'll be "in the bread by Tuesday". I started to say ~~don't let him get lazy~~ and said look after him instead. Marta said, "He's a sourdough starter, Mira." But she set him down on top of her boxes very carefully all the same.

I didn't think I'd mind this much. It's flour and water.

It isn't, though, is it.
