---
title: Hamish stays behind
periods:
  - 2021-07-30
---

Last full day in Leith. The flat is boxes, Marta is packing her own boxes in the next room, and Biscuit is in disgrace for climbing into the crate that's going to Bergen and refusing to come out.

Callum came through from Glasgow on the train this morning to take Hamish. He'd brought a proper tub with a lid and a label, already written: *HAMISH. FEED ME.* We had a last loaf together on the back step, and then he went back to the station with the tub under his arm like a man carrying a baby.

He'll live at the café now. Callum says he'll be "in the bread by Tuesday". I started to say ~~don't let him get lazy~~ and said look after him instead. Callum said, "He's a sourdough starter, Mira." But he held the tub very carefully all the way down the stair.

I didn't think I'd mind this much. It's flour and water.

It isn't, though, is it.
