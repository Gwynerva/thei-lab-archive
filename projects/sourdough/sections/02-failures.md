---
title: Failures
summary: Fourteen loaves that didn't, and why
---

I kept a list. Of course I kept a list.

### The flat ones

Loaves one to five. Hamish wasn't ready, I was impatient, and the dough spread across the tray like a pancake. Lesson: if a spoonful of starter doesn't float in a glass of water, wait.

### The dense ones

Loaves six to nine. Not enough proving, because I didn't trust it. A sourdough loaf takes all day and then all night, and I kept cutting it short because I wanted bread *now*. Lesson: the bread is not on your timetable.

### The gummy ones

Loaves ten to fourteen. Baked long enough, but cut while still warm, because I couldn't wait, and a warm sourdough is still cooking inside. Lesson: the hour after it comes out of the oven is part of the baking.

What it came down to, in the end:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. Patience.
2. A hotter oven than I thought, with a lid on.
3. Wetter dough than I dared.
4. Patience again, for the cooling.
:::

Biscuit ate the crust off loaf eleven while it was cooling on the side, which is the only time one of my failures has been somebody else's success.
