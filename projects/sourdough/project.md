Everyone made sourdough in 2020. I know. I was one of them, and I'm not sorry.

Hamish was born on the 29th of March 2020, a week into the first lockdown, in a jam jar on the kitchen windowsill: flour and water, stirred, left, fed, stirred, left. On his fourth day he bubbled. On his sixth he smelled like nail varnish, which the internet said was normal and Marta said was not. On his ninth he doubled in four hours, and I named him after an uncle of Callum's who also smelled of something and also rose reliably every morning.

## What came out of the oven

Fourteen loaves that were flat, dense, gummy, or all three. One loaf, on the 23rd of May, that was not. After that, about two a week for a year, and [bread for the stair](ref:event:bread-for-the-stair "Saturday mornings in lockdown"): every Saturday I left rolls in a basket on the landing with a note, and they were gone by ten, and sometimes there'd be something in the basket when I came back. Eggs. A jar of chutney. Once, a bottle of wine with a label that said *for the bread lady*.

## Why it mattered

Lockdown was very small. A flat, a balcony, one walk a day. Bread made time into something you could see. You fed him in the morning, mixed in the afternoon, folded every half hour through the evening, shaped at night, baked first thing. The days had a shape again. On the days when nothing else happened, a loaf did.

## Hamish now

He didn't come to Norway. You can't take a starter on a plane without a lot of explaining, and I'd already done a lot of explaining about a cat. He went to Glasgow instead, to [Callum's café](https://example.org/wee-oven), where he works for a living and is fed more regularly than I ever managed. Callum sends a photo every March on his birthday. In Bergen I started again, with a new starter called Sverre, after my neighbour, who was pleased in a way he tried hard to hide.

The notes I kept are on [the bread page](ref:page:bread-notes "hydration, timings, Hamish's diet"), and everything else about bread is somewhere under [tags](/tags/).
