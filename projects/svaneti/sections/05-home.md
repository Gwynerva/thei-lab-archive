The little plane from Mestia to Tbilisi was cancelled for cloud, which everyone in the queue seemed to have expected except us, so it was the minibus down the gorge again and the day train from Zugdidi, seven hours of maize fields and stations with no names I could read. A last night in the guesthouse with the vine. On Monday, Istanbul, Oslo, Bergen, the bus, and the last ferry across the fjord in the dark.

Biscuit was on the step. Kari had fed him twice a day and he wanted us to know it had not been nearly enough.

We said we would do nothing on Tuesday. ~~Nothing.~~ Window eleven, and the insulation for the north wall, and a pile of post. But I kept the hat on the hook by the door where I can see it, Jonas's, the felt one, and when the roof leaked a fortnight later I looked at it and thought of the woman in Ushguli and her eight months of winter, and got the bucket.
