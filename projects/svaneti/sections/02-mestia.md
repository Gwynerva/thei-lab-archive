Four hours up the gorge in a minibus whose driver overtook on blind bends with the calm of a man who has done it ten thousand times, which I suppose he had. Then [Mestia and its towers](ref:event:mestia), which I counted from the guesthouse balcony until the light went.

In the afternoon, the museum: icons in silver, manuscripts older than anything in Bergen, and a room of photographs of Svan families from a century ago, standing in front of the same towers in the same felt hats. Jonas bought one of the hats in the square afterwards. It is grey, round and slightly too small, and he wore it for the whole trek and wears it now to split wood.

Supper was kubdari and a jug of something red from a plastic barrel. I laid out the map on the floor and wrote the four villages down the margin. Then repacked my rucksack, as one does the night before, taking out one thing and putting back two.
