Printed this year's in Jonas's workshop, on the bench beside the actual kayak, which is upside down on the strongback with its deck going on. So the card is a kayak upside down on a strongback. Write what you know.

Fifty-one cards: Bergen people, Scottish people, Whitby people, and for the first time a card to Sigrid Brekke, which I rewrote three times because my Norwegian goes formal when I'm nervous. *God jul og godt nyttår.* I think it's right. Jonas says it's right, but he would.

He made me a rubbing tool out of a scrap of oak: a flat round thing with a knob, smooth as a pebble. Better than any spoon. I'm keeping it.

Rain: day 4 of this spell. Ink takes forever to dry in Bergen.
