Biscuit's first Christmas, so Biscuit is the card. He sleeps on whatever map I leave open (the Ordnance Survey sheet of the Cairngorms is his favourite, possibly because it's the warmest colour), so the card is a ginger cat curled on a map, the contour lines going round him like rings in water.

Two colours this year, my first reduction print: cut the block, print the orange, cut away more, print the black on top. You can't go back. I printed fifty oranges, cut the block again, and then realised I'd cut away his left ear. All fifty cats have one ear. Nobody has mentioned it, which tells you something about how closely people look at Christmas cards.

Biscuit's own contribution was to walk across six drying prints. Those six went to people with cats.
