Back at it. One apple, alone on a bare branch, cut in a single evening with the radio on, the way it used to be.

The apple is real. Three weeks ago we went to look at an old farmhouse above the Hardangerfjord, the house Jonas's great-aunt Ragnhild lived in for most of her life, and in the orchard below it, on a tree that had dropped every leaf, there was one yellow apple left at the top. Jonas wanted to pick it. I asked him not to. I drew it on the back of the ferry ticket on the way home, and here it is.

Forty-three cards. Inside, under the usual verse, in small letters:

:::spoiler
We might be moving to the house the apple came from.
:::

Mum rang the day hers arrived and asked one question, which was "how many bedrooms".
