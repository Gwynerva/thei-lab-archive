Printed the cards tonight on the kitchen floor!! Forty of them. The robin came out of a drawing I did on the bus home from graduation, from a photo, because real robins won't sit still and the one in the back green is a menace.

Borrowed Priya's lino tools from her school days (her name's still scratched on the handles), bought a roller and a tube of black ink, used Callum's best wooden spoon for rubbing and have been told I owe him a new one. Fair.

- 40 cards printed
- 31 good enough to send
- 9 that went to people who won't mind
- 1 spoon, ruined

Nana's is going in the post tomorrow, first class. Knackered and inky and so pleased. (More of tonight is [over here](ref:event:linocut-night-2017#:~:text=robin).)
