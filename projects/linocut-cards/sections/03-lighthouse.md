The West Pier lighthouse this year: a tall fluted column, which in lino means forty thin vertical lines and a great deal of swearing. I cut it over three evenings at the end of November with the radio on and the heating off, because the heating in this flat is a rumour.

It's Whitby because of Nana. She fell at the beginning of November and broke her hip, and for a fortnight everyone in the family talked in that careful voice. She's home now, with a walking frame she calls "the shopping trolley" and a temper that is a very good sign. The card is for her, really. The other forty-six are a cover story.

Printing notes for next year:

- blue-black ink is better than black for the sea
- cut the sky lines across, not following the shape of the tower
- don't print with Marta practising in the next room; I rubbed in time to her scales and the corners came out smudged
