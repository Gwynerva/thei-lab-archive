---
title: How I print them
summary: Lino, a gouge, a roller, a wooden spoon and the kitchen floor
---

No press. Everything is done by hand, on the floor, because the floor is the only surface in any flat I've lived in that is big enough.

First, the block:

1. Draw the design the size of the card in soft pencil, then rub it face down onto the lino, so it prints the right way round.
2. Cut away everything that should stay white: a small V-gouge for lines, a big U-gouge for clearing, always cutting away from the hand that holds the block.

Then the part that makes a mess:

3. Roll out the ink on a sheet of glass until it hisses, not before.
4. Ink the block in two directions, thin, then thin again.
5. Lay the card on it, cover it with newsprint, and rub the back with a wooden spoon in small circles for a full minute.
6. Peel. Look. Swear or don't.

What goes wrong, in order of how often:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. Too much ink, so the fine lines fill in.
2. Too little rubbing at the corners.
3. A letter cut the wrong way round, which you only see on the first print.
4. The cat.
:::
