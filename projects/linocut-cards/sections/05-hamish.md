Cut the block anyway. A loaf, scored on top. Hamish's work.

Nana always said a card is a letter for people who don't write letters. She wrote letters.

Twenty-two cards. One fewer address than last year, and I kept writing it on the list without thinking, and crossing it out.

Printed them on the floor with the radio off. Marta brought tea and didn't say anything, which was exactly right.
