title: Drawing contours
---

How the plotter draws a hill, from the settings file that lives beside it:

- **Pen.** A cheap fountain pen with a fine nib and grey-brown ink, the colour of old Ordnance Survey contours.
- **Speed.** Slow on curves, faster on straights. Too fast and the line goes thin; too slow and it blots.
- **Order.** Lowest contour first, working up, so the pen never drags through wet ink.
- **Index lines.** Every fifth contour is drawn twice, a hair apart, so it reads thicker, the way the old maps did it.
