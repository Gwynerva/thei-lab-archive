title: Why
---

March 2021. The interview with Bergen was on a Thursday; the offer came two weeks later; the permit would take, somebody said, "months, plural". I couldn't plan the move yet and couldn't settle to anything else, and I needed something for my hands.

I'd wanted a plotter since I first saw an old flatbed one at the university, drawing a geological map in six colours with a noise like a sewing machine. It seemed the most honest way a computer could make a map: not dots, but lines, drawn with a real pen, in the order a person would draw them.

So: a plotter, small enough for the kitchen table, that drew contours with a fountain pen. Marta said it was the most me thing she had ever heard of, and she has lived with me for nearly three years.
