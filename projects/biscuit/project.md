Biscuit is a ginger tom with a white bib, green eyes, a torn left ear he came with and an opinion about everything. He was about three when I adopted him from a rescue centre in Edinburgh in February 2019, found in a bin store in Gorgie the November before. He came with his name, more or less: the card on his pen said *Mr Biscuits*, and I only dared take off the Mr and the s.

Since then he has lived in four homes in two countries: the top flat in Leith, two flats in Sandviken, and now the farmhouse at Øvre Lid, where he has a barn. He has slept on most of the Ordnance Survey maps of Scotland, flown across the North Sea in a crate, met snow, met swallows, met a hare, and left a sourdough starter behind in Leith without a backward glance. He is about ten now and has begun, this year, to be an old cat.

## Habits

He sleeps on maps. Any map, unfolded, in any room, within a minute; he can hear one being opened from two floors away. He shouts for breakfast at five. He leaves the room when the cello comes out, at about minute four. He watches gulls with his jaw going, making a noise like a tiny broken typewriter. There is more in [habits](ref:section:biscuit/habits).

## Things he eats

Officially: cat food, the good kind, measured since the vet looked at him on the scales in 2024. Unofficially, and over seven years, a list I started in Leith in 2019 and have kept ever since:

- olives, green, stolen from a bowl at Priya's leaving party
- cheese rind, any cheese, and in Norway *brunost*, the sweet brown cheese, which he would kill for
- toast with marmalade, a corner, once, in Leith
- ~~prawns~~, until the vet, with regret on both sides
- the skin off cold-smoked salmon, at Christmas
- a crust of Hamish's sourdough, in 2020, which he then carried round the flat for an hour like a trophy
- butter, straight from the dish, when nobody was looking, which was often
- sardines, from Sverre downstairs in Sandviken, every Sunday for two years
- one fly, in the kitchen at Øvre Lid, which he caught and then looked surprised about

Never: anything I actually put in his bowl on purpose that is new.
