---
title: Habits
summary: Maps, gulls, the cello and five o'clock in the morning
---

In no particular order, as observed over seven years:

1. **Maps.** If a map is open he is on it. In Leith he slept through Landranger 36, the Pentlands and the whole of the Cairngorms in one afternoon. In Bergen he favoured the harbour charts. At Øvre Lid he sleeps on the atlas sheets as they dry, and there is a ginger hair under the varnish on at least two of the thirty walks.
2. **Gulls.** He sits on the sill and watches them with his jaw going, making a noise like a tiny broken typewriter, and has never once caught one, and never once stopped hoping.
3. **The cello.** Leaves the room at about minute four. Returns when it stops, as if to say: well done, that's over.
4. **Five o'clock.** Breakfast is at five. He knows this. Nobody else has been told.
5. **The treat tin.** Never once failed, from any distance, until a night in June 2025 when it did.
6. **Boxes.** Every box that came to every flat. The bigger the better. Sixty-one boxes when we left Sandviken, and he tried all of them.
7. **The bath.** In both flats in Sandviken, on the first day, he sat in the empty bath for a long time and would not be moved. Nobody knows why.

What he does not do: play with any toy that was bought for him, come when called by name, or sit on Jonas's lap, except when Jonas is wearing the good trousers.
