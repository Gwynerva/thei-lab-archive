---
title: A Norwegian cat
summary: Snow, sardines, a bigger flat and a barn
---

He became Norwegian faster than I did. Within a month he had a regular Sunday arrangement with Sverre downstairs, who was a retired ship's mate and kept a tin of sardines for him in the larder, and who called him *Kaptein*, which Biscuit answered to and has never answered to *Biscuit*.

In January 2022 he met [proper snow](ref:event:biscuit-snow) for the first time: twenty centimetres of it in the garden overnight. He put one paw in it, took it out, shook it, looked at me with real reproach, and then ran in circles in it until he was soaked and would not come in. By February he would walk on it. By the next winter he would sit in it, upright, for the pleasure of looking cross.

In the bigger flat, from 2023, he had a windowsill with a view down to the harbour and a radiator under it, which he regarded as having been built for him. He was there through the worst of the autumn of 2023, on my legs on the sofa every afternoon, as if he had been prescribed.

At Øvre Lid he has a barn. He sits in the doorway of the byre in the afternoons and supervises the orchard. There are swallows in the barn from May, who shout at him and are right to, and mice in the loft, whom he ignores on principle. Kari from the farm below feeds him when we are away, and he goes down to her kitchen for a second breakfast when we are not, which she pretends not to know about.

He responds now, more or less, to *pus pus*, which is what Norwegians say to cats. He has never responded to English.
