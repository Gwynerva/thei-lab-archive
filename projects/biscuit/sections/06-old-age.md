[Seven years](ref:event:biscuit-seven-years) in February, and this winter he has started to be an old cat. He is about ten.

What has changed:

- he sleeps more, mostly on the bench by the stove, and longer, through the five o'clock breakfast once or twice, which has never happened before
- he takes the stairs one at a time, and the step down to the yard with care
- he has stopped going down to the bottom of the orchard and patrols the porch instead
- he wants laps, which he never did, and will sit on mine for an hour while I draw

The vet in Odda says his heart is good and his kidneys are a ten-year-old cat's kidneys and to keep an eye. I keep an eye. The next check is in November.

:::private
I know what keeping an eye means. I knew it when Nana's letters got shorter. He is fine, the vet says, and he is, and I lie awake some nights listening for the thump of him jumping down off the bench, and when it comes I go back to sleep. I am not ready. I am going to have to be.
:::

This section has a long end on purpose: a year from now, at least, and I will move it again when we get there.
