I went to the rescue centre on a Saturday at the beginning of February "just to look", which is what everybody says and nobody means. There was a ginger tom at the back of a pen, about three, found in a bin store in Gorgie in November. He looked at me as if I owed him money. Then he came to the front and pushed his head against the wire, and that was that.

A home check on the Monday, by a kind woman in a fleece who looked at the skylight for a long time and asked if it opened. It did. "Keep it shut," she said. We passed.

He came home on the ninth in a cardboard carrier with air holes, complaining from the centre to the top of our seventy-two steps, and when I opened the carrier on the kitchen floor he walked out, looked round at Marta and me and the kitchen, went under the bed in my room, and stayed there for two hours.

At two in the morning he came out and slept on my feet. He has slept on my feet, or on whatever I am reading, ever since.
