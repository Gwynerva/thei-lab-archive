---
title: Leaving
---

### What I took with me

- reading a harbour from the chart before I've seen it
- the whole west coast, from the sea side
- Norwegian at work speed, technical words and all
- Anna, as a friend rather than a boss, which is better

### What I left

Two hundred and twelve charts, four tools that are still in use, a mug with a gull on it, and a version of myself who said yes before the question had finished.

:::quote[Anna, on my last day, on the roof]{align="center"}
You don't owe the charts anything. They'll still be wrong in the same places next year, and somebody else will fix them.
:::

### What I'd tell someone starting there

1. The coast will still be there tomorrow.
2. Go home when the gulls go quiet.

:::private
And the thing I couldn't say at the farewell: I was ashamed for a long time of having stopped. I'm not now, most days. Tove said shame is just tiredness wearing a better coat. I wrote that on the inside of my notebook cover.
:::
