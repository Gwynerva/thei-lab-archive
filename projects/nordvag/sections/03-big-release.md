---

march. the plan: every chart, all two hundred and twelve, moved to the new system by the end of the summer. I said I could do the drawing engine. I could.

april. late nights. the kayak varnish on Saturdays, the engine every other night.

june. the deadline moves to september. I say fine. it isn't.

august. Jonas moves in on the first. I am at the office until nine on the second.

september. [the release month](ref:event:release-month). two hundred and twelve charts. on the last Friday they all went out and everybody cheered and I sat at my desk and couldn't remember the word for *chair*.\
in either language.

:::private
the week after, I cried on the bus twice and once in the shop by the bananas. I told Jonas I was tired. he said tired is not the word. he was right. I didn't go in on the ninth of October and then I didn't go in for three months.
:::
