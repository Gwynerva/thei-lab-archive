Back on 15 January, four hours a day, mornings only, at a desk by the window that someone had kindly cleared of everything I'd left on it in October.

The rules I agreed with Anna and with myself:

- [x] mornings only, home by one
- [x] no release work, no deadlines with my name on them
- [x] lunch away from the desk, outside if it isn't raining, under the awning if it is
- [x] one thing a day, finished
- [ ] check email in the afternoon (broken twice in January, then never)

By March I knew I wouldn't stay. It wasn't the company. It was that I couldn't go back to being the person who'd sat at that desk, and the desk didn't know how to be anything else.

I handed in my notice on 12 April. Anna said she'd known since February and was waiting for me to know. My last day was 31 May: cake on the roof terrace, a card from everyone, and a framed chart of the little harbour below the flat in Sandviken, which hangs in the hall at Øvre Lid now, a long way from any sea it describes.
