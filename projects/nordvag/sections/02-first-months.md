[First day](ref:event:nordvag-first-day) on 16 August, three days after Biscuit landed. I walked from Sandviken along the harbour in the rain, which I have since learned is the only way to arrive anywhere in Bergen.

The office: twenty-two people, two dogs, coffee strong enough to stand a spoon in, and a window over Vågen with gulls who knock on it.

::audio[The gulls over Vågen from the office window, my first week]{file="sound-gulls-2" title="Gulls, Vågen"}

New words, from the first month at work:

- *matpakke*: a packed lunch of open sandwiches, wrapped in paper, eaten at half eleven, by everyone, every day
- *pålegg*: whatever goes on the bread, which is a whole philosophy
- *dugnad*: the day everyone stays late to tidy the server room for free and calls it fun
- *kos*: what the office became on Friday afternoons, with candles, at three o'clock, in August

Everyone switched to English for me in meetings and back to Norwegian at lunch. By December I could follow lunch. By the Christmas party on 17 December I could make a joke at lunch, a bad one, and three people laughed out of kindness and Anna out of surprise.

Rain at work, first four months: 89 days out of 124. I wasn't counting yet. That came later.
