title: What went wrong
private: true
---

Written in the spring of 2024, for nobody, to have it somewhere.

It wasn't the job. The job was good. It was me in the job, in that year, with no edges.

1. I said yes to the drawing engine in March 2023 because I wanted to be the one who did it.
2. I worked evenings from April and weekends from June, and told myself the kayak was rest. It wasn't; it was another deadline, with varnish.
3. Jonas moved in in August and I was hardly there for it.
4. I stopped counting rain days in February and started counting hours at the desk instead, in my head, all the time.
5. In September I couldn't remember words. In October I couldn't get on the bus.

What helped, in the end: the doctor, who signed me off before I'd finished the sentence; Tove, on Tuesdays; Jonas, who made porridge and didn't ask; the fjord in December, cold enough to stop every thought for ninety seconds; clay again, on Tuesday evenings, my hands busy and nothing to deliver.

What I'd say to myself in March 2023: you are allowed to be the second-best person for the job, and go home.
