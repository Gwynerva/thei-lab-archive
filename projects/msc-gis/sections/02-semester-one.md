Twelve weeks, four modules, one field trip in sleet and roughly four hundred cups of tea.

### The Cairngorms

The [field trip](ref:event:cairngorms-field-trip "GNSS at Glenmore") on 15 and 16 October: we set up a base station by Loch Morlich and walked transects with receivers on poles for five hours. My fingers stopped working at about half two. Best weekend of the term!

::media[The receiver on its pole. I held one of these for five hours and can still feel it.]{file="surveying-instrument-1" layout="centered"}

### GIS Day, and a night with no sleep

[GIS Day](ref:event:gis-day-2016) in the department, with cake shaped like a globe that nobody wanted to cut. Then, on 8 December, three pieces of coursework finished between dinner and dawn ([the night itself](ref:event:coursework-night)). Never again, I said.
