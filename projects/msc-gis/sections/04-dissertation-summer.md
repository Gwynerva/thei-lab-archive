title: The dissertation summer
periods:
  - 2017-06-01..2017-08-18
---

Ten weeks in the library and the lab while everyone else seemed to be at the Fringe. The work itself is written up under [the cliffs](ref:project:whitby-erosion); this is what the summer was like around it.

June was [point clouds](ref:section:whitby-erosion/point-clouds) and a laptop that sounded like a hairdryer. On 20 June somebody took my bike from the stair, which felt personal, given where I spent my Saturdays. July was writing, rewriting and Dr Grieve's green ink. August was the Fringe through the library window, one free show a week as a bribe, and formatting tables at midnight.

I handed it in on 18 August at ten past three: four copies, comb-bound, one hole punched slightly squint. Then I went to the Meadows and lay on the grass until it got cold.

---

> Hand it in, love. It'll never be finished, it'll only be handed in.

Mum, on the phone the night before. She was right, as she usually is about things like this.
