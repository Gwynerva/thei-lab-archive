---
title: Why GIS
summary: A map in the margin of everything, and a stall at a careers fair.
---

Written in my second week, because Priya asked over dinner and I didn't have a good answer. Now I do. Why GIS? Honestly:

1. I have drawn maps in the margins of every notebook I've ever owned. My Year 9 French book has a whole island in it that doesn't exist, with a harbour, a lighthouse and a little legend in the corner. My French teacher was **not** impressed.
2. Geography at university was brilliant but I kept wanting to *measure* things, not only describe them.
3. A woman at a careers fair said people who can make maps *and* write code are rarer than you'd think. I wrote that on the back of my hand.
4. Dad has had a tide table pinned up behind the till at the shop since before I was born, and I have always wanted to know how somebody works those numbers out.

Number four isn't on any application form but it's the true one. Also, and I didn't say this to Priya: I like knowing where I am. A map is the only thing that tells you that without being asked twice.
