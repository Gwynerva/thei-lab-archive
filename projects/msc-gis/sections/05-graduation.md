periods:
  - 2017-11-29

Graduation day, with distinction, which still looks odd written down. By then I had been working at Forthline for eleven weeks and took the day off like a proper grown-up.

Mum and Dad came up on the early train, Dad in the suit he wears for funerals and the regatta dinner. The gown was hired and made for someone a foot taller; the hood slid off backwards every time I moved. We had lunch somewhere with tablecloths and Dad asked the waiter whether the fish was local, which it wasn't.

Nana phoned in the evening, because she wouldn't travel in November:

> A master of maps. Your grandad would have had you plotting his courses.

I keep that one.
