The proposal was [accepted on 20 January](ref:event:proposal-accepted), which made the dissertation suddenly real and slightly terrifying. Then the second half of the taught year, all at once:

- **Spatial programming.** More Python. I wrote a script that downloads tide gauge data and plots it, which had nothing to do with the coursework and everything to do with Dad.
- **Geovisualisation.** Colour, type, the order your eye reads a map in. My favourite module of the whole degree.
- **Environmental modelling.** Rivers, slopes, rain. Useful later, as it turned out.
- Everything else that term:
  - a leather wallet, stitched by hand at the kitchen table
  - Tuesday evenings at the bindery in Stockbridge
  - a weekend in Copenhagen with Priya
  - twenty-five, on Pi Day, with a pie
  - a week on the cliffs in April with a drone and Sam from the department

On 6 May I climbed Ben Lomond as a reward for nothing in particular. On 26 May I sat [the last exam I'll ever sit](ref:event:last-exam "I said"), walked out into sunshine and slept for eleven hours.
