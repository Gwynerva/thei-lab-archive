Mývatn means midge lake. Nobody tells you this until you get there.

They are not the biting kind, mostly; they are the kind that fly into your mouth, ears, eyes and tea in clouds so thick you can hear them. We bought head nets at the petrol station and wore them for two days and looked like beekeepers on holiday.

What was under the midges:

- **Námafjall**: steam coming out of the ground with a roar, mud boiling in pools, everything smelling of eggs, the earth orange and yellow and grey.
- **Hverfjall**: a crater you can walk round the rim of, a huge black bowl of gravel, with the lake far off.
- **Grjótagjá**: a cave with a pool of clear blue water in it, too hot to swim in now, which we sat beside in the dark and did not talk.
