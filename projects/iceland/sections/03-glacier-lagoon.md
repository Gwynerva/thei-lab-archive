The lagoon I had wanted to see since I was fourteen. It is exactly like the photograph and nothing like it. The photograph does not move, and the lagoon does: the icebergs drift towards the sea, slowly, turning over now and then with a noise like a door slamming in another house.

Some of them are white, some grey with ash, and some, the ones that have just turned over, a blue I have no word for. Priya said "gas-flame blue" and that is the best anyone has done.

A seal came up in the middle of the lagoon and looked at us for a long time.

::media[The seal, looking back]{file="jokulsarlon-3" layout="natural"}

Then we crossed the road to the beach where the ice washes up on black sand, small pieces shining like broken glass, and I picked one up and held it until my hand hurt, because it was a thousand years old and I wanted to.
