The road in the east goes in and out of every fjord, round the end of each and out along the other side, so that you drive fifty kilometres to get five kilometres further on. Priya slept. I counted fjords. I lost count.

At Seyðisfjörður the fog came down off the pass with us and stayed. The town is wooden houses in colours, a blue church at the end of a street painted in stripes, and on that day nothing else, because nothing else could be seen. We walked out along the shore into white. It was the quietest place I have ever been.
