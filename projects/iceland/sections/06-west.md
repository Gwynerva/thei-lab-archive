The longest drive of the trip, from Akureyri all the way west to the peninsula, with the radio on and Snowy making a new noise. Snæfellsnes is the whole of Iceland in small, they say: a glacier, lava, black beaches, a mountain shaped like a church hat.

At the very end of it, on black cliffs, there is an orange lighthouse. It stands there for no one, at the edge of everything, looking at the sea. I loved it more than anything else on the trip, and I have never been able to say why.

::media[The orange lighthouse on its lava]{file="svortuloft" layout="natural"}

On the last day we drove back to Reykjavík in rain, gave Snowy back with sand in every crack of her, and sat in the airport with our boots still wet. Priya said "again?" and I said "not in a car that small".
