title: Planning
summary: A spreadsheet, a map, and an argument about tents.
---

Priya made a spreadsheet. I made a map. Between them, in March, on the kitchen table in Leith, we made a plan.

- [x] Flights, Edinburgh to Keflavík, booked in a sale at one in the morning
- [x] The car, the smallest one, because the smallest one was what we could afford
- [x] Guesthouses for every night but two
- [ ] ~~Tent~~ (we argued about the tent for a week and Priya won, which is to say no tent)
- [x] Waterproofs, both halves
- [x] A paper map, because I do not trust phones in a country with that many sheep
- [ ] Puffins (too early, it turned out)

The plan was anticlockwise, south coast first, so that the long drives would come in the middle when we were used to them. It worked, mostly. We were not used to them.

What I drew on the map in red biro was the ring road and a circle at every place we would sleep. Priya added stars at every place there was a hot pool. There were a lot of stars.
