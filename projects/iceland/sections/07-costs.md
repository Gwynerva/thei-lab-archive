---
title: What it cost
summary: In pounds, honestly, with one number under a spoiler at Priya's request.
---

Everything for the two of us, split down the middle:

- **Getting there**
  - flights, Edinburgh to Keflavík and back: £360 for two
  - the bus we did not need, because the car was at the airport: £0, thank goodness
- **Snowy**, the car, ten days, insurance and all: £540
  - fuel: £230, because Iceland is bigger than it looks
  - a car wash on the last day, out of shame: £8
- **Beds**: eight guesthouses and two hostels, £720
- **Food**: mostly from supermarkets, eaten in the car, about £300
  - hot dogs: many
- **Hot pools**: £90, and worth every penny

About £1,100 each, which was most of what I had saved since starting at Forthline. I do not regret any of it.

The most expensive single thing we bought in Iceland:

:::spoiler
Two beers on the last night in Reykjavík: about twenty pounds for the pair. Priya made me photograph the receipt for evidence.
:::
