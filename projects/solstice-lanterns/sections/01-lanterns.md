Saturday the 10th. Rain: day 6. Paper, the bone folder from the bindery (still going), a ruler, glue, the kitchen table.

How each one goes:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. Score a sheet of tracing paper into a grid with the bone folder, sixteen squares by eight.
2. Fold every line both ways, mountain and valley, until the paper goes soft.
3. Collapse it along the diagonals into a tube that folds in on itself like a concertina.
4. Glue the ends into a ring, add a wire handle, and put a light inside.
:::

Six white, six red. The red ones are mulberry paper from a shop on Strandgaten that sells paper the way other shops sell wine, with a great deal of talk about where it comes from.

The 21st: up the hill at three with the lanterns in two big shopping bags, lit them at a quarter past, hung them in the birches at the viewpoint. Bells somewhere down in the city at four.
