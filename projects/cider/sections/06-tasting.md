---
title: Tasting notes
summary: What the first year tasted like, written honestly, with Sigrid's corrections.
---

We bottled the 2025 on the seventeenth of January, nine carboys into two hundred and eighty-one bottles collected since October from everyone we know, and at five Sigrid came to taste. She held the glass up to the window, smelled it, drank, and said *Tørr. God.* Dry. Good. It was very pale, a little cloudy, and it tasted of the orchard in October. I am not objective.

Notes from that winter, bottle by bottle:

:::list{counter="upper-alpha"}
1. **Gravenstein and Aroma**, the main blend: dry, bright, a sharp edge, apples and something like hay. Sigrid: "The Aroma was right." (The Aroma was my idea.)
2. **The two nameless trees**, a little of them in everything: the reason it is not boring. Sigrid: "Hm."
3. **The last carboy**, the one with Sigrid's Discovery in it, bottled a little early: sweeter, fizzy, pink-gold, dangerous. Two of them went off like corks from a gun in the pantry in March.
:::

At the wedding in August we poured the last forty bottles, and people asked for the recipe as if there were one. There is not. There are the trees, the year, and Sigrid.
