---
title: Blossom, 2025
periods:
  - 2025-05-12
---

It came all at once, the way Sigrid said it would: sticks with green tips on the Saturday, and on the Monday morning the whole slope white and pink, with the fjord turquoise under it and Folgefonna white above. I stood on the porch with my coffee going cold and did not get further than the third tree for an hour. In the afternoon Sigrid walked the rows with me and gave every tree its name, except two.

The worry, she told me, is frost. One clear night in blossom time and the year is gone. For a week I checked the forecast at bedtime and again at three in the morning, and the frost did not come.

The bees came from somebody's hives along the road. I have no idea whose. I owe them a bottle.
