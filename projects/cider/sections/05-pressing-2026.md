title: Pressing, 2026
periods:
  - 2026-10-03

The second year, and the trees knew it. After a winter of proper pruning, Sigrid's way, they gave us more than we could pick: the Gravensteins bent to the grass, and we propped the oldest with forked sticks like something in a painting.

This time we knew what to do, more or less. The Nesheims' press came up the track again on the third of October, Anna and her husband came on the early ferry again, and Jonas took the screw again. This time we had the crates ready, the bath filled, the cloths washed, and I had a clipboard, and wrote down every crate and every carboy as it filled, not before: 257 litres in eleven carboys, and nothing on the floor.

The two nameless trees at the barn's north corner gave more than they ever have, as if to make a point. We pressed them on their own into the eleventh carboy, to see what they are when nothing else is in the way. Ask me in January.
