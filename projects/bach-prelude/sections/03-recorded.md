A Saturday afternoon in lockdown, the bedroom window shut for once, Marta out on her walk, Biscuit shut in the kitchen with a bribe. The phone propped on three atlases and a book about tides on the chest of drawers.

Fourteen takes. The first six I stopped myself. The seventh was good until the pedal point and fell apart. The eleventh was the one, except for a squeak in bar thirty that I decided was character. I kept the eleventh.

::audio[The prelude, June 2020, take eleven]{file="music-bach-cello-1" title="The prelude"}

I sent it to Mum, who played it to Nana on her phone in the garden at Robin Hood's Bay, through the window, because nobody was going indoors that summer. Mum said Nana listened to the end with her eyes shut and then said:

> It sounds like the sea going out.

I did not know then that it was the last summer. I am glad she heard it.
