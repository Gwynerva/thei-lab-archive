---
title: The kit
summary: What lives in the red bag, checked every spring
---

It lives in a red pouch at the bottom of my rucksack, and every spring, on the first warm Saturday, I take everything out, check it against this list and throw away whatever has gone soft, sticky or out of date. Then I put it all back in the same order, because in a cold wind with numb fingers you want to find things without looking.

- [x] a survival bag, the big orange plastic kind, and a foil blanket
- [x] a roll of wide tape, which fixes ankles, blisters, tents and boots
- [x] a folding splint, light as a ruler
- [x] two big wound dressings, a crepe bandage and a triangular one
- [x] blister plasters, many, because blisters are most of it
- [x] gloves, two pairs, and a mask for rescue breaths
- [x] tweezers, scissors, a safety pin
- [x] painkillers and antihistamines, labelled
- [x] a pencil and a waterproof card, to write down what happened and when
- [ ] a whistle that works (the old one has a pea that rattles and no sound)

The pencil and card were the Major's idea, and the best one. When help comes, he said, the first thing they will want is what happened and when, and by then you will not remember.

The longer list, of everything else in the rucksack, is on [the packing list for the hills](ref:page:hill-packing-list).
