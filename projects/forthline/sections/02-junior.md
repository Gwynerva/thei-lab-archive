[First day!](ref:event:forthline-first-day) A desk by the window, a laptop with two screens, a mug with a sticker reading JUNIOR that Dougie said was a joke and wasn't.

What a junior does, it turns out:

- digitises drains from scanned plans, by the thousand
- cleans elevation data: removes bridges that the model thinks are dams, fills holes where the lidar saw a lake
- colours in, and gets told the colours are wrong
- makes the tea, the same as everyone else, which I liked

By Christmas I was trusted with a whole catchment of my own, a small one in Fife with one stubborn underpass. In the evenings I started writing a tide library because the work made me want to model water I actually cared about. That turned into [tideline](ref:project:tideline), and by the spring of 2018 I was spending more time on it than was strictly sensible.

Graduation fell in the middle of all this. I took the day off and came back the next morning to find my mug had a new sticker: MASTER JUNIOR.
