title: The company
---

Twelve people, two rooms, one meeting table that was also the lunch table and, on Fridays, the table tennis table. The office was above a shop that sold marine radios, so every so often the floor hummed with a test broadcast from below.

Forthline did three things:

1. surface-water flood maps for councils, the core of it
2. mapping for engineers working on drainage schemes, which paid the rent
3. one-off jobs nobody else wanted, like the survey of every bench in a cemetery, which I did twice

Dougie ran it the way he ran his allotment: carefully, cheerfully, with a lot of talking to things. Nobody was ever shouted at. Things that went wrong were written on the whiteboard under "Lessons", and the whiteboard was never wiped, only added to in smaller and smaller writing.
