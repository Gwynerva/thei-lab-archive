For my own record, because I always wonder later and never remember.

- **September 2017**, junior: £24,000. My first payslip came in an actual envelope, and I photographed it like a certificate.
- **September 2018**: £26,500, after the first flood map.
- **May 2019**, developer: £31,500.
- **April 2021**: £34,000, which I earned for four months.

Rent on my third of the flat in Leith was £410 a month in 2017 and £465 by the end. I saved a little every month from 2019, mostly by not buying lunch, and that was what paid for the move.

Nordvåg's offer was more than half again on paper. In kroner, in Bergen, with Bergen's prices, it turned out to be about the same, plus rain.
