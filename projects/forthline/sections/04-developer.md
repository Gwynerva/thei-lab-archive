### Promoted

On 1 May 2019 Dougie called me into the small room and said I was a developer now, [apparently](ref:event:promoted). It meant writing the code the others used rather than the maps themselves. I wrote the pipeline that took a model run and produced finished, labelled map sheets overnight, so nobody had to spend Friday afternoons exporting PDFs one at a time.

### Amsterdam

In September I talked about it at [a conference in Amsterdam](ref:event:amsterdam-conference): twenty minutes on automating flood maps, to a room of people who knew more about floods than I ever will. My hands shook for the first five minutes. Somebody from a Dutch water board asked a question I couldn't answer, then answered it himself, kindly.

### At home

On 17 March 2020 we carried our screens home on the bus. The pipeline ran on in an empty office for fourteen months. I worked from the kitchen table, then the bedroom, then the kitchen table again. Ines went home to Porto that summer, and the desk opposite mine, which I couldn't see anyway, felt emptier.

### Leaving

The offer from Bergen came in March 2021. Telling Dougie was the worst conversation of that year apart from the ones about Nana. My [farewell](ref:event:forthline-farewell) was on 16 July, outdoors, on the Shore, with a cake.
