---
title: What I learned
---

Things I learned at Forthline and still use, in rough order of how often:

1. Name things properly. A variable called *depth in metres* is worth an hour of comments.
2. A map is for someone. Decide who before you open the software.
3. Write the README first. If you can't explain what it does, it doesn't do it yet.
4. Ask the person who has been there longest. They know where the underpass is.
5. Go home at six. Dougie went home at half five and the company did fine.

The fifth one I learned and then forgot, in another country, and had to learn again the hard way.

:::private
Dougie offered me more money to stay, the week I told him about Bergen. I said no before he'd finished the sentence and then cried in the stairwell for ten minutes, because I wanted to say yes and also didn't, and because he had clearly worked out the figure in advance on the back of an envelope.
:::
