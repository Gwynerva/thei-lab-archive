In February 2021, in the middle of the second lockdown, I decided to leave Scotland. In August I did. In between there was an interview on a laptop, a permit, a cat with more paperwork than me, forty-three boxes on a ship, and ten days alone in a room on Nordnes looking at a slice of fjord.

I am writing this in the autumn, in the flat in Sandviken, with the rain on the window (rain: day 6 of this week) and Biscuit asleep on the radiator shelf, which he has decided is his. I want to put it down while I still remember what it was like not to know any of this.

::media[Bryggen, end to end, from the harbour on my first free afternoon]{file="bryggen-1" layout="stretch"}

## Why

Partly the job: [Nordvåg Kart](ref:project:nordvag "charts of small harbours"), which makes nautical charts of small harbours on the west coast, wanted someone who could read an old survey and draw a new one, and that is the most exactly-me job advert I have ever read. Partly the sea. Partly that I had been in the same flat for nearly five years and in the same three miles of Leith for a whole year, walking them every day.

:::private
And partly Nana. She died in November and something in me came loose. I do not think I would have gone if she were still at the end of the phone on Sunday evenings. I do not know whether that is a good reason or a bad one. Mum said she would have packed my bag for me.
:::

## What it is like

Bergen smells of the sea and wet wood and, near the fish market, of fish. The houses are wooden and white or ochre or red, and they creak. People say *hei* to everybody and nothing else to anybody, until the second or third time, when suddenly they ask you to dinner. It rains, and they do not mention it, and neither, now, do I, except here: by my count it has rained on thirty-one of the forty-six days since I landed.

I have checked [the forecast](https://www.met.no "the Norwegian one, which is right more often than I am") every morning since the first. It says rain. I go out anyway. This is apparently what makes you a *bergenser*.

The list of what I miss is [its own page](ref:section:moving-to-bergen/what-i-miss), and it is shorter than I expected.
