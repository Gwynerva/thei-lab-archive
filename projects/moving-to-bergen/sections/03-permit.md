The list, as it lived on the fridge:

- [x] The application online, in English, which took three evenings and a lot of tea
- [x] The contract, signed and scanned
- [x] My degree certificate, from a box under the bed, certified by a solicitor who charged me for the privilege
- [x] Proof of somewhere to live: a letter from Anna about a studio flat on Nordnes
- [x] Fingerprints and a photograph, at an office in Edinburgh, on a morning of hail
- [x] Wait
- [x] Wait some more

I applied some time in April. I cannot find the date; the confirmation email went to a folder I have never looked in since. The approval came on the eighth of June, by email, which I read on the bus, and missed my stop.

*Oppholdstillatelse.* Residence permit. Literally something like "permission to stay up", which is what you need for the first winter, I am told. I can say it now in three goes.
