Ten days of *karantene*, in a studio flat on Nordnes that Anna found for me: one room, one window, one kettle, and between two white houses a slice of Puddefjorden about the width of my hand held out at arm's length.

Day 1. Islands from the plane, hundreds of them. Light at half past ten at night.\
Day 2. Rain. Anna left shopping on the step and texted *Levert!*\
Day 3. Sun, for an hour. Stood at the window like a cat.\
Day 4. Learned the word for every vegetable from the receipts. *Agurk* is a cucumber. I refuse.\
Day 5. Read the Nordvåg style guide. Twice.\
Day 6. Rain. Marta sent a link to a short cartoon about a secret agent and I watched it three times, laughing alone in a room in a country where I knew one person.

::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mN0zPOpADL4"}

Day 7. Knee exercises with my feet against the radiator.\
Day 8. Counted boats. A boat goes out at eight and comes back at four.\
Day 9. The man with the very small dog waved. I waved.\
Day 10. In the evening the cloud lifted off the hills across the water and everything went gold, and I said out loud, to nobody: all right, Bergen. Tomorrow.
