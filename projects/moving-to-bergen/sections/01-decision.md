February 2021. Lockdown again. The flat, the laptop, the one walk a day that was no longer a rule but still a habit. Biscuit, Marta, the cello, the quilt, Norwegian on a screen three evenings a week, which I had started in January for no reason I could have explained.

An advert came round on a mailing list for people who make maps: a small company in Bergen, harbour charts, an old survey to redraw as part of the application. I read it about forty times. I did the task in one weekend without telling anyone.

Then one Sunday in the middle of the month I walked along the prom at Portobello in the sleet, the sea brown and the sky the same, the place where I had swum in August, and somewhere between the pillars and the end of the prom I decided. Not to apply: I had applied. To go. To stop waiting for things to go back to how they were and go and find another how.

I came home wet through and told Marta, who said "I know," and put the kettle on. She had known before I did. She usually does.
