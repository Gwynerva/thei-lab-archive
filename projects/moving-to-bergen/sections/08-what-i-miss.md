---
title: What I miss
summary: A list, shorter than I expected.
---

Written in the first November, on a wet Sunday, and added to since:

- Marta, coming out of her room with the last of the wine
- the Shore at night
- Mum's voice in the same time zone
- proper tea, which I have since found, in a shop by the fish market, at a price I will not write down
- Hamish, who is thriving in Leith without me, the traitor
- beans on toast, which is apparently not lunch (lunch is [*pålegg*](<ref:page:norwegian-words#h:Food> "anything you put on bread"))
- the orchestra, until I find another one
- the Pentlands, though Bergen has seven of its own and they are steeper
- ~~the sun~~ (Edinburgh did not have that much of it either, to be fair)

What I thought I would miss and do not:

- the noise
- the knee's old route, along the river
- being from somewhere everybody had heard of

What I did not know I would miss: the smell of the brewery on a wet evening in Leith, and being understood the first time, every time, without thinking about it.
