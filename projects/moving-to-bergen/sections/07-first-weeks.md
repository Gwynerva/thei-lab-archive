The flat is the top floor of a white wooden house in Sandviken, up a lane so steep the removal men called it *bratt* with real feeling. From the kitchen window I can see the harbour and the mast on Rundemanen. Downstairs lives Mr Haldorsen, a retired ship's mate with a brass barometer, who has lived here since before I was born and who knocks to tell me when the weather is about to do something.

The first weeks, in order:

- **The eleventh.** Out of quarantine and into the flat: a bed, a table, two chairs, no cat.
- **The thirteenth.** The cat.
- **The sixteenth.** [The first day at Nordvåg](ref:event:nordvag-first-day): a desk by a window over Vågen, lunch at half eleven, a harbour from the 1930s to bring into this century.
- **The twentieth.** The boxes, up the lane. The lopsided mug survived again.
- **The twenty-second.** [Fløyen](ref:section:seven-mountains/floyen), on foot, the first of seven.
- **The eighteenth of September.** Vidden, in a cloud.
- **The twenty-fifth.** Norwegian in a real room, with eleven people from nine countries.

On the last Saturday of September the whole house had its :hint[*dugnad*]{note="a day of shared work: everybody in the house turns out to clean, mend and tidy the common parts, and then there are waffles"}. I swept the stairs, Mr Haldorsen oiled the front door, and the family from the ground floor made waffles. Nobody told me the rules. I watched and copied, which is how I learn most things here.
