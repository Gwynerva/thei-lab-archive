title: Goodbye, Leith

The removal men came on the Thursday and took forty-three boxes, the cello, the hall bookshelf in pieces and the framed map from Forthline, and the flat became an echo with a kettle in it.

On the Friday I gave Hamish to Marta, in his jar, with instructions written out on an index card, and then I went for the last walk: down Leith Walk, along the Shore, over the bridge, back along the river past the bench where I had sat through most of 2020. The Shore was full of people drinking outside on a warm night. I stood by the water for a long time and did not want to go home, because home was the echo.

At half past five on Saturday morning the taxi came. I posted the keys through the letterbox. Marta stood at the stair door in her dressing gown and waved until we turned the corner.

From the plane: the Forth, the three bridges, the whole of Fife, and then cloud.
