The interview was on a video call from the box room, with the bed folded up and the Munro map behind me so I would look like someone who goes outside. Three people. One of them, who laughed in the right places, was Anna, who would be my team lead. I talked about a rock on an old chart that I thought had moved, and then about tides, for too long. Nobody stopped me.

Then fifteen days of not checking my email every ten minutes, which is to say checking it every eleven.

The offer came on the nineteenth, at ten past four, in Norwegian, in the middle of a flood model for a council in Fife. I read it with the dictionary open to make sure *tilby* means offer. It does.
