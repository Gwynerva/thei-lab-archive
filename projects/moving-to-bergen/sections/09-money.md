What the move cost, roughly, in pounds, because in 2021 I still thought in pounds:

1. Biscuit: vet, papers, crate, cattery, cargo flight. About £1,400. More than my own flight by a factor of ten.
2. The removal firm and the ship: £2,100 for forty-three boxes and a cello.
3. The permit, the certified copies, the solicitor: £450.
4. Ten days in the studio on Nordnes: £600, and worth it for the window.
5. The deposit on the flat in Sandviken: three months' rent, which made me feel sick.

Nordvåg paid part of the move, which I had to ask about twice before I signed, and which I am glad I asked about. My savings from four years at Forthline went down to about a month's rent, and stayed there until Christmas.

Nobody tells you that moving country is mostly money and forms. The sea and the mountains are true as well. They just come after.
