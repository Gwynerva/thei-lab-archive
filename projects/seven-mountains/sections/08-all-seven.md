Two days after my last day at Nordvåg, Jonas and I left the house at five in the morning with two litres of water each, a bag of *skillingsboller* and a map I had drawn the week before, and walked all seven.

The order we did them in, for anyone who wants it:

1. Lyderhorn, at half past six, mist below us and the islands floating in it
2. Damsgårdsfjellet, along its ridge
3. Løvstakken, steep, the city below
4. Ulriken, up the stone stairs, at eleven
5. Rundemanen, across the whole of Vidden, which took three hours and went on and on
6. Fløyen, a little way down, for waffles
7. Sandviksfjellet, at seven in the evening, the smallest, which nearly finished me

Thirty-four kilometres, about 2,300 metres of climbing by my map, fourteen hours door to door. Thirty-four kilometres; thirty-four Munros. I noticed that on Vidden and laughed out loud and could not explain it to Jonas.

Somewhere on Vidden I stopped thinking about the last year. Not in a grand way. My legs hurt and my feet were wet and there was no room left in me for anything else, and when there was room again, some of the heavy things did not come back into it.

Five days later I flew to Porto to start [another long walk](ref:project:camino). The event, with the gallery, is [Seven mountains in one day](ref:event:seven-in-a-day "2 June 2024").
