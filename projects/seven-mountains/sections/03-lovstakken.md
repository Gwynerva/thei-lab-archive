The third, and the first on my own. Løvstakken is the steep one across the valley from the centre, a dark wooded hump with a bare top, and on a hazy October Saturday I went up it from the south side, through the trees, an hour and a bit.

It is steeper than it looks, which is saying something. Roots for stairs, then rock for stairs, then the top: a tall cairn with a weather vane on it that did not turn at all in the still air, and the whole city laid out to the north in the haze like a model of itself, Ulriken's mast on one side and the sea on the other. The leaves had turned. The birches on the slopes below were yellow, the rowans red, and I thought of Nana's tree at the Bay, and sat behind the cairn out of the wind for a long time.

::media[The cairn on Løvstakken, with its vane]{file="lovstakken-2" layout="centered"}

On the way down I met an old man coming up with a rucksack and two walking poles who said *hei* and then, in English, "Too fast!" I was going too fast. I slowed down. I have been trying to ever since.
