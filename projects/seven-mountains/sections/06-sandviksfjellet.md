The sixth, and the one I had been looking at every morning for a year: Sandviksfjellet is the mountain directly behind our street, and its path starts about two hundred metres from my door.

I went up on a Saturday evening in August, alone, on purpose, because it felt like it should be mine. The path goes up through the trees behind the houses and then onto open rock, and in forty minutes you are looking straight down on your own roof. I could see the white house. I could see my kitchen window, and a ginger shape in it, waiting for his dinner.

Six. I wrote it on the map on the kitchen wall when I got in, and Biscuit got his dinner late, and let me know.
