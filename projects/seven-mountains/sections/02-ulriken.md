Ulriken is the highest of the seven, 643 metres, and the way most people do it is the way I did: up in the cable car, then across Vidden, the high bare plateau between Ulriken and Fløyen, five hours over rock and bog and little lakes, following cairns and red T's painted on the stones.

I went with a group from work who go every September and said the cloud was normal. It was inside a cloud all day. What I saw: wet rock, cairns appearing out of grey one at a time like the top of Ben Nevis, a sheep with a low opinion of us, my own boots. Twice the cloud tore open for half a minute and there were dark lakes below us, and then it closed.

Anna has done Vidden fourteen times. She lent me a hat. I have not given it back.
