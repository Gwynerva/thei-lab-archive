The seventh, the one with the big mast on top, which I had looked at from the kitchen window every day for fourteen months. Of course it sleeted: *sludd*, horizontal, straight off the plateau into our faces, while Jonas walked ahead saying "nearly there" cheerfully for an hour.

At the top I put my hand on the cairn and said "seven" out loud, the way I said "thirty-four" on Ben Nevis, and nobody heard me over the wind and the mast humming above us like a tuning fork.

Seven, one at a time, in fourteen months. Jonas said I should do them all in one day. I said no. Jonas says many things, and is right about a worrying number of them.
