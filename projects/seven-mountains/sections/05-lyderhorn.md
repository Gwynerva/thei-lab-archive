The fifth, out on the west side of the city, a bare rocky top above the islands where the witches of Bergen are supposed to have met on midsummer night. It was twenty-four degrees, which made the whole city slightly hysterical. No witches. Heather, a family with a dog, and the sea in every shade of blue there is.

Jonas knew a little rocky bay on the way down, and we swam. Fourteen degrees. I lasted three minutes and screamed for two of them, and he floated on his back and laughed at me, and I remember thinking, in the water, with my teeth going: oh, I am in trouble.
