The first and the easiest, right behind the city. The funicular goes up every quarter of an hour. I walked, because Mr Haldorsen said a proper *bergenser* walks, and because I am stubborn.

The path zigzags up through the trees with every bend numbered, and every few bends the funicular slides past through the leaves, full of people looking down at me with pity. Forty minutes, sweating. Then the whole city at once: the harbour, Nordnes, the islands, the sea going on to the edge of everything, and somewhere in it my own roof, and the office.

I watched a shower come in from the sea in a grey sheet, go round the city like a dog round a park, and leave. Then waffles with brown cheese at the top, which I am told is the law.
