Day 41. Walk 40: up Leith Walk, along to Pilrig, back by the Shore.

There's a skip on one of the side streets, a flat being cleared, and sticking out of it the back wheel of a red road bike. I stood there for ages like a lunatic. Then I asked, from two metres away, two men in masks carrying out a wardrobe, whether I could have the bike. One of them said, "Take it, hen, it's going to the tip."

So I walked it home. Well: carried it, because neither wheel turns. Got some looks.

It's a Raleigh. It's red, or it was. It's on the balcony now, and Marta has asked, politely, how long it's staying.
