A checklist, kept on the back of an envelope stuck to the fridge:

- [x] tyres, 700 by 25, black with a tan wall, because it's her
- [x] inner tubes
- [x] brake and gear cables, and new housings
- [x] brake pads (the old ones were like stone)
- [x] chain
- [x] bar tape, cork, in brown
- [x] bearings
  - [x] bottom bracket, a sealed cartridge (cheating, but I don't care)
  - [x] headset, loose balls, the right size, in a little bag
- [ ] the original pump, which clipped under the top tube and is lost to history

Kept: the frame, the fork, the wheels (trued again by me, slowly), the gear levers, the brakes themselves, and the saddle, which is cracked leather and comfortable in the way an old armchair is.
