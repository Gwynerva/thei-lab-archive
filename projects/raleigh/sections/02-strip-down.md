---
title: Strip-down
periods:
  - 2020-05-09..2020-05-31
---

Three weekends and most evenings, taking her apart on the balcony on newspaper, every part going into a labelled jam jar.

- wheels off, tyres off, tubes in the bin (they crumbled)
- brakes off; the cables had rusted solid inside their housings
- chain off and into a jar of degreaser, where it turned the liquid black in a minute
- cranks off, with a crank puller from the Spoke Room's emergency box, left on the doorstep by a very kind volunteer on a bike
- bottom bracket out, which took two evenings, a lot of penetrating oil and some language
- headset out, bearings counted (one missing; it's somewhere on the balcony, or in Biscuit)

By the end of the month she was a bare frame hanging from a hook in the ceiling, and a row of jars along the balcony wall like a chemist's shop.
