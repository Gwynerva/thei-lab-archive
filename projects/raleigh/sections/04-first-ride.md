title: The first ride
periods:
  - 2020-07-18
---

Eleven weeks after the skip. Pumped the new tyres up hard, checked every bolt twice, put on a helmet I'd bought in a panic the day before, and rode her down the stair. Well: carried her down the stair and rode her out of the street door.

Then along the Water of Leith path, from the Shore all the way out past Slateford to Balerno, and back. The gears clunk into place with that old-fashioned shove of the lever. The brakes squeal at the bottom of the hill by Dean Village, which they'll keep doing until I'm brave enough to toe them in.

I'd forgotten what it's like. The air going past. The river going the other way. The fact that you can go *anywhere*, after four months of going one mile from the door and back.

---

Three good things:

1. the wind
2. the squeal of the brakes, actually
3. an ice cream in Balerno from a van, eaten sitting on the kerb with the bike leaning on my shoulder
