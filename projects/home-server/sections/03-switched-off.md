Switched it off this morning, a Saturday, three weeks after we saw the house above the fjord.

- [x] last backup, checked, twice
- [x] copied the disk to a second disk, put in a drawer, for no reason I can defend
- [x] every service moved, every address pointed somewhere else
- [x] ran the shutdown by hand and watched the light go out

It took twenty seconds. The cupboard door went cold over the afternoon.

Five and a half years, from the Munro map to the end of my job in Bergen. I'd thought I would feel sad about it, and I did, a little, the way you feel when a good shop closes. Mostly I felt lighter. There is a house coming with fourteen windows and a roof that leaks, and I wanted to stop being on call for a box in a cupboard.

Jonas asked if I wanted to keep it. I said yes, the case, for the barn. He found a crate and wrote on it.
