The list, kept in a text file on the server itself, as it stood at the end:

1. ~~the Munro map~~, moved to a small paid host in 2021 when I left Scotland
2. ~~tideline's documentation~~, switched off in 2023 with the library
3. ~~the weather station's database~~, read-only since October 2022
4. backups of the diary scans and the photos, every night at three
5. the flat's shared calendar, used by one person in each flat
6. the music, all of it

By November 2024 only numbers four to six were still alive, and all three had somewhere better to go. The backups went to an encrypted service and a disk at Mum's in Whitby. The calendar went wherever calendars go. The music went onto a small player that sits by the stove now and plays the hymn Nana liked best every time somebody leans on it by accident.

<s>Total uptime: 1,987 days, give or take the power cut.</s> Actually 1,936. I'd forgotten the move.
