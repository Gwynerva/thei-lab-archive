---
title: The box
---

What Saltwick was, as far as it was anything:

- **The case:** a small black office computer, ex-council, with an asset sticker I never peeled off
  - one fan, quiet, until 2022, then less quiet
  - a power light I covered with a square of tape because it lit up the whole room
- **Inside:** a low-power processor from 2016 and 8 GB of memory, which was more than it ever needed
- **The disks:**
  - one small fast one for the system
  - one big slow one for everything else, replaced twice, the second time in a hurry
- **The name:** after the bay, because it kept the cliff data and because I wanted it to be somewhere

It lived under the desk in Leith, then in the hall cupboard in Sandviken, on top of the router, where Jonas said it looked like a small dog guarding a smaller dog. It made almost no noise. You knew it was there only by the faint warmth of the cupboard door.
