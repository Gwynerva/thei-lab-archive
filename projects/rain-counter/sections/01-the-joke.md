---
title: The joke
summary: Three days of rain, one colleague, one bet.
---

Bergen has a reputation for rain the way Edinburgh has one for wind: everyone who lives there is a little proud of it and pretends not to be.

In my first year I'd counted rain days in the diary out of habit ("Rain: day 12."), the way Nana counted stitches. In October 2022 a colleague at Nordvåg made the mistake of saying, on a Wednesday, that it would stop raining on Friday, and I made the mistake of saying "want to bet?"

:::quote[What everyone in Bergen says, sooner or later]{align="center"}
*Det finnes ikke dårlig vær, bare dårlige klær.*\
There is no bad weather, only bad clothes.
:::

My Norwegian teacher taught us that sentence in week two, as grammar. Bergen teaches it as a threat. I had good clothes. I also had an evening, a rain gauge on the balcony and an argument to win.

It didn't stop on Friday.
