The log I kept in the back of my work notebook, one line most mornings:

Rain: day 3. App written in the evening. One page, one number.\
Rain: day 4. On the kitchen screen. Someone has drawn a duck on it.\
Rain: day 6. Recorded the first rain for the sound map on the roof of the flat.\
Rain: day 12. In the paper. Not the app, the rain.\
Rain: day 14. Sunday. Didn't go out. Listened to it instead.

::audio[Day fourteen, against the kitchen window in Sandviken]{file="sound-rain-1" title="Rain, day 14"}

Rain: day 17. The background is now navy. I may have to add a darker blue.\
Rain: day 21. Until midnight. Went out at ten without a coat and stood in it, to prove something.\
Rain: 21 days & counting (!). I typed that myself, proud.\
Rain: 21 days & counting (!), still, in the sun. I didn't notice for eleven days.

::media[What day nine looked like on the puddle outside the office, over and over]{file="video-rain-3" layout="natural"}

By 30 October I'd added a history page and a record line, and the counter looked after itself. Mostly it said small numbers. It didn't need to say big ones any more; everyone remembered.
