From the export, October 2022 to March 2023:

:::list{counter="lower-roman"}
1. the record streak: 30 days, 1 to 30 January
2. second longest: 21 days, 3 to 23 October
3. days with rain, the whole six months: 142 of 182
4. the longest dry spell: 6 days, in February, frozen solid
:::

:::spoiler
The total for the six months was 1,412 millimetres. Leith gets about 640 in a whole year. I worked that out on a Sunday and laughed until Biscuit left the room.
:::

On one of those Sundays, day eight of the January streak, Jonas came round with waffles and a film about a girl and a dragon, and we watched it on the sofa with the rain so loud we turned it up twice.

::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eRsGyueVLvQ"}

And then the part I wrote in March 2023, when I switched it off:

the number was the first thing I looked at every morning.\
a 9 made the day heavy before it started.\
it was never the rain. it stopped being a joke in February and I didn't say.
