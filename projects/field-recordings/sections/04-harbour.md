the harbour in spring. I went down early, before work, when there was still something left in me in the mornings.

gulls at the fish market at six, when the boats come in. They do not call. They argue.

::audio[Gulls over the market, six in the morning]{file="sound-gulls-1" title="Gulls"}

the coastal steamer leaving at eight in the evening, three long blasts that you feel in your chest from the far side of Vågen. A foghorn somewhere out on the fjord one grey May morning, slow and patient, like someone very old clearing their throat.

ropes creaking on the old boats at Bryggen. Fenders. A man whistling on a deck.

::audio[The foghorn, out on the fjord in the fog]{file="sound-foghorn-2" title="Foghorn"}

that was June. Then I stopped. I did not decide to. The recorder stayed in the coat, and the coat stayed on its hook, and the batteries went flat in the drawer.
