The week before it started, I bought the recorder. Rain: day six, the day I first used it, the recorder on the windowsill under the skylight and me lying on the floor listening through the headphones, which is a thing I can recommend to anyone.

Bergen has more words for rain than I have umbrellas. :hint[*Yr*]{note="the fine drizzle that hangs in the air and gets in everywhere"}, the fine one that hangs in the air. *Pøsregn*, the bucketing kind. *Sludd*, sleet. And just *regn*, which here is less a weather than a state of being.

What I recorded, that autumn:

1. the skylight, the first night
2. the bike shed's tin roof, which sounds like applause
3. the gutter outside the bakery, running like a mountain stream
4. a bus shelter on Sandviksveien, rain on the roof and a bus going by in a long hiss
5. the leaves of the rowan in the back yard, which made me think of Nana's garden
6. the fjord at Nordnes, rain on the sea, which sounds like frying

::audio[The rowan leaves in the back yard, November]{file="sound-rain-2" title="Rain on the leaves"}

[The twenty-one days](ref:event:rain-streak#:~:text=rain) ended on the twenty-third of October. The rain did not, not really. By Christmas I had forty recordings of it and could tell, with my eyes shut, which part of Sandviken each one came from.
