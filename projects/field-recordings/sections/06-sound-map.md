---
title: A map of sounds
summary: A dot for every place I stood with the recorder, and what it heard
---

Because I am a cartographer and cannot keep anything without putting it on a map.

Every recording has a dot: where I stood, which way I faced, the date, the weather that hour and a line of what you can hear. Click a dot and it plays. The dots are coloured by season, rain in grey, bells in gold, the harbour in blue and the birds in green, and if you zoom out far enough, you can see the shape of my two years in Bergen: thick round Sandviken, a string down the harbour, a scatter over the hills behind the house, and one green cluster far out on Herdla.

I built it in a weekend in July 2024 out of the bones of the Munro map, which was itself built in a winter in 2018 and has aged like milk. The sounds sat on the little server in the cupboard until I switched it off in November 2024; now they live with the rest of this site. The places are in a file anyone can open.
