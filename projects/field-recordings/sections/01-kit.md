---
title: The kit
summary: Everything I carried, which fitted in one coat pocket and one plastic bag
---

Not much, on purpose. A recorder you have to unpack is a recorder you do not use.

- **The recorder**: a small handheld one with two microphones crossed on top, which is called an X/Y pair and means the sound comes out with a left and a right.
  - Batteries: two in it, four in the pocket, because the cold eats them.
  - A memory card that holds about thirty hours, which I never filled.
- **The windshield**, a grey furry sock that goes over the microphones, which everyone calls *the dead cat*. Biscuit hissed at it for a year.
- **Headphones**, the closed kind that go right over the ears, so you hear what the recorder hears and not what you hear.
- **A tiny tripod** with bendy legs, for railings, fences and gravestones.
- **A plastic freezer bag** with a hole cut for the microphones, for the rain. It works. Mostly.
- **A notebook**, for where, when, which way the wind was, and what I thought I had recorded, which was often not what I had.
