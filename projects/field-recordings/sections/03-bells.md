sundays. Eleven o'clock. You can hear four churches from the steps above the flat if the wind is in the north, and they never quite agree on when eleven is.

january, the cathedral, from the corner of the square, my fingers too cold to work the buttons.

february, all four at once from the hill behind the house, the recorder on a gravestone in the old cemetery, me sitting on the wall beside it with my hood up for twelve minutes, waiting for them to stop. They did not stop together. The last one went on alone for nearly a minute, as if it had more to say.

::audio[Sunday morning, the bells over the town]{file="sound-bells-1" title="Sunday bells"}

march, a small one at seven in the morning, struck by hand, I think. I recorded it from bed with the window open. That was the last one of the winter. After that I did not go out much on Sundays.
