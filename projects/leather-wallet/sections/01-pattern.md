title: The pattern
---

Drawn on graph paper on a Sunday, then cut out of a cereal box to check it folded. It didn't, the first time.

1. Outer: one piece, 22 cm by 9 cm, folded in the middle.
2. Card pockets: four pieces, 10 cm by 6 cm, stepped so the cards show.
3. Coin pocket: one piece with a gusset, which is a word I learned that day.
4. Stitching line: 4 mm in from every edge, holes 4 mm apart, marked with a fork.

That last one is true. The proper tool is a pricking iron and I didn't have one, so I walked the stitch line with a dinner fork and pushed the holes through with an awl. It took all evening and Callum asked if I was "doing some kind of ritual".

The gusset turned out to be the mistake. It makes the coin pocket fat, the fat pocket makes the wallet bulge, and the bulge is where it wears. Next time: no gusset, and a popper.
