A second go, on the kitchen windowsill and the front step of the white house, with herbs from the market and one tomato plant in a pot that I carried home on the bus from a garden centre in Åsane like a baby.

What Bergen taught me about growing things:

1. It rains. Don't water anything, ever.
2. Mint will survive the end of the world.
3. The sun comes round the mountain at eleven and leaves at four, and the plants know it to the minute.

Rain: fifteen days of thirty-one in May. The tomato plant gave me three tomatoes, in September, small and green. I ripened them on the windowsill in a paper bag with a banana, which Jonas said was an old wives' tale, and which worked.

The gardens up the hill in Sandviken are full of *ripsbusker*, redcurrant bushes, every one heavy with fruit in July. Next year I'll plant one. Next year I'll have a garden. (I always say that.)
