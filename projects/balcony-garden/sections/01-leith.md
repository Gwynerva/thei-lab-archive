Day 13. Sowing on the balcony! Tomatoes (Tigerella and Gardener's Delight), basil, radishes and lettuce, in yoghurt pots and egg boxes along the windowsill, because it's still too cold out there for anything to live. The seeds came from the swap a woman on the ground floor started on the stair: an envelope on the landing, take what you need, leave what you can. I left a packet of nasturtiums I'd had since 2017.

Day 30. Seedlings! Everything has come up except the lettuce, which has come up and been eaten by something already.

Day 58. Potted the tomatoes on into the long planter on the balcony. Six plants. Named none of them, which shows restraint.

A rainy Sunday in May: no gardening, so I watched a short film about a girl and the spirits of a forest instead, with the rain going on the balcony and the tomatoes loving it.

::embed{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WhWc3b3KhnY"}

Kept it going a second summer, in 2021, smaller, with the packing already in my head. The last tomatoes came in the week before I left. I gave the plants to the family across the landing, canes and all, and two small boys swore to water them.
