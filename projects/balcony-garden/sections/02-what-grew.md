summary: The verdicts, crop by crop
---

The final tally for Leith, summer 2020, with marks out of ten:

- **Tomatoes**: 9
  - Tigerella: stripy, sharp, about forty fruit from three plants
  - Gardener's Delight: sweet, small, prolific, slightly boring
- **Basil**: 7, on the windowsill only; the balcony basil died of Scotland
- **Radishes**: 6, for quantity rather than joy
  - the first sowing was lovely
  - the next four sowings were a mistake I kept making
- **Lettuce**: 1, eaten by pigeons, or possibly by Biscuit
- **Strawberries**: 10 for the three I got, 0 for the effort
- **Nasturtiums**: 8, from my old packet in the swap, which came back to me as seedlings from downstairs and flowered orange all August

Lessons: fewer radishes; net the lettuce; Tigerella every year forever.
